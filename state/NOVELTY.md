**Verdict vocabulary.**
- **NONE FOUND**: no prior statement was found in the searches listed. This is [E for novelty]: a negative search, not a proof of priority.
- **PRIOR ART**: an earlier source states the result. The citation and equation are given, and the campaign text must credit it.
- **PRIOR ART (formula)**: the formula is in the literature and only the application is the campaign's.

**Search protocol** (all searches 2026-11-01).
- arXiv: `lit.py arxiv --raw` over title and abstract, Boolean query exactly as written, top 15 results.
- INSPIRE: title searches (`t "…"`, sorted most recent, top 25), and citation walks `refersto:recid:<recid of anchor>`. A walk lists every citing record and all titles were screened.
- OpenAlex `works`: returned only irrelevant top-cited records, then HTTP 429 (rate limit). It was not counted as a search.
- Raw output: scratchpad battery*.json, recorded in returns/R17/CW8-LIT-log.md §C.
- "n" is the number of hits returned. Every listed title was screened. A full abstract or full text was read only for the candidates named in the table.
