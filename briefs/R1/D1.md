# Round R1, derivation brief D1 (golden-rule route)

## Target
Derive the one-phonon-assisted DM–electron scattering rate in a crystal, from the electron–ion Hamiltonian
up to a formula evaluable from first-principles quantities. This is items T1, T2a, T2b and T3a/b of
STATEMENT.md.

## Route
Time-dependent perturbation theory, second order: one DM–electron vertex and one electron–phonon vertex.
Start from H = H_e(r; R) + H_ion + H_χ + H_int in the Born–Oppenheimer harmonic basis. Work in the
many-body Slater-determinant (Kohn–Sham quasiparticle) basis, not a one-electron caricature, and show
explicitly what happens to Pauli-blocked intermediate states (the hole-scattering paths). You may use
standard textbook results (Fermi's golden rule, DFPT, EPW conventions) without derivation, but state them.

## Required content
1. **Amplitude.** M^{(2)}_{cvν}(k,q,Q): both time orderings, intermediate-band sums, energy denominators
   with ω and ω_{Qν} kept, and momentum conservation including Umklapp. Fix the sign conventions and record
   them so that NOTATION.md can be updated.
2. **Rate.**
   - Γ(v) and dR/dω per unit target mass: spin factor, occupations, emission (n+1) and absorption (n), DM
     velocity integral.
   - Fix the normalisation of S(q,ω) and of σ̄_e so that the zero-phonon limit reproduces the standard
     crystal form-factor rate. Show it.
3. **Response function.**
   - Express the result as ε₂^{PA}(q,ω), so that the total rate uses L = ε₂/(ε₁²+ε₂²).
   - Argue (DERIVED or CONJECTURED, label it) when screening by the static-lattice ε₁ is correct.
4. **Adiabatic form.**
   - Show the relation to the derivative of the frozen-phonon transition density (DFPT first-order
     wavefunctions), with its error.
   - Point out what DFPT's usual projection onto the conduction manifold misses.
5. **Well-definedness.**
   - Resonances (an intermediate state on shell), double counting with first-order transitions, and the
     O(g²) zero-phonon renormalisation (Debye–Waller, zero-point renormalisation).
   - State a precise prescription for when the one-phonon sideband is a separately computable quantity.
6. **Controls C1, C2, C5, C7.**
   - C1: take q → 0 and compare factor by factor with your best statement of the standard phonon-assisted
     optical ε₂.
   - C7: show the acoustic Q → 0 limit (the result may need an acoustic sum rule on g; say which).
7. **Computable recipe.** Wannier/EPW variables: what to interpolate, grids, and how to get ρ_{mn}(k,q)
   at |q| up to a few keV (Umklapp included). Give the cost scaling.

## Forecast (to notice surprises)
Two path families with denominators of order (ε_m − ε_c) and (ε_m − ε_v), up to ω_{Qν}. The q → 0 limit
gives the known indirect-absorption formula with q·r in place of e·r. A surprise would be a third term,
or a non-cancelling Pauli term.
