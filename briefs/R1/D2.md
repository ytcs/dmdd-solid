and the DM–electron scattering rate) of a crystalline insulator at lowest non-trivial order in the

## Route
Kubo / fluctuation–dissipation. Write S(q,ω) as the imaginary part of the retarded density–density
correlator of the coupled electron–phonon system. Expand to O(g²) in the electron–phonon vertex. Identify
the diagrams: self-energy insertions, vertex correction, and phonon-exchange contributions. Then apply
cutting rules / the optical theorem, and identify which cuts give real one-phonon-emission or absorption
final states (the sideband) and which renormalise the zero-phonon line. Do NOT use a golden-rule
amplitude derivation as your primary route; an independent agent is doing that, and the two will be
compared term by term.

## Required content
1. **Diagrams.** The O(g²) diagrams for Π(q,ω), using EPW-convention g (NOTATION.md), with all
   prefactors and the sign conventions recorded.
2. **Cuts.** The one-phonon cut contribution, written in the form Σ |amplitude|² δ(energy) with the
   amplitude made explicit. Also the pieces that do not have this form: zero-phonon line renormalisation,
   Debye–Waller, and Fan terms.
3. **Sum rule.** Show whether the f-sum rule is preserved at O(g²) when all diagrams are kept (C4), and
   what happens if only the sideband is kept.
4. **Screening.**
   - The RPA/TDDFT-type screening of the phonon-assisted polarization: when L = ε₂/|ε|² holds with ε₁ from
     the static lattice.
   - Whether the electron–phonon vertex should itself be screened (DFPT ΔV_SCF), and why, without double
     screening.
5. **Dark-photon extension.** The DM couples to the total charge density, electrons plus ions (T2c).
   Which extra diagrams appear, and do they matter for a non-polar crystal (Si) versus a polar one?
6. **Controls C1 (optical limit), C4, C7, C8.**
7. **Temperature.** T-dependence through Bose factors.

## Forecast
The one-phonon cut gives |A₁ + A₂|², with A₁ and A₂ the two orderings. The sideband weight is exactly
compensated by the Debye–Waller reduction of the zero-phonon weight in the f-sum. A surprise would be a
non-factorisable cut contribution (interference between the sideband and the zero-phonon line).
