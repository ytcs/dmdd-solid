## Target

   (or any particle: neutrinos, electrons in EELS, X-rays in inelastic X-ray scattering) by electrons in a
   crystal, where an interband transition is completed by phonon emission or absorption at finite momentum
   transfer q. This means a phonon-assisted dynamic structure factor S(q,ω) or ε₂(q,ω) at q ≠ 0.
   - A clean priority check needs: EELS / IXS phonon-assisted indirect-gap loss theory; DM papers; and
     theses.
   - Verdict: PRIOR (cite, with the exact equation), PARTIAL (what is covered and what is not), or NONE.
2. **Lemma map.** For the derivation, extract from full texts the exact formulas, conventions and
   prefactors for:
   - (a) first-principles phonon-assisted optical absorption: Noffsinger et al. arXiv 1202.3454; Zacharias,
     Patrick & Giustino arXiv 1510.06352; Zacharias & Giustino arXiv 1604.02394 (special displacements);
     the EPW code paper (Lee et al., npj Comput. Mater. 2023, s41524-023-01107-3);
   - (b) DM–electron rates in the dielectric formalism: Knapen–Kozaczuk–Lin arXiv 2101.08275; Hochberg et
     al. arXiv 2101.08263. Give their rate formula with exact normalisation of σ̄_e, F_DM and S or ELF.
   - (c) the classic indirect-absorption formula (Elliott / Bardeen–Blatt–Hall / Macfarlane et al.):
     α(ω) ∝ (ω − E_g ± ω_ph)² with Bose factors, plus measured low-temperature Si indirect-absorption data
     (phonon energies of the TA, LA, TO assisting phonons, the absorption coefficient α(ω) near the edge at
     ≤ 20 K, and E_g(T→0) = 1.17 eV), and the refractive index n(ω) near 1.1–1.5 eV.
   - (d) The data-driven ELF approaches. Does Hochberg et al. 2101.08263, or DarkELF's Si ELF, populate
     the region E_g < ω < E_dir at small q? With what data or calculation? Do they report reach for
     m_χ < 0.5 MeV in Si?
   - (e) Si sub-MeV reach as computed by EXCEED-DM / QCDark / DarkELF: the lowest m_χ shown, and the
     threshold assumptions (1 e⁻ / 2 e⁻ bins, ionization yield model).
