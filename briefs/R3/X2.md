## Target

DFPT-screened g and an external 1/ε(q,ω). Both referees found that this omits a class of Coulomb-proper
lines carrying the electron–hole pair's momentum and energy (q+Q, ω∓ω_Q). REF-A argues from the
rigid-shift limit that the screening should be evaluated at ε(q, ω∓ω_Q).

- (b) Give the final formula usable in a code, e.g. as screened vertices ρ̃ = ρ/ε(...) at specific

- (d) Decide REF-A's ε(q, ω∓ω_Q) claim: exact, approximate (to what order in ω_Q/ω), or wrong.

- (f) Treat the dark-photon (total charge) coupling in the same framework: does the ionic polarisability
  enter, and is it negligible in Si?
