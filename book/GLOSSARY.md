# GLOSSARY (term → definition → defining section)

The book's glossary: one row per term. Columns: Term, Definition and Defined in (the section that defines the term). A term
defined in a section and derived in App. D gives the appendix in parentheses, e.g. "6.3 (D.16)"; a term whose practical
use is treated in App. E gives that section as "practice E.n", e.g. "5.7 (practice E.8)"; a term with an early working
definition gives both sections. "(op.)" after a term marks an operational row whose precise definition is in another row.
