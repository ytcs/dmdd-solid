\chapter{Phonons from first principles}\label{chap:26}

Every number that \cref{chap:5} attaches to the lattice of silicon, from the \SI{15.5}{THz} optical phonon at the zone center~\cite{nilsson1972study} to the Debye--Waller exponent at a momentum transfer of tens of keV, comes out of one kind of calculation: the first-order change of the Kohn--Sham potential when the nuclei of one species are displaced in a wave of crystal momentum $\vect{Q}$. For dark matter the result matters at both ends of the spectrum: the sound speeds that set the reach of acoustic-phonon searches (\cref{sec:9.2}) and the optical branches of polar crystals (\cref{sec:5.5}) all come out of density-functional perturbation theory (DFPT). The same linear-response equations that give a phonon also give, with an electric field or a strain in place of the displacement, the Born charges, $\varepsilon_\infty$ and the elastic constants, and, extended slightly, the changes of the empty states that a phonon-assisted amplitude needs (\cref{sec:13.2}). The calculation is for a harmonic crystal on a static lattice; the anharmonic and finite-temperature methods of \cref{app:E.8} all start from its harmonic phonons, Born charges and electron--phonon potentials.

\section*{Summary}

Force constants are second derivatives of the Born--Oppenheimer energy, obtained from finite differences of forces in a supercell or from the linear response of the electrons to a displacement wave. A frozen-phonon supercell of $N_Q^3$ cells gives the exact phonons on an $N_Q^3$ grid of wavevectors and nothing more, at a cost that grows as $L^6$ to $L^9$ with the range $L$ of the force constants. DFPT solves the Sternheimer equation for the change of the occupied states, with no sum over empty states, and one perturbation costs about as much as the ground-state density; by the $2n+1$ theorem the first-order orbitals also fix the cubic constants. Fourier interpolation with the acoustic sum rule turns dynamical matrices on a coarse grid into the whole dispersion. The same runs give the Born charges and $\varepsilon_\infty$, through the commutator with its nonlocal term, the potential changes that the electron--phonon vertex needs, and, by finite strain, the elastic constants. In silicon the $\vect{k}$ grid is fixed by the requirement that the Born charges vanish, as symmetry demands, and not by the frequencies. The quasi-harmonic and self-consistent harmonic approximations, TDEP, Allen--Heine--Cardona theory and special displacements all start from these harmonic phonons. For the zero-point renormalization of the silicon gap the last two give $-56$ and \SI{-57}{meV} at fixed volume, about \SI{20}{\percent} short of the electron--phonon part, $-71$ to \SI{-73}{meV}, that the measurement implies once the lattice expansion is removed.
