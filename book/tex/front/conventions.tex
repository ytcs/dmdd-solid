\chapter*{Conventions and units}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conventions and units}
We use natural units, $\hbar = c = k_{\mathrm{B}} = 1$, unless stated otherwise, and quote physical values in conventional units with SI prefixes.

A widely used formula from the literature appears in a gray box, a Limit Check, that plots it against the book's more complete result, with the range of agreement shaded; \cref{sec:9.4} explains how a box is read, and \cref{app:F} lists the most used of these formulas with their source equations and the sizes of their leading corrections.
