\section{BCS gap and coherence factors}\label{app:D.20}

\subsection*{The mean field}

In the reduced Hamiltonian \cref{eq:7.2-HBCS}, with $\xi_{\vect{k}} = \xi_{-\vect{k}}$, write each pair operator as its average plus a fluctuation, $c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow} = b_{\vect{k}} + (c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow} - b_{\vect{k}})$, and drop the product of two fluctuations, which vanishes relative to the kept terms as $\Vol \to \infty$ (\cref{sec:7.2}). What remains is
\begin{equation*}
  H_{\mathrm{MF}} = {\sum_{\vect{k}}}'\Bigl[\xi_{\vect{k}}\bigl(c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow} + c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}\bigr) - \Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}\bigl(c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow} + c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow}\bigr)\Bigr] + \frac{\Vol\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}^2}{V_{\mathrm{BCS}}} ,
\end{equation*}
plus the unpaired states outside the shell. For each $\vect{k}$ this is \cref{eq:C.7-Hfermion} with $c_1 = c_{\vect{k}\uparrow}$, $c_2 = c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}$, $\xi_0 = \xi_{\vect{k}}$ and $g_0 = \Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}$. By \cref{app:C.7}, the operators \cref{eq:7.2-gamma} with $u^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}} = \cos\theta_{\vect{k}}$, $v^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}} = \sin\theta_{\vect{k}}$, are fermions, and the pair terms drop out of $H_{\mathrm{MF}}$ when $\tan2\theta_{\vect{k}} = \Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}/\xi_{\vect{k}}$. Then $\cos2\theta_{\vect{k}} = \xi_{\vect{k}}/E_{\vect{k}}$ and $\sin2\theta_{\vect{k}} = \Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}/E_{\vect{k}}$, which gives \cref{eq:7.2-E}. The quasiparticle $\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}1}$ carries momentum $\vect{k}$ and spin up, $\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}$ momentum $-\vect{k}$ and spin down.

The ground state, annihilated by all $\gamma$, is the BCS state \cref{eq:7.2-BCS}. To see this, note that $\gamma_{\vect{k}1}$ commutes with the factors of the product at other wavevectors, which contain fermion operators in pairs, and act on the factor at $\vect{k}$. With $u = u^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}}$, $v = v^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}}$, $c_{\vect{k}\uparrow}|0\rangle = 0$, $c_{\vect{k}\uparrow}c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}|0\rangle = c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}|0\rangle$ and $(c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow})^2 = 0$,
\begin{equation*}
  \bigl(uc_{\vect{k}\uparrow} - vc^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}\bigr)\bigl(u + vc^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}\bigr)|0\rangle = uv\,c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}|0\rangle - vu\,c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}|0\rangle = 0 .
\end{equation*}
In the same way $\gamma_{\vect{k}2}$ yields $-uv\,c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}|0\rangle + vu\,c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}|0\rangle = 0$, the minus sign from moving $c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}$ past $c^\dagger_{\vect{k}\uparrow}$.

\subsection*{The gap equation}

Inverting \cref{eq:7.2-gamma}, $c_{\vect{k}\uparrow} = u\gamma_{\vect{k}1} + v\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}$ and $c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow} = u\gamma_{\vect{k}2} - v\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}1}$, with the subscripts on $u$ and $v$ omitted. In thermal equilibrium the quasiparticles are independent fermions of energy $E_{\vect{k}}$, so $\langle\gamma^\dagger\gamma\rangle = n_{\mathrm{F}}(E_{\vect{k}})$, and
\begin{equation*}
  b_{\vect{k}} = \langle c_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow}\rangle = uv\bigl[\langle\gamma_{\vect{k}2}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}\rangle - \langle\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}1}\gamma_{\vect{k}1}\rangle\bigr] = uv\,[1 - 2n_{\mathrm{F}}(E_{\vect{k}})] = \frac{\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}}{2E_{\vect{k}}}\tanh\frac{E_{\vect{k}}}{2T} .
\end{equation*}
Inserted into \cref{eq:7.2-Delta}, this gives the gap equation of \cref{eq:7.2-gap}, whose right side decreases with both $\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}$ and $T$. At $T = 0$ the integral is $\operatorname{arsinh}(\omega_{\mathrm{D}}/\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}})$, and
\begin{equation*}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}(0) = \frac{\omega_{\mathrm{D}}}{\sinh[1/(\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}})]} \simeq 2\omega_{\mathrm{D}}\,\ee^{-1/(\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}})} ,
\end{equation*}
the exponential form being valid for weak coupling, $\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}} \ll 1$.

\subsection*{The critical temperature}

At $T_c$ the gap closes, and the gap equation with $\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}} = 0$ fixes $T_c$:
\begin{equation*}
  \frac{1}{\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}}} = \int_0^{\omega_{\mathrm{D}}}\dd\xi\,\frac{\tanh(\xi/2T_c)}{\xi} .
\end{equation*}
The integral is evaluated through the Matsubara expansion of the hyperbolic tangent. The fermionic form of \cref{eq:C.5-matsum} with $g(z) = 1/(x^2 - z^2)$, whose poles at $z = \pm x$ have residues $\mp1/2x$, gives $T\sum_j1/(x^2 + (\omega^{\mathrm{F}}_j)^2) = [n_{\mathrm{F}}(-x) - n_{\mathrm{F}}(x)]/2x = \tanh(x/2T)/2x$. Then
\begin{equation*}
  \int_0^{\omega_{\mathrm{D}}}\dd\xi\,\frac{\tanh(\xi/2T)}{\xi} = 2T\sum_j\int_0^{\omega_{\mathrm{D}}}\frac{\dd\xi}{\xi^2 + (\omega^{\mathrm{F}}_j)^2}
  = \sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{2}{\pi}\,\frac{\arctan[X/(j + \frac12)]}{j + \frac12}, \qquad X = \frac{\omega_{\mathrm{D}}}{2\pi T} ,
\end{equation*}
where the terms $j$ and $-j - 1$ were combined and $\omega^{\mathrm{F}}_j = 2\pi T(j + \frac12)$. Split $(2/\pi)\arctan(X/y) = \Theta(X - y) + r(y/X)$, with $r(t) = (2/\pi)\arctan(1/t) - \Theta(1 - t)$. The step function cuts the sum at $j + \frac12 < X$. The remainder, for $X \gg 1$, becomes the integral $\int_0^\infty\dd t\,r(t)/t$, which vanishes. On $t < 1$, $r(t) = -(2/\pi)\arctan t$; on $t > 1$, $r(t) = (2/\pi)\arctan(1/t)$; and the substitution $t \to 1/t$ maps one piece onto minus the other. The truncated sum is a sum over odd integers, $\sum_{j=0}^{J}(j + \frac12)^{-1} = 2(H_{2J+2} - \frac12H_{J+1})$ with the harmonic numbers $H_n = \sum_{m=1}^n1/m = \ln n + \gamma_{\mathrm{E}} + O(1/n)$, which gives $\ln J + \gamma_{\mathrm{E}} + 2\ln2$ for $J \simeq X \gg 1$. Altogether the integral is $\ln(4\ee^{\gamma_{\mathrm{E}}}X) = \ln(2\ee^{\gamma_{\mathrm{E}}}\omega_{\mathrm{D}}/\pi T)$, with corrections of order $(T/\omega_{\mathrm{D}})^2$, and
\begin{equation*}
  T_c = \frac{2\ee^{\gamma_{\mathrm{E}}}}{\pi}\,\omega_{\mathrm{D}}\,\ee^{-1/(\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}})} = 1.134\,\omega_{\mathrm{D}}\,\ee^{-1/(\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{F}}V_{\mathrm{BCS}})} .
\end{equation*}
Dividing $\Delta_{\mathrm{BCS}}(0)$ by $T_c$, the unknown $\omega_{\mathrm{D}}$ and the exponential cancel, which gives the ratio of \cref{eq:7.2-gap}.

\subsection*{Coherence factors}

For the density $n_e(\vect{q})$ and the spin density $s^z_e(\vect{q})$ of \cref{sec:7.2}, write $u = u^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}}$, $v = v^{\mathrm{B}}_{\vect{k}}$ and primes for $\vect{k}' = \vect{k} + \vect{q}$. With the inverted \cref{eq:7.2-gamma},
\begin{equation*}
  c^\dagger_{\vect{k}'\uparrow}c_{\vect{k}\uparrow} = u'u\,\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma_{\vect{k}1} + u'v\,\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2} + v'u\,\gamma_{\vect{k}'2}\gamma_{\vect{k}1} + v'v\,\gamma_{\vect{k}'2}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2} .
\end{equation*}
For the spin-down part, relabel $\vect{k} \to -\vect{k} - \vect{q}$, so that $\sum_{\vect{k}}c^\dagger_{\vect{k}+\vect{q}\downarrow}c_{\vect{k}\downarrow} = \sum_{\vect{k}}c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{-\vect{k}'\downarrow}$, and expand:
\begin{equation*}
  c^\dagger_{-\vect{k}\downarrow}c_{-\vect{k}'\downarrow} = uu'\,\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}\gamma_{\vect{k}'2} - uv'\,\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1} - vu'\,\gamma_{\vect{k}1}\gamma_{\vect{k}'2} + vv'\,\gamma_{\vect{k}1}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1} .
\end{equation*}
Collect the terms by process. The term that creates two quasiparticles, $\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}$, comes with $u'v$ from spin up and, after $-\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1} = \gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}$, with $uv'$ from spin down. The term that scatters a quasiparticle from $\vect{k}$ to $\vect{k}'$, $\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma_{\vect{k}1}$, comes with $uu'$ from spin up and, after $\gamma_{\vect{k}1}\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1} = -\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}'1}\gamma_{\vect{k}1}$ for $\vect{q} \ne 0$, with $-vv'$ from spin down. The density adds the two spins and the spin density subtracts them, which gives \cref{eq:7.2-coh}. The scattering of the second branch, $\gamma^\dagger_{\vect{k}2}\gamma_{\vect{k}'2}$, has the same factors. Expressed through the energies, with \cref{eq:7.2-E}, the squares are \cref{eq:7.2-cohE}.
