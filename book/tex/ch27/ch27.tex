\chapter{Dielectric response and form factors}\label{chap:27}

An electron-recoil rate in silicon needs a loss function out to \SI{100}{eV} and density vertices at momentum transfers of tens of keV, and neither is measured everywhere it is needed. The independent-particle form factor is where every electronic rate of \cref{chap:10} starts, and screening and local fields use the same vertices as input. For silicon the screening ($\varepsilon_\infty$ of 13.0 computed against 11.7 measured) and the vertex beyond $q \sim 1/r_c$, with $r_c$ the core radius of the pseudopotential (\cref{sec:3.5}), are two of the dominant theory uncertainties.
