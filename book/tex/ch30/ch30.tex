\chapter{Electronic and mixed channels}\label{chap:30}

The phonon-assisted electron-recoil rate of silicon between 0.333 and \SI{0.405}{MeV} is known to a factor of ten (\cref{sec:13.9}), and below $2q_{\mathrm{BZ}}$ the coherent zero-phonon part of its Migdal rate is not computed at all (\cref{sec:12.4}). This chapter and the two after it specify, quantity by quantity, the calculation behind each number that \crefrange{chap:9}{chap:22} use. The specifications share one rule: each calculation is accepted against an exact limit or sum rule and, wherever one exists, a measurement made at the same kinematics, never against the estimate it replaces. Each is placed on the truncation label $\mathfrak{T}$ of \cref{sec:8.5}. The electronic channels come first, with those that mix electrons with phonons or with the motion of a nucleus.
