\chapter{Lattice and special targets}\label{chap:31}

One harmonic DFPT calculation per crystal, about 100 core-hours for silicon, supplies every lattice channel of \cref{chap:11} for silicon, germanium and gallium arsenide (\cref{app:E.17}). The anharmonic and disordered solids of \cref{sec:11.5} and the targets of \cref{chap:14,chap:15,chap:16} have no such common base. Their specifications cost from CPU minutes, for the conserving response of clean aluminum and the ejection factors of graphene, to $10^5$ to $10^6$ hours on graphics processors for the two-level systems of a glass. At millikelvin the response of a quantum crystal is bounded only by the band of its exact moments (\cref{app:E.19}).
