                                                                        we mean a set of localized orthonormal orbitals spanning the same space as thePspecified set of
                                                                        Bloch bands. Although we minimize a functional that represents the total spread     n

                                                                        represented on a mesh of k-points, and carries out the minimization in a space of unitary matrices
