                                                                  Within the framework of ab initio Time Dependent-Density Functional Theory (TD-DFT), we
                                                                propose a static approximation to the exchange-correlation kernel based on the jellium-with-gap
                                                                model. This kernel accounts for electron-hole interactions and it is able to address both strongly
                                                                bound excitons and weak excitonic effects. TD-DFT absorption spectra of several bulk materials
                                                                (both semiconductor and insulators) are reproduced in very good agreement with the experiments
                                                                and with a low computational cost.
