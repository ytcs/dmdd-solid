                                            Sound of Dark Matter: Searching for Light Scalars with Resonant-Mass Detectors

                                                         The fine-structure constant and the electron mass in string theory are determined by the values of
                                                       scalar fields called moduli. If the dark matter takes on the form of such a light modulus, it oscillates
                                                       with a frequency equal to its mass and an amplitude determined by the local dark-matter density.
                                                       This translates into an oscillation of the size of a solid that can be observed by resonant-mass
                                                       antennas. Existing and planned experiments, combined with a dedicated resonant-mass detector
                                                       proposed in this Letter, can probe dark-matter moduli with frequencies between 1 kHz and 1 GHz,
                                                       with much better sensitivity than searches for fifth forces.
