                                                                 Hubbard parameters from density-functional perturbation theory

                                                                                     Iurii Timrov, Nicola Marzari, and Matteo Cococcioni
                                                                                Theory and Simulation of Materials (THEOS), and National Centre for
                                                                                 Computational Design and Discovery of Novel Materials (MARVEL),
                                                                         École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), CH-1015 Lausanne, Switzerland
                                                                                                         (Dated: August 17, 2018)
                                                                   We present a transparent and computationally efficient approach for the first-principles calculation
                                                                of Hubbard parameters from linear-response theory. This approach is based on density-functional
                                                                perturbation theory and the use of monochromatic perturbations. In addition to delivering much
                                                                improved efficiency, the present approach makes it straightforward to calculate automatically these
                                                                Hubbard parameters for any given system, with tight numerical control on convergence and precision.
arXiv:1805.01805v2 [cond-mat.str-el] 16 Aug 2018




                                                                The effectiveness of the method is showcased in three case studies – Cu2 O, NiO, and LiCoO2 – and
                                                                by the direct comparison with finite differences in supercell calculations.
