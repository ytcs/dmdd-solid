                                                                     In this work we derive a ready-to-use symmetry invariant expansion of the full-zone 30-band k·p
                                                                  Hamiltonian for the Td point group. In order to find respective parameters, the band structures of
                                                                  III-V materials were calculated within a state-of-the-art Density Functional Theory (DFT) approach
                                                                  and used next as targets to adjust the k·p bands. A satisfactory agreement of the k·p model with
                                                                  the DFT band structures, for all the tested zinc blende III-V semiconductors, has been achieved.
                                                                  Values of many of the parameters have not been known so far. We also compare the fitted k · p
                                                                  parameters with the values calculated using momentum matrix elements obtained directly from the
                                                                  DFT.
