                                                       First-Principles Calculations of the Electron-Hole-Pair Creation Energy in Semiconductors and
                                                                                                   Dielectrics
                                                                                                 Dallin O. Nielsen and Massimo V. Fischetti
                                                                               Department of Materials Science and Engineering, The University of Texas at Dallas,
                                                                                             800 W. Campbell Rd., Richardson, Texas 75080, USA
                                                                                                          (*dallin.nielsen@utdallas.edu)
                                                                                                            (Dated: October 14, 2026)
                                                                      We present first-principles calculations of the electron-hole-pair creation energy in semiconductors and di-
                                                                   electrics, focusing in particular on AlN, 3C-SiC, 4H-SiC, Si, and SiO2 . We have employed density functional
                                                                   theory (DFT) to calculate the band structure, density-functional perturbation theory to obtain the phonon dis-
arXiv:2610.16949v1 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] 12 Oct 2026




                                                                   persion, and time-dependent DFT (TDDFT) to calculate the electron energy loss function (via the dielectric
                                                                   function). We performed full-band Monte Carlo simulations of electron thermalization for energies up to
                                                                   ∼ 100 eV and calculated the average energy required to excite an electron-hole pair. We calculated pair-creation
                                                                   energies of approximately 12.2, 6.5, 7.3, 3.10, and 16.7 eV/pair for AlN, 3C-SiC, 4H-SiC, Si, and SiO2 , re-
                                                                   spectively. These values are in good agreement with measured data, with the largest discrepancy being that
                                                                   of Si (about 14.9% lower than the accepted experimental value of ∼ 3.6 eV/pair). To improve the agreement
                                                                   for Si, we used the GW approach and the Bethe-Salpeter equation (BSE) to calculate the quasiparticle shifts
                                                                   of the electronic band structure and the dielectric function, respectively. Finally, using the GW/BSE data, we
                                                                   calculated a creation energy of ∼ 3.50 eV/pair. We find that the marked improvement in the accuracy of the
                                                                   calculated creation energy, when using the GW/BSE approach, is primarily attributable to the slight decrease
                                                                   in the magnitude of the electron energy-loss function when using the GW/BSE approach for energies below
                                                                   ∼ 10-15 eV. Overall, it is clear that the competition between phonon emission and ionization near the intersection
                                                                   of the phonon scattering and ionization rates is largely responsible for determining the value of the creation
                                                                   energy, with the other major factor being the location and shape of the plasmon peak in the energy-loss function.
