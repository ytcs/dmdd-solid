Reviewer: derivation verifier (isolated), 2026-11-06. Brief: briefs/reviews/ch21-derivation-1.md.
Method: every non-definitional step redone. Symbolic checks in sympy: the eta_2 recursion, the integration by parts, the debris-flow F and eta, the beta-function width integral, the lens volume, the speed convolution, dm_edge/dv_max, the zero-phonon envelope derivatives, the lab and galactic speed expansions, and the edge coefficient A_eta. Verdicts: CHECKED (correct as printed), WRONG (needs a change; the correction is given), GAP (correct but incomplete, unproved or inconsistent).

## Summary
- No error in any key result. These hold as printed: the linear functional and kernel (21.1-functional/-kernel), the eta form, eta_2 fixed by eta, the speed-only bound and its exactness with a single-bin certificate (21.2-hib, D.46-hib), the defect bound, the final-state leakage bound, the edge power law p_on + k_tail + 7/2 (+4 at a corner), the lens construction, and the onset exponents 5/2, 2, 1.
- All headline numbers reproduce: 1.599e5 (both forms), the floors, the June shift, the debris-flow worked example, Limit Check 21.1a, x2.22/x3.52, 0.034, the x62 year-average edge, and the gap-shift worked example.
- WRONG (all small): the 73 meV vs 6.6 % pairing (21.3); the "one percent error in the phonon energy" sentence (21.3); the 10 % boundary definition in Limit Check 21.1a; bare-symbol clashes (delta four ways, c as a stream mass); the scope of the limit-box attribution (21.2a); omega_max = 64.1 against the NOTATION 63.7.
- Main recommendation: the step-model pair bins (1.11/3.6 eV) drive the narrative of worked example 21.2. The book's own Ch18 cascade changes which bin binds. Switch to the Ch18 energy-only cascade, quantified below.

## Recommendation on the pair bins (21.2 example search; same bins in 21.5)
- The bound eq:21.2-hib holds for any P(i\|Lambda). The step model affects only the example, but the example is wrong in a way the book itself documents. Sect. 18.3 (we:18-step5) shows that the step model overcounts the two-pair bin at 5 eV by x5.9, about x10 with the full-band cascade (SOTA: >= 6x robust). In worked example 21.2 that bin is the tightest at 10 MeV.
