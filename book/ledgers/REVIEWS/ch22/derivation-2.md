6. GAP, s22-3 l.65 (description of eq:22.3-fermi): calling it "the point-charge solution of the Dirac equation evaluated at r_A" is not exact. Per Hayen et al. (Eq. 6 and the text after Eq. 9), the point-charge Dirac solution at R carries 2(1+γ) (Konopinski–Uhlenbeck). The form with 4 is Fermi's traditional F₀, with (1+γ)/2 moved into L₀. So Dror's 2(1+γ_d) *is* the point-charge-at-R form, and the text should not suggest that the 4 form is. Fix: "the traditional point-charge Fermi function F₀ [Eq. (6)]{hayen2018high}; evaluating the point-charge Dirac solution at r_A replaces 4 by 2(1+γ_d), and the finite-size factor L₀ takes the difference". Numbers: (1+γ_d)/2 = 0.961 at Z_d = 53, so F = 6.09 in that convention against 6.34.
7. GAP, s22-3 l.65 ("larger by Γ(1+2γ)²/Γ(1+γ)²"): this ratio holds only at a fixed prefactor convention. Against eq:22.3-fermi as printed (prefactor 4), Dror's function is larger by 3.10 at Z_d = 53, not 3.22. Write "larger, at its own prefactor, by …". The numbers 4.0 (Z_d = 1) and 3.2 (Z_d = 53) are then CHECKED (3.9997, 3.2241).
8. WRONG (rounding), s22-3 l.74: "p_ej alone falls to 0.10 and 0.032". At 1 keV the value is 31.98/1015.7 = 0.03149, so it should be **0.031**. The 0.10 is correct (0.1000).
9. GAP, s22-3 l.68 (WE: "leading correction of order (p r_A)² ≈ 10⁻³"): this is the leading correction to the allowed *operator* expansion only. The Coulomb finite-size terms enter at first order, αZ_dWR = 0.387 × 2.225 × 0.0155 = 1.3 %, and L₀ − 1 is of order a few % (Hayen: "a few 0.1 % up to several %"). Both are larger than 10⁻³ and comparable to the 4.3 % parent/daughter effect. Fix: "… of order (p_ej r_A)² ≈ 10⁻³ in the nuclear operators; the finite-size Coulomb correction L₀ is of order αZ_d W r_A ≈ 1 %". The same caveat applies to l.87, "error of using the parent charge is 4.3 %": that holds at a fixed Fermi-function convention.
