# Volume I end-to-end review: structural reader

Scope: tex/vol1.tex, the chapter drivers ch01 to ch08 (openings, map captions), the first paragraph of every section, every Summary, every table caption, front/plan.tex and front/conventions.tex, the contents of build/vol1.pdf (602 pp.), and E2E-vol1-dups.txt. Items 2 (order and threads), 4 (openings, maps, summaries) and 6 (volume shape) of E2E.md, plus places where a first-year reader (READER.md) would be lost or bored. Findings only; no tex file was edited. Ranked by importance. Line numbers are those of 2026-11-12. Block readers are editing at the same time (Sect. 8.8 titles changed while this was read), so check the text before you apply a fix. Printed numbers come from tools/volmap.py.
