#### ER-33 (tex/ch01/s1-3.tex, Sect. 1.3, speed integral)
Verdict: AMEND (physics of the proposal right; its wording is a five-deep PP chain, pattern 27).
Evidence: the argument is g(cos θ) = ω + Δχ + q²/2mχ − qv cos θ, so g' = −qv. By eq. (C.1) δ(g(x)) =
Σ δ(x − x_i)/|g'(x_i)| (tex/appC/C-1.tex:62) the factor is 1/|g'| = 1/qv, and the ½ of the angular average makes
the printed 1/(2qv). The current text calls 1/qv the derivative itself, which is wrong by an inverse and a sign.
The proposed "the inverse modulus of the derivative of the argument of the delta function with respect to cos θ"
is correct but stacks four prepositional phrases. The amendment opens on a short subject, names the derivative
once and points to it once (*this derivative*, one candidate); the following sentence "This factor is the origin of the factor $1/v$" still has one antecedent, the
factor $1/qv$.
- old: `The factor $1/qv$ is the derivative of the argument of the delta function with respect to $\cos\theta$.`
- new: `The argument of the delta function has the derivative $-qv$ with respect to $\cos\theta$, and the factor $1/qv$ is one over the modulus of this derivative (\cref{app:C.1}).`

#### ER-34 (tex/appE/D-30.tex, "Inversion with time reversal")
Verdict: ACCEPT.
Evidence: n_e(q) = Σ e^{−iq·r}. Time reversal takes n_e(q) to n_e(−q) and j_e(q) to −j_e(−q). Inversion
(r → −r, p → −p, j polar) takes n_e(−q) to n_e(q) and −j_e(−q) to +j_e(q). So PT leaves both rows even at the same
q, as the next sentence says. Inversion maps −q back to q; the text has the direction reversed.
- old: `inversion maps $\vect{q}$ back to $-\vect{q}$`
- new: `inversion maps $-\vect{q}$ back to $\vect{q}$`

#### ER-35 (tex/ch16/ch16.tex, chapter opening)
Verdict: AMEND (the correction holds; "its" has two singular candidates, the rate and the glass).
Evidence: eq:16.7-dp (s16-7.tex:91) gives R = (ρχ/ρT) ζ² (tan δ0/εr) tanh(mφ/2T), and s16-7.tex:94 says the rate
"is fixed by two measured numbers, the low-power loss tangent and the permittivity". A rate is not a loss tangent;
at fixed εr it is proportional to it. The factor seventeen is 5e-3/3e-4 = 16.7 (we:16-tls-dp, s16-9 table), a
ratio of loss tangents, so it survives the change. The amendment fronts the glass as a prepositional phrase and
drops the possessive, so "which" binds to the nearest noun, the measured loss tangent.
- old: `The dark-photon rate on a glass is its measured loss tangent, which changes seventeenfold between preparations,`
- new: `On a glass the dark-photon rate is proportional to the measured loss tangent, which changes seventeenfold between preparations,`

#### ER-36 (tex/ch11/s11-5.tex, section opening)
Verdict: ACCEPT.
Evidence: eq:11.5-moments (s11-5.tex:18) holds "for any potential V of the nuclei": μ0 = 1 and μ1 = E_R are
potential-independent values, but μ̄2 = 4E_R E^kin_κ, μ̄3 (curvature) and μ̄4 (force) are ground-state averages
that move with anharmonicity, as s11-4:100 and s11-5:102 say. Only the form of the expressions is fixed. In the
proposal "whose" has one plural candidate, the exact moments.
- old: `which anharmonicity cannot change.`
- new: `whose expressions hold for any potential of the nuclei.`

#### ER-37 (tex/ch18/s18-5.tex, charge readout of excitons)
Verdict: AMEND (physics right; in the proposal the final "it" follows a clause whose subject is "a uniform field").
Evidence: on a neutral pair the forces eE and −eE cancel in a uniform field, so there is no drift velocity. A
neutral pair that moves (diffusion, phonon wind) carries no net charge and, by Ramo's theorem, induces no net
charge on the electrodes. "drifts as a whole" is wrong; the conclusion is right. The amendment keeps both facts
and repeats the noun.
- old: `A bound pair is neutral and drifts as a whole, so it induces no charge on the electrodes.`
- new: `A bound pair is neutral, so a uniform field exerts no net force on the pair, and a moving pair induces no net charge on the electrodes.`

#### ER-38 (tex/ch20/s20-3.tex, acoustic mismatch, Si/Al)
Verdict: ACCEPT.
Evidence: the caption of fig:20-amm gives Si c_L = 9.00, c_T = 5.40 km/s; Al 6.63, 3.30 km/s. Al L (6.63) > Si T
(5.40), so "every wave … slower than every wave in silicon" is false. Per polarization it is true: 6.63 < 9.00,
3.30 < 5.40. Script check of the critical angles: arcsin(3.30/5.40) = 37.7°, arcsin(6.63/9.00) = 47.4°,
arcsin(3.30/9.00) = 21.5°, arcsin(3.30/6.63) = 29.9°, arcsin(5.40/9.00) = 36.9°: all match the caption, and none
exists for Al L → Si T, consistent with the survival of that conversion beyond 47.5°. s20-3:78 ("the slower
phonons of aluminum") is true per branch and stays.
- old: `From the aluminum side, however, every wave is slower than every wave in silicon, and the critical angles cut transmission off.`
- new: `From the aluminum side, however, each aluminum wave is slower than the silicon wave of the same polarization, and the critical angles cut transmission off.`

#### ER-39 (tex/ch23/D-4.tex, end of the Hartree-Fock gas)
Verdict: AMEND (correction right; "the screened interaction" is definite before introduction in this appendix,
so it gets its symbol).
Evidence: E-3.tex:30 splits Σ into a screened-exchange part (occupied states) and a Coulomb-hole part (all bands),
so the screened exchange is one part of the GW self-energy, not the whole. Σ = iGW is the Fock term Σx with vC
replaced by the dynamically screened W, so "replacing vC by the screened interaction leads to GW" is correct.
The amendment keeps the proposer's sentence and adds the book's symbol (s4-8.tex: "screened interaction
$W^{\mathrm{scr}}$"); the gerund subject stays short.
- old: `The screened exchange is the GW self-energy of \cref{sec:4.8}.`
- new: `Replacing $v_{\mathrm{C}}$ by the screened interaction $W^{\mathrm{scr}}$ leads to the GW self-energy of \cref{sec:4.8}, of which the screened exchange is one part.`

#### ER-40 (tex/ch29/E-12.tex, carrier transport)
Verdict: AMEND (count and section right; "G4CMP … fits them" misattributes the fit, which the user does).
Evidence: sources/review-appE/2302.05998/text.txt, Sect. 2.3 (Charge transport, l. 249–507), l. 409–416: "In total,
G4CMP uses six parameters …, two mean free paths for electron or hole trapping, respectively (Eqs. 13 and 14), and
four mean free paths for either electrons or holes to produce an additional electron or hole via impact

e/h × D/A TrapIonizationMFP (l. 984–991). Fig. 6 and l. 480 say the HVeV spectrum "can be used to fit for this
device's trapping and impact-ionization parameters". "four or six" occurs nowhere else in tex/. G4CMP is named
at E-12.tex:3, and the new sentence keeps the original opening on "these regions" (bound to the previous sentence).
1. - old: `Impurities enter as two processes, each with a mean free path that is fitted to the device.`
   - new: `Impurities enter as two processes, with mean free paths that are fitted to the device.`
2. - old: `The four or six mean free paths are fitted to the shape of these regions~\cite[Fig.~6]{kelsey2023g4cmp}.`
   - new: `The shape of these regions is fitted with the six mean free paths of G4CMP: two for the trapping of electrons and of holes, and four for impact ionization of donors or acceptors by electrons or holes~\cite[Sect.~2.3 and Fig.~6]{kelsey2023g4cmp}.`

#### ER-41 (tex/ch30/E-21.tex, disorder-averaged donor responses)

no grid.
- old: `The Bloch functions at the conduction minima, on the same grid, also give`
- new: `The Bloch functions at the conduction minima, computed at the shared \SI{60}{Ry} setting of \cref{app:E.17}, also give`
