## ER-31 (DERIVATION) Which moments bound the connected part of 𝔥_off, and what the aluminum number bounds

**The conflict.** ch08/s8-6.tex (paragraph after eq:8.6-bin) bounds the square of the connected part of 𝔥_off by the largest eigenvalue of the band matrix of zeroth moments of the *Fan–Migdal* (electron–phonon) spectral function, and concludes "so the connected part is at most about 0.17 eV" in aluminum. appD/D-28.tex ("Gapless targets") says "The electron–electron part adds the analogous sum over final states with two particles and a hole" and builds the matrix from both, then (as applied in ER-16) says "a connected part of at most 0.17 eV; the electron–electron moments are not estimated".

**Correct statement.**
1. Sect. 8.6 defines H = H_d + H_off with H_d = Σ_{n,m} P^qp_{n,m} H P^qp_{n,m}, the sectors counting dressed quasiparticles (n) and phonons (m). H_off therefore contains every term of H that changes n or m: the electron–phonon coupling (changes m) and the part of the Coulomb interaction that changes the quasiparticle number (one quasiparticle → two particles and a hole). D-28 is right that both belong in the bound; s8-6 is incomplete.
2. For the connected part acting on a quasiparticle |nk⟩, H_ep|nk⟩ has one phonon more or less and H_ee|nk⟩ has two particles and a hole and no extra phonon, so the two states are orthogonal and ‖H_off|nk⟩‖² = ‖H_ep|nk⟩‖² + ‖H_ee|nk⟩‖². Over the bands, the moment matrix is M = M_ep + M_ee with both positive semidefinite, and the connected part squared is at most λ_max(M) ≥ λ_max(M_ep).
3. Hence the aluminum value λ_max(M_ep) ≈ 0.03 eV² (5.39 meV / 0.193 eV⁻¹, Sect. 6.4) bounds only the electron–phonon part of the connected term, at about 0.17 eV. It is a floor, not a ceiling, on the quantity that bounds 𝔥_off². "The connected part is at most about 0.17 eV" is not supported in either place. No electron–electron number is in the book, and none is added.

### Review (stage 3)

**Verdict: AMEND.** The diagnosis is right and the 0.17 eV sentence cannot stand in either place. The new wording needs four fixes: it must say that the two-part split holds for H_off acting on a quasiparticle, give the reason for the orthogonality, keep one name for the quantity ("connected part", not also "connected term"), and say that the electron–electron entries cannot lower the eigenvalue.

What I checked:
- **What H_off contains.** s8-6:179 defines 𝒫^qp_{n,m} as the joint spectral projectors of the dressed numbers U_qp N_qp U_qp† and U_qp N_ph U_qp†, and H_d = Σ 𝒫 H 𝒫. H_off = H − H_d is therefore every block of H that changes n or m. The same sentence states that the projectors do not commute with H "since quasiparticles have a width Γ_qp", so the dressing does not remove the on-shell part of the Coulomb interaction that turns one quasiparticle into two particles and a hole (n → n + 2, m unchanged). That part is in H_off. D-28:97 already includes it ("The electron--electron part adds the analogous sum ..."), and s8-6:185 leaves it out. The proposal's point 1 holds.
- **Orthogonality.** e^{−iH_d t′} keeps a state inside its sector (n, m). H_ep is linear in the phonon operators, so on a quasiparticle it gives states in (·, m ± 1). The number-changing Coulomb part gives (n + 2, m). The phonon numbers differ, so the two states are orthogonal, the cross term ⟨H_ep ψ|H_ee ψ⟩ is zero, and the squared norms add. The Debye–Waller (quadratic) term has Δm = 0 or ±2. Its Δm = 0 piece does not change n and lies in H_d, and its Δm = ±2 piece is orthogonal to the Coulomb part as well. The cross-term argument needs ψ in one sector, which holds for the connected part acting on |nk⟩, the case the text treats. Over the bands M = M_ep + M_ee with both positive semidefinite. If v is the top eigenvector of M_ep, then λ_max(M) ≥ v†(M_ep + M_ee)v ≥ v†M_ep v = λ_max(M_ep). The electron–phonon entries bound the squared norms from above (D-28's factors are "at most 2n + 1"), and the electron–electron entries are D-28's own sum over final states with two particles and a hole, ‖H_ee|nk⟩‖². So "square ≤ λ_max(M)" is an upper bound. The amended text keeps that object and does not call it the moment of an electron–electron self-energy spectral function, because that identity holds only for the bare-vertex second-order self-energy and would add physics. √λ_max(M_ep) bounds only the electron–phonon contribution. "So the connected part is at most about 0.17 eV" does not follow and must go.
- **Aluminum arithmetic.** figscripts/ch06/fig6_6.log:12–13 gives `N_F Omega (per spin, per cell) = 0.1931 /eV` and `int a2F d omega = 5.39 meV`. 5.39e−3/0.1931 = 0.0279 eV², √ = 0.167 eV. With Savrasov's 0.2018 /eV (log:23) the values are 0.0267 eV² and 0.163 eV. At T = 0 the per-mode factor is (1 − f) + f = 1, and eq:6.4-a2F (s6-4:84, :87) gives ∫α²F dω/𝒩_FΩ for constant |g|². The numbers are unchanged.

- **Language and physics content.** No electron–electron number is added. The added physics is the split of H_off, the orthogonality and the eigenvalue inequality, which the derivation requires. Fixes against prose-review: "Here H_off has two parts" leaves the referent open (pattern 13), and the split holds only for H_off acting on the quasiparticle. "Both self-energy spectral functions" names an electron–electron spectral function that neither s8-6 nor D-28 introduces (D-28 has only the sum over final states), so the amended text uses D-28's object. "Connected term" beside "connected part" uses two terms for one quantity (clarity check: one term, one sense). "The two final-state sets are orthogonal" asserts orthogonality without its reason, and the D-28 insertion comes after the pair-source sentence, away from the sum it qualifies. "Not estimated" alone hides the sign of the omission, which the inequality fixes. One clause states it.
