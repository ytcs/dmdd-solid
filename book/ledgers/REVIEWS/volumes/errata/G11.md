#### ER-45 (tex/ch12/s12-6.tex:121, sec:12.6, and the last row of tab:12-nuisances, s12-6:151)
Verdict: an error, the same gap as ER-44. It needs a correction at both locations.

Evidence. s12-6:121 splits $c_\kappa/M_\kappa = \tilde c + \delta\tilde c_\kappa$. The mean part has the amplitude $a_0\langle f|n_e(\vect{k})|i\rangle$ with $a_0 = -\tilde c\,m_e$ (eq:12.6-mass). The table gives the size of the row as "Umklapp remainder (\cref{eq:E.18-X})". Neither location shows the middle term, which is linear in $\delta\tilde c_\kappa$. Its ratio to eq:E.18-X is of order $|a_0|/|b| \sim (\tilde c/\delta\tilde c)\,\omega^2/\omega_p^2$ (G10 count), so the middle term dominates wherever $(\delta\tilde c/\tilde c)\,\omega_p^2/\omega^2 \ll 1$. #### ER-47 (tex/ch19/s19-5.tex:319, sec:19.5, hypotheses of the two-bias inversion)
Verdict: an error. Hypotheses 2 and 3 contradict each other as written. ER-47 comes before ER-46 here because the ER-46 verdict depends on how ER-47 is resolved.

Evidence. eq:19.5-linear holds with one unknown $Y_{\mathrm{ph}}(0) = A/B$ at both biases only if $A = (1 - \delta_{\mathrm{coll}})(1 - d_{\mathrm{NR}})$ and $B = 1 - d_{\mathrm{ER}}$ are the same at both biases (D-45:36–45; hypothesis 3). The book's own model ties $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ to $A$ in two ways:
- The gap part of the offset carries the factor $1 - f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ (eq:19.5-dgap, s19-5:169–171).
- $E_{\mathrm{miss}}$ holds the trapped carriers and their electrostatic energy (s19-5:97). s20-1:88 adds that a trapped carrier keeps its share of the gap energy as missing energy, and that trapping lowers $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ below $Y_q$.

So field-dependent trapping, which is the reason hypothesis 2 gives for measuring $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ at both biases, changes $\delta_{\mathrm{coll}}$ and $d_{\mathrm{NR}}$ and violates hypothesis 3. The only exception would be $\rho_g = 1$ together with no change in $E_{\mathrm{miss}}$, and the text never assumes that. Conversely, hypothesis 3 requires both biases to "collect every pair within the window". With the electron recoil fully collected (s19-5:101), that gives $f_{\mathrm{ion}} = Y_q$ at both biases (s19-5:101, "It equals the ionization yield $Y_q$ … when both recoils are collected alike"), so $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ cannot change with bias if $Y_q$ itself does not depend on the field (s20-1 excludes recombination inside a dense track from its drift model). The verdict does not rest on that point. The eq:19.5-dgap argument decides it alone: any change of $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ between the biases moves $\delta_{\mathrm{coll}}$ unless $\rho_g = 1$, and the proposed text uses only that argument.

The text shows the field dependence where collection is incomplete. The ionization of nuclear recoils is collected in full at the \SI{20}{keV} calibration point (s19-5:293), but only about \SI{75}{\percent} of the parameterized yield is collected at \SI{100}{keV} and \SI{4}{V/cm} (s19-5:309). A field-dependent $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ is therefore real where full collection fails, and there the inversion fails too.

The consistent statement is that $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ must be the same at both biases, and the charge channel at each bias tests that. This is the job the existing sentence on $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ can do honestly. The rewrite stays in place as the second hypothesis, so "A third bias … tests the first three hypotheses" keeps its count and its referents (collection ratio, ionized fraction, offset and missing energies). The next base sentence, "The offset and the missing energies must also be the same at both biases, so both biases must collect every pair within the window", now follows from the new text without change. No number moves: bal19_5.py section 5 uses one $f_{\mathrm{ion}} = y_C(\SI{20}{keV})$ at 4 and \SI{40}{V}.
- old: `The ionized fraction must be measured at both biases with the charge channel, since trapping depends on the field.`
- new: `The ionized fraction must be the same at both biases, and the charge channel measures $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ at each bias. Trapping depends on the field. A trapped carrier lowers $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ and changes the gap part of the offset through the factor $1 - f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ of \cref{eq:19.5-dgap}, and the balance law counts the gap energy that the trapped carrier keeps as missing energy.`

binding.py flags:
- REL[offset] and REL[missing]: base names. "the offset" is the collection offset in the base text of the same paragraph, and "missing energy" is $E_{\mathrm{miss}}$ of the balance law.
- PROP[that]: a restrictive relative.
"$f_{\mathrm{ion}}$" is repeated in place of "it", which had five candidates. "the trapped carrier" repeats "A trapped carrier" in place of "its", which had eight candidates.

For the lead, not an edit, since E-25 is outside this entry. The applied ER-43 text in tex/ch32/E-25.tex:55 says "that the ionized fraction $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ is measured at both biases with the charge channel" and "a third bias tests the assumptions on $\mathfrak{r}_{\mathrm{coll}}$, $f_{\mathrm{ion}}$ and the collection offset".
