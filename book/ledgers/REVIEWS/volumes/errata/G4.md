## ER-11 (VALUE) Chap. 21 worked examples on the independent-particle crystal form-factor rate

**Decision: recompute with the screened rate.** The examples are statements about the halo and the statistics, but every number they print is a ratio or an integral of a silicon kernel, and the kernel's shape depends on the response. Swapping the independent-particle table of Essig et al. for the random-phase loss function with local fields of the Knapen–Kozaczuk–Lin tables (the rate of sec:10.2 and sec:10.10, and the central value Sect. 21.4 already uses) changes most of them by more than their printed precision, and changes the structure of one example:
- Absolute rates and limits move by a factor of 2.7 at 10 MeV (1.599e5 → 5.897e4) and 31 at 1 MeV (505 → 16.5).
- The debris-flow ratios move: 1.345 → 1.381 per particle, +7.6 % → +8.4 % mixed, and −13 % → −16 % at 1 MeV.
- The halo-independent cost moves: the smallest bound/SHM ratio at 10 MeV goes from 2.12 to 2.00.
- In we:21-stream the tightest bin at u0 = 500 km/s changes from the one-pair to the two-pair bin, so the certificate, the saturated bin and the decomposition all change.
- Only we:21-combine is response-independent in its ratios: every statistic depends on σ·r, so the factors 1.109 and 1.051 and the fitted θ are unchanged exactly, and only the cross-sections scale, by 0.9140/0.2917 = 3.134. The script recomputes them anyway.

Text that depends only on the halo, the gap or the cascade is unchanged:
- lim:21.1a and fig:21-limit-eta;
- the floors (0.3332, 0.3290, 0.3202, 0.6031 MeV);
- η values, the 1.06 year factor at 700 km/s, and the cascade bin probabilities;
- s21-3 (its scripts do not use the table);
- s21-4, which already uses the screened tables.

**Evidence.**
- Rate model of the book: ch10/s10-2.tex:38 and s10-10.tex:87 give 1.36e5 (IP head of the KKL tables), 8.6e4 (RPA) and 5.9e4 (RPA + LF) at 10 MeV, heavy mediator, σe = 1e-37 cm². The 1.60e5 of the Essig table is quoted only as the other tabulation, the source of the tabulation spread (s10-10.tex:11).
- Swap. scratch figscripts/ch10/si_resp.py returns, under SI_RESP=lf, the table of si_elf_binned.npz (`lf`, RPA + LF) as a crystal form factor, |f|² = Ω q⁵ L/(8π²α²m_e²). This follows from eq:A.8-fcrys with S = (2q²/e²)L (eq:4.4-SL). The table is resampled on regular cells of 50 eV in q (493 eV to 22.52 keV, the table range) and 0.01 eV in ω (1.17 to 89.2 eV), with linear interpolation between the table nodes. It counts from the tables' gap of 1.17 eV, the convention of nuis21.py and Sect. 21.4. kernel21.py and hib21.py import it in place of si_fcrys (the only change to them).
- Normalization check (scratch check_norm.py). The kernel rate P Σ w η agrees with the book's loss-function integrals within 0.1 % at 3, 10 and 100 MeV, and within 0.7 % at 1 MeV for a light mediator:

## ER-17 (DEFINITION) Local-field ratio 1 + δ_LF: head-only reference (Sect. 13.4) against ε_mac reference (eq:E.16-dLF)

**Correct statement.** These are two different quantities.
- **Sect. 13.4's 1 + δ_LF** (the definition used by E-14:13 and NOTATION.md:1005) is L^PA with local fields over L^PA with the head alone. It contains the macroscopic factor |ε_00/ε_mac|², which is 1.25–1.31 at 1 keV, and the vertex wings.
- **eq:E.16-dLF** has |ρ(q)/ε_mac|² in its denominator, so the macroscopic factor is already divided out. It measures only the wing (vertex) part, evaluated with zero-phonon vertices.
- **Relation.** With zero-phonon vertices, 1 + δ_LF = |ε_00/ε_mac|² (1 + δ_wing^(0)).
- s13-4.tex:122 already introduces eq:E.16-dLF right after the wing terms as "a bound from the zero-phonon vertices alone", so E-16's quantity needs its own name, not a changed definition in 13.4.

These agree only to first order, and δ_LF alone is 25–31 % at 1 keV.

- Without the pair channel, the screened vertex is Σ_G (ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} ρ(q+G) = ρ(q)/ε_mac + Σ_{G≠0}(ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} ρ(q+G), because (ε̃⁻¹)_00 = 1/ε_mac.

- Ratio of the squared sums over cv: |ε_00/ε_mac|² × Σ|ρ + ε_mac Σ_{G≠0}(ε̃⁻¹)_{G0}ρ(q+G)|² / Σ|ρ|².
- The second factor is exactly the right side of eq:E.16-dLF.
- Without local fields, (ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} = δ_{G0}/ε_00 and ε_mac = ε_00, so both ratios are one, as E-16 says.
