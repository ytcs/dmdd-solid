## ER-15 (DERIVATION) Complex couplings in the n-phonon weight

### Correct statement
For a complex coefficient the pair factor is $c_\kappa^*c_{\kappa'}$, the conjugate on the species $\kappa$ of the operator taken at time $t$, whose position enters $\Delta\vect{R}_{\kappa\kappa'} = \Delta\vect{R} + \vect{\tau}_\kappa - \vect{\tau}_{\kappa'}$ with the plus sign; the self terms then carry $|c_\kappa|^2$. Every coupling the book feeds into Sect. 11.3 is real, so eq:11.3-exact and eq:11.3-weight are right for every use in the book; what is too wide is the sentence that they hold for "any coupling that multiplies the density of each species by a number".

### Evidence (derivation)
With $O(\vect{q}) = \sum_\kappa c_\kappa n_\kappa(\vect{q})$, $n_\kappa(\vect{q}) = \sum_{\vect{R}}\ee^{\ii\vect{q}\cdot\vect{X}_{\vect{R}\kappa}}$, eq:4.2-Sab (ch04/s4-2.tex) gives
$S = \Vol^{-1}\int\dd t\,\ee^{\ii\omega t}\langle O(\vect{q},t)^\dagger O(\vect{q},0)\rangle$, and
$O^\dagger = \sum_\kappa c_\kappa^*\sum_{\vect{R}}\ee^{-\ii\vect{q}\cdot\vect{X}_{\vect{R}\kappa}}$.
The pair (R κ at time t, R′ κ′ at time 0) therefore carries $c_\kappa^*c_{\kappa'}\langle\ee^{-\ii\vect{q}\cdot\vect{X}_{\vect{R}\kappa}(t)}\ee^{\ii\vect{q}\cdot\vect{X}_{\vect{R}'\kappa'}(0)}\rangle$, which is the average s11-3 :7 writes. The static parts give $\ee^{-\ii\vect{q}\cdot(\vect{R} - \vect{R}' + \vect{\tau}_\kappa - \vect{\tau}_{\kappa'})} = \ee^{-\ii\vect{q}\cdot\Delta\vect{R}_{\kappa\kappa'}}$, the phase of eq:11.3-exact. So the conjugate sits on the unprimed index, the one with the minus sign in the phase: $c_\kappa^*c_{\kappa'}\ee^{-\ii\vect{q}\cdot\Delta\vect{R}_{\kappa\kappa'}}$, as the removed eq:E.17-Wn had it. Swapping $(\kappa,\Delta\vect{R}) \leftrightarrow (\kappa',-\Delta\vect{R})$ with $B_{\kappa'\kappa}(-\Delta\vect{R}) = B_{\kappa\kappa'}(\Delta\vect{R})^{\mathrm{T}}$ (eq:11.3-Bkk) maps each term to its complex conjugate, so $\mathfrak{W}^{(n)}$ stays real. eq:11.3-Sn writes $|\sum_\kappa c_\kappa\ldots|^2$ and the bound at s11-3 :130 writes $|\sum_\kappa c_\kappa\ldots|^2$: both are already right for complex $c_\kappa$.

Are the couplings ever complex? s11-3 :7 names $c_\kappa = A_\kappa$, the rigid-ion charge $Z_\kappa$ and $c_1^{(\kappa)} = [Z_\kappa c_1^{(p)} + (A_\kappa - Z_\kappa)c_1^{(n)}]F_N$ (eq:8.1-nuclear). The operator $\mathcal{O}_1 = 1$ multiplies a real potential, so $c_1^{(N)}(q)$, a function of $|q|$ with $\tilde V(-\vect{q}) = \tilde V(\vect{q})^*$, is real, and so is $F_N$. ch11 scripts (we11_3.py, mph11.py) treat one-species silicon with $c = 1$. The charge vertex $Z_\kappa - f_\kappa(q)$ (s11-3 :150) is real. No complex $c_\kappa$ occurs anywhere in the book; $c_\kappa^2$ is also written in eq:11.3-inc, s11-3 :45, :69, :104, s11-4 :20, :33, :88, s11-5 :35, :40, s11-6 :44, D-31 :24, :26, :51, which would all need $|c_\kappa|^2$ if the scope were widened instead.

### Proposed changes
Narrow the statement of scope; change no equation.
1. tex/ch11/s11-3.tex (:7):
   old: `the results hold for any coupling that multiplies the density of each species by a number, the coefficient`
   new: `the results hold for any coupling that multiplies the density of each species by a real number, the coefficient`
2. tex/appE/D-31.tex (:3):
   old: `A coupling that multiplies the density of each species $\kappa$ by a number $c_\kappa$ probes`
   new: `A coupling that multiplies the density of each species $\kappa$ by a real number $c_\kappa$ probes`

Alternative if the reviewer prefers the general form (not proposed, because no coupling of the book needs it and it touches some fifteen equations): $c_\kappa c_{\kappa'} \to c_\kappa^*c_{\kappa'}$ in eq:11.3-exact (s11-3 :10), eq:11.3-weight (s11-3 :101) and D-31 :24, and $c_\kappa^2 \to |c_\kappa|^2$ at every location listed above.

No number moves. E-17 :21, D-31 :39, :43, :60, s11-9 :5, :47 cite eq:11.3-weight unchanged.
