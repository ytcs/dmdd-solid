## ER-2 (VALUE) Plasmon of silicon: 16.6 eV against 16.4 eV

**Correct statement.** Both values are measured loss peaks, from different data. 16.6 ± 0.1 eV is the EELS plasmon energy that Ramanathan and Kurinsky tabulate. 16.4 eV is the peak of the q → 0 loss function built from the Franta et al. optical constants at 300 K (fig:4-siloss). The free-electron valence plasma frequency, ω_p = 16.6 eV (Sect. 4.3), is a third quantity and only happens to equal the EELS number. The book uses 16.6 for ω_p and 16.4 for "the measured loss peak" consistently everywhere except E-13. E-13 says "the plasmon is at 16.6 eV" without saying it is the EELS value, next to 16.4 eV in E-24 and Vol I.

**Evidence.**
- ramanathan2020ionization (arXiv:2004.10709, sources/18.2/2004.10709.txt, Table I, around l.329): "~ω p l d 16.6 ± 0.1 eV N/A EELSe [35]". Footnote e: "Electron energy-loss spectroscopy". Footnote d: "We did not do an exhaustive survey of plasmon energy measurements ... we expect there is some uncertainty in this value beyond the statistical uncertainty". [35] = Chen, Silcox, Vincent, PRB 12, 64 (1975).
- I ran a copy of figscripts/ch04/fig4_5.py in scratch, with only BOOK and OUT pointing elsewhere (fig4_5_copy.out). It prints: "loss peak [eV] 16.4459 (quoted 16.4)", "eps_1 crosses zero upward [eV] 16.2951", "loss peak height 4.49056". These come from the Franta 300 K optical constants (sources/4.4/si_franta_300K.yml).
- figscripts/ch17/limit17_5a.py already names the two values separately in its docstring: "omega_p^val = 16.6 eV ... measured plasmon energy 16.6 +- 0.1 eV, Ramanathan and Kurinsky ... Table I".

**Changes.**
1. ch29/E-13.tex (sec. on the impact-ionization kernel, after eq:E.13-imsigma):
   - old: `In silicon the plasmon is at \SI{16.6}{eV}~\cite[Table~I]{ramanathan2020ionization}, and for a carrier more than that above the band edge \cref{eq:E.13-imsigma} includes plasmon emission automatically; the plasmon then decays into a pair.`
   - new: `In silicon the plasmon loss peak lies at \SI{16.6}{eV} in electron energy-loss spectra~\cite[Table~I]{ramanathan2020ionization} and at \SI{16.4}{eV} in the loss function from optical constants (\cref{sec:4.4}). ## ER-4 (VALUE) LDA lattice constant of silicon: 5.401 Å against 5.403 Å (and PBE 5.477 against 5.466 Å)

**Correct statement.** These are four calculations, and all four are quoted correctly from their sources. They differ in method:
- 5.401 Å: LDA (Perdew–Zunger), non-relativistic norm-conserving pseudopotential, 45 Ry, 8³ grid, from Poncé et al. Sect. 10.2.
- 5.403 Å (LDA) and 5.466 Å (PBE): all-electron LCAO calculations (BAND code, NAO+STO basis), from Csonka et al. Table II.
- 5.477 Å: the book's own PBE relaxation with SG15 at 60 Ry (we:E-si-bands).

The text fails to name the setting in three places. s3-4 gives 5.401 with no source. s5-1 puts the EPW LDA value and the book's PBE value side by side as if they were one minimization. E-1 calls 5.477 "in line with" 5.466 without saying the two are different calculations. There is also one point that touches ER-6: the LDA gap in tab:E-functionals (0.51 eV, Lucero) was computed at Lucero's own LSDA lattice constant of 5.410 Å, not at the 5.403 Å printed in the same row.

**Evidence.**
- ponce2016epw (arXiv:1604.03525, sources/review-ch02/1604.03525.txt, Sect. 10.2, around l.863–877): "Perdew and Zunger parametrization of the LDA ... 8 × 8 × 8 ... 45 Ry. The lattice parameter obtained after structural relaxation is calculated to be 10.207 bohr, slightly below the experimental lattice parameter of 10.262 bohr". 10.207 bohr = 5.4013 Å. 5.401/5.431 − 1 = −0.55 %, so "0.5 %" is right. The same section gives an LDA indirect gap of 0.497 eV.
- csonka2009assessing (arXiv:0903.4037, sources/E/0903.4037.txt, Table II, l.767): "Si A4 5.403 5.431 5.431 5.466 5.451 5.416 0.014" (LDA, PBEsol, AM05, PBE, TPSS, Expt.−ZPAE, ZPAE). Caption: "calculated with BAND/LCAO". l.292–293: "QZ4P basis set consisting of numerical atomic orbital (NAO) core orbitals and one NAO plus three Slater-type orbitals (STOs) for the valence", which is an all-electron basis.
- lucero2012improved (arXiv:1111.4515, sources/E/1111.4515.txt, Table I, l.231): "Si di 5.430 5.430 5.451 5.410 5.465 5.442 1.17 1.12 I 1.45 1.22 0.51 0.74 0.53". The columns are HISS, HSE, LSDA, TPSS, PBEsol lattice constants, then gaps. The caption gives the gaps "at the relaxed geometries", so LSDA a = 5.410 Å and gap = 0.51 eV.
- 5.477/5.466 − 1 = +0.20 % (conversions.out).

## ER-6 (VALUE) LDA gap of silicon: 0.52 eV (tab:E-gw) against 0.51 eV (tab:E-functionals)

**Correct statement.** Both are quoted correctly and come from different LDA calculations. 0.52 eV is the LDA gap collected in Aryasetiawan–Gunnarsson Table I, which does not state a lattice constant. 0.51 eV is Lucero et al.'s LSDA gap at their own LSDA lattice constant, 5.410 Å. For comparison, Poncé et al. find 0.497 eV at 5.401 Å. A spread of 0.01 to 0.02 eV is expected between LDA calculations with different bases and lattice constants. The fix is to say which is which, not to unify them.

**Evidence.**
- aryasetiawan1998gw (cond-mat/9712013, sources/E/cond-mat_9712013.txt, Table I, l.2049): "Si 0.52 1.29a , 1.24b , 1.25c 1.17β". Caption: "Minimum band gaps of semiconductors and insulators which have been calculated within the GWA". No lattice constant is given.
- lucero2012improved Table I, l.231 (quoted under ER-4): LSDA a = 5.410 Å, LSDA gap 0.51 eV, with gaps "at the relaxed geometries".

**Change.** Change 4 of ER-4 covers this entry. The rewritten tab:E-functionals caption names Lucero's LDA lattice constant (5.410 Å), and its last clause says that the 0.52 eV of tab:E-gw comes from other calculations. The reviewer may accept or amend that clause as part of either entry.

**No change:** tab:E-gw (0.52), tab:E-functionals (0.51), and the uses of 0.52 eV in ch03/s3-4 (l.84, fig:3-si-qp caption, we:3-mmin-gap: 0.148 MeV and 0.407 MeV).

**Downstream numbers.** None move.

## ER-25 (TEXT) Chap. 23 opening: "about 0.5 eV" with no functional

**Correct statement.** The opening sentence describes the LDA gap. "Less than half the measured 1.17 eV" holds for LDA (0.52/1.17 = 0.44) and for PBE at the measured lattice constant (0.476). It does not hold for PBE at its own lattice constant (0.602/1.17 = 0.51). The reach of 0.15 MeV is the LDA value of we:3-mmin-gap: 2 × 0.52/7.023e-6 = 0.148 MeV. With "about 0.5 eV" the same formula gives 0.142 MeV, and with PBE 0.602 eV it gives 0.171 MeV. The first section of the same chapter (E-1) uses PBE and says "near 0.6 eV", so the opening has to name the functional and quote the number that gives its 0.15 MeV.

**Evidence.** we:3-mmin-gap (ch03/s3-4.tex): "LDA, \SI{0.52}{eV}: ... = \SI{0.148}{MeV}", "PBE, \SI{0.602}{eV}: ... = \SI{0.171}{MeV}", "measured, \SI{1.17}{eV}: ... = \SI{0.333}{MeV}". I recomputed these (conversions.out): 0.1481, 0.1714, 0.3332 MeV. The ERRATA reader's note (E2E-vol3-notes.md) also identifies the number as the LDA one.

**Change.** The text was edited after harvest and the reach is now its own sentence. Anchor:
1. ch23/ch23.tex (chapter opening, second paragraph):
   - old: `a density-functional calculation of silicon puts the gap at about \SI{0.5}{eV}, less than half the measured \SI{1.17}{eV}. The computed gap would put the reach of a silicon detector at \SI{0.15}{MeV} instead of \SI{0.33}{MeV}.`
   - new: `a density-functional calculation of silicon in the local-density approximation puts the gap at \SI{0.52}{eV}, less than half the measured \SI{1.17}{eV}. The computed gap would put the reach of a silicon detector at \SI{0.15}{MeV} instead of \SI{0.33}{MeV} (\cref{we:3-mmin-gap}).`
   The pointer is a backward reference to Vol I, where the numbers are derived. The reviewer may drop it under Addendum 3 if it seems unnecessary.

**Downstream numbers.** None move. 0.15 and 0.33 MeV stay; with 0.52 eV they are now consistent with we:3-mmin-gap to the quoted digits.
