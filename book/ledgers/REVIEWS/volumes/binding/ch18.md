## Naming decisions
- "threshold": a bare "the threshold" that means $\theta_{\mathrm{ii}}\Eg$ became "the impact-ionization threshold". Where $\theta_{\mathrm{ii}}$ itself is meant, it became "the threshold ratio" (s18-1 worked example, random-$k$ paragraph, s18-2). "Above threshold" inside the 18.2 variance paragraphs, where only one threshold is in play, is unchanged.
- "gap": in s18-2 random parking, "free gap … two gaps … every gap" became "interval", so that "gap" keeps the single meaning of band gap. "the gap to 0.119" (s18-4) and "close the gap between the cascade and the measured Fano factor" (Summary) now say "difference". The table cell "would close the silicon Fano gap" in tab:18-nuisances was left (inside a tabular).
- "seed": the defining apposition at s18-1:3 became a clause ("The electron and the hole that the dark matter leaves behind form the seed of the cascade").
- "separation of the pairs" (s18-5, two places): this now reads "the electron–hole separation within a pair" in both places. The original could also mean the distance between pairs.
- "generating function" against "generating functional": left as in the base. The text uses "functional" for $\mathfrak{G}_x(\vect\lambda)$ and "function" for the one-variable $\mathfrak{G}_t(x;s)$; the caption of fig:18-tree also says "generating function". I changed only "follow from one function" (s18-1:64) to "one generating functional", because that sentence introduces $\mathfrak{G}_x(\vect\lambda)$.
