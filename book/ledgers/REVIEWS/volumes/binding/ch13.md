## Notes for the lead

- **Readings I chose; please check.** In each of these places I resolved an ambiguous pointer by choosing one referent:

  - s13-5 keyresult: "overestimate the two-branch edge by 38, 16 and 7" became "by factors of 38, 16 and 7". The 16 matches the "16 times" stated for $x = 50$ meV.
  - s13-7:83 "Both numbers" became "Both shifts of the zero-phonon edge, 0.0015 and 0.002 MeV".
  - ch13.tex caption: "which screening dresses" became "Screening dresses the amplitude", following s13-3:21, which says that \cref{sec:13.4} screens the density vertex and the pair.
  - "the missing crystal momentum" became "the crystal momentum that the dark matter cannot supply", in the opening, the caption, s13-4 and the Summary.
- **Unification of "margin".** In s13-2 the original text used "the margin below the zero-phonon edge" for $E_{\mathrm{min}}(\vect{q}) - \omega$. Everywhere else in the chapter "the margin" is $\delta_{\mathrm{w}}$. The s13-2 uses now read "distance" (as s13-8:58 already does), and "margin" names only $\delta_{\mathrm{w}}$. "Edge band" is now introduced in s13-3 with an explicit pointer to its definition in \cref{sec:13.6}.
- **Not resolved.** s13-9:77 "reproduces the two powers": I could not tell from the text which two powers are meant, so I left it.
