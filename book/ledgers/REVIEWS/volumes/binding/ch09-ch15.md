- left-heavy subjects, and reduced or object relatives that read first as a main clause: about 35;
- *which / that is why* standing for a proposition: about 25;
- dangling gerunds before a display ("Dividing … ," then the equation) given a verb: 5;
- two-scope coordinations and ordering that read the wrong way ("falls … from 6.1 keV in silicon to 10.3 keV in GaAs"): about 10.
