## Judgment calls the lead may want to check
- s10-1:57: "the shift accounts for about ten percent" now reads "about ten percent of the first moment". This follows from 0.55 eV over a mean deposit of a few eV. The alternative reading, "ten percent of the excess", would contradict the size of that estimate.
- s10-7:96 (sapphire caption): "whose widths for the field along the axis are borrowed from the perpendicular modes" now reads "with the widths of the extraordinary ray borrowed from the ordinary modes". The listed widths bear this reading out (1.98/0.17 and 0.40/0.19 recur).
- s10-2:115: "at relative order (qa)² and in opposite directions" now states which quantity moves. The bending of the dispersion and the fall of the electron-following charge both enter at relative order (qa)² and move the lattice term in opposite directions.
- s10-9:114: "the factor changes the rate by 1e-4 and the transverse row by at most 1e-4" now reads "…, and adding the transverse row changes it by at most 1e-4". The factor multiplies only the density row, so the transverse row is the second change to the rate, not a second object that the factor changes.
- s10-8:12: "Dark Compton scattering is this case" now reads "In dark Compton scattering the frequencies lie far above the binding energies, and the seagull dominates". This keeps the claim to the stated regime and does not assert the full Thomson limit, which holds only for m_phi << m_e (s10-8:115).
- s10-7:145: "have the same size" now reads "are of the same size as the density row", the comparand the paragraph discusses.
