- I kept pronouns that have a single candidate which is the subject of the previous clause (pilot calibration 3). Examples: s5-1 "It is the potential-energy surface", "and it takes a simple form", "they are $\kappa^2$ and $\kappa$"; s5-2 Debye "It replaces … It also replaces".
- I kept the synonym introductions the text states on purpose: "potential-energy surface, or Born--Oppenheimer energy surface"; "first-derivative (or derivative) coupling"; "adiabatic"/"Born--Oppenheimer" used interchangeably.
- s5-2.tex:50 "both kinds of branch" refers back to the two groups of branches in the section's opening paragraph. Naming the groups there would need the terms acoustic and optical, which the worked example defines only later.
- s5-1.tex caption, inset "from the fit": the caption has two fits, and the text does not say which one the inset uses. I could not resolve this without new information.
