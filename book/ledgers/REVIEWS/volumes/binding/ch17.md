## Left as they were (deliberate)
- I kept pronouns that have a single candidate which is the subject of the previous clause (pilot calibration 3). Examples: s17-1 "it is open up to its top" (the acoustic peak); s17-3 "It is close to one for phonons and magnons" (the factor); s17-4 "it raises the rate by 5.5 parts in 10^4" (the product).
- s17-1 "lattice ceiling": the paragraph introduces two lattice ceilings (the band top and E_R^max) but uses "the lattice ceiling" for the larger reach of the lattice as a whole. Defining it as the larger of the two would add a claim, because multiphonon deposits extend beyond both.
- Table cells: invariant.py treats each table as a display, so I left the prose cells unchanged. The cell "L(q, ω) in the window" in tab:17-fom therefore still says "window" where the text now says "kinematic window".
- s17-6 "the two nuclear channels" (Li2MoO4 not ranked): the text does not say which two channels these are, and I could not resolve this without new information.
