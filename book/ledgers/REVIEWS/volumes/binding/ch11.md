## Judgment calls to check
- s11-1:144, "Above that, Umklapp processes and multiphonons dominate". The nearest antecedent of *that* was "about 0.2 MeV". I bound it instead to $q_{\mathrm{BZ}}/2v_{\mathrm{max}}$ (0.42 MeV), which is the mass at which Fig. 11-rate switches to dashed with "the multiphonon and Umklapp terms … dominate". I reordered the two sentences so that the Umklapp clause joins the $q_{\mathrm{BZ}}/2v_{\mathrm{max}}$ sentence.
- s11-3:106, "the last subsection resums it". The resummation is in the next subsection (Thermal diffuse scattering), and the last subsection is the charge vertex. The text now reads "the subsection on thermal diffuse scattering resums it".
- s11-2:149, "the 1.4 % residual of the inputs". I read this as the amount by which the measured $\varepsilon_0$, $\varepsilon_\infty$, $\omega_{\mathrm{L}}$ and $\omega_{\mathrm{T}}$ miss the Lyddane–Sachs–Teller relation, which the amplification factor $\partial\ln\mathcal{I}/\partial\ln\varepsilon_0$ supports. The text now says so explicitly.
- s11-5:61: "The anharmonic rate is never below the harmonic one there". I bound the vague *there* to "In the single-site model", the model of the previous sentence.
- ch11.tex:4, "which start from this sector" now reads "start from the electron-following sector of \cref{tab:8-sectors}", the term s11-1 uses.
- ch11.tex:2, "masses fifty times smaller" now reads "… than the electronic channels do", which supplies the comparison the sentence already implied.
