### Models judged on merits (Addendum 2)
- **Lindhard quenching** (s19-3): kept. It is the only predictive yield model the book has above about 4 keV (eps_L ≥ 0.1), where it describes the silicon data; the BO-surface partition with the kernel and cascade (Sects. 19.1, 19.2) is more rigorous but not yet computed, and the measured power law is data, not a model. Its integral equation is also the stepping stone of the counting equation eq:19.4-count and of the damage energy T_d used in eq:19.4-loss. Its derivation stays because Sect. 19.3.3 names its implicit assumptions against it. Limit Check lim:19.3a kept (shows the formula's regime and its sensitivity to the soft-collision continuation). Trimmed: "We solve the equation again to see how well the fit represents it" (process) → "Solving it again tests the fit."; the grid-convergence sentence (5×10⁻⁴, an audit number below the 3–5 % differences it serves).
- **Bethe stopping** (s19-3): kept. It is the cheap limit of the dielectric stopping eq:4.4-dEdx, adequate above 1.84 v_F (stated), and ch22/s22-3 uses it for damage tracks of fast heavy ions. Removed: the sentence that maps the PDG bracket and prefactor onto eq:19.3-bethe (literature recognition; the citation stays); in we:19-lnI the damped-oscillator estimate of Ī_val = 20.3 eV (and 19.0 eV), dominated by the measured-loss-function evaluation in the same example (valence 19.5 eV, total 161 eV), which is as easy and more correct.
- **Kinchin–Pease count** (s19-4): kept. The hard-sphere solution is the analytic solution of the counting equation and the unit in which the screened factor ξ_d = 0.70 and the NRT factor 0.8 are expressed; ξ_d itself rests on the x^{1/3} continuation, so it does not dominate the NRT 0.8. Limit Check lim:19.4a kept (size of the cross-section dependence).
- **Binary threshold** (s19-2): kept. It is the kinematic edge of linear-response excitation, one line from the free gas, and the kernel treatment of Sect. 19.2.3 keeps it as one of its three parts; the momentum tail softens it in the next subsection.
- **Removed: published MD scans of stored energy** (s19-4, end of `sec:19.4-single`, 2 sentences: Sassi et al., no quench, 18/13 eV thresholds as properties of the potentials). Campaign critique; dominated by the quench protocol eq:19.4-mean of the same paragraph. The citation stays elsewhere in s19-4.
- **Removed: maximum-entropy exponential for p_LEE** (s19-6, `sec:19.6-lee`, one paragraph with E_slope). The text itself said it derives nothing; the Borel size law of triggered releases stays.
- **Cut: survey of time laws of delayed release** (s19-6, `sec:19.6-spectrum`): the (1 + t/t_0)^{-2} law of inter-seed recombination and the row-drift sentence (the drift case stands in `sec:19.6-crowdion`). Kept: 1/t (uniform exponents, Fig. 19-release), exponential, t^{-3/2} (used by the crowdion paragraph).
- **Cut:** clause on Pushkarov's low-temperature coefficient being half ours (correction to a paper for its own sake), s19-6 after eq:19.6-D.
