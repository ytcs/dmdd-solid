## Removed models (Addendum 3)

1. **Modified-Lindhard quenching models of Sarkis et al.** (constant-binding model with U = 0.15 keV, 2020; U(E) = U_FP + U_EDFT model, 2023). Dominated on both axes: the standard Lindhard formula is simpler and is what published limits rest on; the measured silicon yield with its power-law fit (fig:19-yield) is more correct below a keV, where the constant-binding model vanishes at 298 eV against a measured yield of 0.10 at 100 eV. Removed from s19-3, Sect. `sec:19.3-assumptions`:
   - the passage "Restoring U does not repair this … Energy that the partition stores in defects is counted as ionization" (description of both models, U_FP = 23.6 eV against the Frenkel-pair energy, χ²/dof = 1870/101, the remark that the U of secondaries lands in the electronic share);
   - the two sentences on the term −½ k_L ε_L^{3/2} ε_d'' of the modified equation and its conflict with approximation (D);
   - the three sentences on the reduced-energy convention of the 2023 model (Z_1 = 12, 10 % shift);
   - **Figure `fig:19-yields`** (figures/ch19/fig19_3.pdf; file left on disk) with its caption (k_L = 0.161 variant, constant-binding curve with C_0, C_1), and the paragraph that discussed it ("Raising k_L … Adding a constant binding … Each modified model fixes one assumption …").
   - Label removed: `fig:19-yields` (referenced only inside s19-3; both references removed). Still listed in tex/xref/all.tex until the next full build.
   - Kept: citations of Sarkis et al. where they are the source for Lindhard's own equations (f_LSS form, k_L, approximations (A)–(E), the Z^{1/6} factor).
   - **Left behind, needs a figure change outside my limits:** the Limit Check figure limit19_3a.pdf still draws the constant-binding curve (red dashed), so its caption clause "Dashed: the constant-binding model with U = 0.15 keV …" in `lim:19.3a` had to stay. Suggest regenerating the figure without that curve and then deleting the clause. The Limit Check itself (Lindhard quenching) stays: it is the formula published limits rest on. No Limit Check box was removed, so no Appendix F row needs to go.
2. **First-order form of the gap-energy release law** (s19-5, `sec:19.5-heat`): the sentence "The first-order form, 1 + (1 − Y_q)(E_g/ε_eh)(1 − f_ret), is 1.280 and not usable at this size." Dominated by the exact release law eq:19.5-release, which is as easy to evaluate. No label.
