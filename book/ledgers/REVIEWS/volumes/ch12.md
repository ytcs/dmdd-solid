## Models (Addendum 3)

No model was removed from the chapter text. Each candidate and the reason it stays:

- Atomic limit (Sect. 12.2, `lim:12.2a`, `lim:12.2b`): the only treatment of free atoms and of the core shells; published limits rest on it.
- Scalar-$\varepsilon$ form (`lim:12.3a`), free-recoil soft limit (`lim:12.3b`): the published formulas; the second is the kept model.
- Per-mass rate (`eq:12.3-permass`, `lim:12.3c`): simpler, regime stated, used by Sect. 17.5.
- Phonon-mediated form (`lim:12.4b`): exact in $\omega_{\mathrm{ph}}/\omega_e$, needed for narrow gaps.
- Atomic bremsstrahlung and atomic scalar absorption (`eq:12.6-atom`, `lim:12.6a`): the published formulas, used as the free-atom limits.
- **Wannier picture of the boost (`sec:12.3-wannier`, `lim:12.3d`, `eq:12.3-HW`, `-AW`, `-AWbound`, about 1300 words): by the letter of Addendum 3 it is dominated** (the loss-function form for the valence, the atomic form for the cores). - Removed from the appendix only: the heuristic propagator `eq:D.33-virtual` (dominated by the exact `eq:12.4-pm`) and the derivative-coupling route to the atom (dominated by the exact separation `eq:12.2-cm`).
