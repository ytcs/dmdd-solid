## Superseded models (Addendum 3: Pareto front)

**Removed completely: the balance estimate of the Luke carrier speed** (end of sec:20.1-luke).
- What it was: carrier assumed to move along the field at full speed; balance of field work against
  Luke emission, an unnumbered display for k/k_s; values k/k_s = 4.3 and 7.0 and mean Luke energies
  1.1 and 2.1 meV for silicon holes and electrons at 1 and 4 V/cm; the electron parameters used only
  there (conductivity mass 0.26 m_e, D^n = 9.0 eV, m c_LA^2 = 0.120 meV); the derived 0.3 % of hole
  Luke energy below 2 Delta at birth. The text itself called it an overestimate.
- What dominates it: the transport Monte Carlo value 0.84 meV at 1 V/cm and 0.84-4.6 meV over
  1-100 V/cm (sec:20.5-meanfield), which the mean-field scan and Chap. 21 use; the sub-gap share at
  0.84 meV is s_sub = 0.011 in sec:20.5-sign.
- Mentions removed: s20-1 (paragraph), s20-5 (one clause in sec:20.5-meanfield), s20-summary (one
  sentence, one clause of the row "Luke emission rate and spectrum" of tab:20-nuisances).
- Labels removed: none (the display was unnumbered). No reference outside the unit. No Limit Check
  box and no Appendix F row belongs to it.

Kept, each on the front or a base of a kept model:
- Hecht relation (face event, mu-tau form): eq:20.1-hecht2 and eq:20.1-qavg are built on it; it is the
  standard formula of mu-tau fits (lim:20.1a).
- Binomial thinning of the Fano factor: eq:20.1-Fpos reduces to it, eq:20.5-hv uses it as one limit;
  lim:20.1c places the published form of Ramanathan and Kurinsky.
- Rayleigh law and continuum anharmonic rate: the scaling spectrum is built on them (lim:20.2a, 20.2b).
- Normal-incidence impedance law: taught in Appendix C.18 (Volume I); one sentence plus lim:20.3a, the
  formula a reader meets in the literature. Diffuse mismatch: the other end of the stated range of the
  escape probability, a different physical assumption, not a cruder version of acoustic mismatch.
- Lumped absorption form exp(-4 d_f / l_f) of detector simulations (sec:20.3-escape): one paragraph and
  one clause of we:20-escape. Dominated by eq:20.3-pabs in rigor at equal cost, but it is the form the
  published simulations (G4CMP) use and the text gives the factor by which it errs. Kept; if the
  coordinator reads the rule more strictly, this paragraph and the clause "the lumped form gives 0.232,
  a factor 1.82 less" are the next candidate (no label, no box).
- Lumped-film Kaplan cascade (cheap, used by sec:20.5) and Chang-Scalapino kinetic equations (more
  complete, only quoted in lim:20.4a): both on the front.
- One-film sign formula eq:20.5-sign, flat-yield and "deleted phonons" variants in sec:20.5-sign: steps
  and envelope of the one mean-field solve, cited by the summary, tab:20-nuisances and Chap. 21.

Addendum 2 (appendices): the unit holds no appendix file. Chapter 20 points to app:D.45 (Luke heat
against bias work) and Chapter 22 to app:D.44; neither derivation is repeated in the chapter text.
