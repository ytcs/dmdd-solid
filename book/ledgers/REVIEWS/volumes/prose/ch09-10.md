## Detector hits kept, and other decisions

The 9 remaining detector hits were kept on purpose. Each is a contrast that the reader needs, stated in one clause, or a false positive.
- **s9-2:94** "its minimum lies on the constraint …, not at a stationary point": explains why the stationary-point exponent does not apply.
- **s9-4:24** "misses the skewness of the moment problem, not an anharmonic order": says why a box near the label is a qualifier.
- **s10-1:30** "Two momentum transfers that differ by a reciprocal-lattice vector …": false positive; this is not a count-teaser.
- **s10-2:86** "is an assumption there, not a theorem": the claim is about proof status, and the contrast is the content. The "evaluation" hit on the same line is a false positive ("only near r_s ≈ 5.25").
- **s10-4:95** "far from it": false positive.
- **s10-7:46** "the separation is not unique": a technical statement.
- **s10-8:67** caption "a model, not a prediction": a caution to the reader that the curve is a model.
- **s10-9:61** "set by the loss function, not by the recoil": the contrast with the free-electron line is the point of the paragraph.

Other decisions:
- **s10-3:3, "Can dark matter excite it?"** This question was written in the edit. It was kept: it is the question that the section answers (no, in the standard halo), and STYLE.md ("Concrete openings") allows an opening question.
- **s10-8:138, "Two pieces of the two-insertion response are not computed …".** This was left as written. It is the list of open problems, and "The first …", "The second …" follow at once.
- **The plain "only"**, which REGISTER.md flags as overused, was avoided in the rewrites where a construction without it was available. Two rewrites contain it: s10-2:95 ("comes close … only for", already in the sentence) and s10-8:84 ("need only the inputs of single absorption", where "only" carries the claim).
