## Dominated models (Addendum 3)
None removed. The analytical potentials (Stillinger-Weber, Tersoff) are cheaper than the machine-learned potential and are what the published scans use; the single-parameter cascade model is the calibrated benchmark; the lumped film model and the two mismatch models are the ones detector codes use; the first-order Auger estimate `eq:E.23-fA` is an acceptance test of the full solution.
