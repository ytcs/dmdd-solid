# CONCEPTS ledger (seeded from SPINE at design time; revised after the spine reviews; the editor merges rows)

Concept → defining section → sections that use it. "READER" means assumed by READER.md. App. A–C are reference appendices that the text
may point into from any section. A concept with an early operational definition and a later precise treatment has two rows marked
(op.) and (precise). Rows for every chapter (Part I from SPINE-I; Chap. 8 and Parts II–III from SPINE-II). "Later" lists the chapters or
sections that are already known to need the concept.

**Match terms** are the literal strings by which ledgers/check_concepts.py recognizes a use of the concept in any field of a SPINE
entry. A term is available from the earliest row that lists it. "Used in" is written by the script from that scan over all
chapters (`--write-used`); edit the terms, not that column.
