## The reader now knows
- Two kinds of small denominators in eq:13.2-M: zero-phonon detunings (path A p empty, path C p filled) and intraband 0/0 terms
  (path A p = c, path B p = v) for a soft acoustic phonon, whose interband lab vertices grow as ℓ_Qν ∝ |Q|^{-1/2} (rigid translation).
- Ward identity of the amplitude (eq:D.37-ward): for any one-electron 𝑠̂_Q and δg_mn = (ε_{m,κ+Q} − ε_{nκ} ± ω_Q)s_mn, on shell
  M[g] = M[g − δg] + ⟨c k'|[𝑠̂_Q, e^{iq·r}]|v k⟩, exact at every Q, band by band (ratios ±1, no 0/0).
- Comoving choice 𝑠̂_Q = iℓ ½{e^{iQ·r}, ê·p̂} (eq:D.37-s): contact (recoil) term iℓ(ê·q)ρ_cv(k, q+Q) (eq:D.37-recoil); comoving vertex
  g^cm = g − δg (eq:D.37-gcm) vanishes at Q = 0 for all band pairs (eq:6.2-asr); intraband g^cm_nn = iℓ|Q|[Q̂·D^n·ê ∓ c_ν ê·p_nn];
  lab intraband slope Q̂·(D^n + v^g_n ⊗ p_nn)·ê (eq:D.37-Xi), = eq:6.2-D at extrema.
- Keyresult eq:13.6-soft (= eq:D.37-limit): M → iℓρ_cv(k,q)[ê·q + (Q̂·D^c·ê ∓ c p_cc)/(Q̂·v_c ± c) − (c → v)]; leading O(|Q|^{-1/2}),
  relative correction O(|Q|); valid for |Q| ≪ 2m*c_ν at an extremum. Degenerate bands: diagonalize the O(Q) splitting first.
- Si: D/c_ν = 300–350 keV ≫ q (recoil ≲ 1 % at extrema); m*c_ν = 1.0e-3 (TA, m_t) and 6.7e-3 Å⁻¹ (LA, m_l). Inside the window
  |Q| ≥ δ_w/(v^g_max + c) = 0.018 Å⁻¹ (v^g_max = 16.7 eV Å = 2.5e6 m/s on the symmetry lines) — soft limit never reached; at the onset
  |Q*| ≥ 0.31 Å⁻¹. Outside: soft emission finite (Q dQ at T = 0, dQ at T > 0); Cherenkov cone = real decay, width-regularized.
- Code hazard: zeroing acoustic interband vertices near Q = 0 loses iℓ[ê·(q − p_cc + p_vv)]ρ_cv (×10 in |M|² in WE 13.6, config B).
- Keyresult Window theorem (eq:13.6-window): S^PA from eq:13.2-M defined with no width/i0⁺/subtraction/counterterm iff every first-order
  transition at (q, ω) is off shell, ω < E^ren_min(q, T) (renormalized energies in edge AND denominators); quoted as its own channel for
  ω ≤ E^ren_min(q, T) − δ_w, δ_w ≫ ω_max, Γ̂, T, pointwise in q; Si δ_w = 0.3 eV. E^ren_min includes the bound pair (13.8).
- Margin justified by the resummed propagator of eq:8.6-amp: correction Σ_m(ω)/(ω − E_m); with E^ren, remaining: variation of Σ on the
  phonon scale (thresholds), widths, T; adiabatic error ω/δ (eq:13.2-err); E_x^b. θ_ep at 0.3 eV with the largest (hole optical) vertex is
  ≈ 0.7 but bounds rather than sizes: |Σ_m| ≈ ⟨|g|²⟩/E_w, correction θ_ep δ_w/E_w ≈ 0.2 (E_w = continuum width of 2.4).
- Si ratios at δ_w = 0.3 eV: ω_max (64.1 meV) 0.21; ω_TA(Q*) (14.8 meV) 0.05; |ZPR| (56–73 meV) 0.19–0.24 (absorbed); Γ̂ 3–21 meV (11 meV
  at 1 keV) → 0.01–0.07; E_x^b 0.05; T = 0 (300 K: 0.09). δ_w = 0.1 → ratios > 0.6; 0.5 → 0.13 but window ends at 1.07 keV.
  DECISION: δ_w = 0.3 eV CONFIRMED; 13.3's 0.31 Å⁻¹ and 1.33 keV (parabolic) stand; computed bands give 1.27 keV.
