## The reader now knows
- Density matrix ϱ̂ = Σp_i|i⟩⟨i|, thermal average Tr(ϱ̂A) (eq:2.5-rho); Gibbs weights p_i = e^{−βE_i}/𝒵, β = 1/T, 1 K = 86.17 μeV
  (eq:2.5-gibbs); stationarity ([ϱ̂, H] = 0, correlations depend on t − t′); grand-canonical state with μ_ch (eq:2.5-grand); ensembles
  differ by N^{-1/2}.
- Thermal golden rule: Γ = Σ_i p_i Σ_f Γ_{i→f} (eq:2.5-thermalrate), no interference between initial states; transition sum 𝒮_O(ω)
  (eq:2.5-transum); 2π𝒮_O = ∫dt e^{iωt}⟨O†(t)O(0)⟩ (eq:2.5-corr); keyresult Γ = ∫d³q/(2π)³|Ṽ|²S(q, ω_q), S = (2π/V)𝒮_{n_e(q)}
  (eq:2.5-golden) = eq:A.7-S = Knapen Eq. (4); volume bookkeeping; velocity/angle average → q dq η/(2π)² → eq:1.5-rate re-derived term by
  term, valid at any T and with ω < 0 included (fulfils the 1.5 promise "Chap. 2 derives eq:1.5-rate").
- Detailed balance keyresult 𝒮_{O†}(−ω) = e^{−βω}𝒮_O(ω) (eq:2.5-db), from relabeling i ↔ f and |⟨i|O†|f⟩| = |⟨f|O|i⟩| (fulfils 2.1's
  promise); grand-canonical caveat (N-conserving O; else e^{−β(ω − μ_ch ΔN)}); S(−q,−ω) = e^{−βω}S(q,ω); S(q,−ω) = e^{−βω}S(q,ω) with
  inversion or TR (App. A.4). Holds for interacting targets.
- Two-level system: weights p_0, p_1 at ±Δ; 0.881/0.119 at T = Δ/2; never inverted (Fig. 2.6, fig:2-twolevel).
- ω < 0 suppressed by e^{−β|ω|}; 1 meV at 1 K: 9×10⁻⁶; an ω < 0 event deposits nothing in a calorimeter or charge readout; matters for
  the total rate, sum rules (4.3), FDT factor 1 − e^{−βω} (4.2, eq:A.7-FDT).
- Occupations in second-quantized form (eq:2.5-nF): ⟨c†c⟩ = n_F(E − μ_ch), ⟨d†d⟩ = n_B(E), μ = 0 for phonons/magnons; ⟨cc†⟩ = 1 − n_F,
  ⟨dd†⟩ = n_B + 1 (fulfils 2.3's thermal-n promise); off-diagonal averages vanish.
- Independent-electron transition sum with n_F(1 − n_F′) (eq:2.5-indep), term-by-term detailed balance; T ≪ E_g → valence→conduction sum of
  eq:2.3-ph (→ crystal form factor, Chap. 10).
- WE 2.7 (we:2-oscillator): O = λ(d + d†); 𝒮 = |λ|²[(n_B+1)δ(ω−ω₀) + n_Bδ(ω+ω₀)]; ratio e^{βω₀}; kinematic factor η(v_min(q, ±ω₀)),
  gain allowed for every mass (v_min = 0 at q = (2m_χω₀)^{1/2}); numbers 60 meV: n_B = 0.109 (300 K), ratio 10.2, 2.5×10⁻⁷⁶ (4 K);
  1 meV: 9.1×10⁻⁶ (1 K), 10⁻²⁵² (20 mK), 1.27 (20 K), ratio 1.79; total weight (2n_B+1)|λ|² (1.22 at 300 K); first moment |λ|²ω₀,
  T-independent (oscillator f-sum rule → 4.3).
- Free Fermi gas at T = 0 = jellium without interaction (fulfils 2.3's "jellium is the subject of 2.5"): Fermi sea, k_F = (3π²𝓃_e)^{1/3},
  E_F, v_F, 𝒩_F = m_ek_F/2π² per spin (eq:2.5-fermi keyresult; check 2𝒩_F E_F = 3𝓃_e/2). Si valence 𝓃_e = 2.00e23 cm⁻³ (App. A.2):
  k_F = 3.57 keV = 0.96 q₀, E_F = 12.5 eV, v_F = 7.0×10⁻³ = 2090 km/s = 2.6 v_max, 𝒩_F = 9.2×10⁷ eV²; T/E_F = 2×10⁻³ (300 K), 7×10⁻⁸ (10 mK).
  Computational connection: smeared occupations in DFT for metals (App. E.1).
- Pauli blocking quantified (fulfils 2.3 promise): unblocked fraction 3q/4k_F − q³/16k_F³ (q < 2k_F), 1 above (eq:2.5-blocking, lens
  volume); 7.8% at q = 0.1q₀; energy constraint and Lindhard → 4.5.
