## The reader now knows
- Elastic recap: q·v = q²/2μ_χN (eq:1.3-elastic), q ≤ 2μ_χN v, E_R ≤ 2μ_χN²v²/M (eq:1.3-ERmax; Lin Eqs. (75)–(78)); μ_χe, μ_χp defined alike.
- ω_q = q·v − q²/2m_χ − Δ_χ (eq:1.3-omegaq) and v_min = |(ω + Δ_χ)/q + q/2m_χ| (eq:1.3-vmin), in a keyresult box; derived from the
  angle; endo/exothermic signs; ω < 0 at T > 0 (forward 2.5); sign convention of q and why it matters for non-centrosymmetric or
  magnetic crystals (App. A.4).
- Bound electrons: q ~ μ_χe α, ω ~ αm_e v ≈ 4 eV, independent of m_χ (Lin Eqs. (117)–(119)).
- Maximum deposit ½m_χv² − Δ_χ for a target that absorbs any momentum (eq:1.3-omegamax); ½μv² for a free target (Essig 2016 Eq. (3.4)).
- min_q v_min = (2(ω+Δ_χ)/m_χ)^{1/2} at q = (2m_χω)^{1/2}.
- Threshold energy E_th; threshold mass m_min = 2E_th/v_max² (eq:1.3-mmin), with its hypothesis (q-independent onset; otherwise a strict
  lower bound, forward 3.3); inelastic: E_th + Δ_χ; exothermic spectrum = band of width 2qv about |Δ_χ|.
- Halo sensitivity: δ ln m_min = δ ln E_th − 2 δ ln v_max; v_esc ± 25 km/s → ∓6.3%; annual ∓3.6%.
- Accessible region ω ≤ qv_max − q²/2m_χ (eq:1.3-region); q ≥ ω/v_max; q ≤ 2m_χv_max; ω ≤ 3×10⁻³q (static potential justified).
  Table tab:1-kinematics (0.1 MeV – 1 GeV). Gap band of q at 1 MeV: 0.46–4.8 keV; at 10 MeV: 0.42–53 keV; free Si nucleus reaches
  1.11 eV only for m_χ ≳ 45 MeV.
- q₀ = αm_e = 3.73 keV (eq:1.3-q0); 2π/a ≈ 0.6q₀ ≈ 2.3 keV for Si (a ≈ 10 Bohr radii, Kahn & Lin Sect. V); 2m_χv_max = q₀ at 0.70 MeV,
  > 2π/a above ≈ 0.4 MeV; why q is never folded (forward 3.1).
- Energy scales (one sentence each): electronic excitations (eV), band gap E_g (Si 1.11 eV at room temperature, Essig 2016 Eq. (5.2)),
  phonons (0–100 meV, 60 meV typical in Si), optical vs acoustic; acoustic ω ≤ 2c_ν m_χ v (KL Eq. (93)), fraction 4c_ν/v.
- Computational connection (one paragraph): E_g by DFT with gap corrections (App. E.1, E.3); phonons by DFPT (App. E.4).
- Nuclear recoils deposit little: fraction 4m_χ/M; E_R ~ phonon energies at m_χ ≈ 10 MeV, below which the free-nucleus picture fails
  (forward Chap. 5). Limit box "Elastic nuclear recoil" (Lin Eqs. (77), (78)); v_min = (ME_R/2μ_χN²)^{1/2}; width of the bound peak
  ~ geometric mean of E_R and a phonon energy (KL Sect. IV A).
- Worked example 1.4 (we:1-mmin): Si gap 1.11 eV → m_min = 0.32 MeV at q = 0.84 keV; 30 meV → 8.5 keV at q = 23 eV (factor 37).
- Worked example 1.5 (we:1-nuclear): 1 GeV on Si, μ = 0.963 GeV, q = 5.10 MeV, E_R ≤ 0.50 keV = 14% of 3.5 keV; xenon 0.11 keV

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **v_min convention and D.1.** The book now defines v_min with an absolute value (derived from the angle; required for ω + Δ_χ < 0).
   Then v_min ≥ 0 always and D.1's sentence "For v_min < 0 … η(v_min) = η(0)" is vacuous (harmless but misleading: with the signed
   expression it would be wrong for exothermic DM). Suggest D.1 say "v_min ≥ 0 by Eq. (1.3-vmin)". Also the writer-only conventions
   (signed v_min, η with Θ(|v| − v_min)) should be aligned. I did not edit D.1.
2. **E_g value.** Only the cited 1.11 eV (room temperature, Essig 2016 Eq. (5.2)) is printed; m_min = 0.32 MeV. The low-temperature gap
   (≈1.17 eV, giving 0.333 MeV) is not printed for lack of a fetched public source; the text says the gap is larger at detector
   temperatures and defers to 3.3. The 1.4 text says "about 0.3 MeV for E_g ≈ 1 eV" — consistent.
3. **Lattice constant.** No full-text public source for a = 5.431 Å; used Kahn & Lin's "lattice constant ∼10a₀" (written 10/(αm_e),
   since a₀ is first used in 2.2), so 2π/a ≈ 2.3 keV. 3.1 gives the precise value.
