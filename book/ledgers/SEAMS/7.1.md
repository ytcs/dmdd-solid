## The reader now knows
- Opening: NiO spins order below T_N = 523 K (Fischer Table IV, expt), type II; magnons up to ~117 meV, slope ~250 meV Å (Hutchings 78 K);
  silicon has no spin excitation below the gap; TZZ proposal (spin-coupled DM → magnons).
- Direct exchange: two electrons in orthogonal orbitals, E = C ∓ K_ex (triplet lower); K_ex (eq:7.1-Kex) = exchange term of
  eq:2.3-direct-exchange for localized orbitals, positive (Coulomb self-energy of φ_a*φ_b); Hund's rule; Ni²⁺ |S| = 1.
- Dirac identity: H_12 = E_0 + C − K/2 − 2K S1·S2 (eq:7.1-dirac). The spin coupling is −2K_ex, ferromagnetic.
- Kinetic exchange: two-site Hubbard model (eq:7.1-hubbard) with t_hop (3.2 sign) and U_H. The triplet is Pauli-blocked, the singlet has
  matrix element 2t_hop, so E_s = −4t²/U. Keyresult-level eq:7.1-superexchange J^ex = 4t_hop²/U_H − 2K_ex. Picture: virtual hop allowed only
  for antiparallel spins. Validity t ≪ U (Mott insulator), corrections O((t/U)²).
- Superexchange (Anderson, qualitative): effective hopping through O p, second order in the Ni–O hopping. 180° NNN gives a large AFM J2,
  90° NN a weak FM J1. NiO J2 = 19.01, J1 = −1.37 meV (eq:7.1-NiO, Hutchings, same convention). Estimate J ~ t²/U; with U_H 4.6–8.2 eV
  (E.9), (J2U_H)^{1/2} ≈ 0.3–0.4 eV, so t/U ≈ 0.05–0.07.
- Heisenberg model eq:7.1-heis (pairs once, J > 0 AFM, editor ruling); spin algebra, S² = |S|(|S|+1); conventions differ in the
  literature (→ E.9). Ferromagnet and antiferromagnet defined; NiO type II (Fig. 7.1a). Anisotropy neglected (gaps < 5 meV in NiO,
  dos Santos Sect. III).
- Computational connection: DFT + U energy mapping, spirals, LKAG (App. E.9); DFT J2 spread 11–27 meV (Fischer Table III); LSWT with the
  best constants +14 % (dos Santos). The spread is the theory uncertainty.
- Holstein–Primakoff (eq:7.1-HP) with d_j; [S⁺,S⁻] = 2S^z checked via f(n)d = d f(n−1). LSWT = leading order in 1/|S|; bond expansion.
- Keyresult eq:7.1-fm: ω^M_k = |S|[J^ex(k) − J^ex(0)], J^ex(k) = Σ_{R≠0} J_0R e^{ik·R}, E_cl = ½N|S|²J^ex(0). Branch index ν dropped
  for one branch. Goldstone mode; ω ∝ k² at small k; precession picture; frozen-magnon relation of E.9 agrees.
- WE 7.1 (we:7-chain): ω = 2|J||S|(1 − cos ka), zone boundary 4|J||S|. The one-magnon Bloch state is exact (shown explicitly for any |S|).
  ED: 1-magnon agreement 4e-16 |J| (S = ½, N = 10). Two deviations: 0.328 vs 0.382 |J| (S = ½, N = 10) and 1.083 vs 1.172 |J|
  (S = 1, N = 8), so magnons attract at O(1/|S|). Group velocity 2(ω|J||S|)^{1/2}a → 0 at small ω.
- Antiferromagnet: the pair term creates two deviations; d_A, d_B bosons; eq:7.1-Hk (= eq:D.19-Hk) with A_k, B_k, J^ex_AA, J^ex_AB.
  Bogoliubov eq:7.1-bogo (= eq:D.19-bogo) via eq:C.7-Hboson; u^M, v^M named. Keyresult eq:7.1-afm (= eq:D.19-omega) with E_0 and E_cl.
  Two degenerate branches carrying ΔS^z = ∓1; zero-point energy and moment reduction (→ 14.1); stability A ≥ |B|; Goldstone; linear law
  (eq:D.19-linear) as the geometric mean of exchange field and stiffness.
- Type II with J2 only: eq:7.1-J2 ω = 6J2|S|(1 − γ²)^{1/2}; max 6J2|S|; slope 2√3 J2|S|a.
- Fig. 7.1 (fig:7-nio): (a) structure, (b) LSWT along (½,½,½)–Γ–(½,½,−½)|Γ–(1,0,0); J1 + J2 vs J2 only; linear laws; measured
  maximum. Caption separates the magnetic-Γ zero at (½,½,½) from the soft points of the undistorted model.
- WE 7.2 (we:7-nio): 274.6 meV Å = 1.39e-4 = 41.7 km/s (J2 only); along [111] 2√3(J1+J2)|S|a = 254.8 meV Å (derived); other directions
  255–269 meV Å (numerical); measured ~250. Bound ω ≤ |S|(6J2 − 3J1) = 118.2 meV, reached (zone scan); J2 only 114.1; measured ~117.
  Agreement is partly a fit. v/v_max = 1/19; 4.9 × c_LA(Si, [100]) (C.18); emission is kinematically allowed.
- s_e(q) = Σ e^{iq·r_i}σ_i/2 (eq:7.1-se), first use. TZZ coupling −Σ O_χa s_e,a (their Eq. (4), Table I) makes the s_e,a the O_a of S_ab
  (→ 8.1). Local moments: Wigner–Eckart gives s_e(q) = F^mag(q) Σ_j e^{iq·R_j} S_j (eq:7.1-Fmag), F^mag(0) = 1, falls off at a few Å⁻¹
  (→ C.19 for orbital moments and neutrons).
- FM one-magnon response: S⁻(q) = (2|S|N)^{1/2} a†_{q_r} (crystal momentum via eq:3.1-qsplit). Keyresult eq:7.1-Sfm S_ab =
  (π|S|/Ω)|F^mag|²(δ_ab − ẑẑ + iε_abc ẑ_c) δ(ω − ω^M_{q_r}) at T = 0; eigenvalues 2, 0, 0 (PSD, eq:4.2-positivity); one circular
  polarization (= TZZ Eq. (6) polarization); q-independent weight; FDT pole of R_ab.
- AFM weights: amplitude (2|S|N_s)^{1/2}(u ∓ v) near Γ and near the ordering wavevector (from D.19); eq:7.1-weights (u ∓ v)² =
  |S|(A ∓ B)/ω^M: weight ∝ k near Γ, ∝ 1/distance near (π/a)(1,1,1) (|.| = 1.30 Å⁻¹ = 2.6 keV in NiO, reached for m_χ ≳ 0.5 MeV).
  S^z creates pairs (→ 14.2). Rate → 14.1.
- Closing: the magnon window runs from a few meV to ~0.1 eV; J^ex, u/v and F^mag enter the rate; the coupling is treated in 8.1.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **E.9 attribution (factual).** E.9 writes that Fischer's "measured values for NiO, J2 = −8.66 and −9.51 meV in its convention, are
   J2 = 17.3 and 19.0 meV in ours". In Fischer Table II/III, −8.66 is Shanker & Singh (PRB 7, 5000, 1973; ref. 53) and −9.51 is Hutchings
   (ref. 54). Jacobsson et al. Table I assigns the labels the other way round. The Hutchings abstract settles it: J2 = 19.01, J1 = −1.37 meV in
   the book's convention, so 17.3 meV belongs to Shanker & Singh. 7.1 quotes Hutchings directly. E.9 should say so, or cite Hutchings
   directly for 19.0.
2. U_H typography: NOTATION says "subscript mandatory"; E.9 and 7.1 typeset italic $U_H$, whereas STYLE asks for upright text subscripts
   ($U_{\mathrm{H}}$). The editor should pick one form book-wide.
3. a = 4.17 Å and T_N = 523 K are taken from Fischer et al. Tables I and IV (experimental columns), not from the original measurements.
4. Fig. 7.1 and WE 7.2 neglect anisotropy and the rhombohedral distortion. Hutchings' fit included anisotropy, so our anisotropy-free slopes
   and maximum differ slightly from his full model; the text says the agreement is partly by construction.
