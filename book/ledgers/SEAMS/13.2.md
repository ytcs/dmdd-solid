## The reader now knows
- One-phonon final state |f⟩ = c†_{ck'}c_{vk}|i'⟩, k' = k + q + Q (k' folded, q unfolded, Umklapp inside ρ), E_f − E_i =
  ε_ck' − ε_vk ± ω_Qν, upper sign emission of −Q (eq:13.2-final). Second-order matrix element of n_e(q) (eq:13.2-T): density-first
  denominator E_i + ω − E_m, phonon-first E_i − E_m; DM kinetic energy cancels; replaces ⟨f|n_e|i⟩ in eq:4.2-Sab; needed only on shell.
- Four paths (eq:13.2-paths, Fig. 13.2): A density first, electron in empty p at k+q then phonon; B phonon first creates (p at k+Q; v k),
  density moves the electron; C density first creates (c k'; hole in filled p at k+Q), hole couples to phonon; D phonon first creates
  (c k'; hole p at k+q), density moves the hole. Hole paths carry −1 (operator reordering). Diagonal vertex terms need q = G or Q = 0.
- Keyresult eq:13.2-M: on shell C = B-form and D = A-form with p filled (exchange partners of the Pauli-blocked paths), so
  ⟨f|n_e^{(2)}|i⟩ = N^{-1/2}(n_Qν + ½ ± ½)^{1/2} M^±_cvν(k,q,Q) with M^± the one-electron sum over ALL bands p, incl. p = v, p = c.
  M^± dimensionless, O(1) in N. Operator form: ⟨c|y(ε_v + ω − h)^{-1}x + x(ε_v ∓ ω_Q − h)^{-1}y|v⟩ (eq:D.35-theorem). Limits: on shell
  only (off shell the hole denominators differ by ω − (E_f − E_i)); independent electrons (excitons → 13.8); intermediate states off shell.
- Empty-band truncation is wrong by O(1): factor 2.4 in |M|² in the model of WE 13.2. Intraband terms (p = v in A, p = c in B) cancel
  at q = 0: M^±(k,0,Q) = 0 exactly (eq:13.2-q0), since n_e(0) = N_e; amplitude starts at O(q). The q → 0 limit itself is 13.5's.
- No interference with the zero-phonon line at O(g²): final states differ in one phonon occupation, orthogonal, at any T (thermal
  density matrix diagonal in occupations). O(g²) corrections to ρ_cv (Fan–Migdal, Allen–Heine, virtual-phonon vertex) belong to the
  zero-phonon line (8.6, 13.7). 8.6's Fano term is interference inside the one-phonon sector (resonant part of path A vs other paths).
- Denominator bound (eq:13.2-doff): δ_off ≥ min(E_min(q) − ω, E_g − ω_Qν); the E_min(q) − ω part belongs to path A (p empty) and path C
  (p filled), i.e. the virtual zero-phonon pairs at q; the E_g − ω_Q part to B (p empty) and D (p filled). Inside ω < E_min(q): no width,
  no i0⁺, no subtraction. Si: interband bound ≥ 1.11 eV; at the 13.1 onset the zero-phonon margin is ≈ 0.7 eV (E_min ≈ 1.9 eV vs 1.19 eV).
- Adiabatic amplitude M^ad (eq:13.2-Mad: ω_Q dropped from denominators, same for emission and absorption, degenerate states excluded);
  ∂ρ_cv/∂b = N^{-1/2} M^ad with b the classical amplitude replacing B_Qν; ⟨f|n^{(2)}|i⟩ ≃ ⟨n'|B|n⟩ ∂ρ_cv/∂b (eq:13.2-HT) = the
  Herzberg–Teller term of eq:8.6-electronic = eq:E.4-drho. The unrestricted band sum = first-order perturbation theory of one orbital.
- Error (eq:13.2-err): M^± − M^ad = ∓ω_Q Σ_p[gρ/D_A² − ρg/D_B²] + O(ω²/δ²); relative error O(ω_Q/δ_off) unless terms cancel strongly
  (rigorous bound with ω/(δ_off − 2ω) in D.35); emission and absorption differ at the same order. Si estimates (interband bound):
  1.6 % (TA 18.27 meV), 5.2 % (TO 57.73 meV); 10 % needs E_min(q) − ω ≥ 0.18 eV (TA), 0.58 eV (TO). Valence-band crossings at (k, k+Q)
  make path C resonant, hence lie outside the window.
- Computational connection: DFPT Sternheimer gives only the empty-band part of ∂ψ_v; amplitude also needs the filled-band part (path C,
  short sum, eq:E.4-dpsi) and ∂ψ_c (paths A, D; eq:E.4-dpsic/dpsiperp); SOS route with Wannier-interpolated g, ρ, ε plus Sternheimer
  remainder, f-sum completeness test → E.5, E.14.
- WE 13.2 (we:13-exchange), two-band Rice–Mele chain, N = 6, spinless, Δ = 0.4, t1 = 1.6, t2 = 1.0, g0 = 0.2, ω_ph = 0.060 eV, twist 0.3:
  final pair at the smallest gap 1.448 eV, q = 2(2π/6a), Q = −q, ω = 1.508 eV, E_min(q) = 2.912 eV (margin 1.40 eV). Terms A +0.0176−0.0246i
