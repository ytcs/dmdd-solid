## The reader now knows
- Phonon self-energy = electron bubble with g and g* (fig:6-bubble), same as density bubble eq:4.7-bubble with g as vertex;
  Dyson D_ν = 2ω_Qν/(ω² − ω²_Qν − Π_ν), Π_ν in eV² (eq:6.4-Pi = eq:D.17-Pi), factor 4 = 2 (D₀⁻¹) × 2 (spin); screened g both ends
  (Giustino Eq. (146)) vs bare at one end (Eq. (145)); interbranch bubbles dropped.
- Pole: ω̃ = ω + ReΠ/2ω, γ = −ImΠ/ω full width (eq:6.4-pole); γ_Qν = γ^anh + γ^ep.
- Static Π(Q,0) < 0 in an insulator (softening); in a metal the Lindhard 2k_F singularity → Kohn anomaly (named, Kohn 1959).
- DFPT phonons already contain Π(Q,0); only Re[Π(ω) − Π(0)] may be added (eq:6.4-nonad = eq:D.17-nonad; Calandra Eqs. (22)–(23);
  Giustino Eq. (140)); insulator: relative (ω/E_g)² = 0.003 for Si optical; metal: only Q ≲ ω/v_F (Al: 2×10⁻³ Å⁻¹ for 30 meV),
  up to 30 % at Γ in low-carrier metals (Calandra).
- Phonon-side Migdal theorem: vertex corrections ~ λ_ep ω_D/E_F; Al (ω_max = 40 meV) ≤ 0.0034 → 0.0015; failure modes listed.
- Linewidth γ^ep (eq:6.4-gamma = eq:D.17-gamma), golden-rule/cutting-rule reading, f_n − f_m = forward − reverse; vanishes for
  ω < E_g in undoped insulator at T = 0 (Si 64 meV ≪ 1.17 eV; Raman 0.18 meV is anharmonic); thermal carriers ∝ e^{−E_g/2T}
  (4×10⁻¹⁰ Si 300 K); doped semiconductor ≈ dilute metal.
- Allen's formula (eq:6.4-allen), T-independent, validity (smooth bands, Q ≳ ω/v_F).
- α²F (eq:6.4-a2F), 𝒩_F per spin; α²(ω)F(ω) historical split; keyresult eq:6.4-lambda (α²F and λ_ep from linewidths; full width,
  per spin); λ_Qν mode contributions; 1/ω² weighting.
- Mass enhancement (eq:6.4-mass): z_qp = 1/(1+λ), specific heat × (1+λ); Al: band γ = 0.951 mJ K⁻² mol⁻¹ (Savrasov N(ε_F) = 5.49
  /(Ry cell) both spins) vs measured 1.36 → λ = 0.43.
- Free-electron/empty-core model α²F (eq:6.4-a2Fgas): Al λ = 0.436, ω_log = 250 K (vs 0.44, 270 K linear response; 0.42 tunneling);
  TA peak suppressed (transverse couples only via umklapp); constant α² would give 0.50, 228 K; mode-average γ^ep/ω = 4.4×10⁻³,
  γ = 0.12 meV, ≈ 36 periods.
- WE 6.4 (we:6-al): two Einstein peaks ω_TA = 23.4, ω_LA = 35.1 meV, A_TA = 3.13, A_LA = 2.26 meV (split at 31.9 meV; modes 2.24 : 0.76);
  λ = 0.396 (−9 %); exact parts 0.306 (TA) and 0.130 (LA); TA carries 70 % of λ; two-peak ω_log = 310 K; 1 + λ = 1.44.
- Computational connection: Wannier-interpolated g on fine grids, double delta functions (App. E.5); Eliashberg gap of Al (App. E.9).

## Open questions / for the editor
1. The Al α²F is a model (free electrons + empty core + BvK), not a first-principles curve: no open tabulation of the measured or
   computed Al α²F was found. The core radius is not taken from Ashcroft (1966) because his tabulated value could not be checked
   in an open text; r_c is set at the minimum of λ(r_c) and called a modelling choice in text, caption and script. A cached EPW α²F
   of Al (planned in E.20) could replace the curve later.
2. BvK fit mixes room-temperature elastic constants (Kittel) with the Landolt–Börnstein neutron frequencies quoted by Savrasov; the
   L_L frequency is 8 % low (8.91 vs 9.69 THz), which places the LA peak at 35 rather than ~38 meV. Stated in the text.
3. Allen 1972 and Eliashberg 1960 are cited without equation numbers (texts not accessible here); every formula is given in full and
   derived in D.17. Grimvall's book (spine source) is not used.
4. calandra2010adiabatic in book.bib lacks its eprint (arXiv:1007.2098, verified by title on the arXiv API); not edited.
5. 6.3 quotes ω_D/E_F "about 10⁻²" generically; 6.4 quotes 0.0034 for Al with ω_D = 40 meV (largest phonon energy). Consistent in
   order of magnitude; the editor may want 6.3 to say "10⁻³–10⁻²".
6. D.17 still writes Ω_BZ (NOTATION note: V_BZ at its next touch); untouched here.
7. ω_D vs ω_max: the Migdal ratio is written with the Debye frequency ω_D (as 6.3 and D.17 do); the Al number uses ω_max = 40 meV
   (E.9 symbol), so the 0.0034 is an upper bound (Θ_D of Al would give ≈ 8 % less).
8. The "up to 30 % at Γ" nonadiabatic figure is reported by Calandra et al. from their Ref. 12 (Lazzeri–Mauri on graphite/CaC6);
   the text attributes it as "Calandra, Profeta and Mauri report"; the primary reference could be added.
