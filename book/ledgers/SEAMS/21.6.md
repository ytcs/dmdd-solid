## The reader now knows
- Axes from eq:21.1-kernel: v enters only through 𝔇_ab(q, v) and ω_q, so a library has no velocity axis and no halo; it tabulates
  functions of |q|, q̂, ω per row pair (a, b), class Λ and pair number; stored uncontracted (user contracts Σ𝔇_ab S_ab with
  tab:8-operators); velocity-dependent couplings need no v axis (eq:21.1-eta2).
- Electronic tables: minimal table (tab:10-minimal) 12 real functions in Si/Ge (+4 per version for 𝒪₂, i.e. 20); non-PT targets
  (GaAs) keep imaginary cross terms. E.16 grid 250 × 30 × 1000 = 7.5e6 nodes, 720 MB in double; full R⁰_GG′ 600 TB → coarse
  subset ~10 GB (E.16 values).
- Directions: S̄ for speed-only kernels; q̂ for daily modulation and eq:21.2-multipole; S_ℓ/S₀ (ℓ = 4, 6, 8) away from edges, full
  direction grid near the ZP edge (0.1 rad structure, 13.9). Lattice tables (E.17) 400 × 10⁴ × 3 = 1.2e7 values per quantity.
- Edge sub-grid: PA x³ range 1.3 meV, ZP continuum 49 meV (21.3); E.16 step 50 meV is 38× the PA range → sub-meV steps within
  ~50 meV of each onset; edge entries m_edge, onset energy, p_on (tab:21-edge).
- Final-state tables: full pair list 2.24e5 pairs/node (10³ k, 4 × 56 bands) → 1.7e12 numbers, 13 TB per row; P_seed binned in
  20 electron-share bins → 1.2 GB per row pair; phonon energy for dressed seeds; valley label; retarded weights (Si, Ge = advanced;
  GaAs 15 %); one split convention (window or Dyson) for the whole library.
- Cascade tables: 𝔊_e, 𝔊_h → P_casc per model/parameter set (E.24 grid), plus marks 𝔪. Library ends at the marks; P_det is the
  experiment's (eq:18.3-obs), f_χ the analysis' (eq:21.1-functional).
- Nuisance tables: S^{𝔗_k⁺} stored, not δ_k (geodesic eq:8.8-geodesic needs the matrix; ratio undefined where S^𝔗 = 0, i.e. new
  support); type, basis, key of ϑ_k (coherence); flags: bound (10.10 tabulation spread), missing template, excluded energies
  (below 3Δ_BCS at the Al film edge, 15.2); alternative tables at the same 𝔗 (off-label spread).
- Derived: step columns 𝒜_i(v) (eq:21.2-column), monotone check (eq:D.46-monotone; leakage ≤ 0.034 ϱ_i, eq:21.2-fsleak); stream
  kernels (E.27).
- Table 21.1 (tab:21-schema): 10 rows with "Specified in" column (E.1, E.6, E.7, E.13–E.18, E.20, E.24, E.27).
- Conventions: q from DM to target, ω to target, detailed balance; T metadata vs n_T; per volume (eV²) vs per cell ΩS (eV⁻¹),
  Ω = 40.05 Å³; spin summed; S = (2q²/e²)L, e² = 4πα (Gaussian: ×4π); head ε₀₀ vs ε_mac; unreduced q; |q + G| tables average
  directions when binned; row index order (transposition conjugates cross terms; matters for GaAs, 12.3); retarded labeling;
  split convention; Migdal energy axis ω_e (12.1); full eight-component 𝔗 + reference state, screening set, split convention;
  off-label inputs with their tests; scissor gap and cascade gap as separate entries; node spacing 0.22 eV near 1 eV > 0.06 eV gap
  difference; interpolation rule (L changes 10 % between nodes near 10 eV).
- Versions: content hash + parents; major (𝔗 or convention), minor (added tables), patch (no value moves beyond declared tolerance;
  otherwise major); immutable, persistent identifier. Checks stored as values: f-sum 2 % (E.16), smallest eigenvalue ≥ −1e-6 of the
  trace, KK < 1 %, detailed balance, PT-forbidden parts < 1e-3, cascade normalization/moments 1e-9/single pair below
  (1 + θ_ii)E_g = 2.32 eV (18.3, E.24), monotone step columns; comparison with measured data.
- What a limit publishes with a library (name, version, 𝔗 per table, cascade model, per-bin counts/exposure/detector response) →
  limit, band (21.4) and recast (21.5) become recomputations; 21.7 provenance column reads the same records.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Gap of the KKL tables.** sec:10.10 says the tables carry "a scissor gap of 1.11 eV, a room-temperature value"; KKL Sect. III B
   (and sec:10.6) say the scissor matches the measured gap at T = 0. The 1.11 eV in DarkELF's Si.yaml sits next to e0 = 3.6 eV
   (the step-model conversion). 21.6 does not state the table's scissor value; it says the table's ω nodes (0.22 eV apart near
   1 eV) cannot resolve it and that the package's 1.11 eV is the step-model gap. The 10.10 sentence should be checked.
2. Section numbering: s21-4 was not yet in ch21.tex at build time, so 21.6 printed as 21.5 in my build.
3. Specification thresholds (smallest eigenvalue ≥ −1e-6 of the trace, KK residual < 1 %) come from the planning source's library
   spec; App. E.16 states the f-sum (2 %) and PT (1e-3) values, E.24 the cascade tests. They are printed as acceptance values.
4. The patch rule ("no value moves by more than the declared tolerance, otherwise major") refines the planning source's
   PATCH = numerical refinement.
