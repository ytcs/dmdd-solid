## Notes for the editor / open questions
1. Rajkanan et al.'s original coefficients (A₁, A₂, A_d, E_g1(0)) are not in any fetched source; only C_TA:C_TO = 5.5:4.0 (quoted by
   Stanford et al.) is used. The model edge is built from book quantities (6.3 gap, 6.5 phonons) plus one scale fitted to Franta.
2. Stanford et al. report no statistically significant change of σ_pe between 0.5 and 77 K for photon energies ≥ 1.21 eV (lowest
   source: 1028 nm). Their own fit implies ≈ ×1.2 at 1.21 eV between 77 K and 0.5 K; our ×1.9–2.2 at x ≈ 20–25 meV refers to the free-pair
   edge measured from 1.1875 eV. Not a contradiction at their precision, but a measurement within 25 meV of the cold edge would test it.
3. The ×2.6/1.9/1.5/1.3 reproduce the planned values exactly (TA-only and two-branch). The planned claim that the TA-only edge is an
   upper bound does not hold strictly: with TO included the factor is 1.48 vs 1.47 at 50 meV (TO near its own threshold also feels the gap
   shift); both round to 1.5. The text says "to two digits".
4. 6.5/10.7 quote the cold excitonic edge as 1.1736 eV (E_g = 1.17); with E_g(0) = 1.1692 eV it is 1.1728 eV, free pair 1.1875 vs 6.5's
   1.1883. 13.5 uses 1.1692 (6.3, 10.7) and prints 1.1875 only. Align at review if wanted.
5. The Franta 300 K data are a dispersion-model representation of measurements; its sub-gap tail (thermal broadening) is what produces
   the 1.4 cm⁻¹ residue of the transfer.
