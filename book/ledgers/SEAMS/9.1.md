## The reader now knows
- Table 9.1 (tab:9-atlas): 24 excitation rows × 6 coupling columns; 54 of 144 cells hold a home section, 90 are empty at leading order.
  Columns with their target operators: electron density n_e; nuclei n_κ (coherent weight of eq:8.1-nuclear); spin and current s, j, 𝒥;
  vector absorption j_e, j_κ; ALP and scalar absorption s_e, 𝒥^{αα}_e, 𝒯_e; inelastic/exothermic/boosted/fermionic (same rows, new
  kinematics). Column = target operator + kinematics (region vs point vs line).
- Sector of each row (Sector column): 0 for pairs, excitons, core, Frenkel excitons, dopants; ad for phonons, multiphonons, recoil, solid
  He, color centers, TLS, finite-body modes, mobile defects; + for phonon-assisted; 0 and + for Migdal; ad^s (electron-following on the
  spin manifold) for magnetic rows; (2,0) for SC pairs; qp for gapless (metal plasmons, Dirac, graphene).
- Rule: different sectors add (except the S_eκ interference cells); same sector + same column = parts of one response (the four electronic
  rows of the density column are one S⁰_ee, constrained together by the f-sum rule).
- Empty-cell reasons: operator content (BO orthogonality empties the nuclear column of electronic rows → Migdal row; n_e commutes with
  total spin → no magnons; spin density has no Coulomb feedback → no spin plasmon); absorption kinematics (acoustic c_ν m_φ v ~ 10⁻⁸ m_φ;
  free recoil impossible; q→0 vector/ALP vertex linear in velocities → exactly one phonon in a harmonic crystal); order in the coupling
  (two insertions, 10.8; mobile defects); no new response (inelastic column only where physics changes). Allowed-but-uncomputed cells
  named: core spin flips, spin-coupled pair breaking, spin-dependent Migdal. Material-dependent emptiness: Si Z* = 0 (no dark-photon
  single optical phonon); clean free-electron metal/SC absorbs only with a third body (15.4).
- Model → cells in three steps; examples: electron-coupled heavy scalar, nucleon-coupled scalar, 𝒪₄ (electron or nuclear spin).
- WE 9.1 (we:9-darkphoton): dark photon along its column; scattering via n_Z and in-medium mixing (loss function of total charge),
  acoustic none at small q, polar optical via Z*, Si empty, multiphonon charge vertex, Migdal + interference opens; absorption: 10.7
  (plasmon conversion), 13.8 in Si between 1.17 and 3.4 eV, GaAs LO at 36.35 meV (we:5-lst), magnons via B′ = i p_φ × ê A′ (factor v),
  SC only with impurity or phonon, 16.1/16.3/16.5/16.7; last column 10.9 and 17.3; populates all density-column rows except
  material-forbidden lattice cells, Migdal row, 11 vector cells, 2 last-column cells.
- Phonon-assisted electron recoil (op.): a pair created together with a phonon that supplies crystal momentum; precise in 13.2.
