## The reader now knows
- Force spectrum dσ_κ(Δ) and inverse moments 𝓘_k (eq:12.5-sigma; same symbols as E.18); Migdal spectrum = (q²/M²)ω_e⁻⁴ dσ/dΔ
  (eq:12.5-spectrum). Keyresult eq:12.5-moments: ∫ω^k dP = q²𝓘_{4−k}/M²: P (k=0), E1 = E_R δM/M with δM = 2𝓘₃ (k=1, Born–Huang drag
  mass), E2 = 4E_R E_BH (diagonal Born–Huang correction, k=2), E3 (k=3).
- δM as ω² coefficient of the dynamical on-site force constant (D.34-dynamic); loss-function form of dσ and
  δM_val = (8α/3)∫d³p/(2π)³ Z_ion²∫L̄/ω³ (eq:12.5-dMval).
- Trace rule (eq:12.5-trace, derived D.34-trace: velocity/TRK form, interacting electrons, insulator): Σ_J δM_IJ = Z_κ m_e (one species),
  Σ_κκ' δM_κκ'(Q=0) = N_e m_e per cell. The 14 m_e of 8.6 is this trace. A kick measures δM_II = BZ average of δM(Q); eq:12.5-onsite
  δM_II = Z m_e − Σ_{J≠I}δM_IJ, sign not fixed by any rule.
- Si: cores 10.1 m_e (Cox tables; 2p 7.2), valence 18.6 m_e (KKL LF, Z_ion(p); band 11.8 [Z=4] – 24.0 [no LF]) ⇒ δM_II = 28.7 m_e,
  δM/M = 5.6e-4 (trace 2.73e-4). Atom valence: 4 m_e total, 1.2 m_e in ionization. 96 % of the valence E1 from p > q_BZ, 61 % from
  p > 5 keV, 67 % below 10 eV; table truncation ≤ 24 % (f-sum bound), p > 22.5 keV < 3 %.
- Ge: valence 39.5 m_e (11.5–52.1), cores 26.8 m_e (no 1s) vs rigid 28; frozen 3d (29.4 eV, 0.4 Å) makes Z_ion(p) climb early.

- Lattice mass: incoherent kick → δM_II; coherent phonon at Q → δM(Q); long-wavelength acoustic → exactly M + Z m_e.
