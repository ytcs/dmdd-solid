## The reader now knows
- Energy identity at fixed ω ⇒ ε_eh = ω/n̄ = E_g + ⟨E_heat⟩/n̄, F_eh = Var E_heat/(E_g² n̄) (eq:18.2-moments; = eq:D.43-moments);
  limits at large ω. F_eh is unrelated to the Fano coefficients c^F_ν of eq:8.6-fano (8.6 commitment met, one sentence).
- Keyresult eq:18.2-decomp: F_eh = 𝒱_< + 𝒱_> + 2𝒱_<> (= eq:D.43-decomp), E_< residual energy at becoming terminal, E_> phonon energy
  above threshold; additive deposits solved by eq:D.43-m1/m2; covariance by the E_< − E_> route; identity checks to a few 1e-15.
- Silicon model (book constants, same as 18.4): E_g = 1.160 eV (123 K), ω_AB = 63 meV, B_h = 12 eV, θ_ii = 1, A_AB = 5.674 eV³
  (calibrated in 18.4) ⇒ λ_AB = 3.635, w_AB = 0.0543, ε_eh = 3.232 E_g = 3.749 eV, F_eh = 0.114.
- **Si terms: 0.092 + 0.291 − 0.269 = 0.114** (SPINE/research print 0.091 + 0.288 − 0.265 at E_g = 1.164 eV; the book uses 1.160 eV,
  see open question 1). 𝒱_> is 6.7× Poisson (0.043; 0.93 eV, 14.7 phonons per pair); covariance cancels 92 % of 𝒱_>; 1 % error in
  𝒱_> moves F by 2.6 %. λ scan (Fig. 18.2): F 0.085 → 0.132 over λ_AB 0.003–30; 0.105–0.122 for λ_AB 1–10; λ→0 partition value 0.081.
- Finite deposits (Limit Check 18.2a): b_N = 0.35, c_N = −0.02 (one-sided seed); both ratios within 10 % for ω ≥ 14.9 E_g = 17.3 eV;
  F(ω) = 0 for ω ≤ 2E_g.
- Random parking (WE 18.2, Limit Check 18.2b): mapping, Laplace derivation of ρ∞ = ∫exp(−2Ein) = 0.74759792, ε = 1.3376 E_g;
  F_park = 0.0510387 (recursion 0.051040); gap law ⇒ F_ren = 0.0443977 = 0.870 F_park; source misprints in one sentence (detail in F row);
  with phonons within 10 % for λ_AB < 0.40, ±0.8 λ_AB at small λ.
- Renewal estimate eq:18.2-ren (σ²/ε², energy constraint; Klein population example 54/175 = 0.31 N-fixed vs 27/686 = 0.039),
  cascade form eq:18.2-rencasc; ratios 0.870, 0.696, 0.837, 0.191, 0.415 (Si θ=3/2, A_AB = 5.604), 0.125 (Ge, book E_g 0.744, ω 37.6 meV);
  lower in every computed case (Limit Check 18.2c).
- Linear mean yield (keyresult eq:18.2-linear, tagged-unit eq:18.2-tagged, proof in D.44 under four conditions); lattice remainder in the
  constant; slopes stable to 1.3e-4 (X = 40 vs 80 E_g). Open problems (one home each, sec:18.2-open): second-order renewal (constant and
  variance; b_N is 13 % of n̄ at 10 eV); general lower-bound property of the renewal estimate.
