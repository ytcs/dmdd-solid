## The reader now knows
- Interaction picture |ψ_I⟩ = e^{iH₀t}|ψ⟩, c_n = ⟨n|ψ_I⟩ (same amplitudes as 2.1), H′_I(t) (eq:2.4-ipicture); Dyson series for U_I(t,t₀)
  (eq:2.4-dyson), time-ordered; second-order amplitude (eq:2.4-c2) read as i → m → f; link to 2.3's e^{i(E_γ−E_γ′)t}.
- Adiabatic switching H′e^{t/τ_s}, t₀ → −∞, τ_s → ∞ at fixed t; energy denominator with +i0⁺; cross term of first and second order
  with switching rates 1/τ_s, 2/τ_s shown to → 2π·2Re(A₁A₂*)δ(ξ) by residues; keyresult Γ = 2π|T_fi|²δ(E_f − E_i) (eq:2.4-golden2).
- T-matrix, Lippmann–Schwinger in matrix-element form (eq:2.4-LS); S = δ − 2πiδ(E)T_fi; T_fi = −ℳ / normalization factors of 2.1.
- Virtual, on-shell and off-shell intermediate states; dwell time ~1/|E_i − E_m|; second order ~ |H′|/|E_i − E_m| relative to first;
  second Born term = 2.2's Born parameter (≤ 1e-3), so DM-coupling second order is negligible; second-order processes in the book =
  one DM vertex + one internal target vertex. Harmonic fields at second order (denominators E_i + ω_j − E_m). Time window: t ≫ 1/|E_i − E_m|,
  near resonance t ≫ 1/Γ_w.
- Time orderings for absorb ω₁ / emit ω₂ (eq:2.4-orderings, Fig. 2.4): absorb-first denominator E_a + ω₁ − E_m (resonant at
  ω₁ = E_m − E_a), emit-first E_a − ω₂ − E_m (never zero from a ground state); amplitudes add; = bracket of Giustino Eq. (200); √n₁, √(n₂+1).
  Many-electron denominators = orbital-energy differences; Pauli removes some intermediate states and adds hole orderings (→ 6.5).
- Wigner–Weisskopf (eq:2.4-WW): c_i = e^{−(Γ_i/2 + iΔE_i)t}, ΔE_i = PV Σ|H′|²/(E_i − E_f); Lorentzian (eq:2.4-lorentz), normalized;
  Breit–Wigner named; WE 2.1 check (0.4%, zero shift); limits (t < 1/E_w quadratic; power-law tail after many lifetimes).
- Width in intermediate denominator (eq:2.4-width): i0⁺ → iΓ_w/2, Ẽ_m shifted; validity; widths in solids (phonon emission, e–e; → 6.3).
- Sokhotski–Plemelj (eq:2.4-SP) derived from the Lorentzian; PV = off-shell/real/shifts and dispersion, δ = on-shell/decay; −2 Im T_ii = Γ_i
  (lowest-order optical theorem); Rayleigh amplitude Σ 2Δ|O|²/(ω² − Δ²) as polarizability; dielectric function analogy (→ 4.4).
- Double pole: 1/x² has no PV; Hadamard fp (eq:2.4-fp); expansion 1/(x² + Γ_w²/4) = (2π/Γ_w)δ + fp(1/x²) − (πΓ_w/4)δ″ + O(Γ_w²)
  (eq:2.4-split), derived; δ part = cascade (first-order rate into m × branching Γ_{m→b}/Γ_w); fp part not positive; double counting
  warning (→ 6.5).
- When second order dominates: selection rules (parity, H 2s two-photon decay); kinematics (Si indirect gap: photon 6e-4 Å⁻¹ vs
  2π/a = 1.16 Å⁻¹; phonon tens of meV, any crystal momentum); bosonic DM absorption between indirect and direct gaps (→ Chap. 8);
  DM scattering reaches indirect transitions at first order (q₀ = 3.73 keV = 1.89 Å⁻¹), phonon emission at small q (Kahn–Lin Sect. VI);
  Migdal (→ 12.2); off-shell ratio |H₂|²/(E_i − E_m)² (→ 8.5); first–second-order interference is part of the target response, not a
  separate channel.
- WE 2.6 (we:2-twophoton): levels a (0), m (1.50 eV, Γ_w = 1 meV), flat band b from 3.20 eV; ω₂ = 1.90 eV; |O_ma| = 0.1 meV,
  Γ_{m→b} = 0.1 meV. Off resonance ω₁ = 1.20 eV: 𝒜 = −0.833 eV⁻¹, rate 6.9e-13 eV = 1.1e3 s⁻¹, single ordering ×16 too large; zero at
  1.10 eV. On resonance: |𝒜|² = 4.0e6 eV⁻², rate 4.0e-6 eV = 6.1e9 s⁻¹ (×5.8e6); cascade 4|O|²/Γ_w = 4.0e-5 eV × 0.10 agrees to 5e-6;
  population 0.04. Broadband (Gaussian rms 10 meV): centered exact 2.410e5 eV⁻², δ 2.507e5 (+4.0%), fp −1.00e4, δ+fp within 0.13%
  (= δ″ term); 30 meV off: δ 2785, fp +1795, cross PV 197, nonresonant 6; sum within 0.6% of exact 4753; δ alone −41%. Fig. 2.5(b).
- App. D.3: exact kernel equation (eq:D.3-kernel), Markov step (eq:D.3-markov), resolvent (eq:D.3-resolvent), residue Z and rate
