## Notes for the editor / open questions
1. E.15 l.35 says the unrestricted band sum of 13.2 "uses intermediate states with two pairs". 13.2 states (correctly, for
   independent electrons) that every intermediate state holds exactly one pair. 13.8 locates the two-pair states in the interaction
   (pair creation by the Coulomb interaction, beyond Tamm–Dancoff), which is what the E.15 test measures. Suggest E.15 reword to
   "intermediate and final states that the interaction mixes with two pairs, absent from a Tamm–Dancoff exciton basis".
2. The SPINE/CW1 phrase "the two-pair correction ~ E_x^b/E_g" is a parametric estimate; printed as such.
3. Cold-edge value: 6.5 and 10.7 print 1.1736 eV (E_g = 1.17); 13.5 uses E_g(0) = 1.1692 → 1.1728 eV, which 13.8 prints with the
   reconciliation sentence. Align 6.5/10.7 at review, or keep both with the stated gaps.
4. Wannier–Mott edge model: a single scale fitted at 300 K (as 13.5) cannot follow the data over 0.3 eV (overshoots ×1.8 near the
   gap, low ×1.5–2.5 above 1.3 eV); stated in the text and Fig. 13.8b. The cold check uses the Stanford fit (their 0.5 K data are only
   in a figure; the fit at 1.200 eV is a 6 meV extrapolation below their lowest diode). The 0.61 strength factor (Stanford's
   exponential A_1(T)) is applied as in 13.5; it was fitted with a free-pair form and an effective gap.
5. The "intermediate attraction changes M^x by tens of percent" is argued from the E₁ excitonic change of ε₂ (4.9); not computed for
   the PA amplitude. The text says only that it changes the size, not the k-dependence, of M^x at the extrema.
6. eq:13.8-MT (M_𝒯 = −(1 ∓ ω_Q/ω)g_cv − M_V) is new in this form (derived from 𝒯 = h − V); NOVELTY N7 recorded NONE FOUND for the
   phonon-assisted kinetic channel, but 2106.12586 Sect. V computes it for a superconductor (intraband, Eq. (72)); credit added,
   novelty restricted to the interband edge of an insulator. Update NOVELTY N7.
7. ALP at the edge: the pair channel (Hartree, singlet only) and SOC corrections are unsized; the 13.9 nuisance table could carry
   "ALP: triplet vs singlet edge (pair channel), unsized".
