## The reader now knows
- Random-coupling broadband response (keyresult eq:11.6-random): vertex v_I per site, mass-proportional part drops out for any
  configuration (Σ√M_I e_Iν = 0; coupling-to-mass of 11.8 generalized); random independent isotropic δv_I give
  ⟨S⟩ = (2π/V)Σ_I⟨|δv_I|²⟩ g_I(ω)(n_B+1)/(6M_Iω); exact when the vertex is independent of masses/force constants;
  ⟨f|P_I|0⟩ = iM_Iω⟨f|u_I|0⟩. 𝔗: one-phonon (incoherent, exact), n_A = n_B = 0, n_T = 0 at 10 mK.
- Unpolarized spins (keyresult eq:11.6-spinS): ⟨J^aJ^b⟩ = δδJ(J+1)/3, distinct terms vanish at every order: the incoherent
  approximation of 11.3 is exact for spin rows; one-phonon term (2π/V)N_i e^{−2W}(q²/2M_iω)g_i/3. 𝒪₄ contraction
  Σ|c₄|²|S_χ|(|S_χ|+1)J(J+1)S^self/3. Kinematics: reach to the free-target limit 2ω_th/v_max² (0.28 keV at 1 meV), 21× below
  the acoustic edge; weight ∝ q². Spin splittings ≲ 0.1 μeV vs T = 0.86 μeV at 10 mK; quadrupole alignment of J > ½ not pursued.
- ALP–nucleon absorption (eq:11.6-alp): g_aNN pseudoscalar (g_aNN = 2m_N g_N of 2411.10542), g_ai = (g_app⟨S_p⟩ +
  g_ann⟨S_n⟩)/J; (∇φ)·s term has no final state at m_φ (phonon via p²⟨u²⟩ ≈ 2e-18 at 50 meV); 𝒥^{αα} exactly one phonon in
  the harmonic crystal; R = πρ_χm_φΣx g²J(J+1)g_i/(6m_N²M_cell M_i) = 2411.10542 Eq. (31) (dictionary in text). Si weight
  x_29(0.312)²·¾ = 3.4e-3 g_ann².
- Single mass defect (keyresult eq:11.6-lifshitz, Lifshitz 1956; Dawber–Elliott 1963): 𝖦_i = 𝖦_h/(1 + δ^M M̄ω²𝖦_h),
  g_i/g_ph,κ = (1−δ)Im𝖦_i/Im𝖦_h; first order eq:11.6-first, h_iso = −1 − 2M̄ω²Re𝖦_h (→ −1 at low ω); local mode for
  δ^M > 0.22 in Si (none for any Si isotope); δ = +0.3: mode at 65.1 meV, weight 0.51; sum rule ∫g_i = 3 checked.
  Si: M̄ = 28.0855 u, δ^M = +0.0039, −0.0317, −0.0672; g_md = 2.0e-4.
- Pair term: Σ_{r≠I}𝖦_Ir𝖦_rI = ∂𝖦_h/∂(M̄ω²) − 𝖦_h²; natural mixture O(g_md) = isotope-scattering self-energy, width
  (π/2)g_md ω²g/3 (= Rayleigh rate eq:E.12-iso at low ω), 2.5e-4 of ω at 50 meV; ³⁰Si in natural Si: < 2.2 % to 55 meV,
  > 10 % only within ~1 meV of the 57 meV optical edge.
- Limit 11.6a (fig:11-limit-6a): g_29/g_ph,κ within 10 % for m_φ < 55.0 meV = 0.865 ω_max; −3.5 to +10 % below 55 meV,
  first order to 0.5 %; 1.78 at 57.3 meV, 0.72 at 62 meV; band integral unchanged.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **The SPINE number Γ_iso/Γ_EM ≥ 6.3×10³ is not reproduced.** With the PBE DOS of 5.2, D(50 meV) = g_ph,κ/3 = 15.9 eV⁻¹
   (planning used an estimated 20 eV⁻¹) and Im(−1/ε) = 5.42e-6 (planning rounded to 5e-6), the bound is ≥ 4.6×10³ with the
   host DOS and ≥ 5.0×10³ with the exact per-isotope LDOS (the correct lower bound). The text says ≥ 5.0×10³ and the
   band-wide range 2×10²–1.4×10⁴. Please update the SPINE key result / CLAIMS.
2. **Isotope-correlation correction** is +8.0 % exact (+8.3 % first order, h_iso(50) = −1.55 from the PBE DOS); E.17 quotes
   "about +9 %" from a Debye model (h = −1.72). Same quantity; E.17 may adopt +8 %.
3. **E.27 normalization.** E.27 writes S^inc_κ = (q²/2M)g_ph,κ e^{−2W}/ω (no 1/3 for the q̂-projection on a cubic site, no
   2π/Ω) and a spin weight (4/3)(J+1)/J[..]² normalized to a free nucleon. 11.6 states the contraction explicitly
   (Σ|c₄|²|S_χ|(|S_χ|+1)J(J+1)S^self/3, one-phonon S^self with g/3). E.27 should be aligned at its next pass (not patched here).
4. **Lifshitz / Dawber–Elliott citations** are bibliographically confirmed (Crossref) but the texts were not read; cited as
   attribution for a result derived in full in the text. F row says so.
5. **Limit 11.6b fallback.** "Pair-term boundary" implemented as shaded windows where the leading pair term (first order in the
   second defect, closed-form identity) exceeds 10 %, for the natural mixture (³⁰Si) and for 1 % of strong defects (δ = −0.5,
   +0.3; modelling choice, in caption and script). For natural Si the boundary is only within ~1 meV of the 57 meV edge.
6. **Box order.** lim:11.6a (spin) prints before lim:11.6b (Lifshitz) because the spin box uses the defect result; the
   general box with its validity follows. Labels keep the SPINE meanings.
7. **Symbols.** 𝔟_i chosen for N_i/M_i to avoid ϑ (1.2 angle, 8.8 nuisance); 𝖦_h, 𝖦_i follow the sans-serif Green-function
   family (displacement response (Φ − ω²𝖬)⁻¹); g_aNN by analogy with g_aee; g_{B−L} new.
8. **α reading.** α(403 cm⁻¹, 293 K) = 0.55 cm⁻¹ and the in-band table (12 points, 126–513 cm⁻¹) were read by eye from
   Deinzer–Strauch Fig. 3 (uncertainty ≈ 0.05 cm⁻¹ + 10 %); the data are Ikezawa–Ishigame's. A tabulated source would be better.
9. The 50 meV evaluation sits in a DOS valley (between LA and optical features); the PBE spectrum is ~1.3 % soft (ω_max 63.7
   vs 64.5 meV measured). Said once in the WE.
10. The spine's "Γ_iso/Γ_EM at mK" is stated as a lower bound; the true mK ratio is larger (difference processes freeze out);
    no number is claimed for it.
