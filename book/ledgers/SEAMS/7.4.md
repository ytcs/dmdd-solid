## Open questions / for the editor
1. **ε0 of silicon at low temperature.** The donor p levels require ε0 = 11.4 (fit from 2p±: 11.41), the liquid-helium value used by Faulkner
   and Smith et al.; the book uses the room-temperature 11.7 elsewhere (3.3, 4.3, 4.4, 4.9, D.23, E.10). 7.4 quotes D.23's 29.7 meV at 11.7
   and states the 11.4 value once as a fact about silicon at the temperature of donor spectra. Not edited: D-23.tex, s4-9.tex. 4.9's
   μ_x = 0.148 m_e (from 14.7 meV with ε 11.7) would be 0.142 at 11.4; editor ruling suggested. No primary measurement of ε(4 K) was
   fetched; the citation is to the two calculations that use it.
2. **T-center Huang–Rhys factor is inferred**, not cited: the spine attributes an S_HR to Bergeron et al., but PRX Quantum 1, 020301 gives
   only the zero-phonon (Debye–Waller) fraction 0.23(1). 7.4 writes S_HR = −ln 0.23 = 1.47 and bounds the E³ correction (≤ 0.30) with the
   silicon optical phonon, since no sourced effective phonon energy of the T center was found. A first-principles lineshape for the T center
   would fix it.
3. **Debye–Waller naming** kept as 5.6 rules: the text says "zero-phonon fraction" and names the literature usage once.
4. **ω0** in eq:7.4-cc is the effective mode energy of the displaced oscillator, as in 5.6 (which uses ω₀ for its single mode); NOTATION
   reserves ω₀ for the thermal oscillator of 2.5 (entry 72). 5.6 already reuses it; the editor may want a clash note or ω_eff.
5. **E_d clash** with 4.7's local E_d (level energy of WE 4.7), already flagged in SEAMS/4.7.
6. **Worked-example numbers** print as 7.4 and 7.5 while 7.2 is unwritten (spine 7.5, 7.6); referred to only by \cref.
7. Sb ground state 42.7 meV is cited to the experimental column of Claes et al. (Table 3), who compile it from the literature; Ramdas–Rodriguez
   1981 is the usual primary compilation but was not fetched. Jagannath 1981 (closed) is cited for P 45.59, value confirmed in Vinh Fig. 1,
   Gamble and Smith.
8. bergeron2020characterization is an arXiv preprint (no journal version found on Crossref).
