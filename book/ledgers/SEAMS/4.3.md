## The reader now knows
- KK keyresult (eq:4.3-KK) = eq:C.5-KK, derived two ways: from eq:4.2-spectral + FDT + Sokhotski–Plemelj (real part), and from
  analyticity + decay by the contour (Fig. 4.3a; full steps in C.5); holds for measured/model/causal approximations; off-diagonal via
  i(R_ab − R_ba*). Folded form for even Re/odd Im (eq:4.3-KKhalf) = eq:C.5-KKhalf. Fig. 4.3b: KK rebuilds Re VR of the damped
  oscillator (Γ_w = 0.2ω₀) from Im VR.
- Static limit as inverse moment: R_ee(q,0) = −(1/π)∫₀^∞dω(1 − e^{−βω})S/ω (eq:4.3-static), < 0; reproduces 4.1's static limit;
  large gap ⇒ hard to deform. Large-ω limit of KK vs the 1/z² tail ⇒ energy-weighted Im R fixed by an equal-time average.
- Moments from commutators (eq:4.3-moments): V⁻¹⟨[O_a†,[H,O_b]_k]⟩ = ∫(dω/2π)ω^k[S_ab(q,ω) − (−1)^kS_ba(−q,ω)] for O(−q) = O(q)†,
  any stationary state; k = 0 vanishes for densities. Keyresult first-moment identity 𝓜¹_ab (eq:4.3-firstmoment); = tail coefficient
  of eq:4.1-tail; diagonal symmetric case ∫ωS_aa = π𝓜¹_aa; oscillator check (T-independent first moment, links WE 2.7 and WE 4.1).
  [H,O]_k is local notation for the k-fold commutator.
- f-sum: [H, n_e(q)] = q·j_e(q) exactly (eq:4.3-continuity), j_e = Σ½{p/m_e, e^{iq·r}} (paramagnetic current, named); continuity
  equation in operator form; double commutator N_eq²/m_e. Keyresult f-sum (eq:4.3-fsum) = eq:A.7-fsum: π normalization over ℝ, all
  electrons, any T, moving nuclei; general target: average over ±q. Per-particle ∫₀^∞ form (q²/2m_e) at T = 0 and the (1 − e^{−βω})
  form at T > 0, one line each. Mean energy q²/(2m_eS^st): exactly the free recoil where S^st = 1 (binding broadens, cannot shift);
  small q: plasmon carries the sum (→ 4.5).
- ω_p defined (eq:4.3-wp): π𝓃_eq²/m_e = (πq²/e²)ω_p²; Si valence 16.6 eV (owns the chapter-opening number), all 14 e⁻ 31.1 eV
  (7.00e23 cm⁻³). L form (π/2)ω_p² → 4.4.
- KK + f-sum with ε∞ = 11.7 (working relation ε^{-1} = 1 + v_C R_ee at clamped nuclei, → 4.4): Ē² = ω_p²/(1 − 1/ε∞), Ē = 17.4 eV;
  valence S at small q is collective (no comparison with the measured loss peak; WE 4.10 owns it).
- Nonlocal potentials (eq:4.3-fsumNL): 𝓜¹ = 𝓃_eq²/m_e + V⁻¹⟨[n†,[V_NL,n]]⟩ for pseudopotentials (eq:E.2-KB) and Fock exchange;
  exact rule belongs to the full H; size via App. E.2 (12–19 % deficit with band truncation); velocity p/m + i[V_NL, r]
  (Ismail-Beigi et al.).
- Relativistic (eq:4.3-mv): Darwin and spin–orbit drop out of the double commutator; mass–velocity gives
  𝓜¹ = (𝓃_eq²/m_e)[1 − (5/3)K̄/m_e − q²/4m_e²] under cubic/isotropic averaging; Si: K̄ = 561 eV (virial + HF −288.854 Ha), 1.8e-3,
  0.026 e⁻/atom, 1s alone 8.7e-3; q² term 2.7e-3 at 53 keV; two-body Breit terms same order, not estimated.
