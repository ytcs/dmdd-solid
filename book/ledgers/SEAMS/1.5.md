## The reader now knows
- Four readout channels (KL 2108.03239 Sect. VII): charge, heat (phonons), quasiparticles, light. Table tab:1-quanta (prints 1.4).
- Electron–hole pair, ionization yield N_eh; ⟨N_eh⟩ = ω/ε_eh well above 20 eV (RK Eq. (1), eq:1.5-yield); ε_eh 3.62–3.66 eV at 300 K,
  3.70–3.75 eV at 5–123 K (RK Table 1); Fano 0.1–0.15 (Essig 2016 Eq. (5.4)); 100 eV → 27 ± 1.8 pairs vs Poisson 5.2.
- Step model N_eh = 1 + ⌊(ω − E_g)/ε_eh⌋ (Essig 2016 Eq. (5.1), eq:1.5-step); 5 eV → 2, 10 eV → 3 pairs; data gap 8–50 eV (RK Sect. III).
- Pair thresholds 1/2/3 pairs = 1.11/4.71/8.31 eV → m_min = 0.32/1.34/2.37 MeV.
- Skipper CCD (< 0.1 e⁻/pixel); Luke amplification E_ph = E_prim + N_eh eV_bias (KL Eq. (195), eq:1.5-luke; E_prim = primary phonon energy ≤ ω), 150 V → 150 eV per pair;
  dark counts in the lowest bins.
- Phonons: optical ~60 meV → acoustic meV, athermal at 15 mK; TES; SuperCDMS CPD 10.6 g, 3.86 eV resolution, 16.3 eV threshold (KL VII B).
- Quasiparticles: 2Δ_BCS per broken pair, < 1 meV in Al, 0.6 meV adopted in HPZZ Sect. 2.2.1 and Mitridate Table II; ~60% of a deposit
  ends in quasiparticles (HPZZ); TES/KID read phonons through pair breaking (Snowmass Sect. 3.3.1); single-pair threshold ↔ m_min 0.17 keV.
- Photons: optical/near-UV eV-scale photons in NaI, CsI (gaps 5.9, 6.4 eV); infrared in GaAs (gap 1.52 eV) (KL VII C).
- ℛ (per unit target mass, kg⁻¹ yr⁻¹), dℛ/dω, ρ_T, exposure M_det t_exp, N_sig = M_det t_exp ℛ (eq:1.5-Nsig); pair bins ℛ_n (eq:1.5-bins);
  heavy-mediator spectrum peaks in the two-pair bin (Essig 2016 Fig. 7).
- Rate assembly (eq:1.5-rate) = KL Eq. (11), with S(q, ω) named only (precise in 4.2), fulfilling the 1.2/1.3 promises; dimension check.
- ⟨F_DM²⟩ defined as the η S-weighted average (eq:1.5-FDMavg); exact rewrite eq:1.5-scaling; heuristic ℛ ∝ σ̄_eρ_χ⟨F_DM²⟩/m_χ (eq:1.5-heuristic).
- Computational connection: S(q, ω) from DFT bands and crystal form factors → App. E.7 (E.6 for screening).
- Poisson derived from the binomial limit (eq:1.5-poisson); N_90 = ln 10 = 2.303 (eq:1.5-N90); 95%: ln 20 = 3.00; N_obs = 1, 2 → 3.89, 5.32;
  frequentist meaning stated.
- Keyresult: σ̄_e^lim = N_90/(M_det t_exp r(m_χ)) ∝ 1/exposure (eq:1.5-limit); background-limited ∝ exposure^{-1/2} (derived).
- Annual-modulation test needs N ≥ 144 events per half-year (2σ, v_min = 600 km/s, amplitudes from 1.2).
- Worked example 1.7 (we:1-limit): toy rate = prefactor of Essig 2016 Eq. (3.13) with the response integrated over ln ω from E_g to
  30 eV (Chapter 1 revision); Si, 100 MeV, ρ_χ = 0.3: r = 7.39 × 10⁴¹ cm⁻² kg⁻¹ yr⁻¹; σ̄_e = 10⁻⁴⁰ cm² → 74 events in 1 kg yr;
  limit 3.1 × 10⁻⁴² cm² (10 kg yr: 3.1 × 10⁻⁴³).
- Exclusion plot (eq:1.5-limscaling, Fig. fig:1-exclusion): vertical edge at m_min; heavy slope (falls until 2m_χv_max ≫ few q₀, minimum
  ~10 MeV in the toy, then ∝ m_χ); light slope (minimum ~2 MeV, then ∝ m_χ); toy ratios 46 and 1.5 at 1 MeV.
- Inputs of a limit: measured (exposure, threshold, efficiency, background, counts) vs theory (halo by convention, F_DM by definition,
  S(q, ω) and N_eh(ω) computed); theory uncertainty defined; how an S error and a gap error propagate.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Al gap.** HPZZ ("of order 0.6 meV") and Mitridate Table II (2Δ = 0.6 meV) are the fetched sources. The measured bulk Al gap is, to my
   knowledge, Δ ≈ 0.17–0.18 meV (2Δ ≈ 0.35 meV), not in any fetched source; the text therefore says "below a meV" and attributes 0.6 meV to
   those works. The 0.17 keV single-pair m_min uses 0.6 meV. Fix with a measured value from 7.2's sources if wanted.
2. **Spine source pointer.** The spine says Kahn & Lin §§5–6 for detectors; the detector material is Sect. VII ("Experimental techniques").
   Cited as Sect. VII.
3. **Low-temperature Si gap now has a public source:** RK 2004.10709 Eq. (11) gives E_g(0) = 1.1692 eV (1.3 SEAM open question 2). This
   section kept 1.11 eV (Essig 2016 Eq. (5.2)) for consistency with 1.3 and with the ε_eh step model of that same reference.
4. **Essig 2016 "29 events/kg/day"** (below Eq. (3.13), ρ_χ = 0.4, m_χ = 100 MeV, σ̄_e = 3.6 × 10⁻³⁷ cm²) is not reproduced: the first
   line of their Eq. (3.13) gives 295 kg⁻¹ day⁻¹ with their inputs. Not quoted; WE 1.7 uses only our own toy with ρ_χ = 0.3.
   Essig 2016 Sect. 6.2 calls 3.6 events its 95% C.L.; not quoted (does not match the Poisson ln 20 = 3.0; construction not traced).
