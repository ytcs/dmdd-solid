## The reader now knows
- One-electron H_e = p̂²/2m_e + U(r), U lattice-periodic (eq:3.2-He); spinless with explicit factor 2, SOC → 3.3; U from DFT → 3.4.
- Bloch's theorem (keyresult eq:3.2-bloch): ψ_nk = N^{-1/2}e^{ik·r}u_nk, u periodic and normalized over Ω (A.5 conventions); single-
  electron form of eq:3.1-crystalmomentum. H_k = e^{-ik·r}H_e e^{ik·r} = (p̂+k)²/2m_e + U (eq:3.2-Hk), discrete spectrum per k → band
  index n, ε_nk continuous, band structure; ε_{n,k+G} = ε_nk; periodic gauge (eq:A.5-periodic) adopted.
- Plane-wave expansion u_nk = Ω^{-1/2}Σ c_nk(G)e^{+iG·r} (code convention, = E.7's eq:E.7-pw), Σ|c|² = 1 (eq:3.2-pw); |c_nk(G)|² = momentum
  distribution at k + G; large-G tail = core region (→ 3.5).
- Central equation [(k+G)²/2m_e − ε]c(G) + Σ_G′ U_{G′−G}c(G′) = 0 (eq:3.2-central) with U_G of eq:A.3-periodic; second proof of Bloch.
- Bloch basis orthonormal; c†_nks via eq:2.3-basis; H = Σε c†c; f_nk = n_F(ε_nk − μ_ch) (eq:3.2-fnk, from eq:2.5-nF); 2N per band;
  Si four filled bands, f = 1/0 up to e^{−E_g/2T}.
- Nearly free electrons: empty lattice (ε⁰_k = k²/2m_e, folded parabolas), crossings on Bragg planes; second-order shift away from
  them; two-wave matrix (eq:3.2-twowave), ε_± (eq:3.2-epm); keyresult gap 2|U_G| (eq:3.2-gap); Al: gaps do not overlap → Fermi surface;
  extinct components (Si U_{G200} = 0 for spherical atoms) open no first-order gap; standing waves cos/sin, lower one on the planes for
  U_G < 0; zero group velocity along G on the plane. Si not quantitative in NFE (indirect, strong cores; |G111|²/8m_e = 3.82 eV at L).
- WE 3.3 (we:3-gap1d): U(x) = 2U_{G1}cos(G1x), U_{G1} = −0.5 eV, a = 5.431 Å, G1 = 2283 eV; ε⁰ at boundary 1.275 eV; two-wave edges
  0.775/1.775, gap 1.000 eV; exact (25 PW) 0.752/1.749, gap 0.998 eV; common shift −0.024 eV = U²/(1.275 − 11.47 eV); second gap at k = 0
  0.098 eV (second order, U_{2G1} = 0) vs exact 0.096 eV; |ψ|² nodes confirmed. Fig. 3.4 (fig:3-nfe).
- Tight binding: Bloch sums |jk⟩ (eq:3.2-blochsum); H^TB(k), S^ov(k) (eq:3.2-HS); generalized eigenproblem (eq:3.2-LCAO), the LCAO
  overlap problem; one s band (eq:3.2-TB) with E_s, t_hop > 0, overlap neglected; fcc: eq:3.2-fcc; Γ −12t, L 0, X and X–W +4t, K 3.66t;
  bandwidth 16 t_hop; effective mass 1/(2t_hop a²) (precise → 3.3). Fig. 3.5 (fig:3-tb).
- sp³ (qualitative, Harrison Chap. 3): hybrids (eq:3.2-hybrids) along the four bond directions, on-site (E_s + 3E_p)/4; bond pair →
  bonding/antibonding ∓V_cov (two-orbital problem of 2.3); 8 hybrids/cell → 4 bonding (filled, 8 electrons) + 4 antibonding → 4 valence
  + 4 conduction bands; gap = remnant of 2V_cov minus broadening; bond charge → (200)/(222) components (WE 3.2, 3.4); Ge weaker; GaAs
  partly ionic, wider gap (→ 3.3). NFE and TB as limits; Wannier functions (3.6) recover a TB form.
- Density vertex derived from Bloch form + lattice sum: k′ = k + q − G_U, G_U the folding vector (depends on k; not G_q of eq:3.1-qsplit);
  keyresult eq:3.2-rho (three forms; ρ_nn(k,0) = 1; O(1), no N; Umklapp vector stays in the plane wave); why dropping e^{iG_U·r} is wrong.
- Plane-wave form ρ_mn = Σ_G c*_{mk′}(G + G_U)c_nk(G) (eq:3.2-rhopw = eq:E.7-rhopw); component-by-component momentum conservation;
  empty-lattice check; Essig et al. Eqs. (3.14)–(3.16) mapping (c_nk(G) = their u_n(k+G); ρ = their f_[nk,mk′,G_U]).
- Closure Σ_m|ρ_mn(k,q)|² = 1 for every q (eq:3.2-closure), analog of eq:2.2-closure.
- n_e(q) = Σ_s Σ_k Σ_mn ρ_mn c†_{m,k+q,s}c_{nks} (eq:3.2-ne), fulfils 2.3's promise; eq:2.3-ph = ρ_cv; eq:2.5-indep → Σ|ρ_cv|²;
  crystal form factor (Essig Eq. (3.17)) → 10.1; N-counting via App. A.3.
- p_mn(k), v_mn(k), v = i[H_e, r] = p̂/m_e for local U (eq:3.2-pv; velocity operator without hat, as A.5); plane-wave p_mn; position matrix elements for m ≠ n,
  ⟨ψ_mk|r|ψ_nk⟩ = −iv_mn/(ε_mk − ε_nk) (eq:3.2-r; used by E.4).
- ∇_k H_k = e^{−ik·r} v e^{ik·r} for any H, local or not (eq:3.2-dHk).
- Keyresult group velocity v^g_n = ∇_kε_nk = v_nn (eq:3.2-vg, Hellmann–Feynman); free k/m_e, zero on Bragg planes along G, TB values.
- ⟨u_mk|∇_k u_nk⟩ = v_mn/(ε_nk − ε_mk), k·p (eq:3.2-dudk; Yu–Cardona Chap. 2).
- Keyresult optical limit ρ_mn → q·v_mn/(ε_mk − ε_nk), m ≠ n (eq:3.2-optical); same sign as eq:A.5-optical (both routes shown);
  gauge covariance (eq:A.5-gauge), degenerate sets; ρ_nn = 1 + O(q); |ρ|² ∝ q² like 1s→2p; intraband Pauli-blocked in full bands, metals
  → 4.5; photons and absorption → 4.4, 4.6, 10.7 (𝐩_φ ≈ m_φv).
- Validity q ≪ |ε_m − ε_n|/|v_mn|: always for photons; for DM (q ≥ ω/v_max) needs |v_mn| ≪ v_max, but valence velocities ~α (v_F =
  7.0×10⁻³, 2.5) ⇒ DM transfers at/beyond the edge of the optical limit; the full vertex is needed; eq:3.2-optical is a check.
- Computational connection: ε_nk, u_nk from Kohn–Sham DFT in plane waves (3.4, 3.5); ρ from eq:3.2-rhopw by Miller-index matching or
  FFT; p_mn sum; nonlocal pseudopotential correction to v_mn via eq:3.2-dHk (App. E.7; diamond absorption 15–20%).
- WE 3.4 (we:3-vertex): k = −π/a, bands 1→2. Two-wave angle tan2θ = 2|U|/(ε⁰_{k+G1} − ε⁰_k); ρ_21 = sin(θ_k − θ_{k+q});
  |ρ_21| ≈ qG1/(4m_e|U|) = q/448 eV = optical limit (|v_21| = G1/2m_e = 2.23×10⁻³; exact 2.24×10⁻³, gap 0.998 eV); exact vs optical:
  0.0225/0.0225 (10 eV), 0.210/0.225 (100 eV), 0.496/0.849 (377 eV = 2|U|/v_max; ×1.7, |ρ|² ×2.9); saturation → 0.71 (two-wave);
  |v_21| = 0.85 v_max; validity condition numbered eq:3.2-validity; the 377 eV point is a scale marker (that transition deposits 1.6 eV,
  needing q ≥ 0.61 keV). Umklapp q = G1 = 2.28 keV: G_U = G1, same reduced k and energy; |ρ_21| = 1/2 (two-wave), 0.501 (exact);
  |ρ_m1|² = 0.298, 0.251, 0.226, 0.225 (m = 1..4); closure to 10⁻¹⁰.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **H_e, H_k.** Bare H is not used for Hamiltonians (clash 45; H_0 is the 2.1 unperturbed Hamiltonian). The one-electron Hamiltonian is
   H_e (upright e) and its Bloch form H_k (bold k argument as subscript). E.4/E.7 use H_KS and H for the Kohn–Sham case; at their next
   touch they could write H_KS = H_e with U = V_KS.
2. **G_U.** The folding (Umklapp) vector of the vertex is written G_U, as App. E.7 already does; it is not G_q of eq:3.1-qsplit (it depends
   on k). Proposed as a NOTATION row.
3. **Atomic orbitals φ_j.** φ (varphi) with an orbital/site index j; φ (phi) is the absorbed DM field and A.5 writes gauge phases φ_n(k).
   The index and argument separate them; the editor may prefer another letter (the text uses it only in 3.2's tight-binding subsection).
4. **S^ov(k), H^TB(k).** Superscripts mandatory, following S^bas (S clash 15). E_s, E_p are atomic on-site energies (E family, clash 49).
   V_cov is Harrison's V₂ (bare V is the volume, clash 28).
5. **ε⁰_k.** Free-electron energy k²/2m_e (superscript 0, one subscript), distinct from ε_nk (two subscripts) and the dielectric family.
6. **Velocity operator v̂ with a hat.** Bare v is the DM velocity; the operator is \op{\vect{v}} and its matrix elements v_mn always carry
   two band indices. A.5 and E.7 write the operator as bold v without hat; harmonize at their next touch.
7. **NFE silicon remark.** The text says silicon is not quantitatively in the NFE regime (indirect gap, strong cores) without quoting
   pseudopotential form factors (no public full text fetched). 3.5 may add the numbers.
8. **Single-band TB energy.** The fcc s band is written ε_{1k} (band index 1), keeping the two-subscript rule for ε_nk.
9. The silicon gap is written "about an electronvolt" (no third value next to 1.11 and 1.17 eV); 3.3 fixes the digits.
