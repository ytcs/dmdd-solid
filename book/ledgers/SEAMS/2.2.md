## The reader now knows
- Two-body Born cross-section from the golden rule in the CM frame: dσ_T/dΩ_q = (μ_χe/2π)²|Ṽ(q)|² (eq:2.2-born, keyresult), q = 2p sin(θ/2);
  amplitude −(μ/2π)Ṽ (Sakurai–Napolitano Sect. 6.3); CM/relative separation; q frame independent.
- Rutherford check (Born = exact for Coulomb); Born validity μyy/(4πm_V) ≪ 1, yy/(4πv) ≪ 1 (order-one factors); dark-photon benchmark
  9e-6 coupling product; screening is a target effect, not a higher Born term.
- dσ_T/dq = q|Ṽ|²/(2πv²) = qσ̄_eF_DM²/(2μ²v²), 0 ≤ q ≤ 2μv (eq:2.2-dsdq); contact: σ_T = σ̄_e (eq:2.2-sigma-free). Same as eq:A.9-dsdq.
- Bound target: matrix element Ṽ(q)f_{i→f}(q)/V, f_{i→f}(q) = ⟨f|e^{iq·r}|i⟩ (eq:2.2-ff); σ_T v = VΓ = ∫d³q/(2π)³|Ṽ|²Σ_f|f|²2πδ(E_f − E_i − ω_q)
  (eq:2.2-bound, keyresult) = Essig 2016 Eqs. (3.9)–(3.10).
- Closure Σ_f|f_{i→f}|² = 1 (eq:2.2-closure); elastic form factor; q ≪ 1/r elastic, q ≫ 1/r inelastic.
- Kinematic matching ω ≈ αm_ev ≈ 4 eV; hydrogen ionization needs q ≥ 5.1 keV = 1.4q₀, m_χ ≥ 3.9 MeV.
- Many electrons: elastic coherent ∝ Z², inelastic incoherent ≤ Z; nucleus repeats with A.
- Closed shell: spin factor 2, 2(2l+1) electrons, Unsöld sum ⇒ isotropic. Spectrum d⟨σ_Tv⟩/dω = (1/2π)∫q dq|Ṽ|²η Σ_f|f|²δ (eq:2.2-spectrum);
  identification S(q, ω) = 2π𝓃_T Σ_iΣ_f|f|²δ for a gas of independent atoms (consistent with eq:1.5-rate; precise in 4.2).
- WE 2.2 (we:2-hydrogen): F_1s = 16/(4 + q²a₀²)² (eq:2.2-F1s); ⟨r²⟩ = 3a₀²; F_1s(q₀) = 0.64, F² = 0.41, inelastic 0.59; F = ½ at qa₀ = 1.29,
  F² = ½ at 0.87, F(2/a₀) = 0.25; 1s→2p: |f|² = 72x²/(9/4 + x²)⁶, small-q 0.555x² (dipole), peak 0.084 at x = 0.67, 0.061 at q₀ (≈10% of
  the inelastic probability). Fig. 2.2.
- Dark photon on a neutral atom: elastic amplitude ∝ 1 − F_1s; (1 − 0.64)² = 0.13 at q₀ (screening, → 4.4).
- Atomic ionization form factor |f_ion(p_ej, q)|² named; limit box recovers EMV Eqs. (5), (6) (prefactor σ̄_e/8μ², normalization of
  outgoing waves, spin included); plane-wave reduction (2l+1)p_ej²/(4π³q)∫k dk|ψ̃_nl|² derived and shown to count both spins (EMV text after
  Eq. (6)); Fermi factor, Z_eff = 1, 30% accuracy for outer shells.
- Computational connection: Hartree–Fock orbitals → App. E.7, SCF precise in 3.4; crystal form factor (Essig 2016 Eqs. (3.10), (3.13)) → Chap. 10.
- WE 2.3 (we:2-free): free electron at rest via eq:2.2-bound reproduces eq:2.2-dsdq; σ_T = σ̄_e(m_V² + q₀²)²/[m_V²(m_V² + q_max²)];
  m_χ = 100 MeV, v_max: μ = 0.508 MeV, q_max = 2.69 keV = 0.72q₀ (free electron at rest cannot absorb q₀); ratios 1.015 (10q₀), 3e-8 deviation
  (30 MeV), 2.63 (q₀), 192 (0.1q₀). Light-mediator divergence cut by q ≥ ω/v_max and |f|² ∝ q².
- Nuclei: CM separation, internal ground state, A F_N(q) = ∫e^{iq·r}𝓃_N (eq:2.2-FN); dσ_N/dq and σ_N = σ_p(μ_N/μ_p)²A² (eq:2.2-nucleus,
  derives eq:1.4-sigmaN); dσ_N/dE_R and dℛ/dE_R = ρ_χσ_NF_N²η/(2m_χμ_N²) = Lin Eq. (104).
- Helm form derived (sphere ⊗ Gaussian) (eq:2.2-helm) = Duda et al. Eq. (17); Lewin–Smith parameters (Duda Eqs. (11), (12)); ⟨r²⟩ = 3R²/5 + 3s²;
  Si R_nuc = 3.47 fm (57 MeV), Ge(A = 73) 4.77 fm (41 MeV); Lin's 1.14A^{1/3} fit agrees within 1%; mass = charge form factor assumption
  (Duda Eqs. (7), (8)); Helm vs measured: Si ≤5% at 100 keV recoil, 70Ge 2% at 10 keV and 20% at 100 keV. Fig. 2.3.
- WE 2.4 (we:2-coherent): A² = 784; σ_N/σ_p = 953 (100 MeV), 3100 (1 GeV), matching 1.4; coherence gain A = 28; rate per kg ∝ A;
  q_max = 5.10 MeV at 1 GeV (WE 1.5), qR = 0.090, F² = 0.998; 0.89 (10 GeV), 0.36 (100 GeV); Ge at 100 GeV: q_max = 214 MeV, F² = 0.002.
