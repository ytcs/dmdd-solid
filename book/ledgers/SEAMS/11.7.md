## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Spine wording.** The spine says odd parts appear "when inversion or time reversal is broken". Either symmetry alone keeps S even
   (A.4); odd ℓ need both broken. The text states the correct condition; please align the spine/F planning text.
2. **E.17 number.** E.17 quotes a continuum ℓ = 4 component of 2.2 % (ℓ = 6 0.02 %) for the Si one-phonon term. This section finds
   S4/S0 = 0.028 from the measured elastic constants of 11.1 (S6/S0 < 1e-3) and 0.025 from the PBE force constants at q ≤ 0.3 q_BZ.
   Align E.17 at App. E pass 2 (state the normalization S_ℓ/S_0).
3. **E.16 symbol.** E.16 writes K^{cub}_ℓ for the cubic harmonics; the book (NOTATION, C.3, this section) uses Y^c_ℓ. Align E.16.
4. **Model status of the electronic numbers.** EPM, RPA head, no local fields, q ≤ 2.8 q0; labelled in text, captions, box and F
   row. The first-principles components are the open problem (stated once, here).
5. **WE mass.** The spine's WE 11.8 has no mass; 2 MeV was chosen so that every momentum transfer lies inside the EPM range.
   The lattice amplitude depends non-monotonically on mass (shell crossings); the text says so.
6. **Sapphire/SiC estimate** uses the incoherent approximation (valid m_χ ≳ few MeV); the true single-phonon (coherent) modulation at
   sub-MeV masses is cited from Griffin et al. (up to 30 %). "Single-phonon" in the spine is read as the n = 1 term of the
   anisotropic incoherent response, the EST the spine asks for.
7. **Halo direction.** v̂_E taken along v̂_⊙ (1.2's convention; the orbital motion moves it by a few degrees over the year).
8. Box panel 1 "full" = fitted ℓ ≤ 8 (ten directions); ℓ ≥ 10 enters with Υ10/Υ0 ≤ 1e-3 except within ~150 km/s of the edge;
   panel 2 "full" is the direct angular integral. The 10 % criterion has a floor of 1e-4 of the rate (caption, script, F row).
