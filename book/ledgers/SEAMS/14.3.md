## The reader now knows
- Spin row at k → 0: g^φ_{s_e,a} = i g_aee p_φ,a/m_e; Γ_abs = (g_aee²/2ω_φm_e²) Σ p_a p_b S_ab(p_φ, ω_φ) (eq:14.3-abs). Spin current

  SOC, neglected). Scalar rows act on orbital coordinates only → no magnon channel at leading order (the atlas cell is empty).
  Dark photon: spin row with g^φ = iζe(p_φ × ê)/m_e (Chigusa Eqs. (55), (59)); vacuum ζ, in-medium ζ_eff depends on the dielectric
  constant and enclosure (wavelength ≫ sample); its E field needs magnon electric dipoles (→ 14.5).
- |p_φ|a = 5×10⁻¹¹ (YIG, 10 µeV): S_ab(p_φ) = S_ab(0), F^mag(0) = 1, s_e(0) = Σ_j λ_S,j S_j (eq:14.3-s0).

  λ_S J, dark photon / field / neutron see (g_J/2)J. Table 14.1 (tab:14-lande): Ce³⁺ −1/7, Sm³⁺ −5/7 (ratio −5 to the moment), Gd³⁺ 1,
  Dy³⁺ 1/3, Er³⁺ 1/5. Holds within one J multiplet including its crystal-field levels; fails with J mixing. Quenched 3d ions: λ_S = 1 −

  ⟨S⁺S⁻⟩ = 2⟨S^z_tot⟩/(1 − e^{−βω_Lar}); keyresult eq:14.3-larmor S_ab(0, ω) = (πλ_S²𝔰_net/Ω)(δ − ẑẑ + iεẑ)δ(ω − ω_Lar)/(1 − e^{−βω})

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **g_J for quenched ions.** NOTATION reserves g_J for J multiplets. SPINE 14.4 LO(iv) writes [λ_S/(g_J/2)]² ≈ 0.83 for Ni²⁺, a
   quenched 3d ion, which in the book's terms is the spectroscopic g, not g_J. 14.3 writes "g" (g tensor g_ab) for quenched ions.
   Suggest 14.4 write g (or a new g_ion) for Ni²⁺; 0.83 corresponds to g = 2.2 with λ_S = 1.
2. **NOTATION meaning of λ_S** reads "spin–lattice factor"; the text uses "the fraction of the ionic angular momentum carried by electron
   spin, s = λ_S S (or λ_S J)". Suggest updating the row.
3. **Panel (b) fuller result.** The plan's fuller result for panel b ("two-sublattice response with dipolar and shape terms") changes the
   weight only at O(ω_M/ω_E) = 1.3×10⁻³ (MnF₂) and would show nothing; the box uses exact diagonalization of an eight-site cluster
   instead, which shows the zero-point (1/|S|) correction but cannot reach ω_A/ω_E ≲ 0.3 (the cluster's two lowest states mix). The size
   of that correction for |S| = 5/2 at small ω_A/ω_E (MnF₂) is not computed; it is a candidate for "Unsized leading corrections (App. F)"
   in state/DEFERRED-COMPUTE.md (I did not edit state/, outside book/).
4. **Kittel width.** Γ_M/ω = 10⁻⁵ is the value Mitridate et al. take for YIG's Kittel mode, not a measurement the book cites; the WE
   says so. A measured FMR linewidth of YIG spheres at mK would be better.
5. **MnF₂ gap.** Model 1.021 meV (Vaidya's fit fields) vs neutron 1.1 meV (Bayrakci, 4 K): both printed, not reconciled (7 %).
6. **SPINE source equations.** SPINE cites 2001.10666 Eq. (35); in the fetched v3 Eq. (35) is the cavity–magnon hybrid Hamiltonian.
   The text cites Eqs. (25)–(27), (38) and (55), (59), (19)–(20) instead.
7. **Dark photon in-medium factor.** Stated as depending on dielectric constant and enclosure, not computed (as in 2001.10666).
8. Chapter opening and chapter map: left to 14.1 (placeholder comment in ch14.tex).
