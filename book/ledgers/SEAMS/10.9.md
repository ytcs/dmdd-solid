## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Cherenkov result (new, derived here).** With measured ε, the transverse row of eq:10.9-fermi dominates the density row below 2 eV
   for v ≳ 0.3 (massless mediator). 2608.20715 (HEG) quotes 1e-6–1e-4 pointwise in the plasmon window and ≤ 8 % below 1 eV; our
   rate-weighted plasmon-window share is 7e-4–7e-3. The text explains the difference (gap/transparency; Q down to ω/v) and says the
   photons count only where absorbed. Uses 300 K ε; a cold-crystal edge would lengthen the absorption lengths. The editor may want
   this flagged as an open item for 21.5/E.16 as well.
2. **Spine citations.** "1509.01598 Eqs. (49), (51), (52)" do not exist in the JHEP numbering; the text cites Eqs. (A.18)–(A.21)
   (and the book's eq:2.2-fion). 2011.01940's general Eq. (7) and appendix Eq. (30) are cited for the box.
3. **Box 10.9c model.** The fallback plot uses an exact non-relativistic hydrogen-like 1s shell for Si (Z_eff from the 1839 eV K edge),
   not a crystal; the xenon numbers in the text (0.59, 0.89) use the same model with E_B = 33317.4 eV (2011.01940 Table I). They agree
   in size with the planning estimates.
4. **2006.13918 normalization.** Its Eq. (2) is an order-of-magnitude estimate (∼). The text gives the derived vacuum rate in terms of a
   transition moment μ_M (H = −μ_M σ·B) and states only the scaling μ_M ∼ 1/Λ_d, because the Majorana factor between Λ_d and μ_M was
   not checked.
5. **Solar-core temperature** (1.5e7 K, thermal speed 0.09) is quoted as standard background without a citation; the speed bound is
   stated as "of order 0.1" with the bound's 2 %/9 % at 0.1/0.2.
6. **Gap.** All silicon rates use the tables' 1.11 eV scissor; captions say so and give the physical 1.17 eV (editor ruling).
