## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Coefficient normalization.** c_i^{(s)} are NR-normalized (eV⁻², c₁^{(e)} = Ṽ), following TZZL; A.9's text-only "c₁" is the
   relativistic coefficient (= 4m_χm_e c₁^{(e)}). The text states the dictionary once. Rename A.9's c₁ to c₁^rel if a clash is felt.
2. **q sign.** Operators are written with the book's q (DM loses q), matching TZZL and Catena; Fitzpatrick/Anand use the opposite
   sign. Stated once in the text with the list of odd-q operators.
3. **𝒪₁₅ form.** Followed Anand Eq. (13) (= Catena Table I); TZZL Table III's 𝒪₁₅ is Anand's 𝒪₁₆, which Anand eliminates via
   Eq. (14). Not mentioned in the text.
4. **𝒪₂ row status.** The three-row reduction is derived here (D.24); the published crystal-response lists omit 𝒪₂ (Catena Table I)
   or stop at linear order (TZZL Table III). The text says so neutrally; it does not claim novelty.
5. **Spin-current symbol.** 𝒥_s^{αβ}(q) (script J, two Cartesian superscripts mandatory, velocity index first) clashes with 𝒥(ω) of
   C.20 (bath spectral density); alternatives: 𝖩, or j_s^{αβ}. Editor to decide.
6. **v̄_χ** chosen for TZZL's v_χ to avoid v_χ,max; **𝐉^nuc_I** for the nuclear spin to avoid 𝐒_j (ionic spin, 7.1) and 𝐉_j (C.19).
7. **²⁹Si abundance** not quoted (no fetched source); J_nuc = 1/2 stated without citation (standard).
8. **Coulomb-energy row** named without a symbol and without a forward reference (its spine user is not identified in the
   planned sections I read; 14.6 or 10.8 are candidates).
9. Worked examples print in spine order (8.1 dark photon in the Nuclei subsection, 8.2 the 𝒪₂ example in the velocity-squared
   subsection, which follows Nuclei). Subsection order: operators, rows, nuclei, velocity-squared row, absorption, energy deposited.
10. The table's "Excites" column is qualitative (pairs, plasmons, spin flips, polar phonons, nuclear recoils).
