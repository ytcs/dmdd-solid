## The reader now knows
- The single constant of the random-k cascade fixed on silicon: A_AB = 5.674 eV³ (eq:18.4-AAB), λ_AB = 3.64, w_AB = 0.0543.
- Table 18.1: Si 3.749 eV (fitted) / F 0.114, 2.5σ below 0.119 ± 0.002; Ge (77 K) 2.81 eV vs 2.96 measured, F 0.125 vs 0.06–0.13;
  GaAs (300 K) 3.97 eV vs ≈4.2 measured (quoted, not read); low-T values Si 3.766, Ge 2.83, GaAs 4.13 eV.
- F_eh is nearly calibration-independent (±10 % A_AB → ±0.7 % F); structural variants move it more (θ_ii = 3/2: 0.113; ρ ∝ x: 0.102).
- The moment solution reproduces the R&K Monte Carlo planes to ≤1.2 % (ε), ≤2.4 % (F): agreement of two models only (lim:18.4b).
- Shockley balance ε_eh = E_g + 2Ē_res + r_Kω_K (eq:18.4-shockley), exact in the reduced cascade; Klein = (K1) θ_ii = 3/2 + (K2) uniform
  population (eq:18.4-uniform, eq:18.4-klein); (K2) overstates the phonon-free ε/E_g by 39 % (θ = 1) and 36 % (θ = 3/2); Klein within
  10 % for 0.65 ≤ λ_AB ≤ 84, exact at 3.5 (silicon's 3.64 a coincidence) (lim:18.4a).
- Observed fit eq:18.4-fit, ε/E_g = 1.616 + 2.331(λ w^0.9666)^0.2404 (0.54 % max), and linear law eq:18.4-linear, ε ≈ 1.61E_g + k₂,
  k₂ = 1.2–2.5 eV (1.87 eV Si), agreeing in form with 1.54E_g + 1.89 (lim:18.4c).
- Validation (tab:18-valid): 7 crystals, residuals −6.6 … +24.5 %, mean +8.1 %, rms 12.2 %; the validated range
  0.007 ≤ λ_AB ≤ 14, 0.016 ≤ w_AB ≤ 0.054 (E_g 0.73–9.2 eV). Halides/fluorides (w_AB < 0.006), lead salts and InSb (λ_AB > 200) lie
  outside: their pair energies are estimates (LiF 25.1, NaF 20.5, CsI 11.2, PbSe 1.56 eV).

## Symbols introduced (with defining equation)
- Ē_res: mean residual kinetic energy per terminal carrier (eq:18.4-shockley); Ē_res = ⟨E_<⟩/(2n̄).
- r_K, ω_K: phonons emitted above threshold per pair and their energy (= ω_AB) in Shockley/Klein form (NOTATION row exists).
- k₂: constant of the linear law (eq:18.4-linear) (NOTATION row exists).
- B_h: hole (valence) band width (also used in 18.2).
- λ_AB, w_AB used as defined in 18.2.
