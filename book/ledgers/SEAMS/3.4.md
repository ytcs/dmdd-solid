## The reader now knows
- Variational principle (recap) → best single determinant; Hartree–Fock equations stated (eq:3.4-HF) with U_1, v_H, Σ_x (Fock,
  nonlocal integral operator, same-spin occupied orbitals); self-consistent field; HF orbitals behind the atomic form factors of 2.2.
- Physical reading: Hartree = smooth cloud incl. self-repulsion; exchange removes it, exchange hole integrates to one electron;
  Σ_x is the first nonlocal one-electron operator (→ 4.3 f-sum/velocity).
- Koopmans' theorem (frozen orbitals); in a crystal relaxation vanishes as 1/N (stationarity); HF bands = HF removal/addition energies.
- HF failure: gas numbers from D.4 (E_x/N = −6.22 eV vs 7.5 eV kinetic; band widened 12.5 → 20.8 eV; log-divergent velocity at k_F),
  cause = unscreened long-range exchange; semiconductor HF gap far too large (frozen electrons; no polarization).
- HK1 and HK2 (Levy constrained search 𝓕[𝓃], eq:3.4-HK), proofs by pointer to D.5; K_s + E_H + E_xc split; what E_xc contains.
- Keyresult Kohn–Sham equations (eq:3.4-KS): V_KS = U_1 + v_H + v_xc, v_xc = δE_xc/δ𝓃; exact density/energy with exact E_xc;
  γ → nk, E_γ → ε_nk. V_KS is the U(r) of eq:3.2-He (fulfils 3.2's promise); local vs nonlocal Fock.
- Bond charge in the self-consistent density gives V_KS its G_200, G_222 components (fulfils 3.1/3.2's promise; qualitative).
- Total energy meaningful (eq:D.5-Etot): lattice constant, force constants (→ 5.2); LDA a = 5.401 Å (3.1).
- LDA (eq:3.4-LDA), ε_x = −3e²k_F/16π², v_x^LDA = (4/3)ε_x = Σ_x(k_F) = −8.29 eV at Si valence density; QMC correlation (CA, PZ);
  why LDA works (xc-hole sum rule); GGA (PBE), hybrids (HSE ¼ short-range Fock, nonlocal again), cost ladder (E.1).
- Computational connection (E.1 PBE/SG15): at a = 5.431 Å k_c 0.843, m*_∥ 0.95 vs 0.92, gap 0.557 eV; relaxed a 5.477 Å (+0.85 %), gap 0.602 eV,
  ε∞ 13.04 vs 11.7; bands' positions and curvatures trustworthy, gap not.
- Fundamental gap E_g = I − A (eq:3.4-fundgap); quasiparticle energy E_qp(nk) named (precise 4.7); photoemission bands are QP bands.
- KS eigenvalues ≠ QP energies: Janak, piecewise linearity → HOMO = −I (exact functional); derivative discontinuity;
  E_g = E_g^KS + Δ_xc (eq:3.4-gap; Perdew–Levy, Sham–Schlüter); LDA/GGA have no Δ_xc; Si: error mostly Δ_xc (Godby–Schlüter–Sham 1986).
  Named: band-gap problem.
- Numbers: Si LDA 0.52, PBE 0.56–0.60, measured 1.17; GaAs LDA 0.43 vs 1.52; Ge LDA metal (0.744 measured).
- Fig. 3.8 (fig:3-si-qp): LDA model gap 0.54 eV at 0.84(2π/a); Γ15 2.59, X1 0.69, L1 1.46 eV; G0W0 − LDA 0.53–0.64 eV at marked
  conduction states; panel (b) gaps and m_min axis.
- Screened-exchange picture (qualitative): HF exchange opens the gap; polarization of added electron/hole closes it; screening cuts
  long-range exchange by 1/ε∞ (factor 12 in Si); local KS potential is the other extreme; QP gap between, closer to KS in Si.
  GW (precise 4.8): 1.24–1.29 eV (HL 1.29, Godby 1.24, Rohlfing 1.25), converged 1.10 eV (Klimeš); HSE 1.22 eV.
- Consequences: m_min ∝ E_g; E_min(q) moves; direct-gap masses (Kane; GaAs 0.025 vs 0.067 at 0.5 eV gap); Si valley masses insensitive;
  ε∞ overscreened → in-medium suppression too strong; ~0.1 eV method spread = theory uncertainty.
- Scissor operator H^s_k = H_k + Δ_scis P_c(k) (eq:3.4-scissor); Δ_scis 0.568 (a_PBE) / 0.613 eV (a_exp); accuracy 0.1 eV Si,
  0.2 GaAs, 0.4 diamond.
- Keyresult (derived via eq:3.2-dHk, projector derivative, eq:3.2-dudk): ε_c → ε_c + Δ_scis; ρ_mn unchanged;
  v_cv → v_cv(ε_c − ε_v + Δ_scis)/(ε_c − ε_v) (eq:3.4-vscis); cc and vv velocities (group velocities) unchanged; consistency with
  the optical limit and with eq:3.2-r. Si Γ: factor 3.12/2.55 = 1.22 on v, 1.50 on |v|².
- Scissor limits: keeps band order (Ge PBE); f-sum excess Δ_scis Σ|ρ_cv|² (E.3, 4.3); measured gap includes ZPR (→ 13.7).
- WE 3.7 (we:3-mmin-gap): m_min 0.148 (LDA 0.52), 0.171 (PBE 0.602), 0.313–0.367 (G0W0 1.10–1.29), 0.333 MeV (1.17); factor 2.25;
  false reach 0.148–0.333 MeV; k_c bound 0.407 (LDA) vs 0.474 MeV (diluted by k_c v_max = 5.14 eV); 0.1 eV → 8.5 % in m_min vs 6 %
  from the escape speed; rate just above the edge changes by large factors.
- Closing: pseudo-states smooth near nuclei; large-q vertex needs the core region → 3.5.
