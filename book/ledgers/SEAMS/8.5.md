   redefine to match the planned rows; the text states the uniform rule.
2. **NOTATION row x_R**: amend to "q²/(2M_κ⟨ω⟩), ⟨ω⟩ the arithmetic mean over the per-atom phonon DOS normalized to three (40.9 meV
   in Si); x_R^{−1/2} = relative width of the T = 0 self response; 2W = x_R⟨ω⟩⟨ω⁻¹⟩ ≥ x_R". With the harmonic mean x_R would equal
