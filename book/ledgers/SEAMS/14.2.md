## The reader now knows
- S^z(q) at quadratic order in an AFM creates magnon pairs (one of each branch, momenta k and q − k, ΔS^z = 0) with amplitude
  𝒜^2m_k(q) = u_k v_{k−q} − v_k u_{k−q} (eq:14.2-amp), derived from eq:D.19-inverse. Extended-zone convention: u, v computed from A_k,
  B_k = J^ex_AB(k) with its sign; |𝒜|² representative-independent; (u_q − v_q)² covers both cases of eq:7.1-weights.
- FM: no inelastic longitudinal response at T = 0, exactly. AFM: 𝒜 = 0 at q = 0 and at reciprocal vectors of the magnetic-ion lattice
  (S^z_tot conserved); 𝒜 ≃ q·∇θ_k, weight ∝ q²; largest near the ordering wavevector (→ sinh 2θ_k).
- Keyresult eq:14.2-S2m: S^(2m)_zz = (2π/Ω)|F^mag|² (1/2N_s) Σ_k |𝒜|² δ(ω − ω_k − ω_{k−q}); weights per ion eq:14.2-wpar
  w_∥(q) = (1/2N_s)Σ|𝒜|², w_⊥(q) = |S|(u_q − v_q)²; one-magnon O(|S|), two-magnon O(|S|⁰). Zone averages = W_∥ = ΔS(1+ΔS),
  W_⊥ = |S|(1+2ΔS) of 14.1. Huberman et al. Eq. (5) credited. Rate: 𝔇_zz S^(2m)_zz in eq:8.2-spectrum; for 𝔇 ∝ δ ratio w_∥/w_⊥.
- NiO (J1 −1.37, J2 19.01 meV, |S| = 1, LSWT, no anisotropy): ΔS = 0.078 (J2-only = simple-cubic AFM value; J1 shifts the 4th digit);
  W_∥/W_⊥ = 0.073; small q along [111]: w_⊥ ≃ 0.29 qa, w_∥ ≃ 0.015 (qa)²; at (¼,¼,¼)2π/a (1.29 keV) w_⊥ = 1.00, w_∥ = 0.081;
  w_∥ ≈ 0.25 at the ordering wavevector (0.250 on 96³, 0.262 random sampling: integrable singularity). Fig. 14.2.
- Continuum edges ω_±(q); upper ≤ 2 max ω^M = 236 meV; edges for NiO in the text (from the log); gaps (< 5 meV) raise the lower edge
  to ≥ 2Δ. Kinematics: one-magnon min mass E_th/[2c_M(v−c_M)] vs two-magnon 2E_th/v² (eq:1.3-mmin); ratio eq:14.2-mratio
  v²/(4c_M(v−c_M)) = 5.0 for NiO; at 1 meV: 1.43 vs 0.285 keV; rate near the edges tiny (q² law). Esposito–Pavaskar: two-magnon
  reach ≈ 1 keV (NiO, MnO, Cr₂O₃).
- Final-state interaction: quartic HP terms (−n_A n_B attraction; square-root term), relative order 1/|S|; WE 7.1 numbers recalled;
  ladder eq:14.2-ladder with U^mm_q, 𝖦^2m_q; no cubic vertex for collinear order; bound pair = pole below ω_−. Sum rule: the ladder
  conserves the ω-integrated two-magnon weight exactly (resolvent of a Hermitian pair Hamiltonian), so FSI is a tilt + possible bound
  pair. Wortis (pair momentum decides; Ising anisotropy binds below the continuum); single-ion easy axis: on-site pair lower by 2D_an,
  needs |S| ≥ 1 (Silberglitt–Torrance). FM bound pair invisible without anisotropy (ΔS^z = −2). Open problem stated: NiO/YIG ladder and
  bound pair (E.20).
- Magnon–phonon: exchange striction gives no bilinear magnon–phonon term about collinear order (only phonon + two magnons); hybridizing
  coupling from the magnetoelastic energy eq:14.2-fme (B₁, B₂; Kittel). Derived vertex eq:14.2-gmp g = B_me k/[2|S|M_cell ω_p]^{1/2}
  coupling to the co-rotating circular phonon b_{k+} (= Rückriegel Eqs. (3.23), (3.25)). Two-mode hybrids eq:14.2-hyb, gap 2g,
  contents; off-resonant (g/Δ)². Mixed response eq:14.2-Ssn: rank one per line, saturates eq:8.3-bound.
- WE 14.2 (we:14-hybrid): YIG-model FM (|S| = 14.23, a = 12.375 Å, D_sw = 5.985 meV nm², c_TA = 1.282e-5, M_cell = 5.50 TeV,
  B_me = 8.23 meV, B = 0.1 T illustrative): k_× = 4.63e-3 nm⁻¹, ω_× = 11.71 µeV (2.83 GHz), g = 0.176 µeV, gap 0.351 µeV (85 MHz,
  3.0 %); |X^p|² ≥ 0.146 within 3.1 % of k_×; 0.010 at 10g; upper crossing 0.418 nm⁻¹, 1.06 meV, gap 3.34 µeV (0.32 %); full Colpa
  changes the gap by 3.5e-5 relative.
- Absorption: p/k_× = v c_TA ~ 1e-8; phonon content 3e-12 at m_φ = ω_Lar; acoustic hybrids irrelevant to absorption; hybrid dominates
  only for a k = 0 optical phonon within ~g of a uniform magnon (symmetry-allowed coupling), with borrowed coupling up to one half.
- Nuisances (NiO): two-magnon = new support in the central value (eq:8.8-open), 0.073; its δ′ (FSI) estimate 1/(2|S|) = 0.5, tilt,
  ≈ 0.04 of the full spin response; one-magnon normalization ΔS/|S| = 0.078 (from 14.1); magnon–phonon tilt in windows, unsized for
  NiO (3 % in the YIG model); thermal negligible. One ϑ per LSWT truncation across magnets. Computational connection → E.20.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. 14.1 written in parallel: the text assumes 14.1 defines W_∥ = ΔS(1+ΔS), W_⊥ = |S|(1+2ΔS) (LSWT forms), ΔS = N_s⁻¹Σ(v^M)², and states
   ΔS/|S| as the (lower-estimate) one-magnon defect; NiO ΔS = 0.078 here (J1+J2, 128³ grid 0.0785; J2-only 0.0783). Align numbers.
2. Disagreement with the campaign return: WP4 §2.3 builds the magnon–phonon hybrid from ∂J/∂u. About a collinear state this gives no
   bilinear term (S_i·S_j has no term linear in the bosons); the hybridizing coupling is magnetoelastic (spin–orbit/dipolar), as in
   Kittel and Rückriegel et al. The text follows the latter. E.20 has no magnetoelastic specification; consider adding one.
3. The 1/(2|S|) FSI estimate (0.5 × 0.073 ≈ 0.04) is an order-counting estimate, not computed.
4. Rückriegel Eq. (3.1) carries a prefactor 2 that is inconsistent with their own Eq. (3.23); the book uses the standard form
   f = B₂Σ_{α≠β}m_αm_βX_αβ, which reproduces (3.23), and cites (3.23)/(3.25) only.
