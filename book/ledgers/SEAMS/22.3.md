## The reader now knows
- Scope of the master formula (sec:22.3-scope): three structural properties used by D.25 besides the 8.2 assumptions —
  (i) the dark field acts through species-conserving one-body rows (eq:8.1-rows), once per amplitude (twice in 10.8);
  (ii) whatever leaves on the dark side is a free plane wave, so the matrix element factorizes and closes into a two-point
  function at one q; (iii) Gibbs initial state, observable = excitation read through a final-state class (8.7). Plus a fourth
  class inside the framework: rate defined, no response obeying 8.3's exact relations. 8.2's strong coupling (couplings near 4π,
  m_V ≲ 20Aμ_χT) and coherent/driven targets also bound the framework.
- Table 22.3 (tab:22-scope): six rows — charged-current absorption (22.3-cc), radiative decay (10.9-decay), damage tracks (19.3-bethe),
  strong coupling (8.2), coherent/driven target (8.2), CDW phasons/amplitudons (22.3-cdw); reason, needs, home.
- Charged-current absorption (sec:22.3-cc): operator eq:22.3-cc, Λ_F⁻²[p̄γ_μ(1+λ_Aγ⁵)n][ēγ^μP_Rχ] + h.c., λ_A = 1.27
  (1908.10861 Eq. (4.1); vector form 1905.12635 Eq. (6)); threshold m_th^CC = M_{A,Z+1}^(*) + m_e − M_{A,Z} (nuclear masses);
  ¹²⁵Te GT threshold 374 keV (1908.10861 Table 1). Species-changing rows eq:22.3-rows: O^CC_F = Σ_I e^{iq·X_I}Σ_j τ⁺_j,
  O^CC_GT,β = Σ_I e^{iq·X_I}Σ_j σ^β_jτ⁺_j. The distorted electron makes the amplitude an overlap with ψ^(−), the square a two-point
  transition density, and the distortion depends on the final state ⇒ no 𝔇_ab; exact rate = golden rule with the electron in the
  final state, a response of target + one electron.
- Allowed approximation (p_ej r_A ≪ 1, r_A = 1.2A^{1/3} fm): Fermi function eq:22.3-fermi (Hayen Eq. (6), point charge at r_A,
  prefactor 4), with Z_d the daughter charge, γ_d = (1 − Z_d²α²)^{1/2}, y_S = αZ_d(m_e + E_ej)/p_ej (y_S as in eq:21.7-beta, which is
  its Z_dα → 0 limit). Rate = Σ_f F × phase space × (B_F + λ_A²B_GT) (1908.10861 Eqs. (4.9)–(4.13)). Printed 1908.10861 Eq. (4.10)
  has Γ(1+γ)² for Γ(1+2γ)²: larger by 4.0 (Z_d = 1), 3.2 (Z_d = 53), no nonrelativistic limit.
- WE 22.3 (we:22-cc): ¹²⁵Te, m_χ = 1 MeV: E_ej 626 keV, p_ej 1.016 MeV, y_S 0.433, p_ej r_A 0.031 (r_A 6.0 fm); F = 6.34 (Z_d = 53),
  6.06 with the parent charge (−4.3 %); F 32.5 at 10 keV, 102 at 1 keV; p_ej F → 0.51 of its 626 keV value, p_ej alone 0.10/0.032;
  iodine K-shell a₀/Z 998 fm vs 1/p_ej 194 fm (626 keV), 1942 fm (10 keV): screening by a non-stationary atomic final state below
  ~10 keV.
- Isospin sum rules eq:22.3-sumrules: ΣB_F = A − 2Z (all in the isobaric analog state), Ikeda ΣB_GT(n→p) − ΣB_GT(p→n) = 3(A − 2Z);
  ¹²⁵Te analog-state threshold ≈ 14 MeV (uniform-sphere estimate, 14.19 MeV in the log); below it, vector current only via
  isospin breaking, GT carries the rate; n_j ≃ A − Z of 1908.10861 Eq. (4.6) gives 73 vs Fermi total 21.
- What the charged-current prediction needs: shell-model B_F, B_GT + q-dependent transition densities (first-forbidden where

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Isospin argument against the published projections.** 22.3 states that, in the isospin limit, the vector (Fermi) strength
   A − 2Z sits entirely in the isobaric analog state (≈ 14 MeV up for ¹²⁵Te, uniform-sphere estimate), so the "ΔI = 0" Fermi
   thresholds of 1908.10861 Table 1 (e.g. ¹²⁵Te 430 keV) are reached only through isospin-breaking admixtures, and that n_j ≃ A − Z
   (their Eq. (4.6)) overcounts the Fermi strength (73 vs 21 for ¹²⁵Te). This is standard isospin algebra, derived in the text in
   two lines; a derivation reviewer should confirm the conventions (τ⁺ raising charge, lowest-weight ground state for N > Z).
2. **Misprint in 1908.10861 Eq. (4.10)** (Γ(1+S)² for Γ(1+2S)²): stated as a fact about the printed formula; whether their numerics
   used it is not known and not claimed. Checked against Hayen et al. Eq. (6) and the Z → 0 limit (log).
3. The 14 MeV analog-state threshold is a uniform-sphere estimate (no exchange correction); empirical Coulomb displacement energies
   are somewhat lower. Printed as "about 14 MeV ... in a uniform-sphere estimate".
4. Lee–Rice–Anderson and Lee–Fukuyama are attributed "as quoted by Kim et al." (not read); no bib entries added for them.
5. The CDW calculation specification (supercell, vertex, kernel, checks) follows the research specification; App. E has no CDW item.
   If the editor wants one, it would be a new App. E entry; 22.3 states the needs in the text instead.
6. Chapter 22 Summary: the 22.1 and 22.2 paragraphs were written from s22-1.tex (in progress, ~900 words at the time) and
   s22-2.tex as of 2026-11-06. The 22.1 paragraph names tab:22-calc and "channels sorted by limiting factor" from 22.1's opening;
   recheck once 22.1 is complete (its table label, its figure, the classification wording).
7. Λ_F is reused for the charged-current scale (Dror et al.'s Λ); NOTATION's Λ_F row should read "scale of a fermionic-absorption
   operator, neutral or charged current".
8. y_S and Z_d are taken from 21.7 (eq:21.7-beta), whose SEAM proposes them; 22.3 uses y_S with the total energy m_e + E_ej, identical
   to 21.7's αZ_dE_e/p_e.
