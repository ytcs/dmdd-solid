## The reader now knows
- Chapter 20 opening: 100 eV Si ER → 26.7 pairs (31 eV gap energy), 69 eV phonons; 1 keV NR → 34 pairs, 0.9 keV phonons, ≤ 55 eV stored
  (19.5); readout = efficiencies × fluctuations; 𝔯_coll = 1.154 alone explains the Si datum, so collection is a transport question.
- Shockley–Ramo theorem by Green's reciprocity, eq:20.1-ramo: ΔQ_s = −q[φ_s(r₂) − φ_s(r₁)] (charge delivered to electrode s), current
  −q v·∇φ_s; exact with space charge (linearity). Planar: induced charge per pair 𝔮 = (s_e + s_h)/d_det, eq:20.1-qpair (= 1 if collected).
- Bias work eq:20.1-work: W_bias = Σ q[φ_bias(start) − φ_bias(end)] with the vacuum potential φ_bias = Σ_k V_kφ_k; planar case is
  eq:19.5-Wbias. ΔE_es of one trapped pair negligible (1.3e-5 eV at 10 µm vs 4 meV of work). With interleaved electrodes read charge (φ_s)
  and work (φ_bias) weight trapped carriers differently; the reconstruction E_R = E_φ − E_QeV/ε_eh is independent of the adopted ε (CDMS
  3.82 eV vs book 3.75 eV).
- Exponential trapping, λ_d = 1/Σ𝓃^tr_jσ_cap,j (per path) or v_dr τ_tr; Hecht eq:20.1-hecht (face events); two-carrier depth law
  eq:20.1-hecht2; uniform-depth average eq:20.1-qavg ≃ 1 − d/6λ^e − d/6λ^h (per carrier 1/3 of the face loss: ⟨L²⟩ = d²/3).
  Numbers at λ = d: 0.632 / 0.736 / 0.787 (face/uniform/midplane); λ = 10d: 0.952 / 0.968.
- The voltage form λ_d = μτV/d fails at mK (Luke-limited drift, k_max ∝ ℰ^{1/3}, Leman Eqs. (24)–(25)); Limit Check 20.1a with a
  saturating interpolation (illustrative v_sat τ = 2d): deficit ≈ μℰ/v_sat, ≤ 8.9 % for μℰ ≤ 0.1 v_sat, saturation at 0.787.
- Limit Check 20.1b: Luke work with trapping, W = eV N_eh⟨q̄⟩, within 1 % for d ≤ 0.030 λ (0.015 λ^e at λ^h = λ^e/3); next order
  +d²/24λ² per carrier; planar reconstruction subtracts the Luke heat of partly collected pairs exactly; f_ion < Y_q if the NR track traps more.
- Measured-charge Fano factor eq:20.1-Fpos (underbraced: cascade, partial charge, position): F_meas = [F_eh⟨q̄²⟩ + ⟨σ²_𝔮⟩]/⟨q̄⟩ +
  N̄ Var_r q̄/⟨q̄⟩; binomial limit = eq:C.11-thinfano; 18.5's eq:18.5-Fmeas is thinning with f = 1 − p_ns; σ² ≤ q̄(1 − q̄) (partial charges
  below thinning at fixed position); the position term grows with N̄ (gain spread, constant relative-variance floor).
- Limit Check 20.1c: R&K (A6)–(A7) as printed, F/f + 1 − f, exceeds exact thinning by F(1/f − f); within 10 % for f ≥ 0.923.
- WE 20.1 (95 %, F_eh 0.114): thinning 0.158; printed 0.170; midplane partial charges (λ = 4.83 d, σ² = 0.0156) 0.125; uniform depth
  (λ = 6.41 d): 0.1325 + 1.22e-4 N̄ → 0.136 (100 eV), 0.165 (1 keV), 0.324 (5.9 keV); position term 0.01 at 82 pairs; a 5.9 keV Fano
  measurement needs Var_z q̄ < 6e-6 for 0.01 on F_eh.
- Space charge: thermal emission frozen (45 meV at 50 mK: e^{−1.04e4}); Poole–Frenkel lowering 2eℰr_ℰ with r_ℰ of eq:18.5-rfield: 0.45 meV
  (4 V/cm), 2.25 meV (100 V/cm); flashing defined (1803.02903 Sect. 2). Field change eq:20.1-dE (HL units, ε₀ static; SI ε₀ε₀^SI);
  sheet jump e𝓃_sh/ε₀, max ∈ [½, 1] jump; sheet bound eq:20.1-sheet (single sign; mixed signs e(𝓃⁺ + 𝓃⁻)/ε₀). ξ_SC = 1 % at 1 V/cm needs
  6.3–12.6e4 cm⁻² (ε₀ = 11.4); accumulation 𝓃_sh ≈ R N̄ p_tr t; with 0.127 (as used by albakry2023first) 5–10 h at 1 Hz of 100 eV/cm².
  Ramo and eq:20.1-work unchanged; effects through trajectories; history dependence.
- Luke emission: golden-rule derivation with the LA deformation-potential vertex of 6.2, eq:20.1-luke: Γ_L = (c_LA/3l₀)(k/k_s)²(1 − k_s/k)³,
  angular law ∝ (cosθ − k_s/k)² sinθ; mean and maximum energy eq:20.1-Eluke ((3/2) and 2 × mc²(k/k_s − 1)); energy fraction below E_c =
  (E_c/E_max)⁴. Si holes (Leman Table II): mc² = 0.230 meV, l₀ = 7.5 µm, c/3l₀ = 4.0e8 s⁻¹; at 2k_s: 2.0e8 s⁻¹, 0.35/0.46 meV.
  Energy-balance estimate (upper): holes k/k_s ≈ 4.3 (1 V/cm), 7.0 (4 V/cm), ⟨E_L⟩ 1.2, 2.1 meV; electrons 1.1, 2.1 meV; 0.3 % of hole
  Luke energy below 0.36 meV at birth at 1 V/cm.
