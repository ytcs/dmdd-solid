## The reader now knows
- Lightest mass of a channel, eq:9.2-mch: m_min^ch = min_q q²/[2(q v_max − E_ch(q))], with E_ch(q) the lowest final-state energy at
  or above ω_th at momentum q and q̄ the minimizing momentum. It is eq:3.3-allowed read as a mass bound. Floor 2ω_th/v_max² (eq:1.3-mmin) bounds
  every channel from below.
- Shapes: flat onset → floor at q̄ = 2E₀/v (GaAs LO 36.35 meV → 10.35 keV; Si optical 64.1 meV → 18.3 keV; Al 2Δ → 0.10 keV;
  Ge 3d 8.5–8.7 MeV; Si 2p 28.7 MeV = 2 × 38 keV / v_max). Gapless linear → ω_th/[2c(v−c)] at q̄ = ω_th/c (acoustic edge,
  home eq:11.1-edge), above the floor by v²/4c(v−c) ≈ v/4c (21.5 for Si [111]); NiO magnon c_M = 1.36e-4 → 7.3 keV at 5 meV.
  c ≥ v_max closed: Si bulk plasmon (phase velocity ≥ 9.54e-3 = 3.6 v_max, 10.3), III–V cones v_D ≈ 1.8 v_max (7.3). Gapped linear
  (Dirac pairs, gapped magnons): ZrTe₅ with Δ_D = 11.8 meV: 8.0 keV isotropic (v_D = 7.9e-4), 7.1/9.0/9.6 keV by axis, floor 6.72 keV;
  AFMR (eq:14.3-afmr) named, MnF₂ 1.1 meV, NiO zone-center 4.54 meV. Offset minimum (indirect gap): brackets 0.333–0.474 (Si),
  0.212–0.420 (Ge); PA onsets 0.3332/0.3374 (Si), 0.2119–0.2141 (Ge), 0.433–0.436 (GaAs); ZP 0.405–0.408 (Si), Ge ≈0.32 estimate

  onset 17.5 MeV at 0.164 eV, Ge 21.3 MeV (estimate); touches the floor only at m = M. Migdal opens at the floor (0.333 MeV, Si).
