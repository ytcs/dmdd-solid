  the bounds at all u₀ hold together.

  laboratory frame (a galactic-speed objective is a different program), fixed v_E.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **E.27 notation.** E.27 writes the same program with 𝔎_i, F, 𝔥 and dual weights ξ_i; C.12 and this section use 𝗄_i, Ξ, 𝖿,
   λ_i (NOTATION rows of C.12). 𝔥 also clashes with 𝔥_off (8.6) and D.39's 𝔥. Align E.27 to C.12 at the next App. E pass
   (𝔎_i → 𝗄_i, F → Ξ, 𝔥 → 𝖿, ξ_i → λ_i, μ_i → ∫𝗄_i dΞ), and drop the accepted 𝔎_i, 𝔥 rows from NOTATION.
2. **E.27 wording.** E.27 says that with negative kernel pieces eq:E.27-lp "is no longer an upper bound". Weak duality
   still holds for the computed program; what fails is the mass bound and hence any control of the error. Suggest: "…the
   mass bound fails, and the bound needs the leakage term of sec:21.2".
3. **Model status.** The directional numbers (Limit Check, F row) use the EPM anisotropy ratios of 11.7 on top of the
   Essig table (same (q, ω), held constant outside the model range q < 0.15 q0, ω < 2 eV). Labelled as a model in the text,
   caption and F row; first-principles q̂-resolved responses are E.16/E.27 items.
4. **Pair-bin model.** The examples use the step model with Sect. 1.5's literature values (E_g 1.11, ε_eh 3.6 eV), as Ch. 1
   and the table's scissor gap; the leakage factor is quoted for the book's calibrated 3.75 eV and for 3.6 eV.
5. **Hypothetical search.** 1 kg yr, zero events in the 1-, 2-, 3-pair bins with N^up = ln 30 (union bound) is illustrative;
   a real single-pair bin has a large dark rate. Stated as such in the text.
6. **Limit Check content.** For silicon (cubic, sidereal-integrated) the isotropic formula holds within 10 % almost
   everywhere; the box's finding is that it fails near the onset speed of a bin and that a sidereal-binned search needs the
   program. The sidereal-binned case was not computed (only the crystal-frame kernel range 0.33–). If the editor wants a
   larger effect on show, a uniaxial target (sapphire, ℓ = 2) or sidereal bins would give one; not done.
7. **Novelty.** No novelty wording; prior art credited per state/NOVELTY.md N15 (2105.08101, 1707.07019, plus 1011.1915).
   2209.10902 (halo-independent with in-medium effects) not cited: not read.
