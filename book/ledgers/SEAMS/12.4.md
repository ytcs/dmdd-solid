## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Conflict with 12.3 (needs a ruling).** 12.3's exact free-recoil bracket eq:12.3-bracket uses y = (q − p)·p/M (KKL 2011.09496 Eq. (3),
   q_N = final ion momentum) and drops p²/2M from both denominators. With both denominators exact the free amplitude is
   b/[(ω_e − b + a)(ω_e + a)], b = q·p/M, a = p²/2M, whose leading term is b/ω_e², i.e. exactly the soft-limit numerator; the
   (q − p)·p numerator differs at O(p/q). osc12_4.py checks this numerically (free analytic exact/lead = 0.9994 at q = 2–10 keV, an Einstein
   well with ω_0 = 2 meV gives the same, while the 12.3 bracket gives 1.97 at 10 keV and 4.9 at 5 keV). Consequences in s12-3: the
   "3p²/q²" term, "the soft limit fails first … within 10 % for m_χ ≥ 16.5 MeV", lim:12.3b's soft/exact = 0.495 at 5 MeV, and the 12.3a/b
   F-row lines are artefacts; the genuine correction is (9/5)v_N²p²/ω_e² − 2p²/(Mω_e). 12.4 states the correct physics without referring
   to 12.3's numbers. s12-3 was not edited.
2. The brief's VER items belong to 2603.13052 (Berghaus, Essig, McDuffie), not 2210.06490 (whose only arXiv version has no Eq. (3.11) and
   whose Eq. (4.8) is the f(t) expansion). 2603.13052 Eqs. (3.8), (3.11), (4.1), (4.4) print S(q − k_e + K); 2210.06490 Eq. (3.8) has
   q − k_e − K. 2603.13052 Eq. (4.8) with c_i² = πσ/μ² and F_{1,1} = F_M → A² is twice the standard dσ/dq for O_1. Both in the 12.4a F row.
3. 12.1's ⟨p²⟩^{1/2} = 5.6 keV (Z = 4 model, from the ω_l moment) vs 12.4's 6.1 keV (Z_ion weight): different models, both labelled.
4. Ge numbers are Debye-scaled estimates (no Ge DOS in the book).
