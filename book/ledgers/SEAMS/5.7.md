## The reader now knows
- Expansion of E^ad to fourth order with compact labels i = (R_i κ_i α_i) (eq:5.7-expand); Φ^(3), Φ^(4): real, permutation symmetric,
  depend on cell differences; sum rules over any one index (eq:5.7-asr, from zero total force), eq:5.2-asr is n = 2. Physical
  meaning: Φ^(3) = change of Φ^(2) with displacement. Si bond coordinate: Φ^(3)_opt = −41.5 eV/Å³, Φ^(4) ≈ 90 eV/Å⁴ (5.1, 5.4).
- Computational connection: Φ^(3) from DFPT first-order responses by 2n+1 (Baroni Eq. (123)), supercell finite differences (pairs of
  displaced atoms, triplet cutoff), or fits to sampled forces; App. E.8.
- Cubic Hamiltonian in phonon fields (eq:5.7-H3) with three-phonon vertex V^(3) (eq:5.7-V3): energy dimension, symmetric,
  V(−1,−2,−3) = V*, factor 1/√N, crystal momentum Σ_G δ_{Q1+Q2+Q3,G}; quartic term has 1/N.
- Golden-rule derivation of decay (½ over ordered pairs) and coalescence; detailed balance n_B(ω1+ω2)(1+n1+n2) = n1n2; linearized
  relaxation of an excess population. Keyresult eq:5.7-gamma (Maradudin–Fein; Paulatto Eq. (6) form). γ = relaxation rate = FWHM =
  −Im Π/ω for the two-cubic-vertex bubble (D.17 convention); γ_Qν total linewidth, γ^anh its anharmonic part, plus γ^ep (6.4) and
  isotopes/defects (11.6). T = 0: spontaneous decay only; high T: linear in T.
- Si Γ optical: decays into (Q, −Q) pairs only, no coalescence (top of spectrum). Klemens law eq:5.7-klemens. PBE kinematics (Fig. 5.6):
  TA max 26.4 meV < ω_opt/2 = 31.8 meV → TA+TA closed; LA+TA 89 % of final-state density (TA ≈ 22, LA ≈ 42 meV), LA+LA 7 %,
  optical+TA 4 % (near K); full-vertex DFPT 94.3 / 5.7 % (Debernardi Table 1). Constant-vertex T factor larger than Klemens.
- WE 5.9 (we:5-klemens): measured FWHM 1.45 cm⁻¹ = 0.180 meV (Menéndez–Cardona via Debernardi Table 1), γ/ω_opt = 2.80e-3,
  1/γ = 3.66 ps = 57 periods of 64.5 fs; spread 1.24–2.1 cm⁻¹; DFPT 1.43 cm⁻¹ = 99 %; 10 mK: ω/2T = 3.7e4, factor 1; 77 K: n = 0.008,
  1.016; 300 K: n = 0.407, Klemens 2.63 cm⁻¹; constant-vertex factor 2.01 vs 1.82 → 2.92 cm⁻¹; full calculation follows data up to
  ~ω_opt ≈ 740 K (Debernardi). Lesson: Klemens parametrizes T dependence, does not predict γ(0), wrong channel picture in Si.
- η_anh = |Φ^(3)|ℓ_zp/(3Φ^(2)) (eq:5.7-eta); η_anh ~ κ_BO by the 5.1 counting; quartic ~ η²; cubic 1st order = 0, cubic 2nd order,
  quartic 1st order and width all ~ η²ω (eq:5.7-order) = α²m_e κ⁴ (BO hierarchy); editor's n_A ruling stated: three-phonon width and
  first-order quartic shift both n_A = 2 (Sect. 8.5 bookkeeping); thermal η² ∝ (2n+1) → ∝ T. Si: η = 0.049 vs κ 0.067; length
  3Φ²/|Φ³| = 0.98 Å (≈40 % of bond); η² = 2.4e-3; quartic ratio 1.3e-3; γ/ω = 1.2 η². Weakly anharmonic at 10 mK.
- Failure modes: light atoms (ℓ ∝ M^{-1/4}, H κ_BO = 0.15), soft modes, quantum crystals (He), melting; ω⁵ acoustic decay (→ 20.2,
  E.12); nuclear-response correction (→ 11.5).
- QHA: fits harmonic frequencies to volume; strain changes Φ^(2) through Φ^(3) (eq:5.7-strain) → γ^G is a projection of Φ^(3);
  harmonic crystal no expansion; zero-point expansion; misses fixed-volume shifts and widths.
- SCHA: Gibbs–Bogoliubov bound; derivation of the stationarity conditions from ℱ = ℱ_trial + ⟨E^ad − V_trial⟩ (thermal Hellmann–Feynman,
  Gaussian diffusion identity); keyresult eq:5.7-scha: Φ_aux = ⟨∂²E^ad⟩_ϱ̃, ⟨∂E^ad⟩ = 0 (fulfils the E.8 promise verbatim);
  Errea Eqs. (21)–(22); expansion → first-order quartic shift resummed; cubic only via centroids and the bubble of physical frequencies;
  works with imaginary harmonic frequencies.
- TDEP: least-squares fit to sampled forces (eq:E.8-tdep, Hellman 2011); for a Gaussian sample the fit equals the SCHA average curvature
  (Gaussian integration by parts); classical MD misses zero-point motion; third-order fits → widths (Hellman 2013).
