## The reader now knows
- Five reservoirs at every t after the deposit, eq:19.5-balance (keyresult, underbraced): E_dep + W_bias(t) = E_pairs + E_phon + E_st + E_esc
  + E_sens. E_pairs counts carrier energies from μ_F (free E_c − μ_F, μ_F − E_v; exciton E_g − E_x^b; trapped E_j − μ_F, μ_F − E_j) plus the
  electrostatic energy of uncollected charge; E_prompt ≡ E_phon(t_pr); E_sens = absorbed at all surfaces (signal is part). At t_pr, zero
  field, no capture: = eq:19.1-heat. Proof (D.45): energy conservation with source power ΣV_kΔQ_k; Green's-function E_es; short-range
  interactions only during collisions; holds per realization.
- μ_F is the only closing reference: ∂E_pairs/∂μ = N_h − N_e (uncollected); a left-behind hole stores μ_F − E_v + image energy.
- E_d appears nowhere; double-counting rule (subtracting per-displacement energy and adding the release).
- ΔE_zp inside E_phon, not absorbable; ≈ 0.1 eV per point defect (estimate from 31 meV/atom of 19.1); telescopes along release paths.
- Luke recap: W_bias = eV_bias N_ind (eq:19.5-Wbias; Ramo for planar electrodes, D.45); Luke heat W_bias − ΔE_es; g_L = eV_bias/ε_eh = 1.07
  (4 V), 26.7 (100 V); pre-t_pr drift booked in the same W_bias.
- Fig. 19.4: 1 keV Si NR at zero field: 33.9 pairs (Y_q 0.127 at 0.89 keV), 39.6 eV in pairs, E_st 55 eV (display), 905 eV phonons at t_pr;
  18.5's illustrative kinetics; phonon absorption time 59 µs (2007.14289 fall time); at 10 ms: absorbed 0.943, stored 0.055, trapped 0.002.
- Heat quenching Y_ph(0) = (1 − δ_coll)(1 − d_NR)/(1 − d_ER) (eq:19.5-heatq), d = E_miss(t_win)/ω, E_miss = E_pairs + E_st + E_esc + ΔE_zp.
  Unit heat quenching (2007.14289 p. 4) needs δ_coll = 0 and d_NR = d_ER, which (since ER puts E_g/ε = 0.312 in pairs, NR Y_q times that)
  means all gap energy returned within the window, no stored, trapped or escaped energy.
- Gap-energy release law eq:19.5-release with b(t) the released fraction: Si 100 eV, b = 0: 0.968/0.688 = 1.407 (first order 1.280);
  18.5 rates: 1 µs b = 0.52 → 1.156; ≥ 10 µs b = 0.941 → 1.017 (5.9 % trapped or radiated never return). Within 1 % needs 1 − b ≤ 0.035
  (0.036 at 0.89 keV, 0.041 at 3.91 keV). Linear kinetics: b_NR = b_ER. Dense tracks: annihilation 1/(1 + t/t₀), Auger (1 + t/t₀)^{−1/2}
  (eq:19.5-bimol, the text's t₀ convention: dn/dt = −n²/(n₀t₀), −n³/(2n₀²t₀)); tails Y_q(E_g/ε)t₀/t_win and Y_q(E_g/ε)(t₀/t_win)^{1/2};
  binding alone does not change 1 − b; diffusion saturates two-body loss.
- Signals with bias (eq:19.5-signal): primary (incl. recombination phonons at contacts), Luke; 𝔯_coll = ϵ_Luke/ϵ^ER_prim, δ_coll,
  f_ion = N^NR_ind/N^ER_ind (= Y_q at equal collection). CDMS II calibration (Eq. (1), Sect. 2.1.2): Y_ph exact, eq:19.5-identity; the
  subtracted term f g ω is independent of the adopted ε_eh.
- Keyresult "Calibration identity" eq:19.5-calib: Y_ph = [(1 + g)Y_ph(0) + f g(𝔯′ − 1)]/(1 + 𝔯′g), 𝔯′ = 𝔯_coll/(1 − d_ER); 𝔯′ = 1 ⇒ Y_ph =
  Y_ph(0) at every bias.
- First order eq:19.5-first with C_cal = (1 + 𝔯 g f)/(1 + 𝔯 g): 0.663 (4 V, f 0.346), 0.402 (40 V); first-order error ≤ 2.8e-3 for each
  deficit ≤ 0.05 (Y linear in δ and d_NR separately).
- Exact bound identity eq:19.5-bound (no expansion; D.45) ⇒ E_st/ω ≤ 1 − Y_ph under (a) δ_coll ≥ 0, (b) 𝔯_coll ≥ 1, (c) (1 + g)(1 − δ)x_NR ≥
  (1 − e + f g)x_ER, (d) e ≤ 1 − f. (b) not supported by transport (soft Luke phonons, sub-2Δ_Al loss; sign set by which film absorbs).
  On 𝔯 < 1 (δ = x = 0): eq:19.5-widen, +0.016 at 0.95, +0.031 at 0.9.
- Gap offset eq:19.5-dgap: ρ_g − 1 = 0.254 alone gives 0.048 (f 0.346); Δρ_g = 0.02 ↔ 0.0035.
- WE 19.5: g_L = 1.067; f_ion = 0.346 (Lindhard, k_L 0.146, at the 20 keV resonance, Sect. 3.2 of 1803.02903; full collection there).
  Stored alone (𝔯 = 1): Y = 1 − e exactly at any bias, e = 0.048 (0.055 at the band edge). Collection alone: 𝔯 = 1.154 (band 1.123–1.178;
  1.144 with f = 0.3 and 3.82 eV). Half/half: e = 0.024 at 𝔯 = 1.077. Energy dependence at fixed 𝔯: 0.949 (10 keV), 0.964 (100 keV,
  Lindhard f 0.507), 0.955 with the source's 75 % ionization collection at 100 keV (f 0.380): not decisive.
- Two-bias inversion eq:19.5-linear (exact, linear in (Y_ph(0), 𝔯′)); determinant (Y − f)(g₁ − g₂) at equal ratios; Cramer solution
  eq:D.45-inversion. Si 4/40 V: collection alone 0.9195 at 40 V, stored alone 0.952, half/half 0.935; difference 0.033 ≈ 4× the band
  half-width; 0.005 on Y(40 V) ↔ 0.02 on 𝔯′. Large-bias limit f + (Y_ph(0) − f)/𝔯′ (0.913 collection alone). Hypotheses: 𝔯, δ, d
  bias-independent; f measured at both biases; both biases collect all pairs (zero bias excluded); a third bias tests. Remaining:
  Y_ph(0) mixes δ_coll and E_st → transport efficiencies (E.25), matched-depth sources, two windows.
