## The reader now knows
- Static lattice: nuclei clamped at equilibrium for Chaps. 3–4; justified by M/m_e = 5.1×10⁴ (Si), precise in 5.1; phonons and e–ph
  coupling added in Chaps. 5–6.
- Bravais lattice R = Σn_i a_i (eq:3.1-R), primitive cell, Ω = a₁·(a₂×a₃) (eq:3.1-Omega), basis 𝛕_κ and nuclear sites X_Rκ = R + 𝛕_κ
  (eq:3.1-X); κ is the basis-site index of 2.3 (Z_κ, n_κ(k)).
- Diamond structure: fcc a_i = (a/2)(0,1,1) etc. (eq:3.1-fcc), Ω = a³/4, basis 𝛕₁ = 0, 𝛕₂ = (a/4)(1,1,1) (eq:3.1-basis); tetrahedral bonds
  √3a/4 = 2.352 Å, 109.47°; not a Bravais lattice (sites related by inversion through the bond center). Fig. 3.1 (fig:3-diamond).
- Si numbers: a = 5.431 Å = 2.752×10⁻³ eV⁻¹ (CODATA), Ω = 40.05 Å³, 8/a³ = 4.99×10²² cm⁻³, ρ = 2.33 g cm⁻³ (matches 1.2), 32/a³ =
  2.00×10²³ cm⁻³ (matches 2.5/A.2). Ge: same structure, a a few percent larger. GaAs: zincblende (Ga at 𝛕₁, As at 𝛕₂), no inversion,
  polar (→ 5.5). Precise Ge/GaAs values deferred to 3.3.
- Computational connection: a measured or from total-energy minimization (App. E.1); Si LDA 5.401 Å, −0.5% (Poncé et al. Sect. 10.2).
- Reciprocal lattice: a_i·b_j = 2πδ_ij (eq:3.1-dual), b_i formulas (eq:3.1-b) with check, G = Σm_i b_i (eq:3.1-G), lattice-periodic
  Fourier series (A.3 convention), plane spacing 2π/|G|; reciprocal cell volume (2π)³/Ω derived (eq:3.1-BZvol).
- fcc: b_i = (2π/a)(−1,1,1) etc. (eq:3.1-bfcc), bcc; G = (2π/a)(h,k,l) all even or all odd (proved both ways); G_hkl Miller notation;
  |G_111| = √3·2π/a = 3.95 keV, |G_200| = 4.57 keV; 2π/a = 2.28 keV = 1.16 Å⁻¹.
- First BZ (Wigner–Seitz cell of the reciprocal lattice; Bragg bisecting planes), volume (2π)³/Ω = 4(2π/a)³; truncated octahedron
  (8 hexagons from G_111, 6 squares from G_200). Fig. 3.2 (fig:3-bz). Points Γ, X, L, K, W with coordinates; |ΓX| = 2.28, |ΓL| = 1.98,
  |ΓK| = 2.42, |ΓW| = 2.55 keV; equal-volume sphere radius 0.985(2π/a) = 2.25 keV. Typographic rule stated: zone points upright.
- Crystal momentum: eigenvalue e^{ik·R} of the many-electron lattice translation T_R (eq:3.1-crystalmomentum), defined mod G, taken in
  the BZ; "quasi-momentum" named once; single-electron form = Bloch's theorem (→ 3.2).
- BvK: N = N₁N₂N₃ cells, V = NΩ (eq:3.1-V), k grid (eq:3.1-kgrid), N points, density V/(2π)³.
- Keyresult lattice sum Σ_R e^{ik·R} = N Σ_G δ_{k,G} (eq:3.1-latticesum), derived (geometric sums); δ³ form and grid errors → App. C.2.
- Band filling: 2N states per band; Si 8 valence electrons per cell → four filled bands (Essig 2016 Sect. 4.1); 1 kg Si: N = 1.07×10²⁵.
  Free-electron check: four zone volumes → k_F = 4^{1/3}·2.25 = 3.57 keV (= 2.5's k_F); q₀ = 1.7 × equal-volume zone radius.
- Selection rule from symmetry alone (many-body): ⟨f|n_e(q)|i⟩ ≠ 0 only if k_f = k_i + q − G (eq:3.1-selection); single-electron
  vertex → 3.2.
- q = q_r + G_q, G_q nearest reciprocal vector, q_r ∈ BZ (eq:3.1-qsplit); target's crystal momentum changes by q_r, G_q to the lattice
  (no recoil energy, static lattice); Umklapp ("flipping over") vs normal process, defined by the G in eq:3.1-selection; depends on
  k_i; every transition is Umklapp for |q| > 2|ΓW|. Fig. 3.3 (fig:3-umklapp), k_z = 0 plane.
- q never folded (restated): DM kinematics need |q|; response depends on q_r and G_q separately (large G_q probes cores → 3.5; App. A.3).
- WE 3.1 (we:3-scales): 2π/a = 2283 eV; q ≥ E_g/v_max = 0.42 keV = 0.18(2π/a); max q depositing E_g: 4.84 keV (1 MeV), 52.6 keV
  (10 MeV), 530 keV (100 MeV) = 2.12, 23.0, 232 (2π/a); lower end 0.46 keV at 1 MeV (as 1.3) → 0.42 keV; 14 G (8 + 6) inside at 1 MeV; ~1.3×10⁴ (direct count 13,106) and 1.3×10⁷ G
  inside 2m_χv_max at 10 and 100 MeV; number ∝ m_χ³ (→ 3.5, App. E.7).
- Lattice-periodic sum of atomic terms: f_G = Ω⁻¹ Σ_κ e^{iG·𝛕_κ} ṽ_κ(G) (eq:3.1-fG), derived; basis structure factor S^bas(G) =
  Σ_κ e^{iG·𝛕_κ} (eq:3.1-Sbas); extinction. Clamped nuclei: Σ_κ Z_κ n_κ(k) = N Σ_G δ_{k,G} Σ_κ Z_κ e^{iG·𝛕_κ} (fulfils 2.3's promise);
  e–N potential components with ṽ_κ = −Z_κ v_C(|G|); DM on clamped nuclei: elastic at q = G only, no energy (2.2's Bragg-like part).
- WE 3.2 (we:3-diamond): S^bas = 1 + e^{iπ(h+k+l)/2}; (111) 1 − i, |S|² = 2; (220), (400) 2; (200), (222) 0; X-ray extinction for
  spherical atoms; potential's (200)/(222) components only from non-spherical (bond) charge (→ 3.4); GaAs nuclear factor 31 − 33 = −2
  at (200) vs 64 at (220), ratio 4/4096 ≈ 10⁻³. Mixed-parity absence explained as extinction of the sc four-point description.
- Chapter 3 opener: q₀ wavelength 3.3 Å vs 2.35 Å bond; free gas vs gap ~1.1 eV; electronic structure sets thresholds; DFT forward.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Γ typography.** NOTATION clash 10 says rates are italic Γ; in this preamble (lmodern, no isomath) \Gamma is upright, and Chap. 2
   writes rates as \Gamma. 3.1 writes zone points as \mathrm{\Gamma} and states the rule, but the two render identically. Suggest rates
   use \varGamma (italic) book-wide, or a macro \GammaPt for the zone point.
2. **Ge, GaAs lattice constants** not quoted numerically (no fetched public full text; Griffin et al. 1807.10291 Table I gives only a
   calculated 5.76 Å for GaAs). Text says "a few percent larger" (Ge) and defers to 3.3; please have 3.3 fix both with a source.
3. **New symbols.** 𝛕_κ: bold tau with species subscript; the scalar τ is imaginary time (4.7) — the bold and the mandatory κ separate
   them (Giustino writes τ_κ). S^bas(G): superscript mandatory, follows the S^st pattern (S clash 15). If the editor prefers, the
   letter could change (e.g. F^bas); 3.1 is its only defining use.
4. **App. C.2 overlap.** C.2 also derives the lattice sum (it calls it "the lattice sum of sec:3.1"). 3.1 now carries the derivation
   (keyresult eq:3.1-latticesum); C.2's paragraph could be cut to a pointer and keep only the δ³ form, unfolding and grid errors.
5. The spine's "2π/a ≈ 2.3 keV" is printed as 2.28 keV (as 1.3).
6. GaAs "polar" is asserted from the absence of inversion; 5.5 should define polar (nonzero Born charges) and may point back.
