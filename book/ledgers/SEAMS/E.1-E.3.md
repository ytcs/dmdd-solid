# SEAM: Appendix E.1–E.3 (density-functional theory in practice; pseudopotentials and all-electron reconstruction; quasiparticle corrections)

## The reader now knows
- E.1: the three classes of DFT output (ground-state quantities; auxiliary ε_nk, ψ_nk; responses) and their trust levels; DFT sits at n_B = 0, n_T = 0 and supplies the 𝒳 = IP states of 𝔗₀; the xc functional is not a component of 𝔗 (error by comparison, 8.5/8.8).
- E.1: LDA (Ceperley–Alder/Perdew–Zunger), PBE, HSE (¼ short-range exact exchange); trends: LDA a −0.2…−0.6 %, PBE a +0.9…+2.1 % (Si, Ge, GaAs; tab:E-functionals), PBE V₀ +3.6 % over elemental crystals; HSE gap MAE 0.38 eV over 41 compounds (0.18 eV typical semiconductors); hybrid cost factor n_k N_occ (derived), 10–10³× semilocal.
- E.1: N_PW = Ωk_cut³/6π² (eq:E.1-npw; Si 60 Ry: 2121 PW, density 1.7×10⁴, FFT ≥ 37 → 40³); cutoff scans Si/Ge (tab:E-cutoff); variational convergence; Pulay stress.
- E.1: k grids (points to C.2 for the error structure); Si energy 1.6/0.3/0.06 meV/atom for 8→10→12→14; ε_∞ 14.36/13.57/13.29/13.20 (a_PBE), 13.04 (a_exp, 18³); responses converge slower than energies.
- E.1: smearing: Sommerfeld E and F_sm = E − σ_sm S_sm with ±(π²/3)Ω𝒩_F σ² (eq:E.1-sommerfeld), ½(E + F_sm) correct to σ⁴, forces from F_sm; Methfessel–Paxton; cold smearing (eq:E.1-cold); no smearing for insulators.
- E.1: SCF error propagation δn ← (1 − β_mix ε)δn (eq:E.1-mixing), charge sloshing β_mix ≲ 2(2π/k_TF L)², Kerker, Broyden/Pulay; cost per iteration (eq:E.1-cost), N_at³; spin–orbit ×8 on the N_b²N_PW term.
- E.1: effective masses by finite differences (eq:E.1-fd, errors h²ε''''/12 and 4δε/h²), k·p, Wannier; worked example we:E-si-bands: PBE a = 5.477 Å, E_g 0.602 eV (0.557 at a_exp), k_c = 0.840(3), E_dir(Γ) 2.55 eV, m*_∥ = 0.95 (exp 0.92).
- E.1 pitfalls: gap depends on a (45 meV for 0.85 %); Ge PBE band order (Γ 0.047 eV below L 0.191 eV; metal at a_PBE) not repairable by a scissor; PBE ε_∞ of Si +11 % (13.04 vs 11.7), Ge ~10⁴; smearing hides a closed gap.
- E.2: KB separable form (eq:E.2-KB); frozen core and its three error types; construction (AE atom, r_c, Hamann's residual kinetic energy eq:E.2-residual, unscreening, NLCC, separable form); ultrasoft and PAW; SG15 Si (r_c 2.40/3.56 bohr, q_opt 5.11/3.50 bohr⁻¹, 4 e; inputs of the book calculation as listed in the file, not stated in Schlipf–Gygi) and Ge (14 e, r_c 2.54–2.67 bohr, q_opt ≤ 6.36 bohr⁻¹).
- E.2: tests: logarithmic derivatives, ghosts (PseudoDojo scan to ~200 eV), transferability, Δ gauge (eq:E.2-delta; AE codes 0.3–0.5 meV/atom, PseudoDojo mean ≈ 0.7 meV/atom excl. Cr, Mn, Fe; earlier NC tables 1.4), Si LDA ONCV a = 10.21 bohr = AE, B 95.8 vs 97.1 GPa; functional consistency.
- E.2: semicore Ge 3d (~28 eV below VBM): 80 vs 60 Ry, 7 vs 2 bands/atom; GaAs 25 meV/atom between 100 and 120 Ry.
- E.2: PAW reconstruction of ρ_mn (eq:E.2-PAW, eq:E.2-rhoPAW, eq:E.2-onecentre); NC removes the q⁰ term only; pseudo vertex reliable for q r_c ≪ 1, falls too fast above q_opt; exact zero for |q| > 2k_cut (58 keV at 60 Ry); Griffin et al. (E_cut 1 keV, E_AE 2 keV → 45 keV; heavy-mediator rate raised above ~15 eV; Ge 3d dominance).
- E.2: modified f-sum with a nonlocal potential (eq:E.2-dc, eq:E.2-fsumNL: δ_NL(q)); Si plane-wave f-sum 0.81 (16 bands) → 0.88 (100 bands); v = p/m_e + i[V_NL, r]; density vertex needs no nonlocal correction.
- E.3: QP energies, z_qp, widths; QP-corrected IP response stays at 𝒳 = IP; GW = Hedin with Λ = 1.
- E.3: scissor: Δ_scis = 0.568 eV (a_PBE) / 0.613 eV (a_exp); accuracy 0.1 eV Si, 0.2 GaAs, 0.4 diamond; ρ_cv unchanged, v rescaled, f-sum shifted by Δ_scis Σ_c|ρ_cv|², ε_∞ lowered; target-gap bookkeeping (ZPR, optical vs fundamental, band order).
- E.3: G₀W₀ in five steps; ρ_mn(k, q + G) (eq:E.3-M) shared with dark-matter rates; GPP constraints; Σ_SX/Σ_CH and Σ_x/Σ_c; linearized QP equation (eq:E.3-qp), z_qp 0.7–0.8; tab:E-gw (LDA / GW range / measured; Ge core treatment: LDA core Γ 0.53 < L 0.58, CPP Γ 0.85, L 0.73).
- E.3: QSGW: overestimates Γ–Γ gaps by ~0.2 eV, others ~0.1 eV, ε_∞ slightly low; GaAs 1.77 eV and m*_Γ 0.077 vs 1.52 and 0.067 (measured, 3.3).
- E.3: lifetime 2z_qp|Im Σ|; e–e decay threshold ≥ E_g above the CBM; GPP gives no lifetimes.
- E.3: v^QP_mn = v^KS_mn (E^QP_m − E^QP_n)/(ε_m − ε_n) for a QP Hamiltonian diagonal in the KS basis (eq:E.3-vrescale, derived).
- E.3: convergence (N_b with E^ε_cut tied by one band per plane wave; Si 10 Ry → 144 G, ~150 bands; 15 Ry → 265; Coulomb-hole slow; q → 0 head/wings; frequency treatment; Σ_x to the pair-density cutoff); cost N_at⁴ (derived after BerkeleyGW).
- E.3: worked example we:E-si-gap: three routes (scissor, HSE 1.22, G₀W₀ 1.24–1.29) agree to ~0.1 eV; SOC lowers a no-SOC gap by Δ_SO/3 (15 meV Si, 0.10 eV Ge; derived).
