## The reader now knows
- Two insertions (sec:10.8-rate): contact operator (quadratic in the field, first order in T, two-point S at the pair's
  (q, ω)) vs two linear couplings joined by the target propagator, effective operator 𝒯^(2)_ab (eq:10.8-T2, both time
  orderings, four-point function; static polarizability when off resonance); seagull e²A²/2m_e is the contact term of
  a vector coupling and gives the Thomson limit when ω ≫ binding; contact × second-order interference needs a quadratic
  (three-point) response.
- Pair absorption (sec:10.8-pair): complex scalar, each species ρ_χ/2m_φ; contact Hamiltonian eq:10.8-Hpair (Bauer
  Eq. (3)) with G_S, G_P, G_V, G_A; pair factors 1 and (p − p̄)^μ with 1/(V√(4EĒ)); rate eq:10.8-pair; Bauer's ξ = 44.8
  ⇔ ρ_χ/2m_φ per species (0.99415 cut share; 44.81 checked); their ρ = 0.39 → rates ×1.69 above ours.
  q ≤ 2m v_max = 5.3e-3 m; K̄ = m⟨v²⟩ = 1.60e-6 m (⟨v²⟩^{1/2} = 379 km/s), spread ≈ K̄/2; qa < 1e-4 at 2m = 10 eV.
  Contact form eq:10.8-contact = single absorption at (0, 2m_φ).
  S → kinetic row, g_eff² = (ρ_χ/2m_φ)m_e²G²/m_φ (eq:10.8-geff), rate ∝ ρ_χ²/m_φ⁴.
  P → axioelectric row at 2m_φ, unsuppressed (∂_t(φ†φ) = −2im_φ in eq:1.4-alp), eq:10.8-P = (ρ/2m)² 3mG_P²σ₁(2m)/e².
  V → (p + p̄)·(p − p̄) = 0 exactly: dark photon of 4-momentum p + p̄ and polarization (p − p̄); eq:10.8-V with L(2m) and
  ⟨|v − v̄|²⟩ (402 km/s; 1.8e-6). A → O(v⁴) without SOC or magnetic order (s_e(0) = total spin); comes back for Zeeman
  levels (Bauer hydrogen) or magnons (14.3).
  Si at m = 5 eV, G = G_F (per kg-yr): S 1.6e-12 (EPM model), P 4.0e-17, V 4.7e-24; 3 events need G_S = 1.4e6 G_F
  (Λ ≈ 0.25 GeV); g_eff = 1.3e-21; density row 1/50 of kinetic at 10 eV.
  Real field, linear coupling: second order via eq:10.8-T2, identical bosons n²/2 pairs; matters only where a selection
  rule forbids single absorption.
- Limit 10.8a: Lorentzian line of half-width γ; peak within 10 % for γ ≥ 1.5 K̄ (on-line rate 3.8 K̄); Var(K) = 0.253 K̄²;
  narrow-line limit πγ max p_K = Bauer's discrete-transition regime.
- Dark Compton (sec:10.8-compton): electron Hamiltonian with A + ζA'; transition operator eq:10.8-TC (seagull
  (ζe²/m_e)(ê·ê*_γ)n_e(q) + two orderings of the current rows); rate eq:10.8-CS; free electron at rest: both orderings
  vanish → eq:10.8-thomson, σ_Th = e⁴/6πm_e² = 6.65e-25 cm², polarization average 2/3, isotropic.
  Crystal: |q| ≈ m_φ (47 Å⁻¹ at 100 keV), impulse approximation, Compton profiles, binding = second-order terms; Si K
  (1.84 keV) reached by ω_C at 45 keV, L (≈100 eV) at 10 keV; n_e/ρ_T = 3.00e23 /g; n_r − 1 = −2e-7 at 50 keV.
  Exact tree level: ω_γ = m(m + 2m_e)/2(m + m_e), ω_C = m²/2(m + m_e); eq:10.8-compton (Hochberg Eq. (2)) exact at all
  x (Dirac trace, 12 digits, x = 1e-3…5); |M̄|² = (4/3)e⁴ζ²(m² + 2mm_e + 2m_e²)/[m_e(m + m_e)]; → σ_Th/4 at x ≫ 1.
  Series eq:10.8-series: exact 1 − 3x/2 + 5x²/2 − 15x³/4; seagull −35x³/8; exact-ω_γ seagull −17x³/4; difference 5/8 =

  Si: Compton = absorption at 55 keV, ×6.2 at 100 keV; Ge crossover 144 keV (PE ×15 of Si). 92 keV photon travels ≈ 2 cm

  ALP: spin seagull H_sg = (eg_aee/m_e²)(∂_tφ)A·s_e from eq:1.4-alp + minimal coupling; NR rate eq:10.8-alpNR (leading

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Published axion-like dark Compton rate.** 10.8 states that 2109.08168 Eq. (3) (= App. Eq. (17)) is larger than the
   tree-level rate by (m + 2m_e)/(m + m_e). Verified here independently (compton10_8.py: explicit Dirac traces at
   threshold; their printed |M|², Eq. (15), and the vector Eq. (9) both equal 1/4 of the trace at every x, i.e. a common
   normalization, so the error is in the final-state integration (15) → (17)). The text states it as physics, no
   "refuted" wording. Their Eq. (13) limits were not quoted (PDF extraction drops the ≪/≫ signs; as printed the 1 and 4
   look swapped relative to Eq. (12)); not mentioned in the text. Equation numbers are arXiv v2 PDF numbers.
2. **Pseudoscalar pair absorption is not velocity-suppressed.** The planning notes (and E.26 implicitly) treat the P
   contact coupling as O((p + p̄)·s/m_e). The book's own NR reduction (eq:1.4-alp) has a ∂_t term, which on a pair gives
   2m_φ: P pair absorption = axioelectric (ALP) absorption at 2m_φ, unsuppressed (eq:10.8-P). E.26's text ("for the
   scalar and pseudoscalar couplings the tensor is constant") is consistent with this; its "spin responses of E.20" for
   P should read "the axioelectric (spin-current) row" — check at the App. E pass.
3. **Limit 10.8a deviates from the F-map plan** (L-D9: x = 2m_φ/E_g, "fallback" plot). It is a real comparison instead:
   contact formula vs halo average for a model Lorentzian line, x = γ/K̄. The F row describes what is plotted. In silicon
   continua the formula holds everywhere above threshold, which the fallback plot would only have restated.
4. **Fig. 10.7a scalar curve is the EPM model** of 10.7 (vertical gap 3.12 eV, pseudo-wavefunctions; caption says so);
   P and V use the 300 K Franta/Henke data (warm edge, as Fig. 10.6).
5. **Dark Compton in Fig. 10.7b,c counts all 14 Si electrons as free** (impulse limit); the gray band marks m < 45 keV
   where the K shell is overcounted. Absorption above 29 keV from XCOM photoelectric (ε = 1); Henke/XCOM splice at 29 keV
   agrees to 1.7 %.
6. Word count ≈ 4,260 vs 3,500 budget (two derivations — pair rows and both seagulls — plus the exactness check). Trim
   candidates at review: the real-field paragraph, the detector-geometry sentence.
7. 10.10 nuisance-table candidates: bound-shell corrections to dark Compton near ω_C ≈ E_K; axial pair absorption with
   spin–orbit coupling (O(v⁴) otherwise).
