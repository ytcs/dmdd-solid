## The reader now knows
- Nuclear positions X_I = R + τ_κ + u_Rκ (eq:5.1-X), bridging 2.3's X_I to the lattice labels; u = set of displacements; ∇_Rκ.
- H = T_N + H_el(u), H_el = T_e + V_ee + V_eN + V_NN (eq:5.1-H); adiabatic states Ψ_i(r;u), E_i(u) (eq:5.1-adiabatic), real.
- Exact expansion Ψ = Σ Ψ^nuc_i Ψ_i; coupled equations (eq:5.1-coupled) with non-adiabatic couplings 𝒩_ji (eq:5.1-N), named:
  derivative (first-derivative) and scalar (second-derivative) coupling; ∝ 1/M_κ. Treated in 6.1.
- BO approximation (eq:5.1-BO): drop 𝒩; E^ad(u) ≡ E_0(u) = potential-energy surface; Born–Huang "adiabatic" keeps 𝒩_00; the book
  uses "adiabatic" and "Born–Oppenheimer" interchangeably.
- Computational connection: E^ad = KS total energy eq:D.5-Etot + Ewald; frozen phonon vs DFPT (→ 5.4, E.1, E.4); BO enters only as
  "electrons in ground state at every configuration" (Giustino Sect. III A).
- Equilibrium: ∇E^ad = 0; Si positions fixed by T_d site symmetry; a from minimizing E^ad: LDA 5.401, PBE 5.477, measured 5.431 Å;
  static minimum ≠ measured a (zero-point/thermal expansion → 5.7, E.8).
- Clamped-nuclei approximation = static lattice of Chaps. 3–4: H_el(0), Ψ_i(r;0); distinct from BO; difference = e–ph coupling
  (eq:5.1-expand: u·∇V_eN = bare e–ph Hamiltonian → 6.2, plus the c-number change of V_NN); misses phonons, phonon-assisted transitions (3.3), ZPR ≈ 60 meV (E.8).
- Scaling (eq:5.1-scales): ℓ_zp/a₀ ~ κ_BO, ω_ph ~ κ_BO² α²m_e; cubic ~ κ³, anharmonic level shifts κ⁴, derivative coupling κ³,
  non-adiabatic level shifts κ⁶ (relative κ⁴ = m_e/M). Keyresult "Born–Oppenheimer hierarchy". Solvable model of D.13 named.
- Mixing amplitude (ω_ph/ΔE)(ℓ_zp F_j0/ΔE) (eq:5.1-mixing); in an insulator ω_ph/E_g controls; metals → Migdal ω_ph/E_F (6.3);
  level crossings → failure.
- Hellmann–Feynman theorem derived (eq:5.1-HF), electrostatic form with 𝓃(r) (Feynman's electrostatic theorem); derivative coupling
  as force matrix element over excitation energy (eq:5.1-dcoup); zone-center force F_κ = −∂E^ad_cell/∂u_κ (eq:5.1-Fk) (→ Z*_κ, 5.5).
- KS forces: stationarity → HF theorem; pseudopotential force (3.5); plane waves no Pulay term; Pulay force named.
- Fig. 5.1 (fig:5-scales): HF force vs −dE/du agree < 0.1 % for |u| ≤ 0.2 Å; Φ_opt = 13.6 eV Å⁻² per cell; ω_opt = 63.7 meV (PBE) vs
  64.1 meV measured (15.5 THz); cubic term visible (bond compression stiffer), allowed along [111], zero along [100].
- WE 5.1 (we:5-kappa): κ = 0.0665, κ² = 4.43e-3, κ³ = 2.95e-4; κa₀ = 0.0352 Å; κ²α²m_e = 0.120 eV; ω_opt/α²m_e = 0.53κ²;
  ω_opt/E_g = 0.0548 = 12.4κ² (E_g = α²m_e/23); zero-point and 293 K amplitudes (numbers in the WE; 293 K measured 0.0771 Å,
  ⟨u_x²⟩ = 0.005941 Å², Flensburg–Stewart); cubic/harmonic at ℓ_zp 0.049 vs κ 0.067; non-adiabatic ~1 μeV on the optical phonon.
