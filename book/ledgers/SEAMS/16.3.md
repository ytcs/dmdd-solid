## The reader now knows
- Ejection kinematics ω = E_ej + Φ_wf + E_B (eq:16.3-omega); Φ_wf ≈ 4.3 eV for graphene (substrate-dependent); threshold mass
  2Φ_wf/v_max² = 1.22 MeV, q ≥ 1.62 keV; carbon 2p momentum scale β = Z_eff/2a₀ = 7.51 keV (Z_eff = 4.03, Hochberg).
- Ejection structure factor S_ej (eq:16.3-Sej) with plane-wave final states, M_PW = ψ̃(p − q); only energy conserved; per-state
  cross-section and per-mass rate (eq:16.3-rate) = Hochberg Eq. (5); tube: circumferential momentum n/r_t (Cavoto Eq. (16)–(18), (23));
  Cavoto misprint E_r = k′²/2M_χ → k′²/2m_e. Bloch states fix the in-plane momentum of p − q mod G → diffraction; forward peak,
  forward/backward ≈ 2 (Hochberg).
- OPW (eq:16.3-opw): M_OPW = ψ̃_i(p − q) − Σ_occ ψ̃_n(p) f_{i→n}(q) = O(q); squared-amplitude ratios 0.46–25 at 50–100 eV, q = 5–40 keV
  (hydrogenic Z_eff = 4.03), 0.4–4.3 (Slater, E.21); up to 740 at 10 eV for q ≫ p_ej; not a rate correction.
- Orientation-averaged reduced spectrum (eq:16.3-iso), = eq:2.2-ion for a full shell. Graphene π model (one 2p_z per atom, NN binding
  t = 2.8 eV, 0–8.4 eV, mean 4.41 eV): heavy, σ̄ = 1e-37: 10 MeV 6.9e3 (PW) / 1.0e4 (OPW) per kg-yr; 100 MeV 2.8e3 / 5.2e3; OPW ×1.44, ×1.84;
  light ×1.14, ×1.19. Fig. 16.3 (fig:16-ejection).
- Limit Check 16.3a (lim:16.3a, fig:16-limit-ejection): OPW within 10 % of PW only for E_ej < 0.60 eV and (100 MeV) > 94 eV; factor up to
  3.5 (100 MeV), 2.1 at 10 eV and 4.3 at the end for 10 MeV. Fermi factor bound 7.33, 3.41, 2.57 at 10, 50, 100 eV; OPW × Fermi double counts.
- Semiconducting tubes: v_D = 3ta_cc/2 = 3.02e-3 (t = 2.8 eV, a_cc = 1.42 Å); Δ_n = nv_D/3r_t; 2Δ₁ = 0.80 eV at d = 1 nm; the edge needs
  q_⊥r_t ≥ 2nv_D/3v_max = 0.76 (J₀² = 0.74) and 1.52 (J₀² = 0.25), radius-independent; 2Δ₁ of a 1 nm tube opens at 226 keV.
- 1D massive-Dirac interband bubble (eq:16.3-R1D) from Lorentz invariance + q → 0 overlap; van Hove edge; weight g_deg v_D; metallic limit
  g v q² δ(s); RPA with eq:16.2-kernel1D; kernel → α/(q r_t) = sheet kernel per circumference at q r_t ≫ 1. Excitons and intersubband
  harmonics outside.
- Axial absorption of a dilute aligned tube array (eq:16.3-eps2); isolated tube does not screen a uniform axial field; rate via eq:10.7-dp.
- Quantum dots: EFA hard-walled sphere, gap eq:16.3-gap (Blanco Eq. (5)); PbS a_x ≈ 20 nm (quoted by Blanco); 3.3 nm: 1.2 eV vs 0.41 eV,
  E_conf = 0.79 eV; closure of envelope overlaps; bulk limit = scissored bulk.
- Corrected dot rate (eq:16.3-qdrate) with Blanco's Eq. (10) form factor; bulk limit = ω × eq:D.29-rate; Blanco Eq. (6) wrong by μ_χe/4ω
  (4.05e4 at 10 MeV, 3 eV) with Eq. (10), dimensionally inconsistent with Eq. (12).
- Limit Check 16.3b (lim:16.3b, fig:16-limit-qd): hard-wall model gives 0.70–1.03 of the scissored bulk over the PbS range; within 10 % only
  above 109 E_conf (kR ≥ 33); leading correction −9π/8kR (Weyl surface term), not removed by the scissor; qR hardly matters beyond x ≈ 3.
- Depolarization factor (eq:16.3-depol) from the sphere in a uniform field + dilute Maxwell Garnett; > 1 iff |ε_in|² > (ε_out/2)(Re ε_in + ε_out);
  limits 9, 1, < 1. WE 16.3 (we:16-depol): PbS ε_in = 17.63 (3.5 µm), silica 1.977: inside field 0.275, factor 6.00; 4.62–6.71 for
  Re ε_in = 10–25; silica 2.276 at 5 eV: 0.566 for ε_in = 1 + i, 2.45 for 3 + 3i.
- Open: size and sign of the dot depolarization over 2.5–10 eV (needs ε_in(ω) of real dots).

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **State-sum normalization in Hochberg et al.** Their Eq. (7) counts 2N_C A_cell ∫d²k/(2π)² states per band with N_C = carbon atoms per
   mass = 2/M_cell, i.e. twice the number of Bloch states per spin of one band, unless their cluster function Ψ̃ (one atom + 3 neighbours,
   normalization N_ℓ unstated) carries weight 1/2. The text only describes their form and does not assert a factor; a derivation reviewer
   should decide whether the published graphene rate is high by 2.
2. **Blanco Eq. (11)** (PbS sizing curve) as printed, 1/(0.0252d² + 0.283d³), gives 0.51 eV at d = 3.3 nm, not their own 1.2 eV; the
   linear term 0.283d reproduces 1.24 eV (the Moreels form). Not used in the text (the text uses Eq. (5) and their quoted 1.2 eV).
3. **v_D of graphene tubes.** 16.3 uses 3ta_cc/2 = 3.02e-3 (t = 2.8 eV, Castro Neto); Castro Neto's rounded 1e6 m/s is 3.34e-3; 16.2's
   Limit Check uses 3.41e-3 (source not checked). The edge numbers (0.76, 1.52, J₀²) move by ~10 % across this range. Unify.
4. **OPW numbers vs E.21.** E.21 prints 0.4–4.3 (Slater 2p_z, exponent 1.568/a₀, CW4 errata); 16.3's primary orbital is Hochberg's hydrogenic
   Z_eff = 4.03 (0.46–25) and cites E.21 for the Slater range. The Slater exponent's source (Clementi–Raimondi 1963) was not read and is not cited.
5. **C.8 lacks I₀, K₀.** SPINE says I₀, K₀ are in App. C.8; the current C-8.tex has erf, elliptic integrals, F_L only. 16.2 already points
   to C.8 for them; 16.3 relies on 16.2's kernel. C.8 needs the modified Bessel functions added (or the pointer changed).
6. **Unsized F row.** The 1D massive-Dirac row has \Fsize{unsized} (excitons). It should be added to "## Unsized leading corrections (App. F)"
   in state/DEFERRED-COMPUTE.md; not done here because this brief restricts edits to book/.
7. **Limit Check 16.3b design.** The planned axis "dot radius over a*" is replaced by the energy above the dot gap in units of E_conf at
   fixed qR: in the envelope model the bulk limit is controlled by kR (level counting), not by R/a* (which controls exciton correlation).
8. Limit Check 16.3a uses a one-orbital π model (σ bands omitted); the text and caption say so.
