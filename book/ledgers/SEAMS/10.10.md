## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Tabulation spread at 3 MeV is large (×1.34 heavy, ×1.55 light).** It is the largest single nuisance in the band. The KKL table's
   valence f-sum is 0.93 at 0.3–1.0 q₀ (partly the 89 eV cut), the Essig table's 1.1–1.2. The text treats the spread as the nuisance,
   calibrated by the f-sum (8.8's non-conserving case). A third public table (QCDark, EXCEED-DM) would test which is closer.
2. **BSE template** is a constant +0.28 over the 3 MeV window from a model at one |q| (0.91 keV, EPM gap 0.82 eV, 4 CB, TDA); stated
   as an estimate. The source planning interval (+30 … +100 % within ~1 eV of E_min) is not used.
3. **Gap ±0.1 eV** is applied as a rigid shift of the whole spectrum, an upper estimate for silicon (edge measured); the text says so.
4. **Plasmon branching** is already listed in state/DEFERRED-COMPUTE.md (item "Plasmon decay into e–h pairs plus phonons in Si"), so
   no new entry was added.
5. **10.1's text** still says the scissor explains the small-q f-sum excess as "a correction of order ten percent"; 10.10 states that
   the rest has no identified cause (editor note). 10.1 may want the same sentence at revision.
6. Band in WE 10.10 assumes independent ϑ_k for the four items; the tabulation spread and the large-q LF item could share a cause
   (pseudo-states, band count); treating them as one would change σ_ln R from 0.393 to ≈ 0.44.
