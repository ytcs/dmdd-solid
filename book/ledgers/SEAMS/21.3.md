## The reader now knows
- Speed-integral form of the rate (21.1's Γ(v), eq:21.1-gammav) near the edge: kernel starts as Γ_on (v − v_on)^{p_on+3/2}
  (eq:21.3-kernel; p_on + 1 from the energy integral, ½ from the q width), p_on + 2 at a corner of x_max(q) (kink of the onset or
  end of the q range); free nuclear recoil with a sharp threshold = line (p_on = −1) + corner → linear kernel.
- General edge mass m_edge = min_q q²/[2(qv_max − E_th(q))] (eq:21.3-medge) = eq:13.1-mPAdef/eq:13.1-mZP for any onset;
  dm_edge/dv_max = −2m_edge²/q* (envelope theorem); δ_on = v_max − v_on ≃ (q*/2m_edge²)(m − m_edge).
- Halo tail: Lisanti et al. family f ∝ [exp((v_esc² − u²)/(k v0²)) − 1]^k (1010.4300 Eq. (1)); k_tail = 0 SHM sharp cut, 1 King (Eq. (12)),
  1.5–3.5 simulation fits (Sect. II, Fig. 3). Lens derivation: F_χ(v) ≃ F_esc (v_max − v)^{k_tail+1}, F_esc = 2πf_esc v_esc v_max/((k+1)v_E)
  (eq:21.3-tail); η ∝ δ^{k+2}; k = 0 reproduces eq:1.3-edge (A_η = F_esc/2v_max). Checked numerically to 0.002 in the power, 0.06 % in the
  coefficient. Linear range of the King bracket y ≪ k v0²/2v_esc = 52 km/s.
- Fraction of halo within 12 km/s of v_max (decides Si masses to 0.010 MeV above the onset): 6.0e-5 SHM, 4.7e-6 King, 3.7e-8 (k = 2.5).
- Keyresult eq:21.3-power: ℛ ∝ (m − m_edge)^{p_on + k_tail + 7/2} (smooth), p_on + k_tail + 4 (corner); mediator-independent for smooth
  F_DM at q* ≠ 0. k_tail = 0 → eq:13.9-medge. Si free-pair edge 13/2 (SHM), 15/2 (King), 8–10 (simulation range).
- Excluded mass: distance to the edge ∝ (M_det t_exp σ̄_e)^{−1/n}; ×10 exposure → ×0.70 (n = 13/2), ×0.74 (King). (Answers 21.1's
  "how far above the floor".)
- Table tab:21-edge: PA free pair 13/2, excitonic continuum 6, replica 5; ZP first edge free pair 6 (p_on = 5/2), excitonic continuum
  11/2 (2), 1s line 9/2 (1); free NR with sharp threshold 3 (corner). ZP exponents derived from the counting rule of 13.3: D = 3 (hole k)
  + 2 (q̂ angles, isolated optima with β_θ, β_z), +1 for the second-class vertex (vanishes linearly off the mirror plane), −½ for the
  Sommerfeld step, line → step after the angle average.
- Model check (fig:21-edge b): local power = p_on + k_tail + 7/2 to 0.002 up to 3e-4 MeV (k = 0, 1, 2.5); SHM 6.54 at 0.005, 6.61 at
  0.02 MeV; light mediator 6.500. Validity: x*_max = 3.51 meV per 0.001 MeV (PA), 5.32 meV per 0.001 MeV (ZP); computed L̄^PA ∝ x³
  only to 1.3 meV → 3.7e-4 MeV; ZP 1s power to 14.7 meV → 0.0028 MeV; ZP continuum to 49 meV → 0.0092 MeV. δ_on = 11.8 km/s at 0.01 MeV.
  NR check: 3.000/2.998 (k = 0), 4.000/3.984 (k = 1) up to 1 % above the 137 MeV edge (Si nuclei, 10 eV).
- Degeneracies: position ↔ E_on/v_max² (to u²/v² ~ 1e-5 at the PA onset); 1 % in gap/phonon/n-pair threshold ≡ 0.5 % (3.97 km/s) of
  v_max; v_esc ± 25 km/s ≡ 6.6 % ≡ 73 meV of onset energy; annual −3.9/+3.4 %. Height = (ρ/mρ_T)F_esc Γ_on B(·): σ̄_e, response strength,

## Open questions / for the editor

   paragraph (June floor 3.9 % lower) and with lim:21.1a; 21.1 calls the June shift "part of the halo uncertainty". 21.3 states that near the
   edge the exposure average (eq:21.1-exposure) is the one that belongs to a limit. Check the two read as one position.
2. The zero-phonon onset exponents (5/2, 2, 1) are derived here from 13.3's counting rule and 13.9's statements (isolated off-axis optima,
   second-class vertex vanishing linearly off the mirror plane). A derivation reviewer should confirm, in particular that the 1s line
   energy has the same isolated off-axis minimum in q̂ as the free pair (13.9 treats it as a rigid shift by E_x^b).
3. The amplitude cancellation for v_esc at fixed edge (heavy mediator, p_on = 3) is a coincidence of 2p + 8 = 14 with
   v_max·|∂ln A_η/∂v_esc| = 14.0; it is stated as such (numbers), not as a general property.

   ("same v0, v_esc"); the amplitude ratios (King/SHM limit 316/106/32.6/16.9 at 0.001/0.003/0.010/0.020 MeV) are in the log, not printed.
