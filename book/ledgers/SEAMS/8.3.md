## The reader now knows
- Interference bound (keyresult eq:8.3-bound): for any two groups of rows, |Γ_AB| ≤ 2(Γ_AΓ_B)^{1/2} ("twice the geometric mean"),
  equivalently (√Γ_A − √Γ_B)² ≤ Γ ≤ (√Γ_A + √Γ_B)²; proof by scaling group B by real λ (𝔇 → Λ𝔇Λ stays PSD, Γ(λ) ≥ 0, discriminant);
  pointwise, per bin and integrated; reached by rank-one matrices (WE 8.3 polarized gas, lower edge zero). Band in r = Γ_B/Γ_A:
  [(√r − 1)², (√r + 1)²] (→ 10.4). |S_eκ|² ≤ S_eeS_κκ for Migdal (→ 12, 17.4); constraint on nuisances (→ 8.8).
- Moment matrices (eq:8.3-moments): 𝓜^k_ab = ∫(dω/2π)ω^k[S_ab(q,ω) + S_ba(−q,ω)] = V⁻¹⟨[O_a†,[H,O_b]_k]_∓⟩, commutator for odd k,
  anticommutator for even k; k = 1 is eq:4.3-firstmoment. Units eV^{k+3}. Odd = sum rules (algebra of H); even = equal-time
  correlations (𝓜⁰_ee = 2𝓃_eS^st), computable from static simulations (→ E.19). Odd 𝓜^k = coefficient of z^{−(k+1)} of R_ab (D.26).
- Hankel positivity (eq:8.3-hankel); (𝓜¹)² ≤ 𝓜⁰𝓜²; at T = 0 also (𝓜²)² ≤ 𝓜¹𝓜³ (→ Markov–Krein band, 11.5).
- Existence: for Coulomb electrons and point nuclei 𝓜³ finite, 𝓜⁴ infinite (mean-square force ∫dy/y²; D.26); hydrogen check
  S ∝ q²ω⁻¹df/dω ∝ ω^{−9/2} (WE 4.5's ω^{−7/2}); within BO (n_B = 0) all nuclear moments finite (E.19's setting).
- Mixed third moment (keyresult eq:8.3-M3 exact at all q; eq:8.3-M3lim q → 0 on cubic sites, 1/L ≪ q ≪ G_min):
  𝓜³_eκ = −Z_κe²q²𝓃_κ[𝓃_e^{(κ)} + 2𝓃_e]/(3m_eM_κ). "Derived here" (single-species third moment attributed to Puff 1965).
  Contact term (𝓃^{(κ)}/3, every q) vs quadrupolar term (2𝓃_e/3 from the uniform density at y ~ 1/q; order of limits L → ∞
  first). Sign: negative (spring model −Kq²/(m_eM V), relative mode); low-ω positive (electrons follow, 8.6), high-ω negative.
  Three regimes (q ≪ G_min = 3.95 keV in Si; G_min ≲ q ≪ 1/a_core; q ≳ 1/a_core); corrections O(q/G_min) at T_d sites, O(q²/G²)
  with inversion (D.26). Spherical form eq:8.3-M3sph with j₂ kernel; bracket 𝓃/3 → 𝓃 at large q. Relativistic +8 % in Si → 12.5.
- Computational connection: contact density needs all-electron densities (PAW reconstruction E.2, full-potential; E.17 specifies).
- WE 8.4 (we:8-third): Si free-atom ROHF, E = −288.854 Ha; 𝓃_e^{(κ)} = 1765 a₀⁻³ = 1.19e28 cm⁻³ (core 1757.7, valence 7.6 =
  0.43 %; converged to 0.1 % in the basis); 2𝓃_e/𝓃^{(κ)} = 1.2e-4; q = 10 keV = 2.68 a₀⁻¹ (middle regime); bracket 0.3353 𝓃^{(κ)}
  (+0.6 % over q → 0); core 99.56 %, valence 0.44 %; crystal mean density ≤ 1.8e-4; 0.347 at 30 keV, 0.427 at 100 keV;
  −𝓜³_eκ/𝓜¹_κκ = (8.78 keV)² ((8.76 keV)² at q → 0); 1s orbital energy 1.87 keV.
- Diagonal third moments eq:8.3-M3ss (kinetic (q²/2m)² + 3q²⟨(q̂·p)²⟩/m², potential curvature term); jellium → 𝓃_eq²ω_p²/m_e.
- Mass density n_M = Σ_s m_sn_s; [H, n_M] = q·𝐠 (eq:8.3-mass), 𝐠 the momentum density (𝐠(0) = total momentum).
- Translation Ward identity (keyresult eq:8.3-ward): Σ_s m_sS_{Bs} = (q/ω)·S_{B𝐠}, ω ≠ 0; amplitude form. Presented as the
  standard momentum-conservation identity of multicomponent fluids, "derived here" in this response-matrix form. One line separates
  it from gauge Ward identities (QED; eq:7.2-ward). Hypothesis: interactions depend on positions and spins only.
- Momentum flux [H, g^α] = q_β𝖳^{αβ} (eq:8.3-flux; D.26 eq:D.26-flux): pairwise, translation-invariant, no external potential.
- Soft acoustic limit (keyresult eq:8.3-soft): Σ m_sS_{Bs} = q_αq_βS_{B𝖳}/ω², S_{n_Mn_M} = q⁴-form/ω⁴; O(q⁴) at fixed ω ≠ 0; weight
  on acoustic branches as q → 0. Equivalence-principle picture. Attribution: Campbell-Deem et al. Table I, Sect. V B (q⁴ for
  mass-proportional coupling: optical and two-phonon; acoustic ∝ q); here exact incl. anharmonicity; harmonic instance = eq:5.2-asr;
  amplitude form → 13.6. Decomposition of any coupling into λn_M + remainder: only the non-mass part shakes the interior.
- Boost from momentum conservation (eq:8.3-boostward): monatomic crystal, ⟨f|Σn_κ|0⟩ = −(m_e/M)⟨f|n_e|0⟩ + O(q²); = eq:8.6-boost
  summed over nuclei (D.26, via translation of adiabatic states and P_e = im_e[H, Σr]); electronic part of nuclear response
  (m_e/M)²S_ee = 3.8e-10 S_ee in Si; summed S_eκ = −(m_e/M)S_ee there (negative).
- Third-moment Ward check: m_e𝓜³_ee + ΣM_κ𝓜³_eκ = O(q³); eq:8.3-M3ee 𝓜³_ee → (e²q²/3m_e²)[ΣZ𝓃_κ𝓃^{(κ)} + 2𝓃_e²] (derived in
  D.26: e–N contact, e–e uniform 2/3, periodic part cancels on cubic sites, xc hole higher order); Si: (2.34 keV)² × f-sum vs
  kinetic (98 eV)², (469 eV)² at 10 keV.
- WE 8.5 (we:8-chain): Si [100] plane chain of 5.2 (d = 1.358 Å, ω_max 64.1 meV, c = 6.61 km/s, M = 26.16 GeV); one-phonon ratio
  q/Mω_q (q = 1 keV: qd = 0.688, ω_q = 21.6 meV); two-phonon amplitudes −q²/2M√(ω_kω_{q−k}) and −q(ω_k+ω_{q−k})/2√(...);
  flux element 4iK sin²(qd/2)(N/2Mω)^{1/2} → dispersion; weight factor q²/2Mω = 8.8e-4; cubic springs: O(q) paths cancel.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Priority of the mixed third moment.** Not checked against the closed-access two-component-plasma literature (Adamyan–Tkachenko
   school etc.); the text says "We derive this mixed rule here" and attributes the single-species rule to Puff 1965. Puff's paper is
   on the interacting Bose fluid (Crossref title); it was not opened, and no equation number is cited.
2. **NOTATION unit of 𝓜^k.** The row says eV^{k+1}; for densities S_ab is eV² and 𝓜^k is eV^{k+3} (𝓜¹ = 𝓃q²/m is eV⁴, as the 4.3
   row says). Text states eV^{k+3}. Amend the row.
3. **E.19 consistency.** E.19 says "moments 𝓜^k, k = 0, …, 4 of the self response of 8.3 … exact from a static path-integral
   simulation". Under 8.3's definition the even moments are equal-time correlations ✓; but for point nuclei with Coulomb electrons
   𝓜⁴ diverges, finite only within BO (8.3 says so and points to E.19 as that setting). E.19 could add "within the Born–Oppenheimer
   approximation" at its next touch. E.19 also typesets the moments as \mathcal{M}^k; NOTATION and 8.3 use \mathscr{M}^k (sweep the glyph).
4. **New symbols.** 𝖳^{αβ}(q) (sans-serif T) for the momentum flux, chosen to avoid Π (self-energies), σ_ij (classical stress of
   C.18) and 𝒯 (kinetic-energy density). n_M for the mass-density operator (ρ is taken by ρ_χ, ρ_T). The strain tensor does not
   appear (ruling u_αβ respected by absence).
5. **Novelty wording.** Ward identity: presented as the standard momentum-conservation identity, "in the form … we derive it here"
   (NOVELTY N2 recommendation + spine "new in this form"). Mixed moment: "We derive this mixed rule here".
6. **WE 8.4 model choices.** Free-atom nonrelativistic point-nucleus ROHF density (Python/PySCF, not QE); the crystal valence and the
   l = 4 anisotropy are not computed (bounded by the mean-density term, 1.8e-4). ROHF energy −288.85446 Ha vs Bunge's −288.854 (the
   symmetry-broken 3p² occupation lies 1e-4 Ha below the spherical RHF value).
7. **Claims at physics rigour.** The O(q/G_min) correction at T_d sites, the cancellation of the periodic and xc-hole parts in
   𝓜³_ee (D.26), and the statement that cubic springs' O(q) paths cancel (WE 8.5) follow from the identities; the explicit
   anharmonic amplitude is not computed.
8. **Table 8.2 users** are by planned section labels from the spine (10.4, 11.3, 11.5, 12.5, 13.6, 13.7, 14.4, 17.4, 8.8, chap 22).
9. 2205.02250 was fetched (sources/8.3) but is not cited; its text is kept for the 11.x writers.
