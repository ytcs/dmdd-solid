  Ge 3d); named → nominal, omission stated; evaluation errors corrected first. Halo outside the likelihood (separate band; near the
  edge gap and v_max varied together via E_on/v_max²).

  ln ratio = σ_β² 𝗍'/4 + O(σ_β⁴). One bin: band of the limit = inverse band of the rate, σ_β = σ_lnℛ. Zero counts 𝗍' = 𝗍* = 4.61,
  coefficient 1.15 (1.195 vs exact 1.179 at σ_β = 0.393); N_bkg = 1, no events: 𝗍' = 2.61. Averaged construction (Poisson
  probability averaged over the constraint): 1 + σ²(N_90 − 1)/2, coefficient 0.65, 1.103 at 0.393.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. "Masked cells" (SPINE term) is written "excluded cells", per Ch15 R9 and the GLOSSARY note; SPINE wording could follow.
2. The calibrated threshold (𝗍* = classical-limit value) is a declared convention, as in 8.8; an exact Neyman construction with a
   continuous auxiliary measurement was tried in a scratch check and gives limits below N_90 at zero counts (the auxiliary variable
   randomizes the discrete outcome), so it is not used and not printed.
3. The attraction template outside 0–10.5 eV is set to zero in Fig. 21.4 (stated in the caption); above 3 MeV this overstates its
   sensitivity, since 10.10 states the compensating loss lies above the window.
4. The efficiency width (10 %) in WE 21.4 is an illustrative assumption, stated as such.
5. The tabulation spread at 1 MeV (×3.23 heavy, ×3.09 light) is the two published tables as they are; 10.10 quoted it only at 3 and
   10 MeV. Below 1 MeV it is not used (the first bins of the Essig table give a vanishing rate at 0.5 MeV).
6. F rows: the 9.2 note asks the 21.x writer to settle "no box" F rows; none written here (no literature limit is recovered in
   21.4; the classical Poisson limit is 1.5's).
