   E.17 also writes the per-atom DOS as D_κ(ω); 11.3 uses NOTATION's g_ph,κ (D_κ = g_ph,κ/3 per direction on a cubic site).

5. **The pointwise band is not a 10 % certificate for silicon** (n_at = 2; upper end 2.7× at 10 keV). Stated plainly; the exact
   rate-level statement is δ^coh_n.

   two- and three-phonon weights, and 7.9 q_BZ for the one-phonon weight (shown as a thin line in the box).
