## The reader now knows
- Seed distribution eq:18.3-seed: P_seed(E_e, E_h | q, ω, v), the class-resolved weights 𝔇_ab S_ab(q, ω; Λ) of 8.7 binned
  in the two carrier energies; integrates to the pair share; one variable (electron share f_e = E_e/(ω − E_g)) in the
  zero-phonon sector; pair + phonon seeds have E_e + E_h = ω − ω_Q − E_g; independent of v and model for one coupling row.
  First-generation momenta are dropped (cost: the memory located in 18.1, calculation in E.24).
- eq:18.3-pcasc: P_casc(N_eh|Λ) = [s^N] s 𝔊_e(E_e; s) 𝔊_h(E_h; s) (pair-number component of eq:18.1-seed, s = e^{λ_N});
  eq:18.3-pq: P(N_eh|q, ω, v) = ∫ P_seed P_casc, which is eq:8.7-cond with the class sum done through the seed. Coefficients by
  eq:E.24-dft. Properties: normalized, nonnegative; δ_{N1} below θ_ii E_g, first ionization at (1 + θ_ii)E_g = 2.32 eV (Si);
  eq:18.3-moments: mean and the law of total variance (subtree variance + seed spread).
- Silicon at 10 eV (Fig. 18.3; calibrated cascade A_AB = 5.674 eV³, E_g = 1.160 eV of 18.2; EPM seeds of we:8-pairs at the same
  excess energy): DM seed P(2,3,4) = 0.413, 0.548, 0.040, mean 2.627 (3.81 eV per pair); photon 0.399, 0.556, 0.045, mean 2.646;
  step model 3 pairs (mean +14 %); splits α_RK → ∞, 1, → 0: means 2.986, 2.692, 3.005. Var 0.314 = 0.283 (subtrees) + 0.031
  (seed); equal split 0.079. Subtree mean non-monotonic in the share (2.986 at f_e = 1/2, 2.437 at 1/4, 3.005 at 0).
  Screened vs unscreened seed: d_TV 0.010.
- Worked example we:18-step5 (5 eV): step model 2 pairs; lone carrier at 3.84 eV escapes ionization with 0.175 (43 phonon steps;
  continuous-slowing-down estimate exp(−1.744) = 0.175); equal split P(1) = 0.98; DM seed P(1) = 0.830, P(2) = 0.170, mean 1.170
  (4.27 eV per pair); step model counts 5.9× as many events above a two-pair threshold; photon seed P(2) 12 % higher.
- Ramanathan–Kurinsky model as a limit: four replacements (process-independent symmetric Beta split eq:18.3-beta fitted to photon
  yields; energy-only from the first collision, one yield function; random-k free-particle kernels, θ_ii = 1, hole cap; plasmon
  channel, a double count in the book's accounting). Mean = their Eq. (5); p_n = coefficients of eq:18.3-pcasc; their Eq. (14)
  = renewal Gaussian with the constant term of the mean set to zero (cascade: 0.008 uniform, 0.35 one-sided).
- Step model as a limit eq:18.3-step: deterministic renewal chain (App. C.11) after the first pair; exact in the forced region;
  mean on average ω/ε_eh + 0.19 (Si) against ω/ε_eh + 0.008…0.35; d_TV to a single bin is 1 − P(n), so it never converges
  (σ_N = 0.79, largest bin 0.45 at 20.2 eV).
- Limit Check 18.3a (d_TV against ω, 1.4–21.2 eV): step model within 0.1 below 4.37 eV only, up to 0.86 at 4.91 eV, ≥ 0.2 to
  21 eV (≥ 0.39 above 9 eV); splits α_RK = 1, ∞ within 0.1 above 12.95 eV (max 0.21, 0.39 in between); α_RK → 0 stays 0.18
  at 21 eV (one-sided, mean +0.36); photon seed within 0.062 everywhere (5.67 eV; mean 4.6 %), 0.014 at 10 eV, ≤ 0.03 above
  15 eV: the size of the photon-calibration error promised in 8.7, within the energy-only reduction.
- Observable law of a marked event eq:18.3-obs: P_obs(𝖱|Λ) = Σ_N ∫d𝔪 P_casc(N_eh, 𝔪|Λ) P_det(𝖱|N_eh, 𝔪); step and R–K models
  give only the prompt N-marginal (P_det = δ); binomial thinning F_meas = f_det F_eh + 1 − f_det (eq:C.11-thinfano) is the case
  where the marks drop out.
