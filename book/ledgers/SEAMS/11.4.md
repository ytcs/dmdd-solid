## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Onset numbers vs the plan.** The plan's 91.5/112 keV used ω̄ = 40/23 meV; with the book's ⟨ω⟩ = 40.9 meV the same criterion
   (x_R = 4, q = 2(2M⟨ω⟩)^{1/2}) gives 92.5 keV in Si; Ge 113 keV with the labelled estimate 23.6 meV. The text gives both readings
   (x_R = 4 and 8.5's x_R = 9 = 139 keV) and does not contradict 8.5 or 11.3's "about 140 keV". 11.4's opening says "about 90 keV in
   silicon and 110 keV in germanium".
2. **Germanium ⟨ω⟩ is an estimate** (Debye from Θ_D = 373 K, scaled by Si's DFPT/Debye ratio 0.981). A measured Ge DOS (Nelin and
   Nilsson, 80 K neutron data) would replace it; Ge is kept out of the box plots.
3. **Lewin–Smith is cited at book level** (no equation number; the paper is not open-access). The formula is cited to lin2019tasi
   Eq. (104), as in 2.2. The editor may add the Lewin–Smith equation number at the citation audit.
4. **Label dependency on 10.9** (parallel): eq:10.9-ferm and σ_F. If 10.9's labels move, update s11-4 and D-32.
5. **Physics flag for 1905.12635.** Their Eq. (4) counts every absorption on a free nucleus; in a crystal the zero-phonon weight is
   unavailable (except |G| = m_χ within O(v)), so below ≈ 60 keV in Si the free form overstates the rate (16× at 10 keV). Their target
   discussion is for m_χ ≳ MeV (or H/Li), so their published reach is unaffected; stated as a validity edge, not as an error.
6. **Coherence corrections** for the absorption weight below ~7–18 keV (11.3's δ^coh) are not in the box (stated in the caption/F row).
7. **E.17 'harmonic E^kin' pointer**: the text points to sec:5.4/app:E.4 for the harmonic moment, as 11.3 does for W.
8. **c₃ vs the contact-density third moment.** c₃ in 11.4/D.32 is the BO-surface curvature (n_B = 0); the text and D.32 say that the
   full Coulomb third moment (electron density at the nucleus, electronic excitations) belongs to 12.5. 12.5 should keep that split.
9. **WE 11.4's 93.4 keV** (free nucleus first deposits 3ω_D) sits next to the x_R = 4 onset 92.5 keV by coincidence; the onset paragraph
   says they are different criteria.
10. The section opening states "about 90 keV in Si" as the failure point (x_R = 4); the rate-level 10 % boundary for a heavy particle
   is lower, x_R = 2.3 (70 keV). Both are in the text with their meaning.
