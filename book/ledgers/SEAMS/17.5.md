## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Halo dependence of the window numbers.** The campaign numbers (survivor window 0.063–0.33 MeV, onset 4.2 m_min^ac, C/Si −7 %)
   used the campaign SHM; the book's halo gives C/Si 0.933 (−6.7 %), onsets 3.7–4.1 edges of the softer crystal. 17.6 must use the
   book values or state its halo.
2. **CsI** uses the 4.2 K tensor (Vallin et al. 1964; only the abstract has been read, values as in the validated compute result)
   with the room-temperature density 4.529 g/cm³; the 4.2 K density is not known to us (speeds would drop by ≲2 %).
3. **Polar FOM.** The book's own K_η shows that 𝓘_pol/ρ_T alone ranks LiF vs GaAs to 10 % only above 52 MeV; the text makes the polar
   figure of merit the pair (𝓘_pol/ρ_T, ω̄_pol). The campaign ranking (WP-level "LiF leads the polar rate") is unaffected in order
   (×103 at 1 MeV) but 17.6 should rank at a stated mass.
4. **Partial f-sum vs WE 4.7.** WE 4.7 (we:4-sifsum) infers 3.93 valence electrons below the L edge by TRK closure; direct
   integration of the same data gives the ε₂-sum 1.032 × 4 = 4.13 (the Franta data below 30 eV are 5 % above what closure requires;
   total 14.19). Not contradictory at the stated accuracies, but the editor may want 4.4/4.7 to mention the +1.4 % normalization.
5. **Migdal LiF ω_p^val** 26.1 eV is the free-electron value from the density (8 e/f.u.), not a measured plasmon energy.
6. ZrTe₅ (Ch16 R1 rerun): s17-5 quotes no ZrTe₅ or other Ch16 number, so nothing to refresh.
7. Gopal–Spaldin journal year: arXiv journal-ref says 2005, the DOI is a 2006 volume 35 article; bib uses 2006.
