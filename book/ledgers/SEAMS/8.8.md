## The reader now knows
- Nuisance of a truncation: 𝔗_k⁺ = one step along axis k (next order at which the labeled quantity changes, by 8.5's counting rule:
  n_g, n_B, n_A 0 → 2, n_T 0 → 1; next rung on 𝒳; next term of eq:5.6-multi for 𝒦); ΔS_k = S^{𝔗_k⁺} − S^𝔗, δ_k = ΔS_k/S^𝔗
  (eq:8.8-delta). Power form S(ϑ) = S^𝔗 Π_k(1 + δ_k)^{ϑ_k} (eq:8.8-M), ϑ_k standard normal; ϑ = 0 the formula, ϑ = 1 the next order;
  first order = additive form; additive form negative at ϑ = −1/δ (2σ for δ = 0.5).
- Coherence: one ϑ_k at every (q, ω), bin, final-state class Λ, target and experiment sharing the approximation; only the template varies.
- Three cases of computability: computed next order (rungs, ZPR, next multiphonon term; central value moves, nuisance = step after;
  rung step an estimate, not a bound); small parameter only (δ_k = parameter^step × O(1) coefficient, 1/4–2; labelled estimate);
  neither (pair-channel screening of the 𝒫_+ sideband, 8.4 → 13.4: reported nominal with the omission named, no width invented).
  Off-label approximations (xc, frozen core, numerics): spreads and convergence tests of E.1–E.5 in place of ΔS_k.
- Matrix form (eq:8.8-geodesic): S^{1/2}(S^{−1/2}S⁺S^{−1/2})^ϑ S^{1/2}; PSD for every real ϑ (congruence + real power of PSD);
  requires supp S⁺ ⊆ supp S; truncations applied in sequence, order matters at second order.
- Types: normalization (δ constant in ω at fixed q; moves limit by the same fraction; class weights unchanged), tilt (δ(ω); moves
  weight between bins and changes the class mix), threshold (eq:8.8-shift, S(q, ω − ϑδE_k); acts through the edge, δ ln m_min =
  δ ln E_th), new support (eq:8.8-open, S^𝔗 + ΔS_k(1 + δ′_k)^ϑ; positive iff ΔS_k positive: lines, in-window sideband; outside the
  window acts on 𝒫_0 + 𝒫_+ jointly).
- Table 8.6 (tab:8-nuisances), Si: Debye–Waller e^{−2W} dropped (𝒦): δ = −0.061 at 10 keV (2W = 0.063); residue z_qp (n_g = 2) est.
  ~θ_ep; static vertex screening (n_B) est. 3e-3; anharmonic reshaping (n_A = 2) via eq:8.8-tilt; RPA → RPA + LF (WE 8.10); n_g = 4
  est. θ_ep ≈ 1e-2 below E_dir → 1 near it; anharmonic shift η²ω_opt = 0.15 meV (2.4e-3 × 64.1 meV); ZPR −56.2 meV (computed), next
  step 8 meV (adiabatic vs nonadiabatic) and measured −62/−64 meV; exciton 14.7 meV lowers PA thresholds; new support: two-phonon above
  the one-phonon band, exciton lines at q ≈ k_c (4.9), phonon-absorption branch (n_T = 1) e^{−21550} at 10 mK.
- Residue z_qp and sideband finite part share one ϑ with opposite signs (eq:8.6-zsum); two independent nuisances would double-count.
- Constraints: positivity (eq:4.2-positivity) — power, geodesic and new-support forms by construction, additive form not; interference
  bound (eq:8.3-bound) — largest allowed cross term scales as [(1 + δ_A)(1 + δ_B)]^{ϑ/2}; assign nuisances to the matrix, never to a
  cross term alone; Migdal |S_eκ|² ≤ S_eeS_κκ; f-sum (eq:8.8-fsum) ∫ω ΔS_k = 0 for every rung (static local kernels): rung nuisances are
  tilts, compensation above the rate window; non-conserving truncations (band/q cut, nonlocal pseudopotential, scissor) calibrated by
  the violation; partial sector sum rules (8.6) the same; detailed balance (eq:2.5-db, eq:8.2-db): one ϑ for both signs of ω; recoil
  moments (zeroth and first fixed at any n_A, eq:5.6-sum): constrained nuisance (eq:8.8-tilt) S^𝔗 exp[ϑψ + λ₀ + λ₁y], y = ω − E_R,
  ψ = ln(1 + δ_A); existence/uniqueness from a strictly convex F whose Hessian is the Hankel moment matrix (eq:8.3-hankel); λ(1) = 0, so
  the tilt passes through the next order; second moment (kinetic energy along q) a separate nuisance.
- Likelihood (eq:8.8-like): Π_j P(N_obs,j | N_sig,j + N_bkg,j) Π_k e^{−ϑ_k²/2}, N_sig,j = σ̄_e M_det t_exp r_j(m_χ; ϑ), r_j from
  eq:8.7-handoff with S(ϑ); halo fixed; detector nuisances → 21.4; profiling eq:C.14-profile; linearized power form = C.14's Gaussian

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Writer-only numbers not used.** The source package's interim widths (pair-screening factor, Sommerfeld factor, scissor f-sum
   violation, relativistic f-sum factor, ε_eh prior) are unverified estimates and do not appear; the text says the pair-channel screening
   nuisance is undetermined until 13.4.
2. **Threshold t* = 2N₉₀ in eq:8.8-cost** is a calibration choice for the illustration (asymptotic statistic calibrated to the exact
   zero-count limit); the text declares it and points to 21.4. The structure (profile O(δ²) with coefficient t*/4, envelope O(δ)) does
   not depend on the choice.
3. **Clash flags.** δ_k (bare δ is the delta function; decorated as δ_off, δ_F); ΔS_k (Δ_χ, Δ_BCS); δE_k; r_j (r(m_χ) of 1.5 extended
   to bins; r is also the local rate ratio of 8.3 and the correlation of C.14); 𝖫 (C.14, against the pair propagator of 4.9, clash 74);
   ψ (local; ψ is used for wavefunctions elsewhere); y (local, as in 8.3/C.14 usage of y_R); k (local exponent; k is crystal momentum).
4. **WE 8.10 vs 8.7.** 8.7's "further 13 percent" compares |(ε⁻¹)₀₀|²S⁰ with the full RPA + LF result within one rung; δ_𝒳 of WE 8.10
   compares two rungs (head-only RPA against RPA + LF). The WE says so.
5. The f-sum identity eq:8.8-fsum for rung steps is stated for static, local kernels (RPA, RPA + LF, ALDA); BSE on scissored or GW
   bands is a non-conserving rung and falls under the "calibrated by the violation" case.
