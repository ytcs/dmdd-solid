- Impact-ionization kernel (keyresult eq:18.1-ii): carrier couples to total charge n_Z (as a dark photon, 8.4); one coupling row per G,
  Ṽ_G = v_C(|p+G|)ρ_{n′n}(k,−p−G); master formula with sum over n′ and the zone, Pauli factor 1 − f; FDT S = −2(1+n_B)(R−R†)/2i;
  vRv = W^scr − v ⇒ Γ_ii = −2Σ∫_BZ ρ†[(W−W†)/2i]ρ(1−f)(1+n_B). Diagonal form eq:18.1-iidiag: 2v_C|ρ|²L(p,ω) over all p, L of the total
  dielectric matrix at unreduced p; ρ = 1 gives the out part of eq:4.8-lifetime. Anti-Hermitian part ≠ element-wise Im W unless
  W_GG′ = W_G′G (Si, Ge with inversion origin; not GaAs), as 8.7's retarded/advanced weights.
- Out-rate vs width: −2ImΣ = Γ_out + Γ_in (cutting rule), Γ_in ∝ f n_B negligible at T ≪ E_g; Γ_qp = −2z_qp ImΣ, z_qp ≈ 0.8
  (Kotani Sect. II) → kernel sets z_qp = 1, uniform 20 % absorbed by a calibrated model (E.13). Exchange = vertex correction, beyond
  first order → 18.5 open problem.
- Plasmon inside the kernel (Si: ε₂ = 0.224 at the plasmon, we:4-siplasmon); no separate plasmon channel; undamped plasmon (Al, q < q_c)
  is a cascade particle with its own decay kernel.
- Threshold eq:18.1-theta: parabolic bands, common final velocity ⇒ θ_ii = (2m*_e + m*_h)/(m*_e + m*_h); 3/2 for equal masses; hole: swap.
  Real bands: soft threshold, rate scatter (Kotani Fig. 2). Random-k: θ_ii = 1.
- Phonon kernel: master formula with Ṽ = g, one-phonon response → out part of eq:6.3-ImFM = eq:E.13-gph; emission only at mK; static
  DFPT vertex, corrections higher order in ω_Q/E_g (6.2). Polar double count: W^scr with total ε contains the Fröhlich line; phonon kernel
  uses g^S = g − g^L; three matching conditions (same Z*, ε∞, ω_L; same multipoles; same Gaussian range separation). Si: Z* = 0, absent at
  dipole order. 𝒫_0 pair (weight z_qp(m)): first emission = first cascade step; 𝒫_+ phonon booked once (fulfils 8.7's promise).
- Keyresult "Cascade kernels" (every kernel a master-formula rate with an internal projectile; hypotheses). Capture and Auger kernels
  have the same structure (→ 18.5).
- Branching process: state x (type, band, k), kernels 𝒦_c (\mathscr{K}_c), Γ_x, deposits δN_c; terminal below θ_iiE_g (kinetic energy →
  heat; capture/excitons → 18.5). Hypotheses: Markov (incoherent limit of E.13, worst within a width of threshold); independent siblings —
  Pauli: Si 𝓃_e = 2.0e23 cm⁻³ over 12 eV ⇒ 1.7e22 cm⁻³ eV⁻¹; 1 keV = 267 pairs in a 10 nm sphere (assumption) = 6.4e19 cm⁻³, fills 4e-3;
  Coulomb: n a_x³ = 4.7 (1 keV), 0.47 (100 eV) with a_x = 4.2 nm (4.9) → marginal for tens of eV, unestimated for keV; termination.
- Keyresult eq:18.1-backward (branching generating functional 𝔊_x(𝛌) = ⟨e^{𝛌·N}|x⟩, minimal solution); = eq:C.11-functional with branching
  ratios; time drops out (only rate ratios); moments by eq:C.11-mean/-second; upward sweep.
- Energy identity eq:18.1-energy ω = N_eh E_g + E_heat (no capture, excitons, escaping photons; lone carrier: ω → x, new pairs);
  𝔊(λ_N, λ_H) = e^{λ_Hω}𝔊(λ_N − λ_HE_g, 0). 18.2 builds ε_eh, F_eh on it.
- WE we:18-toy: θ_ii = 3/2, phonon 1/2 (units E_g), carrier at 2: 𝔊₂ = p_a s + (1−p_a)(p_b s + 1 − p_b); heat = 2 − N; pair seed
  𝔊_Λ = s𝔊₂²; p_a = 3/5, p_b = 2/5: 19/25 per carrier; P(1,2,3) = 36/625, 228/625, 361/625; mean 2.52, var 0.365, 1.98 E_g/pair,
  var/mean 0.145.
- Energy-only reduction (Γ_k ≫ Γ_E, or ii products iso-energy independent of parent k): 𝔊_nk → 𝔊_t(x), kernels eq:18.1-isoenergy.
  Fails in Si above 3–5 eV (model: p_ii = 1/2 at 5.8 eV); error O(Γ_E/Γ_k) per generation, estimate; E.24 specifies the bound; 18.5 owns the
  open problem. Only the first generation keeps k, hence q (fulfils 8.7's promise).
- Random-k: Kane golden rule (as quoted by Kotani Eq. (5)) with constant |M|² and no momentum conservation → three-body phase space
  𝔓_t (eq:18.1-phase), product density ρρρ/𝔓, ratio eq:18.1-randomk Γ_ph/Γ_ii = A_AB ρ_t(x−ω_AB)/𝔓_t(x−E_g), θ_ii = 1; free DOS:
  𝔓 = (2π/105)y^{7/2} (Dirichlet integral) → eq:E.13-ab = RK Eq. (8); A_AB in eV³ with ρ in eV^{1/2}; hole cutoff 12 eV; A_AB = 5.2 tuned.
- Fig. fig:18-tree (model: E_g = 1.17, ω_AB = 63 meV, A_AB = 5.2, B_h = 12 eV, Dirichlet(3/2) products): 50 eV electron → 13.06 ± 0.02

  below 6 eV, heat 34.8 eV.
- Seeding eq:18.1-seed: 𝔊_Λ = e^{λ_N}𝔊_e(E_e,k_e)𝔊_h(E_h,k_h); RK Eq. (5) mean = its first moment; P_casc → 18.3. Three properties: split
