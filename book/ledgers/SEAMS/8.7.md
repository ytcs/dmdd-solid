## The reader now knows
- Prompt final state: after dressing (~1/δ_F, about a femtosecond) and before the first decay (~1/Γ_m); same window as 8.6's
  P_0/P_+ split; the Dyson δ part (weight z_qp(m)) is a zero-phonon pair class, its later phonon emission is the cascade's first step
  (fulfils 8.6's promise, s8-6 line 160).
- Final-state classes Λ refine the sectors 𝒫_ad, 𝒫_0, 𝒫_+: pair (c, k+q; v, k; spin if spin rows; valleys, ε_e, ε_h); pair +
  phonons (branch, momentum, energy); core hole (shell, Auger, 10.6); phonon content or free recoil (E_R, direction).
  Plasmon not a class in Si (ε₂ = 0.224 at ε₁ = 0, we:4-siplasmon): its weight is in the screened pair sum; a class only below q_c
  (4.5) or where it escapes/converts (films, 16.2).
- Keyresult eq:8.7-resolved: S_ab(q,ω;Λ) with P_Λ on both sides of δ (as eq:8.6-sector); Σ_Λ = S_ab and PSD when P_Λ commutes with
  H; positive in the off-shell window; near resonance only sums with the zero-phonon pair positive.
- Rate into a class eq:8.7-classrate Γ(v;Λ); absorption via g*g/2ω_φ; direction dependence of the class mix in a crystal.
- IP pair response eq:8.7-IP: (4π/V)|ρ_cv(k,q)|²δ(ω − ε_{c,k+q} + ε_vk), spin summed, q unreduced, G_U in the vertex.
- Test-electron column (ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} = δ + Σ(f_Hxc)_{GG′}R_{G′0} (6.2's operator; RPA → eq:4.4-epsGG).
- Factorization eq:8.7-factor: (1 + R†f)(R⁰ − R⁰†)(1 + fR) = R − R† for Hermitian f (RPA, adiabatic real kernels), from
  R = R⁰ + R⁰fR alone; FDT turns it into the keyresult eq:8.7-screened (retarded weights; sum = (q²/2πα)L of eq:4.4-SL with LF).
  Second ordering (R = R⁰ + RfR⁰) gives advanced weights δ + fR*_{0G}, same total.
- Out-state labeling: the cascade receives the late-time state; Lippmann–Schwinger ⟨ψ_f^(−)| = ⟨φ_f|(1 + VG^(+)) (Sakurai Chap. 6);
  causality of the induced potential in mean field. In-state = advanced.
- Labelings agree pair by pair when R is Hermitian (off-shell window: difference O(g⁴) in the rate) and, derived here, in every
  PT-invariant target with even N: amplitudes real, R_GG′ = R_G′G, advanced weight = conjugate of retarded (Si, Ge). GaAs differs.
- Computational connection: ρ_cv on the k grid (E.7) and the column (ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} (E.6); the library must store per-pair weights
  binned in carrier energies and valleys per q, ω and row (21.6).
- WE we:8-pairs (EPM Si, q₀ = αm_e = 3.73 keV along [100] = 1.633·2π/a, ω = 10 eV, 24³, 4 + 26 bands, 51 LF vectors, η = 0.10 eV;
  model indirect gap 0.82 eV, CBM 0.853 X): S⁰ = 1.7e8 eV², (ε⁻¹)₀₀ = 0.55 − 0.16i, S(RPA+LF) = 4.9e7 eV² (×0.29); |ε⁻¹₀₀|² = 0.33,
  other LF elements ≤ 0.05, lower the rate a further 13%; free gas m²ω/πq₀ = 2.2e8 eV². Screened pairs share 9.2 eV: ⟨ε_e⟩ 4.8,
  ⟨ε_h⟩ 4.4 eV, electron > half in 63%, lopsided (one carrier > 3/4) 44%, lowest valence band 5%; IP within 3 points. Optical
  (photon, dark photon): ⟨ε_e⟩ 5.4, ⟨ε_h⟩ 3.3 eV, electron > half 83%. Transverse current at q₀: 5.4 / 3.9 eV, lopsided ~50%.
  Si: RET = ADV to machine precision, R symmetric. GaAs (same model, q₀, 10 eV): Σ|w_R − w_A| = 15% of the total, mean carrier

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **In/out labeling: a result beyond the planning source.** The planning source states that the two labelings coincide only
   where R is Hermitian. The section derives (and the WE shows numerically) that they also coincide pair by pair in every PT-invariant
   target with even electron number, whatever (q, ω): real amplitudes ⇒ R symmetric ⇒ advanced weight = conjugate of retarded.
   For targets with spin–orbit coupling the statement is made for each set of degenerate pairs (Kramers pairs), as in 8.6's Fano
   argument. The derivation reviewer should confirm.
2. **ε̃⁻¹.** E.14 and E.16 print (ε̃⁻¹)_{G0} without definition or NOTATION row; 8.7 defines it as the test-electron inverse dielectric
   matrix 1 + f_Hxc R (the operator of eq:6.2-screen, at (q, ω)); row proposed below. If the editor prefers to call it ε⁻¹ with a
   TDDFT qualifier, E.14/E.16 need the same change.
3. **P_casc first use** moves from 18.3 to 8.7 (the hand-off formula needs it); 18.3 keeps the precise definition. ε_e, ε_h scope
   widened from edge-local (6.5, D.18) to general carrier kinetic energies; the clash with ε_e(q, ω) of A.7 is unchanged (argument
   distinguishes).
4. The hand-off is written for N_eh (with other cascade counts named in words: phonon energy, E_st); the planning source's vector of
   quanta (pairs, heat, stored energy, Frenkel pairs, escaping energy) is not imported because its symbols have no NOTATION rows.
5. WE numbers are from the empirical-pseudopotential model (gap 0.82 eV against the measured 1.17 eV), stated as a model in the text.
   The 5 eV results are less converged (optical ⟨x⟩ moves 0.52 → 0.57 between 12³ and 16³) and are not quoted.
6. Fig. 8.3 bakes chapter numbers into the PDF (as Fig. 1.8 does); if chapters are renumbered the script must be rerun.
