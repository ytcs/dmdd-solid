## Open questions for the editor
1. T_c of bulk Al: 1.5 quotes 1.2 K (yeh2023doubling); 7.2, the chapter opener, 6.4/E.9 use 1.18 K (Savrasov Table III, experimental row).
   Consistent to the digits shown; the editor may want 1.5 to say 1.18 K.
2. ⚑ Notation clash: dressed vertices are written \varGamma_η, \boldsymbol{\varGamma}_j (italic capital Gamma), as in D.21, while
   NOTATION reserves italic \varGamma for rates. Proposed ruling: rename the vertices to sans-serif 𝖵_η, 𝗩_j (\mathsf{V}) in both 7.2
   and D.21 (one sed each; no other use of \mathsf{V} in the book found by this writer). Until ruled, 7.2 follows D.21.
3. ω_D for Al: no measured Debye temperature of Al is quoted anywhere in the book; WE 7.3 uses the largest phonon energy, 40 meV (6.4
   model), as the cutoff stand-in and shows ω_log = 270 K as the alternative (stated as a modelling choice).
4. eq:7.2-NVren is an estimate from the Eliashberg equations of E.9 (constant λ below a cutoff, Z = 1 + λ), not a cited formula.
5. The spurious f-sum moment's q-independence and the 8.6Δ crossing are computed on a linearized band with a sharp shell (fig7_2.py);
   the shell model puts the spurious weight up to 2ω_D. A real Δ(ω) falls off beyond the phonon energies, which would change the
   number but not the conclusion that the bare bubble's total weight is wrong at DM momenta.
6. The case-I/II pair-creation ratios are bare-bubble quantities; 15.1/15.2 must state how the conserving correction and screening change
   the density jump (the edge loss function of 15.2 is suppressed, not enhanced). 7.2 says the density line is the bare bubble.
7. Mattis–Bardeen is named and credited for the case-II integral; their paper is not readable here, so no equation number is cited.
