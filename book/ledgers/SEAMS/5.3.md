## The reader now knows
- Harmonic nuclear Hamiltonian H_N with p_Rκ = −i∇_Rκ, [u, p] = iδδδ (eq:5.3-H).
- Normal coordinates and momenta u_Qν, p_Qν (eq:5.3-normal), inverse (eq:5.3-inverse) via the lattice sum and completeness;
  u†_Qν = u_{−Qν}; [u_Qν, p_Q′ν′] = iδδ; H_N = ½Σ(p†p + ω²u†u) (eq:5.3-Hnormal); Heisenberg equations = classical (fulfils 5.2).
- Phonon operators b_Qν (eq:5.3-b), boson algebra derived, inverse u_Qν = (b + b†_{−Q})/√(2ω); H_N = Σω(b†b + ½) (eq:5.3-Hb), bb and
  b†b† terms cancel. Phonon (precise): one quantum of a normal mode; bosons, number not conserved, μ = 0; crystal momentum Q (via T_R
  of eq:3.1-crystalmomentum: b†_Qν → e^{iQ·R}b†_Qν). Zero-point energy 123 meV per Si cell (½ × 40.9 meV × 6; computed DOS), expands the
  lattice (→ E.8). b(t) = b e^{−iωt}.
- Keyresult: u_Rκ = Σ(2NM_κω)^{-1/2} e e^{iQ·R} B_Qν (eq:5.3-u) = eq:A.6-u; p_Rκ = −iΣ(M_κω/2N)^{1/2} e e^{iQ·R}(b − b†_{−Q}).
  B_Qν = b_Qν + b†_{−Qν}, B† = B_{−Q} (displacement field); A&M Chap. 23, Giustino Eqs. (16)–(18).
- Zero-point length ℓ_Qν = (2M_cellω)^{-1/2}, 1/√N outside (eq:5.3-ell = eq:A.6-ell); acoustic: ℓ/√N per nucleus; Si Γ optical
  0.024 Å = half the bond-coordinate ℓ_zp = 0.048 Å of 5.1 (each atom moves half; e = ±ê/√2); TA X (18.6 meV) 0.045 Å; 5 meV 0.086 Å;
  one optical phonon in 1 kg Si moves each nucleus 7×10⁻¹⁵ Å.
- Matrix elements (eq:5.3-me): ⟨n+1|u_Rκ|n⟩ = e*e^{−iQ·R}√(n+1)/√(2NM_κω), ⟨n−1|u|n⟩ = e e^{iQ·R}√n/√(2NM_κω); n_Qν = ⟨b†b⟩ =
  n_B(ω_Qν); weights n+1, n = eq:A.6-weights; each mode = WE 2.7 oscillator; Si 300 K: n = 0.09 (optical), 0.95 (TA X); cold detector:
  emission only.
- Nuclear density n_κ(q) expanded to first order in q·u (valid for q²⟨u_x²⟩ = ⟨(q·u)²⟩ ≪ 1, q well below 26 keV, Si 300 K); single-phonon
  emission amplitude i e^{iq·τ}(q·e*)√(N(n+1))/√(2M_κω) Σ_G δ_{q,Q+G}: crystal-momentum conservation, rate ∝ N, per-nucleus weight
  |q·e|²(n+1)/(2M_κω) (→ 11.1, 11.2).
- ⟨B_QνB_Q′ν′⟩ = δ_{Q′,−Q}(2n+1); displacement tensor B_κ (eq:5.3-B = eq:D.15-B), coth form; zero-point motion; limits (1/(2Mω) per
  mode; equipartition T/(Mω²)); acoustic convergence in 3D.
- Cubic site: B_κ = ⟨u_x²⟩𝟏, ⟨u²⟩ = tr B_κ = 3⟨u_x²⟩; isotropic displacement parameter 8π²⟨u_x²⟩ named.
- DOS form (eq:5.3-u2): ⟨u²⟩ = ∫(g_ph/n_at) coth(ω/2T)/(2M_κω), per-atom DOS normalized to three (equivalent atoms of one species);
  several species: atom-projected g_ph,κ(ω) = N⁻¹Σ|e_κν|²δ(ω − ω_Qν), normalized to three. T = 0: (3/2M)⟨ω⁻¹⟩; high T: (3T/M)⟨ω⁻²⟩.
- Debye (eq:5.3-debye = eq:D.15-debye written for ⟨u²⟩): 9/(4Mω_D)[…]; ω̄ = 2ω_D/3; high-T 9T/(Mω_D²)(1 + ω_D²/36T²); thermal =
  zero-point at T = 0.44Θ_D (Si 284 K). Trends: zero-point ∝ M^{-1/2} at fixed springs, high-T independent of M.
- Computational connection: B_κ from DFPT on a grid or the DOS (5.4, E.4), Γ-cell correction eq:E.4-dBgamma; Si 4³ grid (5.1) 0.050 /
  0.081 Å vs 48³ DOS 0.0495 / 0.0799 Å (0 K / 293 K).

   dashes for GaAs (a PBE low-ω fit would mix provenance with the elastic-constant Θ_D of Si and Ge).

6. The DFPT ⟨u²⟩ uses the 48³ tetrahedron DOS of 5.2 (from 6³ force constants); no separate Γ-cell correction was added. Scaling the
   8³ room-temperature estimate of E.4 (3.6e-4 Å², ∝ Q_c ∝ 1/N_Q) to 48³ bounds it by ~6e-5 Å², 1 % of ⟨u_x²⟩.
