## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Stieltjes / C.10 (no D item).** The section uses only the Hamburger band eq:C.10-band (valid at T = 0). Where all canonical
   nodes are ≥ 0 it is sharp on the half line; the script prints the windows where it is not and bounds the extra narrowing from
   below with eq:C.10-stieltjes (≥ 6.8 % at 100 keV, ≥ 24.5 % at 50 keV). The sharp half-line band is never used numerically, so
   no D derivation was written (D.33 is taken by Migdal anyway).
2. **𝔗 relabel.** F planning row L18 says n_A = 1; by the rate-order ruling and the time-reversal cancellation it is n_A = 2.
   The F row says so; please update the App. F planning table.
3. **Box 11.5a and 11.5b are labelled models** (single-site oscillator; He cell model with a Morse pair carrying the Aziz well and a
   12-6 curvature). Spine did not mark 11.5a/b as fallback. 2409.02439 has no SCHA; the "SCHA correction" is from 5.7/E.8.
4. **Box order.** Limit 11.5c precedes 11.5b in the text (the band is introduced before helium needs it); labels keep the spine names.
5. The n = 3 coefficient 18λ₃²k⁴ is a fit to the exact model, not derived analytically (stated as "the exact solution gives").
6. 2309.10839's Eq. (77) uses λ₃^{1/2} for n = 3; the model coefficient gives a crossover linear in λ₃ (4.2 keV at λ₃ = 0.01,
   coinciding numerically with theirs). The text quotes the model crossover and their rate conclusions, not Eq. (77).
7. E.19 says the He Lindemann ratio near melting is "about 0.3"; the cell model gives 0.23 (fixed neighbors underestimate); the
   text says "about a quarter". Align E.19 or keep.
8. Cazorla–Boronat's QH-vs-DMC kinetic-energy discrepancies (their abstract "≥ 50 %") were not quoted, since their E_harm/E_kin
   comparison mixes zero-point and kinetic energies.
