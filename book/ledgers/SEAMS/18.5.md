## The reader now knows
- E_np ≡ ω − N_eh E_g (eq:18.5-Enp), equal to E_heat of 18.1's energy balance until something is captured; Var N_eh = Var E_np/E_g².
  N_eh counts conduction electrons, free or bound in excitons (N_eh = N_e^free + N_X).
- Keyresult (eq:18.5-Eg, eq:18.5-thin): each captured electron raises E_np by exactly E_g, split (E_c − E_j) phonons at capture,
  (E_j − μ_F) trapped electron, (μ_F − E_v) unpaired hole (energies referred to the electrode Fermi level); binomial thinning with equal,
  cascade-independent f_cap: ε' = ε_eh/(1 − f_cap), F' = (1 − f_cap)F_eh + f_cap. General law of total variance eq:18.5-thingen for
  f_i = f(x_i) on the terminal energies (correlated with N, per 18.2). Hole capture leaves N_eh unchanged (readout matter, 20.1).
- F sensitivity: a loss raises F by f(1 − F); f = 0.56 % (0.34–0.79 %) would turn the cascade 0.114 into the measured 0.119 ± 0.002;
  ε_eh then +0.57 %, absorbed by the calibration.
- Capture kernel has the structure of the 18.1 kernels (promise of 18.1 kept in one sentence); size by r_C(E) = α/(ε_0 E), σ ≤ π r_C²
  (ε_0 = 11.4, α/ε_0 = 0.126 eV nm): 0.1 eV → 1.26 nm, 5.0e-14 cm², capture length ≥ 20 cm at 1e12 cm⁻³; micrometers only near 1e18.
  Cold carriers at 50 mK: k_BT = 4.3 µeV, Coulomb radius 29 µm; CDMS II neutralization by IR LEDs ("flashing", 1803.02903 Sect. 2).
- WE we:18-capture: ω = 37.5 eV, 10 pairs, E_np 25.80 → 26.97 eV; P donor 45.6 meV heat, 1.124 eV stored; 33.75 eV reported; 4.17 eV
  per counted pair (+11.1 %); random f = 0.1: ⟨N'⟩ = 9, ε' = 4.17 eV, F' = 0.207, σ 1.09 → 1.37, variance 1.86 = 0.96 (thinned
  cascade) + 0.90 (binomial).
- Excitons: E_x^b/k_BT = 3400 at 50 mK; formation after the cascade; N_eh, ε_eh, F_eh unchanged. Heat: E_heat = E_np + N_X E_x^b;
  F_heat (eq:18.5-Fheat); all bind → F_heat = F_eh(1 − E_x^b/E_g)²: −2.5 % Si, −1.3 % Ge (E_x^b ≈ 5 meV, 12.2); mean heat ≤ 0.39 % of ω.
  Charge: never-split excitons thin the charge, F_meas = (1 − p_ns)F_eh + p_ns (eq:18.5-Fmeas). Field-balance radius r_𝓔 =
  (α/ε_0 e𝓔)^{1/2} (eq:18.5-rfield): 0.56 µm at 4 V/cm, 0.11 µm at 100 V/cm; exciton field-ionization scale E_x^b/(e a_x) = 3.5e4 V/cm.
  Non-geminate formation: not independent; variance part grows faster than ⟨N⟩. Impact excitation ≤ 1.70 E_x^b = 25 meV (0.67 %) Si, 8.5 meV (0.30 %) Ge, with the cascade slope ∂ε/∂E_g = 1.70 of 18.4 (sec:18.4-rk).
  Ionic crystals: bound pair may be the cascade quantum; self-trapping (10.5); outside validated range (18.4).
- Rate equations eq:18.5-rates (per-pair occupations p_F, p_X, p_B,j; Γ_FX, Γ_FT, Γ_X with η_X, Γ_XB,j = σ_XB,j v_X 𝓃_j, Γ_B,j with
  η_B,j); trap occupation eq:18.5-traps (σ_cap,j, Γ^em_j, σ^rec_j, 𝓃_c, 𝓃_v, u). No reverse steps at mK. D0X Auger decay ejects the
  extra carrier; lifetime 200–300 ns (P), as quoted by Lo et al. Thermal emission frozen: 45.6 meV/k_BT = 1.06e4; Poole–Frenkel
  lowering 2(αe𝓔/ε_0)^{1/2} = 2.25 meV at 100 V/cm (maximum at r_𝓔), exponent 1.0e4. Athermal release → history → delayed charge (E.25).
- r_gap(t) = p_F + p_X + Σp_B,j = Σ w_k e^{−γ_k t}, Σw_k = 1, w_k of either sign (eq:18.5-rgap); overstatement ≤ E_x^b/E_g = 1.3 %.
  Split eq:18.5-split (b_trap, b_light, b_heat) derived from ∫p dt = −M⁻¹p(0).
- Fig. 18.4 (illustrative rates except the 250 ns Auger): bind within 1 ns; p_B peaks 0.15 at 0.45 µs; r_gap 0.5 at 0.83 µs, 0.01 at
  4.4 µs; weights +1.28, −0.33, +0.05; split 0.050 / 0.009 / 0.941; 42 % of gap energy still held at 1 µs. Dense tracks: formation
  leaves r_gap unchanged, annihilation/Auger give power laws, NR ≠ ER (19.5).
