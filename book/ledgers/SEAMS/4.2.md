  electron vs nuclear density) → Chap. 8.

  𝒢_VH(r,t) = N_e⁻¹∫d³r′⟨𝓃̂_e(r′,0)𝓃̂_e(r′+r,t)⟩ (eq:4.2-GVH); S = 𝓃_e∫dt d³r e^{i(q·r − ωt)}𝒢_VH (eq:4.2-vanhove), derived;

  imaginary part ↔ S(q,ω) vs S(−q,−ω) detailed-balance asymmetry, gone for |ω| ≪ T; free electron at rest: correlation e^{−iq²t/2m_e},
  S ∝ δ(ω − q²/2m_e) (WE 2.x free electron), self term spreads.

  → 1 at large q (incoherent); suppressed at small q by the hole around each electron; Bragg term (2π/V)|⟨n_e(q)⟩|²δ(ω) in the
  ground state at q = G, deposits nothing.
