## The reader now knows
- Harmonic expansion of E^ad(u) (eq:5.2-expand): force constants Φ^{(2),αβ}_{κκ′}(R,R′) = ∂²E^ad/∂u∂u at u = 0, real, symmetric under
  interchange of the two nuclei (pair symmetry), Φ(R,R′) = Φ(0,R′−R); linear term zero at equilibrium; harmonic approximation named, cubic ~κ_BO (→ 5.7).
- Classical equations of motion (eq:5.2-eom) justified for the quantum problem (Heisenberg equations of a quadratic H are the same; → 5.3);
  recap of small oscillations (mass-weighted coordinates); Bloch ansatz u = M_κ^{-1/2} e_κ e^{i(Q·R−ωt)} (eq:5.2-ansatz).
- Dynamical matrix (eq:5.2-Ddyn) = eq:A.6-Ddyn (phase on cell vector R only; τ_κ-phase convention of some codes changes e by e^{iQ·τ_κ},
  not ω); branches ν = 1…3n_at; polarization vectors; N problems of size 3n_at.
- Properties: D Hermitian; ω² real ≥ 0 at a minimum (imaginary frequency = not a minimum or unconverged); orthonormality and completeness
  (eq:5.2-ortho) incl. Σ_κ|e|² = 1; D(Q+G) = D(Q), Q in first zone; 3n_at N modes; D(−Q) = D(Q)* → ω_{−Q} = ω_Q, e(−Q) = e(Q)*.
- Estimate Φ ~ α²m_e/a₀² ~ 100 eV/Å² vs Φ_opt 13.6 eV/Å² (factor 7 high); frequencies tens of meV.
- Computational connection: DFPT gives D(Q) (→ 5.4); coarse grid + eq:E.4-ifc interpolation; App. E.4 for practice.
- WE 5.2 (we:5-chain): diatomic chain force constants, 2×2 D(Q), ω²_± closed form; acoustic c = a_c[K/2(M₁+M₂)]^{1/2}, e ∝ (√M₁,√M₂);
  optical ω² = 2K/μ_red, u₁/u₂ = −M₂/M₁; zone-boundary gap √(2K/M₁), √(2K/M₂); equal masses → folded monatomic chain; Si longitudinal
  [100] = chain of (100) planes a/4 apart, a_c = a/2, LA = LO at X exactly (12.32 THz); nearest-plane model K = 6.90 eV/Å² per atom,
  ω_X = 45.3 meV (meas. 50.95), c_LA = aω/8 = 6.61 km/s (meas. 8.43); GaAs nearest-plane gap 3.7 %.
- Acoustic sum rule (eq:5.2-asr) derived from E^ad(u+d) = E^ad(u); three zero modes at Γ with e_κν = (M_κ/M_cell)^{1/2}ê_ν; optical
  modes keep the cell's center of mass at rest; Goldstone modes (Lin p. 69); computed constants violate the rule (0.16 THz here) → E.4.
- Long waves: expansion D = D₀ + D₁ + D₂ (eq:5.2-Dexp); D₁ acoustic block vanishes: T^{αβγ} antisymmetric in αβ (pair symmetry) and
  symmetric in βγ (rotational invariance, zero torque, derived) → zero. Second order: D₂ plus D₁ through optical modes (internal
  relaxation); ω = c_ν(Q̂)Q (eq:5.2-sound), Λ matrix; ρ_TΛ = Christoffel matrix of relaxed C_ijkl, ρ_T = M_cell/Ω (→ C.18);
  [100]: ρc_LA² = C₁₁, ρc_TA² = C₄₄; c/v_max ~ 10⁻².
- Silicon: branch names LA, TA, LO, TO; degeneracies on Δ, Λ; Σ splits TA; measured Γ 15.5 THz, X_TA 4.49, X_LAO 12.32, X_TO 13.90,
  L_TA 3.43, L_LA 11.35, L_LO 12.60, L_TO 14.68 THz; no LO–TO (→ 5.5); flat TA from soft bond bending; narrow optical band.
  PBE comparison (PBE 60 Ry/12³ k/6³ Q at a_exp): Γ 15.40 THz = 63.69 meV (−0.8 %); X_TA 4.13 (−8.1 %), X_LAO 12.26, X_TO 13.85; L_TA 3.17
  (−7.6 %), L_LA 11.15, L_LO 12.41, L_TO 14.69 THz; six of eight within 2 %, mean |dev| 2.6 %; TA soft (bond bending); static vs room temperature; γ_Qν = 0 in the harmonic crystal (→ 5.7); γ^G sign and lattice constant (→ E.8).
- g_ph(ω) = (1/N)Σδ(ω−ω_Qν), ∫ = 3n_at per cell (eq:5.2-dos); per atom g_ph/n_at; Van Hove singularities; low-ω law
  g = Ωω²/(2π²)Σ_ν⟨c_ν^{-3}⟩ (eq:5.2-dosac).
- Debye model: atom-density sphere Q_D = (6π²n_at/Ω)^{1/3}, ω_D = c̄Q_D, Θ_D = ω_D (eq:5.2-debye); g_D = 9n_atω²/ω_D³ (eq:5.2-gD);
  c̄ from 3/c̄³ = Σ_ν⟨c_ν^{-3}⟩ (eq:5.2-cbar); acoustic-only convention lower by n_at^{1/3}.
- WE 5.3 (we:5-debye): c from C₁₁, C₄₄ (8.43, 5.84); [110] 9.13/5.84/4.67, [111] 9.35/5.09 (as C.18); per-branch ⟨c^{-3}⟩^{-1/3} 9.00,
  5.66, 5.04; c̄ = 5.89 km/s (LA 9 % of the sum); Q_D = 1.435 Å⁻¹ = 1.24(2π/a); ω_D = 55.6 meV (13.4 THz); Θ_D = 645 K vs calorimetric
  645 K (Kittel); acoustic-only 512 K; isotropic convention 6.35 km/s, 697 K (+8 %). PBE slopes [100] 8.34/5.44, [110] 8.94/5.44/4.38, [111] 9.13/4.76 km/s (TA 7 % soft);
  computed g_ph: TA peak 17 meV, 25–52 meV structure, TO peaks 58 and 61 meV; 22 % of modes below 20 meV; ω² law to ≈7 meV (+10 %);
  PBE ω² fit 1–6 meV → c̄ = 5.42 km/s, Θ_D 594 K; 38 % of modes above ω_D. ASR violation before correction 0.163 THz.
