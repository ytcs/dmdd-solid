## The reader now knows

  (dynamic structure factor, operational: "how readily the solid absorbs q together with ω, per unit volume"), energy conservation.
- Why it matters: particle physics and condensed-matter physics separate; a new model changes |Ṽ|², a new target changes S.
- Which part supplies what: halo 1.2, kinematics 1.3, |Ṽ|² = πσ̄_e F_DM²/μ_χe² 1.4, S Chaps. 3–7 → 8 → 10–17, ω → quanta 1.5, 18–21.

  σ₁ at ω = m_φ (1.4, 10.7); Migdal (12) and phonon-assisted (13) joint/second-order responses; approximation label and error on each S (21).
