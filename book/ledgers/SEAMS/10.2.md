## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Spine's "1.4–2.5 in rate".** That pair is KKL's ε₂^LF/L^LF (local fields kept in the "unscreened" rate), not IP→RPA+LF. The text
   now distinguishes four numbers (pointwise static, IP/(RPA+LF) = 2.3/3.7, RPA/(RPA+LF) = 1.46/1.27, KKL's 1.56/2.73 at 10 MeV).
   The spine's pointwise 0.117/0.451 is the Cappellini model (reproduced with book values); the tables give 0.12/0.32. Both quoted.
2. **Large-q local fields.** In the KKL tables RPA/(RPA+LF) per q reaches 1.7–1.8 at 8–10 keV; the heavy-mediator rate lives there.
   Consistent in direction with Dreyer et al. 2603.12326, but the size depends on a truncated spectrum (17 % of the f-sum below
   89 eV at 10 keV, 70 bands, pseudopotential). Stated as open and as a nuisance; 10.10 should carry it.
3. **Limit 10.2a fuller result.** The planned row named "the total-charge rate with lattice screening"; in Si it coincides with the
   clamped L (Born charges zero), so the plot would show nothing. The box compares the dark photon (total L) with an electrophilic
   coupling (clamped L + model acoustic peak), and the F-row says so. The acoustic peak is a pedagogical model (Z^{(e)} = 14 rigid,
   linear LA to 2π/a, no DW/Umklapp/TA/multiphonon), named in caption and script; ">10⁴ ×" is a model value.
4. **𝔗 relabel.** The planned L17′ row put the relaxed term at n_B = 1; per the NOTATION ruling (n_B = 1 only for interference) and
   E.16's "adiabatic, harmonic, one-phonon", it is n_B = 0, 𝒦 = one-phonon in the F-row and the text.
5. **Limit 10.2b shading.** At 1 MeV the lattice term exceeds the clamped step over the whole kinematic window (it equals one only at
   18 keV), so "q above the rise of the lattice term" is off the plot. The shaded region is q ≥ E_g/v_max (pairs open), where the
   clamped bound holds for both couplings with an electronic threshold; stated in the caption.
6. **E.16 symbol.** E.16 writes c_L in eq:E.16-step's limit; NOTATION has c_LA (clash entry 72 renamed c_L → c_LA). Rename in E.16.
7. **Gap value.** The chapter opening (10.1 writer) says the gap is 1.17 eV; this section and Chap. 1 use 1.11 eV (room temperature;
   1.17 eV is the low-temperature gap). The editor should make the chapter consistent.
8. **For the 10.1 writer / Limit 10.1a:** figscripts/ch10/rate10_2.py provides the RPA+LF loss-function rate that 10.1's box names as
   its fuller result. IP rate from the KKL no-LF ε₂ at 10 MeV, heavy, σ̄_e = 1e-37 cm², ρ_χ = 0.3 GeV/cm³, threshold E_g:
   1.36e5 /kg/yr (light: 3.66e4); KKL report that this agrees with QEdark (their Fig. 5 text), so 10.1's QEdark-based numbers should
   agree to the halo difference.
9. Static step held at its first tabulated value below q = 0.49 keV in the bound integrals (kinematic window starts at 0.42 keV at
   threshold E_g); the n = −1 bound uses the valence-only step, slightly low as a bound (said in the WE).
