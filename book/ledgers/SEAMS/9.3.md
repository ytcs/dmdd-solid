## Differences from the SPINE entry (book followed)
1. The SPINE learning outcome mentions an "overlap of the phonon-assisted region with the multiphonon band"; in the accepted
   Ch13 (and on the computed plane) they do not overlap in silicon: the PA region sits at ω ≥ 1.18 eV, the lattice band below
   ≈0.3 eV up to the impulse onset; the nuclear band reaches the gap only above 212–247 keV. The text states the overlaps
   that do exist.
2. "Cross-sector interference" (SPINE) is written as the book has it: sectors add; interference = cross cells within a sector
   (8.6/17.4 terminology) plus the Fano term at the P_0/P_+ boundary.
3. Added tab:9-overlaps and tab:9-supports (not in SPINE) to place every atlas row; numbering of later Ch9 tables shifts
   (labels unaffected).
4. Physical gap 1.17 eV used for E_min and reach; the gray loss function is the 1.11 eV table (caption says so).
