## The reader now knows
- First-order amplitude c_f(t) from the exact amplitude equations (eq:2.1-exact, eq:2.1-c1); validity Σ P ≪ 1; first Dyson term (→ 2.4).
- Constant perturbation: P = 4|H′_fi|² sin²(ξt/2)/ξ² (eq:2.1-Pstatic), ξ = E_f − E_i; peak t²/4, zeros 2πk/t, area πt/2, central lobe
  90.3%; δ-limit (eq:2.1-deltalimit, stated, proof → App. C.1); derivative form 2|H′|² sin(ξt)/ξ → 2π|H′|²δ.
- Golden rule keyresult (eq:2.1-golden); rate is an energy (10 meV ↔ 1.52e13 s⁻¹); switching independence; becomes δ(ω − ω_q) (→ 2.2).
- Density of final states 𝒟_f(E), total rate Γ_i = 2π⟨|H′|²⟩𝒟_f (eq:2.1-total); window 1/E_w ≪ t ≪ min(1/Γ_i, 2π𝒟_f) (eq:2.1-window);
  V → ∞ before t → ∞; window exists iff Γ_i ≪ E_w; depletion → exponential decay (→ 2.4). DM continuum supplied by the outgoing DM
  momentum even for discrete target levels. Failure cases: vanishing first order (→ 2.4), diagonal shift (Ṽ(0)/V → 0 for scattering).
- Harmonic perturbation H′ = Ô e^{−iωt} + Ô† e^{iωt}: absorption δ(E_f − E_i − ω), emission δ(E_f − E_i + ω) (eq:2.1-harmonic), ωt ≫ 1;
  sign tied to App. A.3 temporal convention; |⟨f|Ô†|i⟩| = |⟨i|Ô|f⟩| (→ 2.5 detailed balance); stimulated vs spontaneous (√n, √(n+1),
  → 2.3, 2.5); ω → 0 limit.
- Bosonic DM absorption: φ = φ₀cos(m_φt), ρ_χ = m_φ²φ₀²/2, spatial phase neglected (1/(m_φv) ≈ 10³/m_φ); rate ∝ φ₀² = 2ρ_χ/m_φ²: density,
  not flux (explains the 1.4 statement; → Chap. 8).
- WE 2.1 (we:2-continuum): flat continuum, P(t) = 4|O_fi|²𝒟_f t[Si(E_wt/2) − sin²(E_wt/4)/(E_wt/4)] (eq:2.1-WEP); short time N_f|O_fi|²t²;
  long time Γ(t − 4/(πE_w)); numbers E_w = 1 eV, N_f = 2000, |O_fi| = 0.892 meV, Γ = 10 meV; 0.66 fs, 0.84 fs, 66 fs, 8.3 ps; first order
  +10% at t = 21.9 eV⁻¹ (Γt = 0.22); 0.99 vs exact 0.63 at 1/Γ; exact within 0.4% of 1 − e^{−Γt} for t ≥ 1/Γ (rotating-wave model).
- Box normalization (eq:2.1-box, Lin TASI (A1)–(A2)); why (squared momentum δ); free-particle 𝒟_f = Vmp/2π² (eq:2.1-Dfree);
  spin sums, initial-state averages (→ 2.5).
- Box matrix element of a separation potential Ṽ(q)δ_{P_fP_i}/V (eq:2.1-Vbox, the CONVENTIONS identity, derived); Γ ∝ 1/V;
  σ_T = VΓ/v (eq:2.1-sigma) via eq:1.2-flux; rate per unit mass (ρ_χ/m_χm_T)∫d³v f_χ v σ_T.
- Relativistic normalization: 2E per unit volume, |p⟩_rel = (2EV)^{1/2}|p⟩, ℳ defined via ⟨f|H′|i⟩_rel = −ℳ(2π)³δ³; box element
  −ℳδ/[V∏(2E_j)^{1/2}]; σ_T in flux-factor/phase-space form; |ℳ|² = 16m_χ²m_e²|Ṽ|² (NR); σ̄_e of eq:1.4-defs = Essig 2012 Eq. (3) form.
