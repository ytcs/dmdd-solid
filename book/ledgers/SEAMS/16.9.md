## The reader now knows
- Three kinds of input: measured constants with sample spread (tanδ₀, line width, work function, g_c); computed constants with a spread
  between calculations (ZrTe₅ tensor and gap, 𝒰_{j'j}(G), α_ohm), an estimate not a bound; yields/parameters no measurement isolates
  (Y_CC factors, P̄ separately from the dipole), for which 8.8 assigns no width and the reach is conditional.
- Table 16.1 (tab:16-inputs): 11 targets × inputs, uncertainty and effect on the reach, limiting factor (theory / calculation / data),
  home of the open problem.
- Theory-limited: Dirac cones at α̃ ≳ 1 (BNQ-TTF ~10); Si:P at n_D/n_c ≈ 0.3–0.6 (isolated-donor response wrong at O(1) near the edge);
  interacting TLS. Calculation-limited (formalism known, App. E.20/E.21/E.22/E.10/E.11): Dirac edge ladder, ejection final state,
  periodic finite-q BSE of molecular crystals, tube excitons, donor valley-orbit coefficients, stacks beyond EMT, first-principles α_ohm.
  Data-limited: ZrTe₅ tensor and gap, ε_in(ω) of real dots, donor oscillator strengths, LiF E_d(r̂), the four yields, TLS film parameters.
- Published-formula corrections (ε_bg² = 4800–6600 for ZrTe₅; μ/4ω = 4.05e4 for dots; ×4 nanotube; 4.84 donor factor) are errors of a
  formula, not uncertainties; ZrTe₅ and dots exceed their input bands by ≥ 3 orders.
- Reach band rule: R ∝ x^{−p} ⇒ σ̄^lim ∝ x^p (eq:1.5-limscaling), band (x₂/x₁)^p; absorption half the log-width; threshold inputs via
  eq:1.3-mmin / eq:8.8-shift (diverging near the wall); no size ⇒ no band.
- eq:16.9-power: ∂ln L/∂ln ε_bg = −2ε_bg Re(ε_bg + ε_D)/|ε_bg + ε_D|² = −2[1 − Re ε_D/ε_bg + O(|ε_D|²/ε_bg²)].
- WE 16.9 (we:16-band), we16_9.log: central tensor (308.4, 20.75, 126.1) vs earlier (187.5, 9.8, 90.9), ratios 0.61/0.47/0.72, with the
  Dirac parameters of 16.1 for both (modelling choice). Flat-numerator estimate 0.327; 64 % of ⟨(q̂·ε·q̂)⁻²⟩ within 30° of y; (9.8/20.75)² =
  0.22. Full: reach ratio 0.345–0.362 (20 keV–1 MeV), 0.31 near threshold; at 49 keV 2.24e-40 → 8.1e-41 cm² (band 2.8). α̃_y 0.155 → 0.327.
  Power p = 1.87–1.94 (20 keV–1 MeV), 1.84 at 12 keV; factor-2 common scaling → 3.6–3.8. Dirac term removed (9.8, 1.57, 6.7 →
  298.6, 19.18, 119.4): reach ×0.893–0.896. Absorption ζ ratio 0.49–0.55; 1.21e-15 → 6.2e-16 at 100 meV; y-only estimate 0.47.
  Gap 24–35 meV (Δ_D 12.0–17.5 meV): threshold along y 9.4 → 7.2–10.5 keV.
- Other bands (inputs16_9.log): Si:P 2p₀ ζ 1.3–2.3e-16 (×1.8); a-SiO₂ tanδ₀ 3e-4–5e-3: rate ×16.7, ζ ×4.1; dot depolarization 0.57–6.7:
