## The reader now knows
- Macroscopic field ℰ (cell average, G = 0 component) and polarization 𝐏; 𝐃 = ℰ + 𝐏 (HL); ε∞ = 1 + ∂P/∂ℰ at clamped nuclei.
- Energy at fixed field E^ad_ℰ (eq:5.5-U = eq:D.14-U) with C^an, Z*, ε∞ as second derivatives; F and P (eq:5.5-FP); Born charge
  eZ^{*,αβ} = ∂F^α/∂ℰ^β = Ω∂P^β/∂u^α (eq:5.5-Z), Maxwell relation, force-index-first convention (= eq:E.4-epsZ), transpose in codes.
- Physical meaning: dynamical charge (nucleus + electrons that follow + charge flow along bonds), not an integer, not a partition charge.
- Charge neutrality ΣZ* = 0 (eq:5.5-neutral), derived from translation of a neutral cell; GaAs one number Z*; Si Z* = 0 (→ E.4).
- Polarization of a periodic solid: cell dipole ill defined; changes measurable; Berry phase (King-Smith–Vanderbilt), Wannier centers
  ×(−eg_deg/Ω), quantum eg_degR/Ω; formula eq:E.4-berry.
- Computational connection: DFPT field perturbation; GaAs PBE numbers above; sum rule as convergence test; ε∞ converges slowly
  because the PBE gap (0.57 eV vs 1.52) is small near Γ; converged ε∞ 26 % high (functional, eq:E.4-kk); Z* robust (2.094).
- Electrostatics of a long wave (curl-free, Q̂·D = 0); keyresult nonanalytic term (eq:5.5-na = eq:D.14-na/(√MM), Gonze–Lee, Baroni
  (98)); direction dependence, no Taylor series; cubic diatomic: relative coordinate w, eq:5.5-w, ω_T² = C₀/μ_red, eq:5.5-LOTO;
  estimate ~2e-4 eV², splitting a few meV.
- Fig. 5.4 (fig:5-gaas): dispersion and Γ zoom; isotropic LO in cubic crystals, anisotropic in sapphire; splitting 2.12 vs 2.73 meV
  traced to ε∞; 2.65 meV with measured ε∞.
- Interpolation in polar crystals: R⁻³ dipole tail, Ewald subtraction and re-addition (fulfils 5.4's promise).
- Polaritons (qualitative): Q ~ ε0^{1/2}ω_T ≈ 0.1 eV; scattering (q ≳ eV) electrostatic; absorption (q ~ 10⁻³ω) uniform-field regime.
- ε(ω) of the driven lattice (eq:5.5-eps); ε0 = ε∞ + e²Z*²/(Ωμω_T²); keyresult LST (eq:5.5-LST) and generalized form (eq:D.14-LSTgen);
  poles at TO, zeros at LO (cf. plasmon of 4.5); reststrahlen band; loss function peaks at ω_L, ε₂ at ω_T.
- WE 5.6 (we:5-lst): ε0/ε∞ = 12.9/10.88 = 1.186 vs (8.79/8.13)² = 1.169 (1.4 %); predicted ω_L 36.61 meV (+0.7 %), splitting 2.99 vs
  2.73 meV (+9 %), error amplification (r − 1 = 0.17); Z* = 2.11 from the splitting (Ω = 44.80 Å³ = 5.831e-9 eV⁻³, μ_red = 36.11 u =
  33.64 GeV, e² = 0.0917, ω_L² − ω_T² = 1.910e-4 eV²); static route 2.21; PBE Z* 2.094 (−0.7 %), ε∞ 13.7 (+26 %).
- Uniform-field coupling H_ℰ = −eΣu·Z*·ℰ → −e√N Σ_ν (z_ν·ℰ)/√(2ω_0ν) B_0ν (eq:5.5-HE), mode charge z_ν; acoustic modes decouple
