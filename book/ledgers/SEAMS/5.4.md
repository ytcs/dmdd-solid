## The reader now knows
- Frozen phonons: HF-force finite differences; one-sided error ∝ Φ⁽³⁾h, central (eq:5.4-fd) O(h²) from Φ⁽⁴⁾; one displacement gives
  a column (3N_at forces); Si needs one displacement (± h) by space-group + T_d symmetry; step 0.01–0.03 Å.
- Supercell aliasing (eq:5.4-alias): Φ^SC(R) = Σ_T Φ(R+T); D(Q) exact at the N_Q³ commensurate wavevectors only; Γ = primitive cell,
  X needs a doubled cell (link to folded chain WE 5.2); cost ∝ range⁹ (→ E.4).
- WE 5.5 (we:5-frozen): E(±0.03 Å) = 5.943 / 6.316 meV; one-sided Φ 13.21 / 14.04 eV/Å² (±1.5 % in ω; ±3 % at 0.06 Å); Φ⁽³⁾ = −41.5
  eV/Å³ from the spread (= 5.1); symmetric 13.62 (0.03 Å), 13.64 (0.06 Å); Φ⁽⁴⁾ ≈ 90 eV/Å⁴; ω = (2Φ/M)^{1/2} = 63.68 meV; forces
  13.63 eV/Å², 63.71 meV; DFPT 63.7 (5.1) and 63.69 (5.2); measured 64.1 meV (0.7 % above).
- Second derivative of E^ad (eq:5.4-hess, Baroni (10)): needs only ∂𝓃; nonlocal pseudopotential form; monochromatic derivative
  ∂_{κα,Q}; D^dyn as a double wave derivative (eq:5.4-Dwave, Baroni (82)); Q decouple.
- Linear response: eq:5.4-dV (= eq:E.4-dV) with static f_xc = δ²E_xc/δ𝓃δ𝓃; δ𝓃 = R⁰δV_KS (eq:4.6-R0, ω = 0); δ𝓃 = RδV_ext with
  eq:4.6-tddft at ω = 0; Pick–Cohen–Martin form of D^el; bare stiffness softened by screening; DFPT phonon = full static response of
  the functional; static e–ph self-energy already in the adiabatic phonon (for D.17).
- Sternheimer: linearized KS (eq:5.4-lin, Baroni (25)); occupied–occupied cancellation in δ𝓃 derived; P^c δψ equation derived from the
  sum over states; δε_v removed; a_sh P^occ makes it positive definite; monochromatic bookkeeping k → k+Q; keyresult eq:5.4-sth
  (identical to eq:E.4-sth) and eq:5.4-dn (factor 4 = spin × time reversal, Baroni (35)); periodic parts, primitive cell, any Q;
  self-consistency loop; screened ∂V_KS = e–ph vertex (6.2). History: Sternheimer 1954; Baroni–Giannozzi–Testa 1987; Giannozzi et al. 1991.
- 2n+1 theorem (keyresult) with a complete stationarity proof; orthonormality via non-orthonormal functional / Lagrange multipliers
  (Gonze 1995a); n = 0 = HF; n = 1 → Φ⁽²⁾ and Φ⁽³⁾ (→ 5.7); mixed derivatives need one response (→ Z* either way, 5.5, E.4);
  variational E⁽²⁾ error quadratic vs eq:5.4-hess linear in ∂𝓃 (reconciles E.4's "error enters linearly").
- Interpolation: inverse grid transform = supercell aliasing (same information as frozen phonons in N_Q³ supercell); WS assignment;
  error = Φ beyond WS cell; smoothness ↔ range; metals (Kohn anomalies), polar (R⁻³, nonanalytic, subtract dipole part → 5.5, eq:E.4-na).
- Fig. 5.3 (fig:5-dfpt), Si PBE: 2³ grid exact at Γ, X, L (X_TA 17.06 meV equal to 6³) but off-grid errors up to 4.0 meV (~25 %);
  3³ misses X (X_TA 14.75 meV, −14 %), off-grid ≤ 1.2 meV; 6³ off-grid ≤ 0.3 meV (≤ 1.5 %, below the 2.6 % functional–experiment
  mean). |Φ| blocks: on-site 23.3, NN 8.0, ⟨110⟩ zigzag chain 0.59 at 3.84 Å, 0.21 at 5.92 Å, 0.12 at 7.68 Å (1.5 % of NN), others < 0.1 beyond 2nd shell.
- ASR: violation Δ_κ from the fixed xc grid; unimposed Γ acoustic 0.675 meV (0.163 THz); on-site repair eq:5.4-asrfix; non-unique,
  pair symmetry, least-change variant; Si Δ ∝ 1 (cubic); rotational invariance not imposed. Near Γ ω² ≈ ω₀² + c²Q²: TA at Q = 0.02 (2π/a)
  1.07 vs 0.83 meV (+29 %), 1 % at 0.1; imposed slopes [100] 5.44 / 8.34 km/s (= 5.2); meV-threshold acoustic searches (9.2).
