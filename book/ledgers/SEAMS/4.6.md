## The reader now knows

  cancel), keyresult eq:4.6-R0 = eq:E.6-R0 (2 for spin written explicitly, i0⁺; E.6 writes g_deg and η_b); gas limit = eq:4.5-R0.
- Optical limit: head eq:4.6-head O(q²) via eq:3.2-optical; ε₂^IP eq:4.6-eps2 (golden-rule absorption, σ₁ = ωε₂); ε₁^IP(0) eq:4.6-static
  with Δ⁻³ weighting → too-small KS gap overestimates ε∞ (PBE 13.04 vs 11.7); scissor: ρ fixed ⇒ rigid ε₂ shift, static lowered.
- Computational connection (one paragraph) → E.6.
- RPA in a crystal eq:4.6-rpa; two levels: head only (= IP for ε₂ exactly; screening only via L) and full inversion eq:4.6-rpaLF (R̄ form,
  D.7). Local-field picture: layered capacitor, harmonic vs arithmetic mean = inverse of average of inverse vs average; bond dipoles'
  microscopic field opposes the applied one where the polarizable charge sits.
- Fig. fig:4-sirungs (model): IP E2 4.26 eV / 41 (measured 4.24 / 46), E1 shoulder 15 at 3.42 eV (measured peak 36); LF: E2 4.32 eV / 34,
  13 at 3.42 eV, weight moves up; ALDA E2 37; L: ε₂/L ~ 10²–10³ below 6 eV; LF raise L 10–35 % at 3–12 eV, plasmon peak 7.6 → 3.4; model
  loss peak ~2 eV above 16.4 eV. LF small in DM rate (Knapen); larger at q ~ few G and semicore energies (E.6). Louie 1975, Albrecht 1998.
- Local fields = BSE exchange kernel K^x (Coulomb without G = 0) with the attraction off; cannot bind excitons (→ 4.9).
- TDDFT: Runge–Gross (attribution); linearized KS: δV_KS = φ_ext + v_Cδ𝓃 + f_xcδ𝓃, f_xc eq:4.6-fxc; keyresult Dyson eq:4.6-tddft
  R = R⁰ + R⁰f_HxcR, R̃ = R⁰ + R⁰f_xcR̃ (= eq:E.6-dyson); RPA = f_xc = 0; Petersilka et al. (poles); macroscopic kernel v̄ + f_xc.
- What the exact kernel must do (move KS poles to true gap; bind excitons, long range). ALDA eq:4.6-alda (Gross–Kohn), negative, f(G−G′)
  only; misses: frequency dependence (no gap opening), long range −α_xc/|q+G|² (Reining); head/wings drop out in optical limit, acts only in
  body; ALDA ≈ RPA+LF for absorption, fine for EELS (Onida; Botti). Model ε∞ 10.81, E2 37.
- Gas: G_LF defined by ε = 1 − vR⁰/(1 + vG_LFR⁰); f_xc = −v_C G_LF eq:4.6-GLF; compressibility sum rule eq:4.6-compress
  (∂μ_ch/∂𝓃_e = 1/2𝒩_F + f_xc(0)); ALDA exact there; exchange f_x = −e²/4k_F², G = q²/4k_F²; r_s = 2 with PZ: 0.267 q²/k_F², gas 1.55×
  more compressible; ALDA wrong for q ≳ k_F (QMC G peaks near 2k_F, stays O(1), cited GV Chap. 5 qualitatively); at q₀ = 1.04 k_F,
  G_LF = 0.29, ε(q₀, 0) 2.10 → 2.62.
- Ladder in plain words: IP → RPA → RPA + LF → TDDFT → (BSE, 4.9); not an expansion; spread = many-body uncertainty, same numerics for every
  rung (E.6); small for heavier DM, leading for light DM near the gap.
- WE 4.11 (we:4-siepsinf): ε∞ IP 11.45 (2 % below 11.7), RPA+LF 10.22 (−11 %; 113 G same to 3 digits), ALDA 10.81 (+6 %, undoes ~half);
