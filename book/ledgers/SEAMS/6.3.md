- Keyresult Σ^FM (eq:6.3-FM) and Σ^AH (eq:6.3-AH): reading of n + 1 − f, n + f; occupied bands in the sum; zero-point enters via 1 and f;
  Σ^AH static, real, weight 2n + 1. Fan 1951 / Migdal 1958 / Allen–Heine 1976; Giustino Eqs. (157), (158), (160), (161).
- Renormalized energy eq:6.3-ren; ZPR defined (state; gap = c − v); AHC theory named; on-shell vs solved (WE 4.14: −9.14 vs −8.73 meV).
- Adiabatic limit eq:6.3-adiabatic (Giustino Eq. (167)); occupations drop out; frozen-phonon picture: half curvature × ⟨uu⟩.
- Adiabatic failure: m = n small Q, degeneracies (band edges); Fröhlich: adiabatic intraband integral ∝ ∫dQ/Q² diverges; non-adiabatic
  Re Σ^FM at band bottom = −α_F ω_L (GaAs −2.6 meV): the polaron shift promised by 6.2; adiabatic ZPR infinite for every IR-active
  crystal. Si: −64.3 (adiabatic) vs −56.2 meV (non-adiabatic), LDA, Poncé Table VI.
- Q → 0 acoustic cancellation (eq:6.3-cancel; 6.2 promise delivered): g → −iℓ(ε_n − ε_m)ê·p_mn from eq:6.2-asr; g^AH → −(ℓ²/2)⟨[p_ν,[p_ν,H]]⟩
  = −ℓ²Σ(ε_n − ε_m)|ê·p_mn|²; term-by-term cancellation; translational modes at Q = 0 excluded from both sums; remainder O(Q²); the
  intraband m = n term survives and needs the phonon energy; double commutator as in the f-sum rule (eq:4.3-firstmoment).
- Rigid-ion approximation and Allen–Cardona form eq:6.3-ria (= D.16-AHria) with B_κ of eq:5.3-B; sum rule D.16-sr2; neglects non-rigid
  screening (large in molecules, standard for crystals).
- Computational connection (one paragraph) → E.8: active window + Sternheimer, Q grids 10⁵–10⁶ or Wannier, iδ → 0, residual Z*.
- Σ^AH vs Debye–Waller (eq:6.3-smear): rigid-ion average of v_κ gives v_κ(G)e^{−W_κ(G)}; first order = Σ^AH ("fuzziness", Giustino
  below Eq. (154)); different from e^{−2W_κ(q)} of 5.6 (probability, multiplies a rate, at q; vs energy at G, first order, half the shift).
- Si ZPR: −56.2 meV non-adiabatic AHC; VBM up 35, CBM down 22 meV (ZG); special displacements named (Zacharias–Giustino; one
  configuration, all orders, adiabatic; −57 meV in 8×8×8) → E.8; measured −62/−64 meV (Cardona 2005 via Poncé), +9 meV zero-point
  lattice expansion, e–ph part −71/−73, calculations ~20 % low (overscreening).
- Einstein model eq:6.3-einstein: Δε(T) = Δε(0)coth(ω_Ein/2T); high-T line through clamped value (linear extrapolation); ZPR ∝ M^{-1/2},
  ZPR = −2M ∂E_g/∂M (isotope route).
- WE 6.3 (we:6-zpr): ω_Ein = 30.4 meV (353 K) from ⟨ω⁻¹⟩⁻¹ (we:5-u2); ZPR −56.2: coth(0.588) = 1.891, E_g(300) − E_g(0) = −50.1 meV vs

  ⟨u²⟩ scaling ratio 2.661 → −93.3 meV (twice measured): soft modes couple less than their ⟨u²⟩; clamped-lattice model gap 1.2254 eV;
  inverse (extraction) direction: ZPR from the 300 K shift −51.8 meV, from the 500 K slope −59 meV, bracketing −56.2;
  ³⁰Si +1.8 meV; at 1 K 2n_B ~ 10⁻¹⁵³; detector sees E_g(0), no double counting (we:E-zpr). Fig. 6.4: model within ~5 meV of the
  measured curve 0–600 K (max −5.0 meV near 390 K; +3.1 meV near 100 K).
- Lifetimes: Im Σ^FM eq:6.3-ImFM; Γ = −2z ImΣ; cutting rule reading; Σ^AH no width; CBM zero width at T = 0 (4.7 promise); iδ widths
  are artifacts; at 10 mK absorption absent above ~10 μeV.
- Cherenkov emission of acoustic phonons eq:6.3-cherenkov: Γ = ΩΞ²m*Q_max³/(12πM_cell c k), Q_max = 2(k − m*c), threshold k > m*c.
  Si estimate (spherical valley m* = 0.322 m_e; direction-averaged Ξ²: LA (4.75 eV)², one TA (3.23 eV)²; c = 8.43, 5.84 km/s):

## Revision after the Chapter 6 review (2026-11-02)
- Fan sign: the interband (translation) Fan term ℓ²Σ(ε_n − ε_m)|ê·p_mn|² has either sign; "shifts … and the Allen–Heine term shifts it back".
- Degenerate levels: after the keyresult, the self-energy is a matrix in the partners, partner terms with vanishing denominators are left out
  (Poncé Sect. III A); summed over the zone it commutes with the little group, so on an irreducible level it is a multiple of the identity
  and each partner shifts by the average of the diagonal elements. D.16 says the same in one clause. The VBM shift is defined this way.
- ZPR: one paragraph after Fig. 6.4 separates the three scales: −52/−58 meV (WE inversions; depend on ω_Ein and on where the slope is read;
  a consistency test), −62/−64 meV (Cardona, isotope and linear extrapolation, includes +9 meV zero-point expansion), −71/−73 meV (e–ph part).
  Computed −56.2 meV is ≈20 % short of the e–ph part; overscreening stated as "consistent with", with Zacharias' GW-based 66 meV cited.
  WE: −59 → −58. Summary carries the same comparison. (E.8 l.92 still attributes the 20 % to Zacharias directly: editor.)
- Migdal counting: full statement here only (6.4 and D.17 point here); "10⁻³ to 10⁻²"; insulator corrections (ω_ph/E_g)²; α_F vertex
  correction marked as an estimate; v_F and λ_ep glossed; m*_d with m*_∥, m*_⊥ (0.322 here and in 6.5).
- 10 mK point stated once, in the closing paragraph (with the do-not-add-twice instruction); WE ends with "therefore sees E_g(0)".
- Q² remainder justified (linear term drops out of the angular sum); scalar-QED remark (rainbow/seagull; Rayleigh cancellation).
- "Translation invariance" (was "translational") in 6.3, D.16 and the Summary. "modelling choice" → "an approximation".
- A.6: the "opposite effects in sum rules" sentence replaced by what 5.6 and 6.3 show (shared B_κ; first-moment double commutator:
  elastic share of the weight in 5.6, Fan–AH cancellation in 6.3).
