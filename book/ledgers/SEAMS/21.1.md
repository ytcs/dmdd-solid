## The reader now knows
- Halo functional of a channel (keyresult eq:21.1-functional): ℛ_{N_eh}[f_χ] = (ρ_χ/m_χρ_T)∫d³v f_χ(𝐯)Γ_{N_eh}(𝐯), with the rate kernel
  Γ_{N_eh}(𝐯) of eq:21.1-kernel = the q-integral and class sum of eq:8.7-handoff at fixed 𝐯 (21.2 and D.46 cite eq:21.1-kernel). Any observable bin: replace P_casc.
- Kernel ≥ 0 (PSD contraction × probability) → rates monotone in f_χ (basis of 21.2). Γ = 0 below the onset speed v_on(m_χ) (smallest v_min over
  the support of the response). Crystal: kernel direction-dependent; exposure average eq:21.1-exposure in galactic variables, ⟨Γ(𝐯_gal − 𝐯_E(t))⟩_t =
  the single-stream rate on which 21.2 builds (E.27's stream kernel 𝔎_i is this times the exposure; not named here).
- Absorption: kernel Γ_abs of eq:8.2-abs, constant across the halo where S is smooth on m_φv ≈ 10⁻³m_φ → depends on the halo through ρ_χ only.
- Angle-averaged response: eq:21.1-gammav, Γ(v) = (1/v)∫dω∫q dq/4π² Σ𝔇S̄ Θ(v − v_min); vΓ(v) nondecreasing; ℛ = (ρ/mρ_T)∫dv F_χΓ.
  Integration by parts → eq:21.1-etaform, ℛ = (ρ/mρ_T)∫du η(u) d[uΓ(u)]/du; d[uΓ]/du ≥ 0 is the response at v_min = u; = eq:A.8-rateiso.
- Velocity-dependent couplings (operators of tab:8-operators, polynomial in 𝐯_T; O₂ quadratic): orientation average kills odd powers; even powers via
  v_T² = v² − v_min²; eq:21.1-eta2 ∫f(v² − u²)Θ/v = η₂(u) = 2∫u′η (proved by differentiation); (v² − u²)ⁿ recursion → all fixed by η.
- Standard rate: density row + S̄⁰ → eq:10.1-rateiso → lim:10.1a (Essig Eqs. (3.13), (3.17)); η closed form D.1 = Essig App. B (B.4), (B.5).
- Exposure average vs fixed v_E^ref: ⟨δv_E⟩ = 1.61 km/s, ⟨δv_E²⟩ = 108.3 (km/s)², rms 10.4; second-order correction eq:21.1-annualcorr; convex near the
  edge → year average above the reference value.
- Limit Check 21.1a (lim:21.1a; fig:21-limit-eta): closed form at v_E^ref vs year average (7305 days, exact |v_E(t)|) vs no escape cut. Ratio 0.9965 (0),
  1.015 (500), 1.026 (600), 1.057 (700), 1.20 (760), 1.78 (780 km/s); 1 % at 430.3, 10 % at 735.6 km/s (brentq); second-order term within 1 % to
  786.3 km/s; year average positive to 810.4 km/s. Untruncated/truncated 1.18 (600), 1.90 (700), 6.8 (760).
- Year-averaged η raises the Si rate (heavy) by 0.8 % at 10 MeV and 4.3 % at 1 MeV.
- Fig. 21.1 (fig:21-kernel): Si kernel (heavy, σ̄_e = 1e-37) at 1/10/100 MeV with F_χ of SHM and debris flow. 10 MeV: v_on = 146.9 km/s (1.2 eV table
  onset), 6.29e4 (300), 3.31e5 (500), 7.71e5 /kg/yr (v_max); ∫F k = 1.599e5 = WE 10.1 (η form agrees to 5 digits). 1 MeV: opens near 470 km/s
  (472.5 on the table grid), rate 505 /kg/yr, from the fastest 19.6 % of the halo. 100 MeV: opens at 46.4 km/s; k(700)/k(300) = 3.7 (vs 9.9 at 10 MeV).
- Floors (sec:21.1-floor): m_min = 2E_th/v_max² is a floor, never an edge: (1) q-dependent onset (Si floors 0.3332 MeV at E_g(0) = 1.170 eV, 0.3290 MeV

  (3) the floor belongs to its halo: June v_max 810.4 km/s → floor −3.9 % (0.3202 MeV), a halo-band entry (A.8); a component lowers it only if faster
  than v_max; debris flow alone (≤ 590.5 km/s) gives no Si electron recoil below 0.6031 MeV.

   Γ_{N_eh}(𝐯) (lab, per particle, per unit time) and says in words that its exposure average is the single-stream rate. 21.2 should state the
   relation 𝔎_i(𝐯_gal) = M_det t_exp (ρ_χ/m_χρ_T)⟨Γ_i(𝐯_gal − 𝐯_E(t))⟩_t (or the reverse convention) once. NOTATION has no 𝔎 row yet; 𝐰 clashes
   (renamed 𝐮_rel in 5.5; C.17 uses 𝐰). I used 𝐯_gal (NOTATION row exists).

   Not checked against Gaia-era fits (Necib et al.), which the text does not cite.
