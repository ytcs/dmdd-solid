## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Normalization of the released table.** The tabulation's file convention is not stated in the paper. It was fixed by
   reproducing the released analysis notebook's printed rate (75276.69 events/kg/yr, its halo, 10 MeV, n_e = 1 bin) to all printed
   digits with my own η, which fixes |f_crys|²(q_i, E_m) = 2.0 eV·(w_k/4)·i·T_im/(0.1 eV) (the notebook's "dR/dE" is a rate per
   0.1 eV point). Checked against the f-sum rule (1.03 at q₀) and the book's EPM (0.76–0.83 of EPM S⁰ at q₀). The f-sum ratio is
   above 1 at small q (1.1–1.2); attributed in the text to the scissor shift (+Δ_scis∫S̄, ≈ 10 %), the rest unexplained (coarse k
   grid at small q, ultrasoft augmentation). Editor may prefer a QE-free cross-check against a second public table (QCDark,
   EXCEED-DM data) at the Part II review.
2. **"Eq. (17)" of 1509.01598** (SPINE 21.1): there is no Eq. (17) in the arXiv/JHEP numbering. The crystal rate is Eqs. (3.13),
   (3.17), boxed here; η(v_min) closed forms are Eqs. (B.4)–(B.5) (App. B). The 21.1 brief should cite those.
3. **Gap values.** Text gives 1.17 eV (0 K, Sect. 3.3) as the physical value and labels 1.11 eV as the tabulation's scissor
   (300 K). The ch10 opening says "at least the gap, 1.17 eV".
4. **Limit box (10.1a).** The box compares within one model (Cohen–Bergstresser EPM, model gap 0.82 eV, along [100], ω = 5 eV,
   heavy mediator, m_χ = 10 MeV): IP vs RPA head vs RPA + local fields. Result contradicts the F-map guess ("large q, where screened and unscreened agree"): at 5 eV the macroscopic screening fades above 2.2 q₀, but local fields hold S at ~0.55–0.6 of IP up to 2.8 q₀ (the model's limit; set-size uncertainty ~10 %: 0.58 vs 0.53 at 2.4 q₀ with the set enlarged by |q+G|² ≤ 11). Along [100] the points q = |G₂₀₀|, |G₄₀₀| (1.22, 2.45 q₀) have |q+G| ≈ 0.05 (2π/a) and give LF/IP 0.50/0.41 (set-dependent), a measure-zero Umklapp feature; the box uses (5,3,1). For the 10.2 writer: check the large-q local-field suppression with first-principles RPA + LF (EPM has no high-momentum tails, so its S⁰ falls ~40× from q₀ to 2.4 q₀, faster than the table). F row validity cell records the fallback. NK = 16 vs 12 at q₀: S⁰ 1 %, ratio identical.
5. **Directional vs averaged.** The box uses q along [100] in place of the angular average (named in the caption).
