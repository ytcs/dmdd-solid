## Open questions / for the editor
1. **WE 8.6 needs revision (editor-notes check).** With DFPT eigenvectors at q (we8_6_check.py), the LO nucleon vertex of GaAs is
   |a_N| = 0.2375 at 13.7 eV and 1.739 at 100 eV along the Ga→As bond (WE 8.6: 0.61 and 4.5, from zone-center eigenvectors with
   site phases), and 2.06e-3 / 0.061 along [100]. The energy-weighted optical share becomes 1.1e-5 (13.7 eV) and 5.8e-4 (100 eV)
   along [111] (WE 8.6: 7e-5, 4e-3). Mechanism: the site phases of the zone-center estimate are gauge (they depend on which As is
   assigned to the cell) and the eigenvectors at q compensate them; what survives is a real center-of-mass admixture of the LO mode
   from the internal strain of zincblende (shear ↔ sublattice displacement, the same coupling as the ionic piezoelectric charge of
   6.2), present where a longitudinal wave contains shear ([111]) and absent at first order along [100]. Check: a pure mass-density
   coupling (A_κ = M_κ) gives 0.237 / 1.737 along [111] and 1.1e-3 at 13.7 eV along [100] (we8_6_check.py). The q → 0 value 9.4e-4
   is confirmed (1.0e-3 at 0.02 eV). The qualitative statements of WE 8.6 (TO → LO pole shift, weight unchanged; optical
   suppression) stand; its finite-q numbers and "the phase term exceeds the mass term above q ≈ 0.02 eV" should be updated by the
   8.4 reviser. 11.2 states the corrected numbers in one paragraph after Limit 11.2a.
2. **Acoustic piezoelectric channel.** The rigid-Born-charge acoustic contribution is shown as an estimate only (dotted). A proper
   value needs dynamical quadrupoles (E.5); with no threshold it dominates the GaAs dark-photon rate just above the LO edge (it is open
   below it), so detector thresholds matter. Worth a sentence in 11.8 or 17.5, or a DEFERRED-COMPUTE line (quadrupoles of GaAs).
3. **Sapphire inputs.** Griffin v1 Table I ε0 (9.3 ⊥, 11.5 ∥) disagree with the LST product of its own Table II frequencies and
   ε∞ = 3.2 (9.78, 12.10) by 5 %. 11.2 uses the factorized model throughout (consistent per axis), and the F row says ε0, ε∞ must be
   of the same axis and model. Table I gives one ε∞ = 3.2 for both axes.
4. **LiF values are computed (DFT) values** of 1910.10716 Table I; no measured LiF set was fetched. The text labels them as computed.
5. ω̄ clash with the NOTATION ω̄ row (see Symbols).
6. Limit numbering: the four boxes print as Limits 11.4–11.7 after 11.1's three (auto counter). The text refers to them through

   only in the figure scripts and this file.
