## Open questions / for the editor
1. SPINE LO (iii) asks to bound the nonadiabatic correction at ≲ 2 meV (CW1 §25 own estimate, vertex 0.12 eV). The same-method
   calculation of Poncé et al. 2015 (1504.05992 Table VI: −64.3 adiabatic vs −56.2 nonadiabatic; VBM 40.87 vs 34.87 in the text) gives
   8.1 meV, as 6.3 and E.8 already print and as Zacharias–Giustino 2020 Sect. VII B quote. The CW1 errata only considered Miglio's
   method difference. 13.7 prints 8 meV as the nonadiabatic correction itself, says explicitly it is the two-form difference of one
   calculation, and explains why the ≲ 2 meV estimate is too small. The window bound ≤ 0.002 MeV stands (measured gap; k-dependence).
   SPINE/CW1-L9 F row and CORRECTIONS should be updated.
2. map13.py hard-codes Limit Check numbers; with lim:13.7a now present it should be rerun once Ch13 is complete.
3. The model in Limit Check 13.7a is a labelled model; a silicon ZG supercell series is not tabulated in the sources (only 4³ within
   10 % and 8³ = −57 meV).
