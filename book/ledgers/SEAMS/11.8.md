2. **E.26 relativistic number.** E.26 writes |2δ^a_rel| ≈ 0.01–0.02 for copper "(estimated)"; Pašteka et al. (now in book.bib)
   give −1.97e-2 (Table II). E.26 could cite it.
3. **Core-electron kinetic-energy row.** The 𝒯_e/m_e term of eq:8.1-scalar enters the phonon vertex at the same order in p_φ as n_e
   and, for the core electrons that ride with each nucleus, shifts each site by ~0.1 electrons; against the GaAs near-cancellation it
   may move d by ~10 % (estimate). Neither the source nor this section computes it. A DEFERRED-COMPUTE line (atomic ⟨T⟩ per species,
   scalar-relativistic) or a 10.7/E.17 note may be appropriate.
4. **Source statement on the GaAs figure.** The text attributes the rigid-ion structure to 2308.06314's Eqs. (40)–(41) and its
   N_Ga,e = 28, N_As,e = 36 sentence (text after Eq. (44)); it does not mention the released code. The 7–9 factor follows from those
   equations.
5. **Widths.** GaAs uses γ_ν = 1e-2 ω_ν (the source's own modelling choice); a measured low-T GaAs LO width (e.g. Lockwood et al.
   2005, already in book.bib) could replace it at review; only peak heights change (∝ 1/γ), not the line integral.
6. **Sapphire model sign.** The four-parameter factorized ε with γ_L > γ_T gives |L| < 1.2e-4 of the wrong sign below the lowest
   TO line; the figure is cut at 45 meV and the caption says so.
