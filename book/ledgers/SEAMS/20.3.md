## The reader now knows
- Acoustic mismatch model: welded plane interface, continuity of displacement and traction. Isotropic media from Voigt averages of the C.18 constants
  (shear (C11 − C12 + 3C44)/5, bulk B): Si c_LA 9.00, c_T 5.40 km/s (= C.18's code tensor, λ 52.4, μ 68.0 GPa); Al 6.41, 3.11 km/s; ρ 2.33, 2.70.
- Kinematics: conserved Q_∥ (eq:C.18-snell), Q_z (eq:20.3-Qz), evanescent waves carry no flux; L, SV, SH; SH decouples.
- SH in closed form, eq:20.3-SH (transverse impedance Z^T_ac = ρc_T; t = 0 beyond the critical angle). L–SV: the four boundary quantities
  eq:20.3-b, the 4×4 system, flux fractions eq:20.3-flux; sum to one within 3e-11 up to 89.9°.
- Si/Al numbers: normal incidence L 0.991, T 0.960. From Si: L > 0.98 to 70°, < 0.9 beyond 78°; L→SV 0.036 at 30°, max 0.19 near 76°; critical angles
  36.9° (reflected L for SV), 57.5° (SV → Al L). From Al: 20.2°, 29.0°, 35.2° (T totally reflected beyond), 45.4° (L loses Si L; 0.30 into Si T there).
- Frequency independence for lattice spacing ≪ λ ≪ d_f; absorbed phonons at 0.7–0.9 THz in the 60 nm films (1805.02495 Sect. V); λ(Al, 0.8 THz)
  8.0 nm L, 3.9 nm T; λ_L = 60 nm at 0.107 THz, close to 2Δ_BCS = 0.087 THz.
- Flux counting eq:20.3-count: d³Q v_z = dω d²Q_∥, every d²Q_∥ patch carries the same flux; angle weight 2 sinθ cosθ, branch weight c_ν⁻².
  Mean transmission t̄_12 and 𝔫_1 = c_LA⁻² + 2c_T⁻² (eq:20.3-avg).
- Reciprocity 𝔫_1 t̄_12 = 𝔫_2 t̄_21 (eq:20.3-recip, keyresult), derived from unitarity of the flux-normalized S-matrix at fixed (ω, Q_∥) (trace identity);
  holds for anisotropic and layered interfaces (𝔫 → area of the Q_∥ projection); detailed balance in equilibrium but population-independent.
- 𝔫_Si 0.0808, 𝔫_Al 0.231 (km/s)⁻² (ratio 0.350); t̄_Si→Al 0.934 (L 0.977, SV 0.892, SH 0.960; L/T 1.06), t̄_Al→Si 0.327 (L 0.606, SV 0.270, SH 0.318);
  residual 6e-16. Trapped: T beyond 35.2° = 0.669 of T flux = 0.598 of all film flux (T carries 0.895).
- DMM eq:20.3-dmm from t_12 = 1 − t_21 plus reciprocity: 0.741 / 0.259.
- Limit Check 20.3a: impedance law vs t̄ for an Al-like film family; within 10% only for speed ratio 1.175–1.273 (𝔫 equal at 1.204); leading correction
  t(0) min(1, 𝔫_out/𝔫_in) within 7.1%; for Al the law overstates escape ×3.03; slow-side estimate 0.347 vs 0.327.
- Film absorption: fixed-angle series eq:20.3-pabs-theta; randomized film eq:20.3-pabs with survival 2E_3(y_f), y_f = 2d_f/𝔩_f; thin limit 4d_f/(𝔩_f t̄_esc).
- Escape time t_l = 4d_f/(v_s t̄_esc), v_s = Σc⁻²/Σc⁻³ (DOS-weighted mean speed) (eq:20.3-tl, keyresult), four hypotheses (mixing, λ < d_f,
  no top loss, clean interface); P_abs ≃ t_l/t_pb, t_pb = 𝔩_f/v_s.
- Lumped form exp(−4d_f/𝔩_f) of App. E.12 = t̄_esc = 1 with the average in the exponent: within 10% for d_f/𝔩_f < 0.074, above by ≤ 14%; for Al/Si it
  understates thin-film absorption by 1/t̄_esc = 3.06.
- WE 20.3 (we:20-escape): Al v_s 3.29 km/s (Σc⁻³ 0.0702); t̄_esc 0.327 (AMM) / 0.259 (DMM); t_l 0.223 / 0.281 ns (×1.26); t₀ ≈ 0.26 ns near 2Δ
  (Kaplan et al. as quoted by Guruswamy et al. p. 2) → t_l/t₀ 0.86 / 1.08; at 𝔩_f = v_s t₀ = 0.855 µm, P_abs 0.467 (rate form 0.462).
- Fitted Si→Al 0.30–0.55 (1805.02495): the MC has no propagation in Al, so T_fit = t̄_Si→Al P_abs (eq:20.3-Tfit); P_abs 0.321–0.589 (AMM pair);
  𝔩_f/d_f 25.8–9.1 → 𝔩_f 1.55–0.54 µm, t_pb 0.47–0.17 ns (same order as t₀); specular 1.43–0.50 µm; DMM pair 1.36–0.36 µm; 𝔩_f/d_f ≈ 6–26 overall.
  Read as a transmission → t̄_esc 0.105–0.193 (below both models). Leman's ~33% (aSi/Al) is the same absorption type.
- Sensitivities (not bounds): Si [100]/[111] speeds → t̄_esc 0.291/0.360, t_l 0.251/0.203 ns, t̄_Si→Al 0.925–0.937; Al 0 K constants (Kamm–Alers
  via Malica–Dal Corso) → 6.63/3.30 km/s, t̄_esc 0.368, t_l 0.187 ns (−16%). Anisotropic 6×6 problem → App. E.25; reciprocity unchanged.
- Interlayers: a flat lossless layer keeps the critical angles and the 60% trapped flux (Q_∥ conservation); quarter-wave minimum
  t_min = 4Z1Z2Z3²/(Z1Z2 + Z3²)²; a-SiO₂ (ρ 2.2, ρc² 7.40e10 Pa from 16.7: 5.80 km/s, 12.8e6) → 0.855 vs 0.991 at 0.72 THz for 2 nm; 1 nm: 0.907 at
  0.8 THz. Diffuse layers release trapped modes (DMM limit). Working uncertainty: 0.259–0.327, ×1.26 in t_l.

## Notes for the editor
- E.25 prints the transverse critical angle as 34.9° and "some 60 %" trapped flux (research Al ρ 2720, K 76, G 26 GPa; Si from 1109.1193). With the book's
  one Al set (C.18) and Si Voigt the angle is 35.2° and the share 59.8 %; E.25's "34.9°" should become 35.2° at its next touch. The factor 1.26 agrees
  (log 1.261). E.25's "3 % to 12 %" for d/𝔩 0.01–0.2 is the difference relative to the lumped value (2.9/8.3/11.9 %); this section quotes it
  relative to the exact form (3.0/9.1/13.4 %, maximum 13.6 % at d/𝔩 = 0.24, printed as "up to 14 %"). Both are in amm20_3.log.
- Book Al elastic constants are room-temperature (Kittel, C.18). DATA-ADDENDA lists the 0 K Kamm–Alers set (114.3, 61.9, 31.6 GPa), which changes
  t̄_esc by +12 % and t_l by −16 %. The section uses the C.18 set (one Al set) and gives the 0 K numbers as a sensitivity; the editor may prefer to
  switch the book's Al set to the 0 K values for mK physics (then C.18's aluminum paragraph and this section change together).
- The research return's numbers (t̄ 0.934/0.323, DMM 0.743/0.257) are reproduced to ≤ 1.2 % with the book constants (0.934/0.327, 0.741/0.259).
- Swartz & Pohl (RMP 61, 605) is cited for the AMM/DMM models without an equation locator (not read; content derived in the text).
- Pair-breaking time 0.26 ns is the near-2Δ value; the absorbed phonons of the 60 nm films are at ~18Δ, so the comparison with the fitted t_pb is
  stated only as "the same order".
