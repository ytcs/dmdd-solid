## Open questions / for the editor
1. **8.4's Table (tab:8-screening) row "Transverse current, spin current, 𝒯_e … local fields (G ≠ 0); at G = 0 only through L–T
   mixing".** 10.4 says the spin current is unscreened (triplet, no SOC/magnetic order; D.30) and 𝒯_e is head-screened (scalar;
   consistent with 8.4's own mixing paragraph and E.16's 𝒯 subsection). Ch08 not edited (under review); the row should be split.
2. **Spine wording "in-medium spin–current ratio ≥ 12 at r_IP ≳ 20".** Written as physics: the 𝒪₈ rate exceeds the static-electron
   formula by ≥ (√r − 1)²; computed r_IP = 22–56 for 3–30 MeV (EPM), so the text quotes ≥ 16 (IP) and ≥ 29 (screened, δ ≥ −0.3)
   at 10 MeV rather than "12 at 20".
3. **Limit 10.4a validity relabel.** The planned row's "band within 10 % of the IP value" is replaced by "the formula within 10 % of
   every rate the band allows", (√r + 1)² ≤ 1.1, r ≤ 2.4e-3; the symmetric criterion 2√r/(1 + r) < 0.1 adds r > 398.
4. **𝔗 of the box.** 𝒳 = IP for the current and spin rows, RPA + LF for the density row, n_B = 0 (the plan's "𝒳 = IP, density row
   screened").
5. **Catena's W̄₅ attribution.** Their Sect. III B attributes nonzero W̄₅ (and Im W̄₂) in Si/Ge to approximate axial symmetry; under
   PT these vanish exactly for any Hamiltonian, so the text says small values measure numerical noise. Editor may want a softer
   wording or a citation audit.
6. **Model mixing in WE 10.4 and the box.** r_IP from the EPM; density screening 2.31 from the KKL GPAW tables. Stated in text and
   caption. The EPM's own density screening at (q0, 5 eV) (0.22) agrees in direction.
7. **EPM window.** Plane waves end at 2.8 q0 while 27 % of the real heavy-mediator density rate at 10 MeV is from q > 2q0 (10.1). The
   text argues r_IP is if anything low (impulse limit: Tr S/S_nn → ⟨p_T²⟩/m_e², which pseudo-wavefunctions underestimate). Continuity
   residual (|q j_L − ΔE ρ| / |ΔE ρ| over all pairs) 2–6 % for q ≤ 2.4 q0, 13–16 % at 2.8 q0 (plane-wave sphere edge).
8. **Pointwise cross block.** At fixed ω windows the EPM gives |S_njT| ≈ 3–6 % of √(S_nn Tr S_jj) even on [100], where it must vanish:
   the shifted k grid breaks the cubic symmetry. Only ω-integrated values (≤ 0.0015 on axes, ≤ 0.012 on (5,3,1)) are quoted.
9. **Symbol δ^j_LF** chosen to avoid δ_LF (13.9/E.16, phonon-assisted window). CW1's δ_LF^{jT}.
