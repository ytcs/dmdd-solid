## The reader now knows
- Plane-wave basis truncated at |k+G| ≤ k_cut, E_cut = k_cut²/2m_e (eq:3.5-pw); variational convergence; N_PW ∝ E_cut^{3/2} (2121 at
  60 Ry, E.1); FFT applies kinetic and potential terms where each is diagonal.
- Hydrogen-like 1s: momentum density (8/π²)p0⁵/(p²+p0²)⁴, p0 = Zαm_e (52 keV for Si); p⁻⁸ tail from the cusp; Si 3s nodes 0.08,
  0.38 Å, 3p node 0.38 Å (orthogonality); 1s alone needs ~1800 Ry (E.2).
- Vertex cutoff: pseudo vertex ≡ 0 for q > 2k_cut (58 keV at 60 Ry); since ω ≤ qv_max, final state low-lying with p_f ≈
  [2m_e(ω + E_F)]^{1/2}; k_cut ≳ q_max + p_f, E_cut ≳ (q_max + p_f)²/2m_e (eq:3.5-Ecut, derived).
- Phillips–Kleinman cancellation (orthogonality kinetic energy vs nuclear attraction) — why NFE/EPM work.
- Pseudopotential, core radius r_c, HSC conditions; norm conservation; Wronskian identity eq:3.5-logder (derived) ⇒ first-order
  scattering; generalized NC (Hamann 2013 Eq. 23); nonlocality from l-selective orthogonality, semilocal Σ V_l P_l → KB (E.2);
  consequences: v ≠ p/m (3.2), f-sum (4.3), vertex unaffected except missing core region.
- SG15 Si: r_c 1.27/1.88 Å, q_opt 19/13 keV, 60 Ry (computational connection → E.1, E.2).
- Fig. 3.9: TM potential at SG15 radii; same charge inside r_c (0.662, 0.767); AE/PS agree to 10 % below 2 keV (3s), 5 keV (3p);
  above 10 keV AE 3s keeps 7 % vs PS 0.14 %; at 30 keV AE/PS density ratio > 10⁴ (pseudo 3p tail below ~10⁻⁸ per keV is under the integration floor; only bounds quoted).
- PAW: partial waves φ^AE_j, φ^PS_j, projectors p̃_j, duality, completeness; eq:3.5-PAW; general one-center formula eq:3.5-PAWop
  (derived); keyresult all-electron vertex eq:3.5-rhoAE with Δ_κ,ij(q); small-q multipole reading (0th order = 0 for NC, augmentation
  for PAW; then q r_c, (q r_c)²); limits (frozen core, partial-wave span, no core states); Griffin: heavy-mediator rate raised even at
  ~10 eV thresholds.
- WE 3.8 (we:3-Ecut): q = 30 keV ⇒ m_χ ≥ 5.7 MeV, ω ≤ 79.5 eV, p_f = 9.7 keV, k_cut ≥ 39.7 keV, E_cut ≳ 1.54 keV = 113 Ry, ≈5500 PW
  (2.6 × 2121), ≈80 bands per spin; 60 Ry (28.9 keV) truncates; converged pseudo vertex misses the tail (< 10⁻⁷ vs 0.7 %, 0.4 %
  above 30 keV); Griffin 1 keV/2 keV (45 keV) fits the budget.
- Core shells: Si 2p 99.4 (2p1/2 99.8), 2s 150, 1s 1839 eV; radii 0.21/0.24/0.038 Å; median momenta 14 (2p), 37 keV (1s), valence 2–3.
  Kinematic openings (derived from eq:1.3-vmin): q ≥ ω/v_max for any mass, 2ω/v_max at the threshold mass: Si 2p 38 keV (76 keV at 29 MeV; 3 % of shell above 38 keV), 2s 57 keV (114 keV at 43 MeV), 1s > 0.5 GeV;
  Ge 3d 29 eV, 0.27 Å, 11 keV for any mass, 22 keV at 8.5 MeV (74 % above 11 keV) = semicore; frozen vs valence 3d (E.2); 3d dominate Ge heavy-mediator rate for
  m_χ ≳ 10 MeV (Griffin Sects. 1, 3.3); deeper shells as atomic Bloch sums (E.7). Core electrons outnumber valence above ~5 keV;
  at 30 keV Si 2p/valence ≈ 40 per keV.
- Relativistic: H_mv, H_Dar, H_SO derived by eliminating the small component + renormalization (eq:3.5-HBP; matches eq:C.15-HFW and
  the 4.3/D.6 forms with U_1); Darwin contact for −Zα/r; SO = eq:3.3-HSO with Thomas factor derived; ⟨v²⟩ = (Zα/n)²; 1s ⟨p⁴⟩ =

   4.3 and D.6 point here); App. C.15 derives them by Foldy–Wouthuysen and points the reader there for the vertex correction. Both
   are kept; 3.5 says C.15 reaches the same operators systematically.
2. **Symbol clashes.** D.6 writes H_mv, H_D, H_so; C.15 writes H_D for the Dirac Hamiltonian and H_Dar for the Darwin term. 3.5 uses
   H_mv, H_Dar and H_SO (3.3's symbol). Suggest D.6 rename H_D → H_Dar and H_so → H_SO. The Dirac components are written ψ_A, ψ_B as in C.15.
3. **(Zα)² wording vs E.7/C.15.** E.7 and C.15 say "(Zα)², about 1 % (Si 1s), 5 % (Ge 1s)" — correct as term size; 3.5 adds that the
   net binding shift is (Zα)²/4 (0.26/1.36 %; atomic check 0.27/1.46 %). No contradiction, but E.7 could add "(binding shift a quarter
   of that)".
4. **Si 2p doublet.** X-Ray Data Booklet 99.82/99.42 eV (0.40 eV) vs the Dirac–KS atom 0.65 eV (high-resolution photoemission is
   usually quoted near 0.6 eV, not fetched). The text says "a few tenths of an eV" and does not quote 0.40 as the splitting.
5. **HSC 1979 and Blöchl 1994 are closed access** (Unpaywall): cited without equation numbers. The generalized NC condition is cited to
   Hamann 2013 Eq. (23) (read).
6. **Fig. 3.9 pseudopotential** is a TM single-projector potential generated with ld1.x at the SG15 radii (ld1.x cannot read the SG15
   linear mesh: "mesh not supported"); caption says so. The SG15 tail above q_opt may differ in detail; the qualitative statement
   (orders of magnitude at 30 keV) is robust.
7. **p_f estimate** uses the free-electron E_F = 12.5 eV (4.2/4.5) as the valence bandwidth; the EPM width 12.6 eV of 3.3 agrees.
8. **Ge 3d dominance** stated per Griffin Fig. 2 (heavy mediator, m_χ ≳ 10 MeV); E.2's wording ("above about 30 eV") is compatible.
