## The reader now knows
- 2m_χv_max reaches k_c = 1.94 keV only for m_χ ≥ 0.366 MeV; gap favours q ≈ m_χv_max, valley needs q ≈ k_c.
- Zero-phonon threshold m_min^ZP = min_q q²/2[qv_max − E_min(q)] = joint min over (q, k, v, c) (eq:13.1-mZP); fixing the hole at the VBM or
  restricting q̂ gives upper values.
- Parabolic on-axis Si (M_eh = m*_∥ + m_hh = 1.19 m_e): 0.425 MeV at q = 1.46 keV (shortfall 0.48 keV, pair KE 0.19 eV, E = 1.36 eV);
  48³ PBE grid [100] 0.420, [110] 0.548, [111] 0.615 MeV. Joint minimum lower, off axis → 13.9. Bracket of WE 3.5 narrowed to ≈0.40–0.42.
  1s exciton lowers it by m E_x^b/(qv − E) = 0.0025 MeV (0.6 %).
- Phonon-assisted threshold E_min^PA(q) = E_g + min_ν ω_{k_c − q, ν} (eq:13.1-EPA), flat within 64 meV; pair relaxation < 0.02 meV.
- Emission vs absorption: n_B(14.8 meV) = 0.12 (77 K), 2e-19 (4 K), 3e-75 (1 K); absorption sideband (0.329 MeV) absent at mK.
- m_min^PA (eq:13.1-mPAdef) = 2[E_g + ω_ν(Q*)]/(v_max² − u_ν²), Q* = k_c − q*, q* ≈ 2(E_g + ω)/v_max (eq:13.1-mPA, derived; stationarity ⇒
  slope enters squared, u²/v² ~ 1e-5). Keyresult "Window kinematics": PA only for m_min^PA ≤ m_χ < m_min^ZP; bound pairs E_g → E_g − E_x^b.
- Si: TA wins, on axis (tilt 0.3 rad raises ω_TA by 0.7 meV); LA 0.341, TO 0.350 MeV. Dark matter needs a smaller phonon than light
  (Q* ≈ 0.46(2π/a), 14.8 meV vs 18.27 meV at k_c).
- WE 13.1 (we:13-onset): iteration 883 eV → Q* 0.463 → 14.86 meV → q* 894, Q* 0.458 (0.53 Å⁻¹), 14.79 meV; m_min^PA = 0.3374 MeV
  (1.3 % above 0.3332 floor); 2.8e-4 MeV per meV; neutron-scaled TA (16.1 meV) 0.3378; all-k_c phonon 0.3384; replica 0.3332 MeV
  (ω_TA ≈ E_x^b cancel), strength → 13.8.
- Ge: |ΓL| = 1.90 keV, floor 0.212 MeV at 0.56 keV; measured TA monotonic along Λ to 1.9 THz (7.9 meV) ⇒ onset 0.2119–0.2141 MeV.
  Zero-phonon: parabolic L-axis (M_eh = 2.06 m_e) 0.32 MeV, orientation only, not a bound (warping, SOC, PBE inverts Γ/L); open → 13.9.
- Direct gap (GaAs): no electron recoil below 2E_g/v² = 0.433 MeV; Γ pair at q* = 1.15 keV costs q*²/2M_eh = 3.1 or 1.3 eV;
  m_min^ZP(direct) = m_g M_eh/(M_eh − m_g) for M_eh > m_g (eq:13.1-mZPdir; m_g = 0.847 m_e: [100] hh never, [111] hh 3.7 MeV);
  PA onset 0.433–0.436 MeV; L-valley zero-phonon edge between 0.517 and 0.560 MeV. GaAs has its own window, above Si's.
- General criterion: window whenever |q* − k_c|²/2M_eh ≫ phonon energies; indirect gap adds position (lower floors) and a phonon-assisted
  optical edge (→ 13.5).
- What needs more than kinematics: joint ZP minimum with full bands (13.9), weak ZP vertices ⇒ window end is a rate ratio (13.3, 13.9),
  onset exponent (13.3), replicas (13.8), E_g(T) (13.7). Computational connection: E_min on joint space + ω_TA(Q*) by DFPT (5.4; App. E.14).
