## The reader now knows
- BSE as a Hermitian eigenproblem in the transition basis at finite q (keyresult eq:4.9-bse, kernels eq:4.9-kernels):
  H^exc = Δ + g_deg K^x − K^d, g_deg = 2 (singlet). K^x = exchange kernel WITHOUT its G = 0 term (for q beyond the zone: without
  the G_q term, unreduced indexing of 4.4); K^d from static W^scr_GG′(p = k′ − k, 0). = eq:D.12-eig with eq:D.12-crystal (D.12's
  bar dropped per the ruling; text never mentions the relabeling) and eq:E.6-Hexc at finite q.
- Physical reading: diagonal = quasiparticle pair energy (scissor with KS bands); K^d = screened e–h attraction, diagonal > 0,
  long-range tail e²/(4πε∞r); K^x = microscopic Hartree (local fields of 4.6), repulsive; G = 0 term = macroscopic field, handled by
  ε_mac (eq:D.12-epsmac), restored → full response (D.12); singlet g_deg K^x − K^d, triplet −K^d, dark to density coupling.
- Three approximations, each with its validity: kernel = δ(v_H + Σ^GW)/δG with δW/δG neglected (Hanke–Sham, Strinati); static rung,
  valid vs ω_p = 16.6 eV in Si (D-b promise kept); Tamm–Dancoff, error 2nd order in K/2E_g.
- Limits: no kernels → bubble eq:4.6-R0; K^d = 0 → H = D + g XXᵀ, Woodbury (eq:C.7-woodbury) → resonant part of eq:4.6-rpaLF
  ("RPA + LF = BSE without attraction", 4.6's promise fulfilled, and checked numerically to 1e-4 in fig4_11_data.py).
- Computational connection paragraph → App. E.6 (N_vN_cN_k, k grid bottleneck, ε^{-1}(p) for all grid differences, Haydock).
- Wannier–Mott: derivation from eq:4.9-bse (parabolic bands, ρ_cc ≈ ρ_vv ≈ 1, K^d → e²/(ε∞V|k−k′|²), short-range K^x dropped →
  e–h exchange splitting named), envelope F(r) = V^{-1/2}Σ A_k e^{ik·r}, Parseval normalization; hydrogenic equation
  eq:4.9-envelope; keyresult eq:4.9-WM: E_x^b = μ_xα²/(2ε∞²) = (μ_x/m_e)13.61 eV/ε∞², a_x = ε∞(m_e/μ_x)a₀, E_n = E_g − E_x^b/n²,
  F_1s, |F_ns(0)|² = 1/(πa_x³n³). Validity: a_x ≫ a; static screening needs E_x^b ≪ response energies; polar lattice: E_x^b ≪ ω_LO →
  ε₀ (5.5), ≫ → ε∞, in between not 1/r.
- Silicon: indirect exciton (electron at k_c, hole at Γ, momentum k_c); spherical estimate (electron (1/3)(1/m∥ + 2/m⊥) → 0.26 m_e;
  heavy hole 0.28–0.72) → 13–19 meV; measured 14.7 meV (Shaklee–Nahory) → μ_x = 0.148 m_e, a_x = 4.2 nm ≈ 7.7a; 1/a_x = 0.024 Å⁻¹,
  ~1 % of |G_111| = 2.0 Å⁻¹ → refined grids (E.6).
- WE (we:4-gaasexciton; spine WE 4.15): hole mass averaged over the two branches of eq:3.3-holes = m_e/γ₁ (exact mean of the ±
  roots); μ_x = 0.0457 m_e (m_e/μ_x = 21.9); ε∞ = 10.89 → 5.24 meV, a_x = 12.6 nm (22a); measured 4.2 meV (Sell); optical phonons
  ~35 meV = 8× binding → ions follow → ε₀ = 12.9 → 3.73 meV, 15.0 nm; remaining 0.5 meV from heavy–light mixing (zero 1st order,
  lowers in 2nd; Baldereschi–Lipari, attribution).
- Exciton dispersion eq:4.9-dispersion: E_n(q) = E_g − E_x^b/n² + (q − k_c)²/2(m* + m_h), derived by the relative/center-of-mass
  change of variables; same mass as 3.3's E_min near a valley (eq:3.3-Eminpar); Si exciton at q ≈ k_c: photon needs a phonon (6.5),
  dark matter at q ≈ k_c can create it directly.
- ε_mac from eigenpairs eq:4.9-epsmac (TDA with antiresonant mirror), ε₂ and static limit eq:4.9-eps2 (= eq:D.12-eps2 at q → 0;
  reduce to eq:4.6-eps2, eq:4.6-static without kernels). Oscillator strength |Σ A ρ*|² (coherent sum, as D.12); weight moves down,
  ε∞ rises; f-sum (4.3) fixes the total.
- Elliott form eq:4.9-elliott: |Σ A ρ*|² ≈ V|F(0)|²|ρ_cv(k₀,q)|²; only s states; n⁻³ strengths and n⁻² crowding join the continuum;
  Sommerfeld-type enhancement named (Elliott, attribution, no formula); finite step instead of (ω − E_g)^{1/2}.
- Fig. fig:4-bse (model): at 3.42 eV IP 15, RPA+LF 13 (as 4.6); BSE lower peak 34 at 3.36 eV (measured 36 at 3.42) — not a bound
  state, coherent mixing of near-parallel-band transitions (vertical transitions start ~3.1 eV); BSE upper peak 3.88 eV, 0.36 eV
  below measured 4.24: fitted EPM bands already contain the excitonic shift (double counting; GW energies fix it, Albrecht);
  ε∞ +17 % over RPA+LF in the same basis → 10.22 × 1.17 ≈ 11.9 vs 11.7, stated as an upper estimate (part of the rise is the same double counting); L(BSE)/L(RPA+LF) = 1.36 at 3 eV, within 3.5 % at 4–6 eV;
  K^d diagonal mean 81 meV; TDA error 3.4 %.
- Exciton S(q, ω) eq:4.9-S = (2πg/V)Σ|Σ A ρ*|²δ(ω − E_λ(q))/|ε_mac|², from eq:4.4-SL and L = ε₂/|ε_mac|²; long-range-exchange shift
  of strong lines (electronic LO–TO analog) and the equivalent full-kernel form eq:D.12-S; negligible at q ~ keV. Wannier–Mott lines
  in S: 2πg|ρ_cv(k₀,q)|²/(πa_x³n³|ε|²). Si: 14.7 meV = 1.3 % of the gap: small for m_min, but the zero-phonon edge starts with a
  line (→ 10.5, 13.1).
- Ladder completed (IP → RPA → LF → TDDFT → BSE), each rung a limit of the next; what remains outside (dynamical rung, coupling block,
  phonons → Chap. 6). Si spread: ε∞ 11.45 / 10.22 / 10.81 / ≈11.9 → 1/ε∞² varies by
