## The reader now knows
- Valence/conduction bands (subscripts v, c only); VBM, CBM; E_g = min ε_ck − max ε_vk (eq:3.3-Eg), indirect vs direct; vertical
  transition; E_dir = min_k(ε_ck − ε_vk) ≥ E_g (eq:3.3-Edir).
- Si: VBM at Γ, threefold (sixfold with spin), pure p (no s by symmetry); CBM at k_c = 0.85(2π/a) along ⟨100⟩ (eq:3.3-kc; Feher ENDOR),

  indirect by 0.15 eV (PBE inverts, E.1).
- GaAs: a = 5.653 Å (room temperature; Vurgaftman 2001), direct 1.52 eV (low T) / 1.42 eV (300 K), one isotropic valley, L and X valleys at 1.815 and 1.981 eV
  (+0.30, +0.46 eV); wider gap than Ge from the ionic part of the bond (3.2).

  Δ_SO (0.044, 0.296, 0.341 eV), m_hh, m_lh along [100]/[111] from Luttinger parameters.

- Model check: Si valley m_l, m_t = 0.872, 0.184 by finite differences and by the k·p sum over 113 bands (agree to 3 digits); dominant
  transverse term: the doubly degenerate valence level 3.73 eV below, whose two states together contribute 4.53 of m_e/m_⊥ = 5.43 (Σ|v^y|² = (4.1×10⁻³)²). Model inputs stated in the text: form factors −0.21, +0.04, +0.08 Ry on |G|² = 3, 8, 11 (2π/a)², τ = ±(a/8)(1,1,1), |G|² ≤ 20 (113 PW).

- Kane energy E_P = 2m_e|v^x_cX|²; two-level m_e/m* ≈ 1 + E_P/E_g (eq:3.3-kane2); model GaAs E_P 18.32 eV, gap 1.417 eV → 0.0718 vs curvature 0.0721;
  E_P 16–23 eV for B/Al/Ga/In pnictides (Gawarecki Table VIII); m* ∝ gap; DFT gap → too light; |v_cX| = 4.7×10⁻³ = 1.8 v_max →
  DM beyond optical limit (eq:3.2-validity).

  4.5×10⁻⁵ eV = 0.365 cm⁻¹; ionic ²P splittings Si⁺ 35.6 meV, Ge⁺ 219 meV, As²⁺ 0.365 eV, Ga 0.102 eV (NIST ASD).
- Crystal: VBM p triplet → ξ_eff L·S, Δ_SO = (3/2)Σ w_κ ξ_SO,κ (eq:3.3-DSO); j = 3/2 fourfold up, j = 1/2 down by Δ_SO; agreement with
  ions to ~30 % (Si 0.044 vs 0.036, Ge 0.296 vs 0.219; ratios 6.7 vs 6.2); Rideau G0W0 0.050/0.312; GaAs As-dominated.
- Gap lowered by Δ_SO/3: 15 meV (Si, ~1 %), 0.10 eV (Ge, 13 %).

  E_g = 0.5 eV, 0.076 at 1.77 eV (cf. QSGW 0.077, E.3).
- Holes: degenerate k·p → Luttinger form (eq:3.3-holes) with γ^Lu_1..3; heavy/light holes, split-off band; [100]/[111] masses; warping
  (Si hh 0.28 vs 0.72).
- Keyresult E_min(q) (eq:3.3-Emin): periodic in G, depends on q_r only (the transition strength does not), E_min(0) = E_dir, min E_g at
  q = k_c (+images, +G). Parabolic form E_g + (q − k_c)²/2(m_∥ + m_h) (eq:3.3-Eminpar, derived); pair mass 1.2 m_e; +0.1 eV within
  0.35 keV. Fig. 3.6(b): along [100] near E_g from k_c to |G_200| − k_c = 2.63 keV (Umklapp to −k_c), back to E_dir at 4.57 keV;
  [110] lowest 1.26 eV near 3.4 keV; [111] 2.2 eV.
- Kinematic condition E_min(q) ≤ qv_max − q²/2m_χ (eq:3.3-allowed); m_min = 2E_g/v² assumes absorption at q = m_χv_max — not so in Si.
- Photons (q = ω = 6×10⁻⁴ k_c) need E_dir, or a phonon between E_g and E_dir (→ 2.4, 6.5); absorbed bosons the same; scattering DM
  reaches k_c above a few hundred keV; just above m_min it needs a phonon: the phonon-assisted window (qualitative; → 13.1, 13.3, 13.9).
- WE 3.5 (we:3-kc): k_c = 1.94 keV = 0.98 Å⁻¹; 1 MeV window at 1.17 eV: 0.49–4.81 keV (1.3: 0.46–4.84 at 1.11 eV); ω_max(k_c) = 3.26 eV;
  2m_χv_max = 5.30 keV covers nearest images; lightest mass depositing E_g at exactly k_c: 0.474 MeV; m_min = 0.333 MeV at q = 0.88 keV

- WE 3.6 (we:3-mmin): v_max² = 7.023×10⁻⁶; Si direct 0.968 MeV (q 2.57 keV), Ge direct 0.253 MeV (0.67 keV), Ge indirect 0.212 MeV
  (0.56 keV); direct gap irrelevant for scattering threshold (E_min < 3.4 eV in all directions at 2.57 keV); Ge |ΓL| = 1.90 keV (a = 5.658);
  depositing 0.744 eV at exactly |ΓL| needs 0.420 MeV (counterpart of Si 0.474): Ge zero-phonon threshold between 0.212 and 0.420 MeV
  (→ 13.1 joint (q,k) minimization); Γ route: 0.253 MeV is a flat-band floor, pair (≈ ½ m_e) recoil ≈ 0.8 eV at 0.67 keV, opens only
  at a few MeV (parabolic).

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Ge Δ_SO.** Fixed here as 0.296 eV (Rideau Table II, measured). App. E.3 prints 0.29 eV (its Δ_SO/3 = 0.10 eV is unchanged). Suggest
   E.3 write 0.30 or 0.296 at its next touch.
2. **Lattice constants.** Si 5.431 Å is room temperature (CODATA, as 3.1); Ge 5.658 Å and GaAs 5.648 Å are the zero-temperature
   extrapolations of Lucero Table II (the only fetched public source); room-temperature GaAs is 5.653 Å (Vurgaftman), a 0.1 % difference.
   tab:E-functionals' "measured" column (5.416/5.640/5.638) is ZPAE-corrected, a convention value; no contradiction, but the two tables
   should not be read side by side without that caption.
3. **GaAs Γ mass.** Fixed as 0.067 m_e (Vurgaftman); App. E.3 quotes 0.065 from van Schilfgaarde et al. (their "experiment" value,
   likely polaron-corrected). Suggest E.3 say so or switch to 0.067.
4. **κ for k − k₀.** 3.3 uses bold 𝛋 for the distance from an extremum (standard in k·p). Bare κ is the basis-site index (3.1), and
   eq:3.3-DSO uses κ as a site index in the same subsection (Σ_κ w_κ ξ_SO,κ). The bold/subscript use keeps them apart, but the editor
   may prefer another letter (e.g. 𝐩̃ is taken; 𝛅𝐤?).
5. **Luttinger parameters γ^Lu_1..3.** γ₁…γ₄ are single-particle state labels (2.3); the superscript Lu is proposed to separate them.
6. **Not fetched (credited from standard knowledge, DOIs checked):** Vurgaftman et al. 2001 (GaAs m*, Δ_SO, L/X valleys, Luttinger
   parameters; no table number cited), Cohen–Bergstresser 1966 (form factors; widely reproduced values, verified only through the
   model's agreement with known CB results: Si Γ₁₅ 3.42 eV, valence width 12.6 eV), Feher 1959 (k_c = 0.85), Luttinger 1956 and Kane
   1957 (formulas; eq:3.3-kaneSO is derived in the text, eq:3.3-holes is cited without equation number), Li 1980 (n = 3.42 → ε∞ = 11.7).
   The brief's "Yu and Cardona Ch. 2 tables" could not be checked (no full text); Rideau et al. (full text read) supplies the Si and Ge
   values instead, and Yu–Cardona is cited by chapter only for k·p and the Luttinger form.
7. **ε∞ = 11.7.** Placed in the computational-connection paragraph; Poncé et al. 2018 (in sources/E-b) quote the static 11.94. 4.4 should
   use 11.7 as the electronic (high-frequency) value with the same source, or the editor may choose another.
8. **Band model in figures.** The local EPM gives Si E_g 0.82 eV (measured 1.17), Ge L 0.96 eV with Δ below Γ (measured order L < Γ < Δ),
   GaAs 1.42 eV. Captions give model next to measured values; panel (b) of Fig. 3.6 uses a +0.35 eV scissor. A nonlocal EPM with SOC
   (Chelikowsky–Cohen 1976) or the E.1 PBE calculation with a scissor would be closer; swapping is a script change only.
10. **𝐋, 𝐒, j, l, a₀.** 3.3 writes the orbital and spin angular momenta as bold 𝐋 and 𝐒 (𝐋_κ for atom κ). Bold 𝐒 collides with the
   planned ionic spins 𝐒_j (7.1) and |𝐒| (14.1); 𝐋 with upright L (zone point) and italic L (loss function). The vector font and the
   context separate them here; the editor may prefer ℓ/𝐬 or a clash note. j is also the tight-binding orbital index of 3.2 (local there).
9. **Phonon-assisted window** is kept qualitative; no m_min^ZP or m_min^PA symbol is introduced (13.1 owns them). 2.4 already explains the
   mechanism, so 3.3 points back to it.
