## The reader now knows
- Photon spectrum of a suddenly moved charge in a medium, eq:12.6-classical = n_r (2α/3π)(e_c²/e²)v²/ω_γ (C.16 recap).
- Atomic bremsstrahlung (Kouvaris–Pradler Eqs. (3), (8)): Z*_at(ω) ≡ f(ω) = −(m_eω²/e²)α_pol(ω) (eq:12.6-atom, HL); ∝ ω² at low ω;
  Si atom Z*_at(1 eV) = −0.050 (α_pol = 37.3 a.u.).
- Dynamical Born charge Z*_κ(ω) (dipole per displacement of one nucleus at ω, others at rest, zero macroscopic field); coupling
  eq:12.6-Hgamma. Amplitude by the double commutator [[H,D],K]: lattice potential drops out; validity ω_γ ≫ ω_ph (u changes phonon
  number by one), soft photon, wavelength ≫ a, transparent medium, q above the zone (incoherent).
- Keyresult eq:12.6-brems (rate, any lattice regime). Reduces to KP Eq. (8) (gas) and Eq. (3) (bare charge).
- Translation sum rule at frequency ω, eq:12.6-sum: Σ_κ Z*_κ(ω) = −(Ωm_eω²/e²)[ε(ω)−1]; limits 0 (ω→0), Σ Z_κ (ω→∞), dilute → atom.
  Si/Ge exact: eq:12.6-ZSi Z*(ω) = −(Ωm_eω²/2e²)[ε−1] = −(N_v/2)(ε−1)ω²/(ω_p^val)². Si: C_κ = 0.155 eV⁻², Z*(1 eV) = −0.165 (ε = 12.37).
  Rate ∝ ω_γ⁴ relative to a fixed charge. Crystal/atom per photon: Si 33–38 (0.3–1 eV), Ge 86–96 (0.3–0.65 eV); solid polarizability per
  atom 228 Å³ vs atom 69.5 Å³ (HL) at 1 eV.
- GaAs: Z*(0) = 2.11; sum rule fixes symmetric dispersion; antisymmetric part bounded by E.17 (0.9(ω/eV)²) → band.
- Numbers (σ_n = 1e-38 cm², heavy mediator, free recoil): photons per 100-eV recoil Si 4.0e-13 (0.1–1.1 eV), GaAs 1.7e-10 (0.1–1.27 eV);
  rates at 100 MeV: GaAs 9e-7, Si 2e-9 /kg/yr vs Si Migdal 30 (12.3). Median GaAs photon 0.36 eV. Bremsstrahlung = check, not channel.
- WE 12.6 (we:12-gaas): Ω m_e/e² = 3.249e-2 eV⁻², n_r(1 eV) = 3.4185, ε_el = 11.688, ΣZ* = −0.347, Z*_Ga = 1.936, Z*_As = −2.284;
  f_Ga = −0.0675, f_As = −0.0405; per-photon ratio 4.94e3 at 1 eV; band integrals 75.4 vs 4.03e-3 → 1.87e4 (rate 1.88e4 at 100 MeV);
  Ga at E_R = 100 eV: 1.7e-10 photons; band −19 %/+25 %.
- Migdal-type absorption: atom = 12.2's boost at k (k_e = m_e k/m_A); acceleration form eq:12.6-accel (exact, all BO orders);
  mass-proportional coupling → −c̃ m_e n_e(k) (eq:12.6-mass; equivalence principle; electronic analog of 11.8 cancellation);
  keyresult eq:12.6-absrate Γ = c̃²m_e²p_φ²L/(e²m_φ), R = (ρ_χ/ρ_T)c̃²m_e²⟨v²⟩L/e² (dark-photon spectrum with ζ² → c̃²m_e²⟨v²⟩/e²);
  ⟨v²⟩^{1/2} = 379 km/s. Dilute limit = Dzuba Eqs. (14), (17). Medium factor n_r/|ε|² = 1 + (3/2)ω_p²/ω² (eq:12.6-medium); Si 1 keV:
  1 + 1.2e-3 (ω_p = 28.8 eV, 12 e/atom). Limit 12.6a: within 10 % for m_φ ≥ 57 eV; atomic formula ~400× high at 4.2 eV, 7.7× low at 17 eV.
