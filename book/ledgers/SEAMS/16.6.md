## The reader now knows
- A color center is not a prompt final state: the class is the recoil (κ, ω, q̂) of 8.7; the center rate is eq:8.7-handoff with
  P_casc → Y_CC(Λ); seeding → 18.3, stored energy → 19.4; q̂ must stay inside the class sum (direction-dependent yield).
- eq:16.6-yield: Y_CC = p_FP(E_R, r̂_R) p_surv p_q f_opt (definitions; p_FP's lowest crossing is E_d(r̂) of eq:7.4-Ed; F center needs an
  anion displaced, directly or by a struck cation).
- Seed is impulsive (x_R several hundred), coherence corrections negligible at q ~ MeV; eq:16.6-ccrate: rate per unit mass with the
  impulse Gaussian, η(ω/q + q/2m_χ), orientation-averaged yield, (M_κ/M_cell) σ_N,κ/(2 m_χ μ_χκ²). Limit σ_ω → 0, step yield → Budnik
  Eq. (1) (their coherence factor and σ̄_n mapped to σ_N/μ²); their threshold surface Eq. (2), a = 0.4; their caveats (dislocated nucleus,
  immediate electron occupation).
- Doppler width in LiF (F site, ⟨ω⟩_F bracketed 33.5–77.2 meV by the Griffin optical range): σ_ω = 0.95–1.44 eV at 27 eV (3.5–5.4 %);
  zero-point momentum 17–26 keV = 4.6–7.0 αm_e vs Budnik's αm_e; Δp/q_min = 0.018–0.027 (q_min = 978 keV).
- Electronic route: STE → F–H (E.21); yield ⟨N_X⟩ p_FH p_surv f_opt; threshold ≤ gap 14.2 eV → m_χ ≥ 4.0 MeV (46× below 0.186 GeV);
  γ/neutron response 1/50 per dose (Araujo).
- LiF facts: F band ≈ 5.1 eV, no observed F luminescence; F₂⁰ 650 nm, F₃⁺ 525 nm, excited at 450 nm; aggregation time constants 6.5/9 h;
  1200 vacancies/μm in neutron-recoil tracks (TRIM), one aggregate per 2–3 vacancies; stable months, anneal 350 °C.
- Fig. 16.6: m_min for F recoil 0.186 GeV at 27 eV (0.113 at 10, 0.255 at 50); Li→F head-on fraction 0.788, 0.129 GeV; Li own 0.114 GeV
  (cation vacancy); exciton route 4.0 MeV; endpoints 31/188/711 eV at 0.2/0.5/1.0 GeV.
- eq:16.6-lindhard (Lindhard partition, single-element form, Agnese et al. Eq. (3); derived in 19.3) and the Kinchin–Pease count (as quoted
  by Cogswell). WE 16.6 (we:16-fyield): 1 keV F recoil: k 0.132, ε 0.0683, g 2.21, y_L 0.226, 226 eV electronic, 774 eV damage, 14.3
  displacements (18.5 with no loss); F vacancies ≤ 14.3 (7.2 equal share); ≤ 16 pairs. 0.5 GeV: 188 eV, 155 eV damage, 2.9 displacements,
  ≤ 2.3 pairs; 0.3 GeV: 69 eV, ≈ 1, ≤ 0.7 pairs; 0.2 GeV: 31 eV (T_d = E_d only without loss). Near threshold fewer electrons than vacancies
  → p_q decides; vacancies immobile at mK → single centers.
- eq:16.6-conv (keyresult): S_11 = S_CM ∗ S_int(q_12), q_12 = (μ12/M1)q, under the free-rotor hypothesis; dissociation = S_int above
  E_bond; hypothesis fails for fragments in the cage (absorbed in p_esc).
- eq:16.6-qmin (= Essig (3.3)); H₂: E_bond 4.75 eV, q_min 134 keV, m_min 25.9 MeV, ω_vib 0.546 eV (Huber–Herzberg), E_bond/ω_vib 8.7.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Equation numbering of 1608.02940.** SPINE cites the journal numbering (4), (12), (23), (25); the text cites the arXiv numbering read
   here, (3.3), (3.11), (4.3), (4.5) (same equations). One scheme per source is kept (arXiv).
2. **LiF inputs are model values.** E_d = 27 eV is the TRIM input of Cogswell et al. (not a measured or MD threshold); ⟨ω⟩_F is bracketed
   by the computed LiF optical range (Griffin Table I) rather than an F-projected DOS. The Doppler correction in the box is therefore a
   labelled bracket. A direction-resolved E_d(r̂) of LiF would replace the a = 0.4 angular model (DEFERRED: DC-73 already lists it).
3. **WE uses Lindhard below its range** (single-element form for F in LiF, sub-keV); stated as a scale estimate in the text. The
   sublattice share of Kinchin–Pease displacements is not computed (upper value and an equal-share assumption are both printed).
4. **⟨ω⟩_CM = 10 meV for solid H₂ is an assumed value** (labelled) used only to show the CM Doppler shift is negligible (2e-6 in v_min).
5. **E.21 uses m_1** for the struck-atom mass; 16.6 uses M_1 (m₁ is the first moment of 4.7 in NOTATION). Suggest E.21 → M_1.
6. **Budnik et al.'s author spelling**: Crossref "Cheshnovsky"; the arXiv PDF prints "Chesnovsky". The book follows Crossref.
7. **Huber–Herzberg values were read from the NIST WebBook compilation**, not the book itself; the citation note says so.
8. "Cage effect of photochemistry" is named without a citation (textbook term); add Franck–Rabinowitch at the citation audit if wanted.
