## The reader now knows
- At q → 0 a metal absorbs only with a third body: boson momentum ~1 μeV at 1 meV vs ω/v_F = 0.15 eV for an electron.
- Nambu structure of uniform operators (eq:15.4-Vnambu, eq:D.40-O, eq:D.40-bogo): an odd weight with equal spin factors (current)
  → unit matrix; with opposite spin factors (spin current) → η. The density (even weight) is η, the spin density the unit matrix.
- Current commutes with H_MF: clean BCS absorbs nothing at q → 0, ω > 0; intraband weight (π/2)(ω_p^intra)² at ω = 0 (supercurrent).
  ω_p^intra² = 2e²𝒩_F⟨(ê·v)²⟩_FS (eq:15.4-wp); Al 12.29 eV computed (Savrasov Table IV), free-electron 15.78 eV.
- Impurity-assisted (Mattis–Bardeen, keyresult eq:15.4-MB): exact impurity states, Anderson pairing (15.3), current T-odd → unit
  Nambu → case II; dirty limit γ_rel ≫ Δ → σ₁^MB = σ_n 𝔤_II(ω/Δ), σ_n = σ_dc γ_rel²/(ω² + γ_rel²); 𝔤_II closed form (eq:D.40-g),
  0.67 at 4Δ, within 10 % of 1 from 8.1Δ; no pair-field ladder (odd v vs even pair vertices). 50 nm film (15.3 WE): σ_dc = 9.46e3 eV
  (0.79 μΩ cm), σ₁^MB(1 meV) = 7.65e3 eV = 1.4e6 × phonon-assisted.
- Phonon-assisted amplitude (keyresult eq:15.4-T, derived exactly in eq:D.40-T): T = −[ê·(v_{k+Q} − v_k)/ω] N^{-1/2} g (u′v + v′u);
  no off-shell assumption; case-I factor because the phonon vertex is T-even (g(−k−Q,Q) = g(k,Q)). "The third body decides":
  phonon → density factor (case I), impurity → current factor (case II). Density analogue vanishes (eq:13.2-q0).
- α²F_tr definition (eq:15.4-a2Ftr), λ_tr; keyresult eq:15.4-holstein: σ₁^S with 𝔤_I and bound ω′ ≤ ω − 2Δ; σ₁^N = (ω_p²/ω²)γ_H (Allen).
  Debye α²F_tr = 2λ_tr ω′⁴/ω_D⁴ → HLZ Eqs. (16), (17). Ladder dressing (Δ/v_FQ)² = 2.5e-4 at 2.2Δ, 8e-7 at 1 meV.
- Limit Check 15.4a (lim:15.4a): σ₁^S/σ₁^N vs ω/Δ: within 10 % of the normal formula from 11.4Δ = 2.05 meV (ω′⁴), 7.4Δ (ω′²);
  crosses 1 at 16.2Δ, max 1.047 at 31Δ; case-II factor would need 54Δ; onset ∝ (ω − 2Δ)⁵ (0.2 % at 2.05Δ, 2.1 % at 2.5Δ).
- WE 15.4 (we:15-holstein), 1 meV: γ_H = 8.72e-11 eV, σ₁^N = 0.0132 eV, ratio 0.421, σ₁^S = 5.54e-3 eV; ε₁ = −1.51e8 (clean
  condensate σ₂ = ω_p²/ω), 1/|ε|² = 4.4e-17, L = 2.43e-16; R = 1.29e18 ζ²/kg/yr, ζ(3 events) = 1.5e-9 (normal formula 9.9e-10).
  Phonon momenta ≤ 30.5 (LA) / 59.4 (TA) eV ≪ k_F = 3.45 keV.
- Spin current (keyresult eq:15.4-sJ; definition eq:15.4-sJdef, σ₁^𝒥 = (2e²/3ω)S_𝒥𝒥 so that eq:10.7-alp holds with σ₁ → σ₁^𝒥):
  𝒥^{zz} = ½Σv^zΨ†ηΨ creates pairs at q = 0 with amplitude ½v^zΔ/E; clean σ₁^𝒥 = 2π(ω_p^intra)²Δ²/[ω²(ω² − 4Δ²)^{1/2}],
  integral (π/2)ω_p² (the whole normal spin "Drude" weight; 87 % below 4Δ, 99.5 % below 20Δ). Dirty limit σ_n 𝔤_I (weight
  conserved to 0.05 %). 1 meV: 3.30e4 eV = 6e6 × σ₁^S; above σ₁^N up to 11.5 meV. ALP at 0.72 meV: 0.11 events/kg/yr at
  g_aee = 1e-13; g(3 events) = 5.1e-13 vs 5.6e-9 with σ₁^S (published projections: HLZ Eq. (22), Mitridate Sect. V).
- Strong coupling: pair weight N^El N^El′ + P^El P^El′ (sketch); surviving renormalization factors open; 1/(1+λ_tr) = 0.72 per
  current eigenvalue would give 0.52 in the rate (E.20 computes with and without).
- Computational connection: α²F_tr from DFPT/Wannier (E.5, E.9), σ₁ spec in E.20. Fig. 6.6 model: λ_tr = 0.32 (λ_ep 0.44);
  its low-frequency tail is 1.25e4 × the ω′⁴ weight below 1 meV, all umklapp near |G₁₁₁| (plane-wave vertex violates eq:6.2-asr).
- Scalar: kinetic row is η-type, even weight, couples to the amplitude channel; size near the edge open.

## Open questions for the editor
1. DERIVATION-REVIEW PRIORITY (new derived statement, no novelty wording in the text): the uniform spin current of a singlet BCS
   superconductor is η-type in Nambu space and breaks pairs at q = 0 (eq:15.4-sJ). Checks done: Bogoliubov algebra by hand;
   the ω-integral equals (π/2)ω_p² exactly (the normal spin Drude weight), and the dirty-limit case-I form conserves the
   normal-state weight to 0.05 %; the static paramagnetic spin-current response then cancels the diamagnetic one (no spin
   supercurrent), which is the known statement for singlet superconductors. Published Al ALP projections (HLZ Eq. (22);
   Mitridate et al. Sect. V, their Eq. (71) with Π_{1,1}) use the phonon-assisted σ₁ and contain no direct term. If confirmed, the
   ALP reach of clean Al near 2Δ improves by ~10⁴ in g_aee (5.1e-13 vs 5.6e-9 at 0.72 meV). Consider a NOVELTY.md check.

   diagonal); the text says its size is open and that the direct term dominates below ~10 meV. Not listed in SPINE's open problems.
