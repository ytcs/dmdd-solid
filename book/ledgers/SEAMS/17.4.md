## The reader now knows
- Cross terms enter the rate as 2Re Σ_κ 𝔇_eκ S_eκ with 𝔇_eκ = Ṽ_e*Ṽ_κ; zero for electron-only or nucleon-only couplings (why 17.1
  compared cells one by one). Dark photon: Ṽ_e = Ṽ, Ṽ_κ = −Z_κṼ.
- Electron-following cell at long wavelength: nucleus Z_κ, cloud F_κ = q̂·(Z_κ1 − Z*_κ)·q̂; rate terms Z² : F² : −2ZF, sum Z*².
  Si: F = Z = 14, cross −392 vs 196 + 196, exact cancellation. GaAs (|Z*| = 2.11): F = 28.89 (Ga), 35.11 (As); cross removes 99.75 %,
  99.81 %; without it the optical-phonon rate would be 216× (Ga), 245× (As) larger.
- Electronic cells carry the negative first moment of eq:8.6-fsum-ek: m_e/M_κ of the electron f-sum (1.95e-5 in Si).
- Zero-phonon cell from eq:8.3-boostward: dark-photon amplitude Ṽ(1 + Z m_e/M)⟨f|n_e|0⟩; rate fraction (1 + Zm_e/M)² − 1 =
  5.47e-4 (Si); cross 2Zm_e/M, Migdal cell (Zm_e/M)² = 7.5e-8. Inertial picture (as behind eq:12.6-sum). Called the ionic correction
  of the zero-phonon line; contained in the total-charge loss function (8.4), never added to it.
- Polyatomic: only the mass-weighted sum is fixed; Ṽ_κ = λM_κ + δṼ_κ (as 8.3). GaAs dark photon: Z/M = 0.4446 (Ga), 0.4405 (As)
  per u (0.9 %); 2m_e z̄ = 4.83e-4 … 4.88e-4 for positive sublattice weights.
- One-phonon nuclear amplitude (eq:17.4-Mmig) from eq:12.1-amp × one-phonon term of the lattice factor with eq:A.6-u; electrons get
  p_G = q + Q + G, lattice keeps −Q − G; factor i, 1/(ΩM_κω_e²(2M_κω_Qν)^{1/2}).
- Keyresult eq:17.4-Sek: S^+_eκ(q;ω_l,ω_e) = (4π/Ω)⟨⟨Σ(n + ½ ± ½)M^{±*}M^{Mig,±}_κ δ(ω_e − ε_ck′ + ε_vk)δ(ω_l ∓ ω_Qν)⟩⟩; phase-
  convention free; one set of bands/phonons; n_B = 1 and first order in g. Frame caveat (crystal vs ion frame changes the one-phonon
  factor; 12.1/12.4's ×2.4) and the G-sum must be kept whole (diagonal approximation of 12.3; App. E.18). Inside the window labels
  unique to O(θ_ep); Dyson convention near resonances, only the sum of the electronic cells convention-free.
- Vanishing: 𝔇_eκ = 0; phonon-number mismatch (zero-phonon Migdal, ≥ 2-phonon Migdal at this order; 96 % of phonon-emitting
  Migdal events at 5 keV are one-phonon, 12.4); spin couplings (triplet, 10.4); q → 0 (both amplitudes linear in q, ratio finite).
- Positivity: |S^+_eκ|² ≤ S^PA S^+_κκ pointwise; rate bound eq:17.4-bound; sector-restricted only inside the window or on bins wider
  than the detuning; otherwise apply to the two electronic sectors together.
- Keyresult eq:17.4-lock / eq:17.4-rate: monatomic, every one-phonon pair state, Σ_κ M^Mig = −(m_e/M)M^±[1 + O(qa)] → rank one, bound
  saturated; rate |Ṽ_e − (m_e/M)Ṽ_nuc|² S^PA. One-phonon Migdal below the zone = phonon-assisted recoil × (m_e/M)² = 3.8e-10 (Si).
  Dark photon +5.47e-4 (Si); mass-proportional coupling: zero (lower edge; soft limit eq:8.3-soft); Ṽ_nuc = −(M/m_e)Ṽ_e: upper edge (4×).
- Validity: remainder O(q) from the momentum flux, no small coefficient; scattering needs q ≥ E_g/v_max = 0.19 (Si), 0.26 (GaAs) of
  2π/a; Si window 0.39–0.57 of 2π/a, GaAs q* = 0.52 → order of magnitude only at the window. Exact in the absorption limit (consistent
  with 12.6's mass-proportional scalar → loss function; App. E.18 remainder).
- Fig. 17.4 (fig:17-interference): (q, ω) plane for Si and GaAs: zero-phonon edges (parabolic models), window, edge shell, PA
  threshold, kinematic curves, q_BZ (Si 2.25, GaAs 2.16 keV), top axis q/(2π/a).
- WE 17.4 (we:17-gaas-interference): heavy dark photon in GaAs, the three cells: 216/245 suppression (electron-following);
  4.83–4.88e-4 (zero-phonon, above the L opening 0.52–0.56 MeV); same fraction at leading order in the window (Migdal cell 5.9e-8),
  uncertain by O(1) at q* = 1.15 keV; screening cancels; verdict: only the electron-following cell matters for a dark photon.
- Closing: cross term competes only for |Ṽ_nuc/Ṽ_e| ~ M/m_e = 5.1e4 (Si), per nucleon ~ m_u/m_e = 1823; nearly mass-proportional
  couplings → electronic sectors see Ṽ_e − (m_e/M)Ṽ_nuc.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. Possible tension (not stated in the text): eq:8.3-boostward forces, at q ≪ 2π/a, the summed nuclear amplitude into every
   electronic final state (zero- and one-phonon) to be −(m_e/M) times the electron amplitude, so the coherent small-q Migdal response
   is (m_e/M)²S_ee in spectrum and size. 12.4 applies the diagonal (G = G′) soft-limit probability, eq:12.3-soft, at every q (its
   sum rule, down to q = 2 keV in the regime table). An order-of-magnitude check at q = 2 keV gives the 12.4 zero-phonon Migdal
   ≈16× (m_e/M)²∫S_ee, with a different spectral shape (ω⁻⁴-weighted large-p loss vs the q → 0 loss function). Since q = 2 keV is
   ≈ q_BZ, the O(q) remainder of the identity is not small there and this is not a contradiction, but below the zone the G ≠ G′
   elements must enforce the identity. A derivation reviewer should check whether 12.4's sum-rule claim needs a "q ≳ q_BZ" qualifier.
2. The "dipole extrapolation five to twenty times larger" is quoted from 13.9 (Si near 0.40 MeV); no GaAs analogue exists.
3. The phrase "ionic correction of the zero-phonon line" replaces the planning name "ionic screening": the cell is an
   absorption (Im) effect, not a change of Re ε.
