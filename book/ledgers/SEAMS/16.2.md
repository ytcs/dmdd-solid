## The reader now knows
- Embedding of independent confined units (eq:16.2-embed): S = 𝓃_u|F_⊥(q_⊥)|²S_u(q_∥,ω); F_⊥ = sinc(q_zd/2) (uniform sheet),
  J₀(q_⊥r_t) (shell); in the rate S/ρ_T = |F_⊥|²S_u/ρ_u (per unit mass, packing-independent); 2607.15338 per tube length, 2312.00866
  Eqs. (13)–(14) per areal mass.
- Kinematics: S depends on q_∥ only, so a confined mode faster than v_max is reached by oblique q, needing only q ≳ ω/v_max; price
  |F_⊥|² beyond 1/thickness (tube 0.7 nm: 282 eV, ω up to v_max/r_t = 0.75 eV unsuppressed). Bulk plasmon unreachable (contrast 10.3).
- 2D kernel e²/((ε_a+ε_b)q_∥) (points back to 15.3 for images); ε_2D = 1 − v^{2D}R̃^{2D}; S_2D = −2Im(R̃/ε_2D) (eq:16.2-kernel2D);
  finite thickness F_th(qd) = 2/x − 2(1−e^{−x})/x² = 1 − x/3 (eq:16.2-thick): 0.95 at 100 eV, 0.85 at 300 eV, 0.9 at 191 eV for
  d = 3.35 Å (2312.00866 uses the zero-thickness kernel).
- Keldysh form ε̄ε_2D = ε̄ + r_0q − (e²/2q)R̃_low (eq:16.2-keldysh): remote bands do not screen a sheet at long wavelength (vs ε_bg in
  3D, 7.3/16.1); substrate only through ε̄, remote bands only through r_0, vertex never rescaled; polar substrate → plasmon–phonon mixing.
- Tube kernel 2αI₀K₀/ε_env (eq:16.2-kernel1D), m = 0 term of the cylindrical expansion; log form 10 % below exact at qr_t = 0.38
  (108 eV at 0.7 nm).
- Computational connection: App. E.21 (monolayer response with Coulomb cutoff or Wannier bubble; substrate ε(ω); RPA with the kernels).
- Drude weight D_Dr = Σ(−∂n_F/∂E)(q̂·v)²: 𝓃_2D/m* (parabolic), g_deg E_F/4π (Dirac). 2D plasmon (eq:16.2-2Dpl)
  ω² = e²𝓃_2D q/((ε_a+ε_b)m*) + (3/4)v_F²q² (disc average v_F²/4 in eq:4.5-moments). Graphene in vacuum 2αE_F q; E_F = 0.2 eV:
  phase velocity 20/6.4 v_max at 1/10 eV; ends at 30 eV, 0.30 eV (interband edge). v_D = 3a_CCγ₀/2 = 3.41e-3 (quoted by 2312.00866).
  2312.00866 Eqs. (13)–(19) = eq:16.2-embed + eq:16.2-kernel2D at ε_a = ε_b = 1; their gated BLG has no intraband plasmon.
- 1D linear channels: R⁰ = N_ch v q²/π(ω² − v²q²); keyresult eq:16.2-wire S_1D = N_ch g_c|q|δ(ω − v|q|/g_c), g_c = [1 +
  N_ch v^{1D}/πv]^{−1/2}; = 2607.15338 Eqs. (3)–(4) at N_ch = 1 with g₂ = g₄ = v^{1D}; RPA exact for the model (bosonization); first
  moment N_ch v q² fixed (projected f-sum); only the charge mode couples; source N_ch = 1 with measured g_c: rate ×4 at fixed g_c
  (×2.1 at fixed kernel, g_c 0.225 vs 0.419 at 10 eV).
- Nanotube numbers (r_t 0.7 nm, vacuum, v_D): N_ch v/πv_D = 31 (1 eV), 7 (100 eV); g_c = 0.18, 0.22, 0.36 at 1, 10, 100 eV; v_pl =
  7.3, 5.7, 3.6 v_max; daily modulation from the tube angle.
- Band-curvature correction (closed-form parabolic zero ω² = (ω₊²e^y − ω₋²)/(e^y − 1), y = 2πq/(N_ch v^{1D}m*)):
  ω^par/ω = 1 + (q²/2k_F²)g_c²[1/4 + g_c²/3(1−g_c²)]. Validity: subbands at 2v_D/r_t = 1.9 eV, q ≪ 1/r_t; forest Coulomb below the
  inverse spacing (1–2 eV for 100–200 nm).
- Limit 16.2a (fig:16-wire-limit): parabolic/linear within 10 % for q < 1.28 k_F (k_F = 1/r_t model); +4.6 % and share 0.96 at k_F.
- Confined plasmons are undamped in RPA → final-state class of their own (fulfils 8.7's pointer); decay by propagation to contacts
  (quasiparticle conversion proposed by 2607.15338), disorder, phonons, substrate; cascade → Chap. 18.
- Two components (eq:16.2-demon): ε = 1 + (k_TF,l²/q²)f₃(ν) − ω_p,h²/ω²; ω_a = ω_p,h q/(q² + k_TF,l²)^{1/2} ≃ v_a q;
  v_a = ω_p,h/k_TF,l = (𝓃_h/2m_h𝒩_F,l)^{1/2}; validity v_F,h ≪ v_a ≪ v_F,l. Share of the heavy f-sum (eq:16.2-share)
  (q²/(q²+k²))² (3D), (q/(q+q_s))² (2D); neutral mode, density probe O(q⁴/k⁴). Width (eq:16.2-width) (π/2)v_a/v_F,l (3D),
  (v_a/v_F,l)(1 − v_a²/v_F,l²)^{−1/2} (2D; Re f₂ = 1 exactly, no π/2). 2D: q_s = 2e²𝒩_F,l/(ε_a+ε_b); v_a independent of dimension and
  substrate. Sr₂RuO₄ demon (80–800 meV along the axis; plasmon 1.6 eV in plane) and hole-doped diamond (~200 meV), 2507.07164

- Stack of sheets (eq:16.2-sheets): v^L = (e²/2ε_bq_∥)sinh(q_∥a_s)/(cosh q_∥a_s − cos q_za_s); S = −(2/a_s)Im[R̃/(1 − v^L R̃)];
  limits (isolated sheet; 3D jellium of 𝓃_2D/a_s); layer-plasmon band from the bulk plasma frequency (q_z = 0) to the acoustic mode

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Limit 16.2a plot spec.** The App. F planning row names "1D RPA with subbands and inter-tube Coulomb" as the fuller result. The
   subband response of a nanotube is 16.3's massive-Dirac RPA, so the box compares linear channels with parabolic ones of the same
   velocity (x = q/k_F, as planned), with k_F = 1/r_t a labelled model for the curvature scale. Inter-tube Coulomb is stated in the text
   and the F row as the next correction.
2. **Limit 16.2b.** The planned formula (π/2)v_a/v_F is the 3D (Pines, Sr₂RuO₄) width; the plan's fuller result is "the full 2D RPA".
   The 2D width has no π/2: (v_a/v_F,l)(1 − v_a²/v_F,l²)^{−1/2}, from Re f₂ = 1 and Im f₂ = ν/(1−ν²)^{1/2}. The box shows both
   dimensions, each against its full-Lindhard width (light carriers, heavy cold). The 3D formula holds to 10 % only below 0.157.
3. **New closed form.** Eq:16.2-inplane (exact in-plane response of a two-layer stack, tanh form) and its expansion eq:16.2-corr; the
   one-layer limit reproduces 2110.01587 Eq. (19) exactly (checked numerically, difference = real constant d_A), and the ka_s → 0 limit
   is the EMT. A derivation reviewer may want to confirm the (ka_s)² coefficient (sympy series in lowd16_2/limit16_2c checks agree to
   the plotted precision).
4. **2607.15338 ×4.** Stated as at fixed g_c (both R11 referees); the fixed-kernel ratio 2.1 is added as the other reading.
5. **Graphene velocity.** v_D = 3.41e-3 (1.02e6 m/s) from a_CC and γ₀ quoted by 2312.00866 is used for graphene and nanotubes; 2607.15338
   takes 0.8e6 m/s. One value in the book.
6. **2312.00866 journal reference.** The arXiv API gives no journal reference (the comment says it matches a PRD version); the bib entry
   is arXiv-only.
7. Pines 1956 is credited for the prediction and the name only (Crossref-verified; not read). The Sr₂RuO₄ EELS observation is
   attributed to 2507.07164's statement.
8. Two model choices are named in the text: doped graphene at E_F = 0.2 eV in vacuum (Fig. 16.2) and the WE sheet parameters.
