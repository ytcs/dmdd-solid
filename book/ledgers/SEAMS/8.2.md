## The reader now knows
- Master formula, scattering (keyresult eq:8.2-rate, eq:8.2-spectrum): Γ(v) = ∫d³q/(2π)³ Σ_ab 𝔇_ab(q,v) S_ab(q, ω_q); dℛ/dω =
  (ρ_χ/m_χρ_T)∫d³v f_χ ∫d³q/(2π)³ Σ 𝔇_ab S_ab(q,ω) δ(ω − ω_q); any T, ω of either sign, Δ_χ included in ω_q. Integrand ≥ 0
  (contraction of two PSD matrices). Derived in D.25 (spin trace, thermal average, Σ_p′ → V∫d³q/(2π)³, volume cancellation).
- Index order: Σ_ab 𝔇_ab S_ab = Tr[𝔇Sᵀ]; Tr[𝔇S] differs by 2Tr[𝔇_I S_I]; equal when either is real; differ for explicit-i operators
  (𝒪₃) against SOC current–spin responses. Only Tr[𝔇Sᵀ] is guaranteed ≥ 0.
- Exact reduction: density row only + Δ_χ = 0 → eq:1.1-rate = eq:A.8-rate; with eq:A.8-eta (η/2q) and |Ṽ|² = πσ̄_eF_DM²/μ² → eq:1.5-rate.
- Four conditions with failure modes: first Born (|f| = μ|Ṽ|/2π vs range; dark-photon benchmark |f|m_V = 2×10⁻⁸); target exact (every
  point of 𝔗); plane waves (incoherent halo; fails for ultralight classical fields → 14.6); Gibbs target (out-of-equilibrium target keeps
  eq:8.2-rate with its populations if ρ̂ is diagonal in energy, but loses detailed balance and FDT); unpolarized DM (else DM spin density
  matrix).
- Absorption (keyresult eq:8.2-abs): Γ_abs = (1/2ω_φ)Σ g^φ_a* g^φ_b S_ab(p_φ, ω_φ); Γ_net = (1 − e^{−βω_φ})Γ_abs = −(1/ω_φ) Im Σ g*g R_ab;
  event rate (ρ_χ/m_φρ_T)⟨Γ_abs⟩. Detector counts Γ_abs; stimulated emission e^{−βω}Γ_abs; equal at eV and 10 mK. = Mitridate et al.
  Eqs. (20)–(22) with Π_φφ = Σg*gR. Scalar example: g^φ_{n_e} = −g_φee, g^φ_{𝒯_e} = g_φee/m_e.
- Occupation ρ_χ/(m_φ⁴v³) > 1 below ≈ 7 eV (v = 10⁻³): classical field; quantized and Gaussian-field-average routes agree up to
  spontaneous emission. Golden rule needs t ≫ t_coh ~ 1/(m_φv₀²) (≈ 1 ns at 1 eV) and t ≫ inverse target linewidth; else coherent driving,
  amplitude ∝ t (→ 14.6).
- Exact reduction to eq:1.4-dp-abs: row ê·j_e, g = ζe; proper R̃ᵀ_jj → ζ²σ₁ (diamagnetic term real; cubic: all polarizations equal,
  eq:4.4-ET); photon-reducible chain → ζ_eff² (derived in D.25 from J = −Π_em(ζA′ + A), (k² − ω²)A = J); matters near ω_p = 16.6 eV.
- Negative ω: closed at T = 0; open in a warm target with weight e^{−β|ω|} via S_ab(−q,−ω) = e^{−βω}S_ba(q,ω). Electronic e^{−E_g/T}
  dropped at every thermal order; one-phonon ω < 0 part ∝ n_Qν, at n_T = 1 (counted as power in the rate). Absorption's emission partner
  at (−p_φ, −ω_φ).
- Exothermic DM shifts the support up by |Δ_χ| (reaches q → 0 at ω = |Δ_χ|, absorption-like, no temperature needed); endothermic shifts
  it down.
- DM detailed balance (eq:8.2-db): Γ(p′→p) = e^{−βω}Γ(p→p′), for one final momentum, any masses, equal spins; from Hermiticity of
  H_int and Gibbs weights only (D.25). Exothermic down-scatter and thermally driven up-scatter are one process; DM in contact relaxes to T.
- Fig. 8.1 (fig:8-plane): elastic band ±qv_max − q²/2m_χ (max 35 eV at 26.5 keV for 10 MeV), ω < 0 hatched (warm target), exothermic
  band (Δ_χ = −20 eV), absorption point and emission point; fermionic line off-scale.
- Cross terms (subsection sec:8.2-cross): need 𝔇_ab ≠ 0 and S_ab ≠ 0 at once; S_χ-linear vs S_χ-free never interfere; n–s
  uncorrelated without magnetic order. WE 8.3 (we:8-cross): fully polarized free gas (toy half-metal) at Si valence density,
  k_F↑ = 4.50 keV, v_F↑ = 8.80e-3, E_F↑ = 19.8 eV; s^z = n/2 on reachable states → 2×2 matrix S↑[[1, ½], [½, ¼]], rank one,
  saturates eq:4.2-positivity; at q₀, 5 eV: S↑ = m²ω/2πq = 5.57e7 eV² (half the unpolarized value). Contraction S↑(Ṽ_n + Ṽ_s/2)²;
  sum of single-operator rates misses Ṽ_nṼ_sS↑ (destructive at Ṽ_s = −2Ṽ_n). O₁ + O₄: cross term killed by the DM spin trace.
  O₁ + O₇: cross term c₁c₇(v_T·ẑ)S↑ exactly (longitudinal parts cancel, eq:8.1-transverse); 1.5e-3 c₇/c₁ at m_χ = 100 MeV,
  v = 2.0e-3 (v_min 1.36e-3, v_T 1.47e-3); O₇ alone ≤ c₇² × 5.7e-5 × (S↑ + 4.0S↑-bound) → cross ≥ 5 × O₇-alone at c₇ = c₁. Halo
  average at fixed q (Maxwellian, escape cut aside): ⟨v_T⟩ = −v_E,T → −c₁c₇(v_E,T·ẑ)S↑, ≤ 8.4e-4 c₇/c₁; zero for isotropic halo; flips
  with the magnetization. Partial polarization: S_ns = ½(S↑ − S↓) = 0 in the linear region (per-spin S independent of k_F); P = 0.5 at
  q₀: 0 at 5 eV, 0.068 S_nn at 10 eV (minority edge 7.07 eV).
- Fermionic absorption: same formula, sum over ν̃ momenta, ω = eq:8.1-omega (m_χ − q, massless ν̃), line at q ≈ m_χ; relativistic 𝔇_ab → 10.9.
- Closing: cross terms first order in the weaker coupling; bounded by twice the geometric mean of the single-operator rates (positivity), which the polarized gas saturates → 8.3; screening of rows → 8.4.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **WE 8.3 deviates from the spine wording.** "Density and spin couplings with the 2×2 response matrix of the free-electron gas" cannot
   give a cross term under the master-formula conditions in the obvious reading: for O₁ + O₄ the unpolarized DM trace kills 𝔇_ns, and
   the unpolarized gas has S_ns = 0. The WE therefore uses a fully polarized free gas (toy half-metal) and shows (a) the rank-one matrix
   and generic interference, (b) that O₁ + O₄ still gives none, (c) the surviving O₁ + O₇ cross term with numbers and its halo average.
   O₇ arises in none of the standard UV models of 8.1 at leading order; it is used as the simplest S_χ-free spin coupling. Editor may
   prefer another pair.
2. **Spine (iii) wording.** "ω < 0 terms describe energy given up by a warm target or by exothermic dark matter": exothermic DM deposits
   ω > 0 (it shifts the support up); only a warm target makes ω < 0. The text states it that way; Fig. 8.1 follows.
3. **KKL equation numbers.** The spine cites 2101.08275 Eqs. (4), (6); the rate itself is Eq. (10) and (6) is the ELF form. s8-2 does
   not cite KKL directly (eq:4.2-rate and A.8 already do); it cites 2009.13534 Eq. (1) and 1910.08092 Eqs. (14)–(16) for the rate form,
   Mitridate Eqs. (20)–(22) and Hochberg 2017 Eqs. (5)–(6) for absorption and ζ_eff.
4. **Born criterion** is stated as |f| small against the range of the potential (Sakurai Chap. 6, by chapter, not checked against the
   book); the benchmark number is computed in we8_3.py.
5. **t_coh** first appears here (NOTATION lists 14.6); estimate ~1/(m_φv₀²) only, the precise definition stays with 14.6.
6. **Truncation label** reads "Sect. 8.3" in the present build because 8.3/8.4 are not written; resolves itself.
8. **Interference bound wording.** The cross term 2Re[𝔇_abS_ab] is bounded by twice the geometric mean of the two single-operator
   rates; 4.2 ("bounded by the geometric mean of their separate rates") omits the factor 2. Editor may sweep 4.2.
7. Figure parameters (m_χ = 10 MeV, Δ_χ = −20 eV, m_φ = 40 eV) are illustrative; the exothermic band is the elastic band shifted by
   |Δ_χ| (recoil correction of eq:8.1-omega-inel, relative order Δ_χ/m_χ = 2e-6, neglected).
