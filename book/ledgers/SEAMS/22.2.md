## The reader now knows
- Definition (keyresult "Exact remaining gap"): strongest derived statement with its conditions + the single missing step
  (proof, calculation or measurement); one home per gap; a calculation points to its App. E specification.
- Gap vs nuisance: a nuisance (8.8) has a computed next order or a small parameter and enters with a width; 8.8's third case
  (neither) and any claim held only under a removable hypothesis are gaps. Items with a sized and an open part split between
  the chapter nuisance table and tab:22-gaps.
- Five classes of what a gap limits, with counts (gaps22_2.log): proof 5 (8.6 ×3, 18.2 ×2), bounded 10, estimate 18,
  unsized 34, test 5; kinds: calculation 55, measurement 11, theory/method 6; 72 rows.
- Table 22.2 (tab:22-gaps): every open item of Chaps. 8, 10–21 and the two items of 22.3 (charged-current absorption,
  CDW collective modes), each with home \cref, strongest statement, missing step, class.

- Shared missing steps: finite-q BSE (10.5 attraction and triplet, 13.8 replicas); Ge quasiparticle bands with SOC (10.6,
