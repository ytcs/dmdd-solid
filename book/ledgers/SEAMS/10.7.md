## Open questions / for the editor
1. **E.16 normalization of 𝒯_e.** E.16 (subsection "The kinetic-energy row") writes "𝒯_e = p²/2m_e², the operator conjugate to a
   shift of m_e (Sect. 10.7)" and a squared element "1/4m_e⁴". The book's 𝒯_e (eq:8.1-rows) is Σp²/2m_e (eV). E.16's
   eq:E.16-TT is the response of 𝒯_e/m_e, and its sum rule "⟨|∇V|²⟩/(2m_e³) per electron" is that of 𝒯_e/m_e. 10.7 uses 8.1's
   𝒯_e; E.16 should read "the response of 𝒯_e/m_e" or drop the 1/m_e² (one-line fix at the App. E pass). 10.7 does not say
   "operator conjugate to a shift of m_e".
2. **Scalar density row near ω_p (new, EST).** In the EPM model (pseudo-wavefunctions) the screened density row at v = 1e-3 equals
   the kinetic rate at 16–18 eV; Mitridate et al. (all-electron) report the kinetic term dominant. The O(p²) cross and mixing terms
   are uncomputed. Stated in the text as open; candidate for the 10.10 nuisance table and for E.16 (extend the kinetic-row spec to
   O(p_φ²) near ω_p). Spine lists no open-problems field for 10.7; this is a new item.
3. **Fig. 10.6 scalar and ALP-model curves are models** (Cohen–Bergstresser EPM, vertical gap 3.12 eV, no scissor, no core); the
   caption says so. An all-electron curve would replace panel (c) once E.16 is run.
4. **Panel (b) of Limit 10.7a uses sapphire phonons**, since silicon is cubic and the tensor form only matters off principal axes;
   widths of the e-ray are the source's borrowed o-ray values (caption says so).
5. **Limit 10.7b plot is a hydrogen-like model**, not silicon (the silicon sum-rule numbers are in the text). The "formula" is the
   sum rule, the "fuller result" the cumulative spectrum; controlling parameter Λ (and r_c).
6. 77 K numbers (n_TA = 0.068) are 6.5's; "gap a few meV smaller at 77 K" is not given a number (Varshni shift ≈ 4 meV in the
   planning notes; no public value fetched).
7. Fig. 10.6 is the chapter's sixth figure by plan (fig:10-absorption); its printed number depends on 10.5/10.6 figures.
