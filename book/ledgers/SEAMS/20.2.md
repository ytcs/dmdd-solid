## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Memory-loss times (SPINE LO (iii), SOTA IF-R6 "≈ 10 ns (Si) / 30–100 ns (Ge)").** Reproduced only for births concentrated
   near the band top (the research toy's pair: E³ spike vs a line at 0.95 f_top): 6.8 ns Si, 47 ns Ge. The worst pair of births
   in the upper factor two of the band needs 0.15 µs (Si) / 1.0 µs (Ge); the TA daughters of optical decay (22 meV) ≈ 1 µs (Si).
   The general rule is E_* ≲ 0.52 E_birth (time ∝ E_birth⁻⁵). With realistic ER births (optical-decay pairs), the hard parts
   agree to 1e-3 only near 1 µs, when the near-face shell of a 4-mm chip is 13.5 % of the volume. The text states all three cases
   and calls them estimates. SPINE/SOTA wording should be qualified ("for births near the band top"); 20.5's verdicts depend on it.
2. **Transfer-kernel bound** (CW6-L2, CW6-errata A8 form): derived as in the errata (L1 norm, single-sign condition). Evaluated
   for model births it is loose (0.15 vs actual 0.012 at 1 µs) because E_mem cuts through the TA daughters. It reduces to f_s only
   when every hard birth lies above E_mem.
3. **f_s = ω_opt/ε_eh** is the research estimate (uniform residual kinetic energy below ω_opt); the book uses the measured
   ω_opt = 64.1 meV (research: 62 meV "Debye energy" proxy), giving 1.71 % (research 1.7 %).
4. **Rayleigh-law validity in Si is low (1.36 THz).** The continuum rates are applied at 5–15 THz in the memory estimates; the text
   says so. A first-principles three-phonon calculation of acoustic decay in Si (DFPT Φ^(3), App. E.8) would replace the f⁵
   extrapolation; not run (compute policy).
5. **Ge A_anh**: Leman (Table I) and Hernandez (Table 3) quote different values (6.43e-55 vs 1.65e-54) from the same Tamura 1985
   paper (unread; possibly direction ranges). Both are quoted as "quoted from Tamura's work by …".
6. **Daughter distribution**: P_d = Beta(3,3)-symmetric stand-in (as the research toy). The shape constants (0.667, 0.52, crossing
   times) depend on it; the scaling form does not. A run with the isotropic L→L′+T / L→TT distributions of E.12 would be a check.
7. NOTATION ⚑ rows need clash-list entries (above).
