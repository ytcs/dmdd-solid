## The reader now knows
- Reciprocity eq:14.5-recip: S_ab(q) = σ^T_aσ^T_b S_ba(−q) (time reversal), S_ab(q) = σ^P_aσ^P_b S_ab(−q) (inversion); σ^T: n, 𝒯 +, j, s −,
  𝒥 +; σ^P: n, 𝒯, s +, j, 𝒥 −. Derived from ⟨f|A|i⟩* = ⟨Tf|TAT⁻¹|Ti⟩.
- Current block eq:14.5-GOA: S_jj = S^R + iε𝒢_OA; T: S^R even, 𝒢_OA odd in q; P: both even ⇒ 𝒢_OA needs P broken, survives T; odd symmetric
  part needs P and T broken. Opposite-T-parity blocks (s–𝒥): symmetric part odd in q; static value needs P broken too. Table tab:14-odd.
- eq:14.5-gyr: 𝒢_OA^γ = (2ω²/e²) Im g_γl q_l/(1 − e^{−βω}) + O(q³), from FDT and ε^{αβ} ⊃ iε_αβm g_ml q_l (Zabalo–Stengel normalization,
  Eqs. (2)–(4)); only Im g enters S; Re g ↔ Im g by Kramers–Kronig.
- eq:14.5-helicity: S_± = S_T ∓ q̂·𝒢_OA; dissymmetry q̂·𝒢/S_T = q Im g_q̂q̂/ε₂^T; reciprocal (helicity, not direction). eq:14.5-rot:
  ρ = ω² Re g_q̂q̂/2 (= 2304.00048 Eq. (4)); n_± = n_r ∓ gω/2; Δα = 2ω² Im g.
- Quartz numbers (we14_5.log): g33 = 0.125, g11 = −0.071 bohr (LDA, Table I); ρ = 21.6 °/mm at 2.105 eV; q g33 = 7.1e-5 (photon),
  0.034 at 1 keV; 1/v_max = 377; orientation average (2g11 + g33)/3 = −0.0057 bohr = −0.045 g33.
- O5–O8 interference: 𝔇_{jαjβ} ⊃ −(i/2m_e)Re(c5*c8)ε_αβγq^γ; contraction (1/m_e)Re(c5*c8) q·𝒢_OA. Keyresult eq:14.5-enant (enantiomer
  difference, orientation-averaged, η(v_min)); needs parity-violating DM (magnetic dipole × anapole); even in q ⇒ ℓ = 0, 2, no wind
  asymmetry; relative size = dissymmetry × 2|c5|q/(m_e|c8|). The n–j block adds only orientation-averaging-zero terms at this order.
  Agrees with the campaign's corrected chiral formula (the v1 factor −½ is not used; derived afresh in book normalization).
- WE 14.5 (we:14-quartz): point group 32: polar-vector invariants 0 (no ℓ = 1), ℓ = 3 invariants 1, N_gyr = 2, g = diag(g11, g11, g33);
  rank-3 polar invariants 4 (log only). GaAs N_gyr = 0 (C.17).
- Chiral phonons: eq:14.5-Lph (= 2301.07617 Eq. (6), 2511.20461 Eq. (11)); |L| ≤ 1; T: L(−Q) = −L(Q) (checked 1.5e-13); P+T ⇒ 0 (Si
  < 2e-7). Final-state-resolved S^(1)_A(q,ω;L) = eq:11.1-S1 × δ³(L − L^ph). Net ⟨L⟩ per event ∝ G_ij v̂_E,j, an axial–polar invariant counted
  by N_gyr: none in GaAs at linear order; two in quartz/Te.
- eq:14.5-muph (= 2301.07617 Eq. (7), 2511.20461 Eq. (12)): one-phonon expectation value of the bilinear operator Σ(eZ*/2M)u×P; GaAs
  (Z* = ±2.094) lowest band ≤ 0.015 μ_N, bound 0.030 μ_N; μ_B-scale reported moments are 10⁴–10⁵ larger (orbit–lattice, not Born
  charges). Magnetic-dipole DM creates single phonons through the transverse ionic current, not through μ^ph.
- Detection estimate (2511.20461 Sect. III): band average of |L| as detection probability (GaAs 0.07, LiNbO3 0.22, CuCl(pyr) 0.16;
  InF3(bpy) 0.15 from 2301.07617 Sect. VII). [H, L_z] = (i/2)u·[J_z, D]u: L conserved only for site-isotropic force constants; decay
  changes L; branching 𝒩^ch = (1 − M)⁻¹b in app:E.19.
- Limit Check 14.5a (lim:14.5a, fig:14-limit-chiral), panel a (limit14_5a.log): GaAs band average 0.124 (20³ shifted; 0.118/0.126 on

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **κ for GaAs.** The band average of |L^ph| in GaAs is 0.124 with the book's PBE force constants (0.118–0.126 on 10³ grids), against
   0.07 that 2511.20461 quotes from a literature dynamical matrix. Both are printed; the difference is not resolved (their matrix not read).
2. **Symbol ω_M.** eq:14.5-GL quotes the source's magnon frequency ω_M, which clashes with 14.3's ω_M = 2μ_B M_s. Suggest renaming the
   14.5 symbol to ω_mag or ω_ν at the merge (it is local to one display and one sentence and the box).
3. **E.20 wording.** E.20 says 14.5 uses 𝒢_OA "for a directional signal" and calls 𝒢_OA dimensionless; in the book 𝒢_OA has the
   dimension of S (eV²) and enters an enantiomer difference (even in q). Align E.20 at its next pass.
4. **Limit Check 14.5a validity relabel.** The F plan's "where the decay selection rule holds" is replaced by the computable criterion
   "rate-weighted lowest-band chirality within 10 % of the band average" (m ≥ 2.2 MeV in GaAs); the decay is the stated leading
   correction. The plan's x axis "phonon angular momentum" is replaced by m_χ.
5. **Limit Check 14.5b** is the F plan's fallback made into a comparison inside a model (Landau vs Weiss mean field vs spin waves for
   |S| = 5/2, gap 0.02 T_CM, a modelling choice); the low-T ratio depends on the gap. No material is computed.
6. **2601.01043 points stated positively** (not as errata): the n_r/|ε|² factor, the square in the ALP rate, and that for absorption the
   trigger is a condition on m_φ (their boosted fraction f concerns kinetic energy, irrelevant for an absorption deposit). The editor may
   want to check the last point against their Sect. III before it stands in print.
7. **GaAs force constants.** gaas_ifc14.py is a validated Python replacement for matdyn (not available on PATH); it may serve 11.x
   scripts too.
8. **Edwards et al.** cited as arXiv only (journal reference not on the arXiv record).
9. DEFERRED-COMPUTE (outside book/, not edited): candidates are the finite-q 𝒢_OA (already DC-46), the MOF branching (DC-37), the
   multiferroic near T_CM (DC-84 / LE-92). No new unsized F correction.
