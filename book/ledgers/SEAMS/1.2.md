  (eq:1.2-nesc) = 7.393 × 10⁷ (km/s)³.

  (eq:1.2-vmax); annual range 781–810.
- Lab speed distribution F_χ(v), two branches (eq:1.2-Fspeed); tail fractions (36%, 15%, 4.2%, 0.58% above 400–700 km/s).
- ⟨1/v⟩ closed form (eq:1.2-inv) = 1/(287.9 km/s) = 1041 (natural) at v_E^ref; June/December 1/298.3, 1/279.8.
- v_⊕(t) (eq:1.2-vorb, Baxter Eq. (11)); |v_E(t)| ≈ v_E^ref + 14.6 cos ω_yr(t − t_peak) (eq:1.2-annual), peak 2 June, min 2 December;
  exact 266.4 / 237.4, mean 252.1. Phase reversal of η at v_min = 211 km/s; modulation +21/−16% at 600, +42/−28% at 700.
- Direction: θ_E = 42.5° to the Earth's axis (eq:1.2-thetaE), RA 20.9 h, Dec 47.5°; lab-axis angle over a sidereal day (eq:1.2-angle);
  east/north/up components (eq:1.2-local); uniaxial toy: rate = constant + constant × cos²θ_lab (no symbols, to avoid a₀/a clashes).
- Φ_χ = 𝓃_χ⟨v⟩ (eq:1.2-flux), ⟨v⟩ closed form (eq:1.2-vmean) = 354.1 km/s; Φ_χ(100 MeV) = 1.06 × 10⁸ cm⁻² s⁻¹.
- Sensitivity of η to v₀ ± 6 and v_esc ± 25 km/s (×2.26/×0.10 at 760 km/s); focusing and rotation negligible.
