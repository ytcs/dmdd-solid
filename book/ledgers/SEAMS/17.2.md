## The reader now knows
- Each boson measures one response at q = p_φ, ω = m_φ: dark photon L (total ε); ALP (e) σ₁ / σ₁^𝒥 / the magnon spin row;
  ALP in B₀ = dark photon with ζ = g_aγγB₀/m_φ; scalar (e) S_𝒯𝒯 (nonpolar) or Z_κ − Z*_κ/ε∞ (polar); nucleon scalar ∝ L (12.6).
  Per mass: L/ρ_T, m_φσ₁/ρ_T, S_𝒯𝒯/(m_φ²ρ_T).
- Screening ceiling eq:17.2-Lbound: L ≤ 1/(2|ε₁|) (equality at ε₂ = |ε₁|). Si below gap 0.043; a-SiO₂ 0.13 (TLS L = 7.7e-5);
  ZrTe₅ 0.024 (y) / 0.0016 (x); Al condensate 3.3e-9 at 1 meV (actual 2.4e-16). Large L at zeros of ε₁: GaAs LO 1.33
  (width-limited), sapphire 7.9 at 112.4 meV, Si plasmon 4.49 at 16.4 eV; GaAs at ω_T only 0.0054.
- ALP has no ceiling: clean Al 5.1e-13 at 0.72 meV vs Si best 1.4e-13 at 4.3 eV (near the ε₂ peak); dark photon favors zeros of
  ε₁, ALP maxima of ε₂. ALP in 10 T: GaAs line 5.4e-13, sapphire 5.9e-13, Si plasmon 8.8e-11 GeV⁻¹.
- Coverage (Fig. 17.2, Table): dark photon gap 0.36–1.17 eV (no book target at leading order); ALP(e) gap ≈10 meV–1.17 eV;
  scalar only GaAs line (3.1e-14) and the Si model (1.1e-15 at 5.2 eV); Si phonon-assisted scalar uncomputed (App. E.14).
  YIG dark photon via B′ (vacuum ζ, random polarization, ⟨|(v×ê)_⊥|²⟩ = (⟨v²⟩ + ⟨v_z²⟩)/3 = 6.53e-7): ζ = 7.6e-17 at 10 µeV.
  Tuned lines: scan over one e-fold spends (π/2)Γ_M/m = 1.6e-5 per mass: YIG averaged 4.1e-15 (peak 1.6e-17), MnF₂ 4.8e-14
  (peak 1.9e-16); averaged values independent of Γ_M.
- WE 17.2: GaAs factorized-model tail at 1.17 eV (ε₂ = 2.5e-4, 4.6 cm⁻¹) is an artifact (→ ε∞(γ_L − γ_T)/ω); Lorentz with one
  width 4.8e-7, 0.008 cm⁻¹. Equal per-mass rates need α_GaAs = 2.15 α_Si (n 3.48 Skauli 22 °C, 3.56 Franta, ρ 5.361/2.33).
  Cold Si 1s TA replica 1.17277 eV, α = 13.3 cm⁻¹ √(Δ/eV). Handover 0.012 meV (0.1 cm⁻¹), 1.2 meV (1 cm⁻¹), 40 meV (10 cm⁻¹)
  above threshold; Si ζ = 5.6e-14 at 1 meV above (≈ 2000 × GaAs line center).
- Coherent vs counting: eq:17.2-ratio N_lin/N_cnt = [(n_B + 1)/n_B]^{1/2} = e^{m/2T} (coupling e^{m/4T}), from the bunched
  thermal variance (14.6) and the n_B + 1 linear-readout noise (Lamoreaux Eq. (9)); background-free counting (N_th < 0.05, 3 quanta)
  above 17 / 20 µeV (1 h / 1 day, 10 mK), 0.19 / 0.22 meV (100 mK), m ≈ (20–26)T. Crossover at m ≈ T (0.86 µeV, 210 MHz at 10 mK).
  Examples: 24.8 µeV, 1 day: 4.9e4 vs 3 quanta (×127 in coupling); 10 µeV: ×330 signal, ×18 coupling; 1 neV: n_B = 3.6e5 (4.2 K),
  861 (10 mK). Caveats: squeezing, dark counts.

   14.3 does not print a dark-photon number. If the editor prefers, drop the point and keep only the qualitative sentence.

   curve is shown over 0.37–30 meV.

   quoted; the WE factor 2.15 would be 2.13 with 5.317.
