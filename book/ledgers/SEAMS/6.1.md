## The reader now knows
- Derivative-coupling vector d^{Rκ}_ji = ⟨Ψ_j|∇_RκΨ_i⟩ (eq:6.1-d), antisymmetric, d_ii = 0.
- Both non-adiabatic couplings of eq:5.1-N from d: ⟨Ψ_j|∇²Ψ_i⟩ = ∇·d_ji + (d·d)_ji; T_N + 𝒩 = Σ(1/2M)[(−i∇ − i d)²] (eq:6.1-Pd);
  nuclear momentum in the adiabatic basis is the matrix −i(∇ + d) (vector-potential analogy); BO keeps the diagonal (d_00 = 0, 𝒩_00).
- Velocity form: derivative coupling acts as −i u̇·d_ji; classical-path equation iċ_j = E_jc_j − iΣ u̇·d_ji c_i (eq:6.1-tdep);
  = u̇·F_ji/(E_i − E_j). Scalar coupling = quantum correction, ratio |d|/(Mv): κ_BO for zero-point motion, 1/(qa_0) for a kick
  (q ≫ 3.7 keV ⇒ classical projectile).
- Velocity counting: zero-point nuclear speed ~ ακ³ (κ³ = 2.9e-4 in Si); derivative coupling κ³, shifts κ⁶, relative κ⁴ = m_e/M;
  kicked Si nucleus (1 GeV, 5.10 MeV) v_N = 1.96e-4 = 59 km/s, 91 × ακ³.
- Insulator: ω_ph/E_g (0.055 Si); metal: fraction ω_ph/E_F ~ κ² of electrons cannot follow; Migdal's adiabatic theorem named with this
  counting (precise in 6.3).
- H_ep general form derived (eq:6.1-Hep) from V_eN with v_κ, density operator, eq:5.3-u, ∂_{κ,Q}V Bloch property ⇒ k → k+Q; equals
  eq:A.6-Hep/A.6-g with bare V; Giustino Eqs. (31)–(38). Left out: g^AH (6.3), V_NN (in E^ad), screening → V_KS by DFPT (5.4, 6.2, E.4).
- Two bases, one interaction: displacement-proportional mixing = adiabatic dressing; real transitions need velocity, extra ω_ph/ΔE.
- Adiabatic vs sudden: first-order amplitude split into velocity-following term and acceleration term (eq:6.1-accel); sudden amplitude
  v_N·d_j0/(iΔE) (eq:6.1-sudden1); adiabatic: Fourier component of acceleration at ΔE, faster than any power.
- Model (fig:6-sudden): |α_d|² = (m_e v_N²/2ω_e)[(πω_eτ_acc/2)/sinh(πω_eτ_acc/2)]² (eq:6.1-model); 0.47 at ω_eτ = 1, 0.074 at 2;
  asymptote π²x²e^{−πx}; P_0 = e^{−|α_d|²} (C.6 coherent state).
- Crystal: kick duration ~4e-21 s (Si, 3.5 fm, v_max) vs 1/E_g = 0.56 fs; lattice deceleration correction [1 − (ω_ph/ΔE)²]^{-2},
  1.006 for Si ω_opt/E_g; sudden fails for ΔE ~ ω_ph and for q²/2M ≲ ω_ph (→ 5.6, 12.1).
- Boost formula (eq:6.1-boost), Migdal 1941 / Ibe Eqs. (1)–(2); first order = dipole; d_j0 = m_eΔE_j⟨j|Σr|0⟩ for an atom.
- Keyresult: hydrogen P_0 = |F_1s(m_ev_N)|² = [16/(4 + v_N²/α²)²]² (eq:6.1-P0), exact (Born, CM separation, reduced-mass corrections
  cancel with v_N = q/m_p); Ibe Eqs. (55)–(58); many electrons: m_e/M correction (→ 12.2).
- Migdal effect (recoil-induced ionization) named; treated in Chap. 12.
- WE 6.1 (we:6-hydrogen): 1 GeV at v_max on H: μ = 484.2 MeV, q = 2.566 MeV, E_R = 3.51 keV, v_N = 2.735e-3 (820 km/s), v_N/α = 0.375,
  F = 0.933, P_0 = 0.871, 1 − P_0 = 0.129 (small-v 0.140, +9 %); P_0 = ½ at v_N/α = 0.87 (q = 5.96 MeV, E_R = 18.9 keV);
  ΔE = 10.2 eV, 1/ΔE = 0.065 fs, proton crossing 1.1e-21 s, ΔEτ = 1.6e-5; Si kick hydrogenic 1 − P_0 = 7.2e-4.
- Two Migdals paragraph: 1941 sudden-kick ionization (α, β decay) vs 1958 adiabatic theorem.

   the text and cited also to Ibe et al. Eqs. (1)–(2).
