## The reader now knows
- eq:16.8-golden: Γ₁ = ½(Δ₀/Δ_tl)² J_s(Δ_tl) coth(Δ_tl/2T) for a coupling ½σ_zX; valid for Γ₁ ≪ Δ_tl; D.42 derives it in the eigenbasis.
- eq:16.8-Jph: J_s^ph = (ω³/πρ_T)(γ_l²/c_LA⁵ + 2γ_t²/c_TA⁵) = 2K_ph ω³ (K_ph of eq:16.7-tau1); with eq:16.8-golden it gives τ₁⁻¹ of
  eq:16.7-tau1 (Jäckle; Meißner Eq. (7); Sahling Eqs. (4)–(5) with 8π³/ρh⁴ = 1/2πρ). Transverse/longitudinal 16.6 (v-SiO₂, 5.8/3.8 km/s
  of 16.7), 49.9 (Al). Convention δΔ_as = 2γS (Müller Eq. (16)); Meißner's text δε = γS is inconsistent with its Eq. (7) (×4).
- eq:16.8-black: Γ₁^el = πα_ohm(Δ₀/Δ_tl)²Δ_tl coth (Black, as quoted by Meißner Eq. (8), W_el² = 4α_ohm).
- eq:16.8-born: α_ohm = 4K_b, K_b = (𝒩_F V_d Ω)², per-spin DOS (= Maillet's K); with a both-spin K the relation is α_ohm = K. Resonator
  fits K = 0.07/0.11 → α_ohm = 0.28/0.44 (Born). Exact one-channel contact (eq:D.42-alpha): α_ohm = (4/π²)arctan²(πK_b^{1/2}), saturates at 1;
  10 % below Born at K_b = 0.017; at K_b = 0.07 gives 0.195 (×0.70); the contact strength reproducing α = 0.28 is ×1.32 the Born one
  (×1.64 for 0.44).
- eq:16.8-sc: Γ₁^s/Γ₁^el = [2/(1 + e^{Δ_BCS/T})]·sinh(u)K₁(u), u = Δ_tl/2T, for T, Δ_tl ≪ Δ_BCS (D.42: case-I coherence, closed form via
  ∫e^{−py}(y+a)/√(y(y+2a)) = a e^{pa}K₁(pa)); general: eq:D.42-Rs. Meißner Eq. (9) is its Δ_tl ≪ T limit; within 10 % only for
  Δ_tl ≤ 0.69–0.73 T (0.784 T for the closed form); 0.86 at Δ_tl = T, 0.71 at 2T; leading term 1 + (u²/2)[ln(u/2) + γ_E − 1/6].
- Fig. 16.8 (5 GHz defect, Δ₀ = 0.3Δ_tl, γ = 1 eV model, α = 0.28): phonon 1.8e5 s⁻¹ below 0.1 K; normal electrons 2.5e9 s⁻¹ at 10 mK;
  superconducting electron rate equals phonon at 0.207 K (0.176 T_c); 1.9e-9 s⁻¹ at 50 mK; Born normal rate reaches Δ_tl at 1.51 K.
- eq:16.8-scaling: symmetric incoherent rate = [Γ(1+α)/Γ(1−α)] Δ_r^{2(1−α)}(2πT)^{2α−1}/α (from eq:C.20-ohmrate), anti-Arrhenius
  T^{2α−1} (T^{−0.44} at 0.28).

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Superconducting factor (new derived result, PRIORITY for the derivation review).** Meißner et al. Eq. (9) (the factor
   2/(1 + e^{Δ/T}) with the normal coth form) is the Δ_tl ≪ T limit. The quasiparticle golden rule with the case-I factor gives the extra
   factor sinh(u)K₁(u), u = Δ_tl/2T, for T, Δ_tl ≪ Δ_BCS (D.42), confirmed numerically (7e-4 at T = 0.005Δ). Thermal defects (Δ_tl ≈ T–2T)
   relax 14–29 % slower than the published form. Not checked against Black–Fulde 1979 (closed access). 16.9 and s16-summary quote the
   bare factor: suggest adding "for Δ_tl ≪ T".
2. **Exact one-channel α_ohm** (eq:D.42-alpha) uses the unitarized s-wave contact t-matrix with the real part of G(0) absorbed into V_d.
   It is a model statement (one channel per spin); real defects have several channels (E.22). Derivation review should confirm the
   per-spin counting against E.10 (α = (1/2π²)Σ_σΣ_ν δ̃²).
3. **Symbols to unify.** 16.7 writes τ₁⁻¹ (eq:16.7-tau1) for the relaxation rate; 16.8 writes \varGamma_1 (book's italic-Γ rate rule) and
   states Γ₁^ph = τ₁⁻¹ once. Barrier height: 16.8 follows 16.7's V_b; E.11 writes E_b for the same quantity. Editor to pick one each.
4. **Fused-silica density** is taken from 16.7 (ρ_T = 2.2 g/cm³, Buchenau Table I, which I did not read); the WE quotes it via \cref.
5. **Eckart test uses Lawrence's hydrogen parameters** (chemistry scale, T_× = 240 K), chosen because the source gives the exact rate for
   them and its quoted ratios (×6 at T_×, ×2 at 1.5 T_×) are reproduced. A defect-scale barrier would only rescale T_×.
6. **Limit Check 16.8d is a model comparison** (uniform distribution with cut λ range and exact Δ_tl): the prefactor is exact inside the
   window; the plot shows the window, not a material correction. The material discrepancy (P_Q vs P_C) is stated in text and F row.
7. **Fig. 16.8 uses α_ohm = 0.28 and γ = 1 eV for a TLS "in aluminum"** (model values, labelled). The fitted α refers to interface TLS of
   Al-coated SiN resonators.
8. The returns' claim that 2103.06977 Eqs. (4)–(7) all need δε = 2γS: I verified only Eq. (7) (its Eqs. (2)–(4) are internally consistent
   with δε = γS). The book states only the Eq. (7) inconsistency (D.42, F row).
9. map16.py caption could add \cref{lim:16.8a,lim:16.8b,lim:16.8c,lim:16.8d}; not edited.
