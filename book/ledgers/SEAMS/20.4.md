## The reader now knows
- Y_qp(E_ath) ≡ Δ_BCS⟨N_qp⟩/E_ath for a phonon that has broken its first pair (absorption probability is 20.3's P_abs, separate);
  energy balance E_ath = Δ_BCS N_qp + E_sub + E_leak (eq:20.4-Y). Counting convention: Δ_BCS per quasiparticle (as 1401.1937).
- Three T = 0 kernels from the golden rule with eq:7.2-cohE, at v_F q ≫ E_ath (v_F/v_L = 311 in Al): split P_pb(E|E_ath)
  (eq:20.4-split, normalization E_ath 𝔤_I, 𝔤_I of eq:7.2-pairspec/eq:15.1-gI); pair-breaking time 1/t_pb = E_ath𝔤_I/(πΔ_BCS t₀)
  (eq:20.4-tpb; normal width × 𝔤_I; width ∝ E_ath from α²F = bE² via eq:6.4-lambda; t₀ = t_pb(2Δ); Al t₀ = 0.26 ns as used by
  Guruswamy); emission density Γ_em(E_ath|E) ∝ α²F ρ(E − E_ath)[1 − Δ²/E(E − E_ath)] (eq:20.4-emit; scale 438 ns in Al irrelevant to Y).
  Photon split: case II (−Δ² in the numerator). Conserving correction Δ/v_F q ≤ 1.6e-3: negligible.
- Keyresult eq:20.4-below: Y_qp = 2Δ_BCS/E_ath for 2Δ ≤ E_ath < 4Δ, exactly, independent of film, α²F, split; proof: a pair-breaking
  emission needs E ≥ 3Δ, the hotter quasiparticle has ≤ E_ath − Δ. Also holds for photons and dark-matter deposits (15.1). N_qp = 2 with
  no fluctuation in 87–174 GHz (Al).
- WE 20.4: 3Δ = 0.54 meV = 131 GHz; t_pb = 0.792 t₀ = 0.206 ns (𝔤_I(3) = 1.322); N_qp = 2, Y = 2/3, E_sub = Δ = 0.18 meV, phonons below
  43.5 GHz; film enters only through absorption. 0.7 THz = 16.1Δ: Y 0.30–0.59.
- Kaplan cascade in a lumped film: p_pb = 1/(1 + t_pb/t_l) (eq:20.4-ppb); depends on the film only through t_l/t₀; mean-number recursion
  eq:20.4-cascade (branching process as in 18.1); solution method stated.
- No loss: 0.5 at 4Δ → max 0.600 at 5.46Δ → 0.590 (0.5901–0.5905 over 10–60Δ), matching the published 0.57–0.6; 41 % lost to sub-gap
  relaxation; self-similar cascade (emission mean 3E/4); phonon vs photon seed 0.007 at 5Δ, < 0.001 at 10Δ.
- Finite t_l (fig:20-yield a): at 10Δ Y = 0.349/0.415/0.477/0.524/0.554 for t_l/t₀ = 0.5/1/2/4/8; small rise above 4Δ (0.560 at 5.2Δ for
  t_l/t₀ = 4), gone at 0.5; Y(60Δ) = 0.238 at 0.5. p_pb(3Δ) = 0.84, p_pb(10Δ) = 0.93 at t_l/t₀ = 4.
- Chang–Scalapino correction (Limit Check 20.4a): kinetic equations (Fig. 4 of 1401.1937, photon seed, measured α²F, T/T_c = 0.1, probe)
  above the cascade by 0.001–0.011 for t_l/t₀ ≤ 4, 0.036–0.041 at 8; probe +2 %; excess-energy counting +4 %.
- Arrival band 0.7–2.7 THz = 16–62Δ: Y falls by 20.5 / 6.5 / 1.7 % (t_l/t₀ = 0.5 / 2 / 8), flat without loss → film contribution to
  𝔯_coll (19.5), used in 20.5. Thin clean resonator films: t_l/t₀ ≈ 0.5 (Guruswamy estimate), loss up to 40 %.
- Electrode (recombination) phonons: hot electron in Al → cascade → returned energy = sub-gap + leaked + recombination at 2Δ; at
  t_l/t₀ = 4 (seed 40Δ) 85 % at or below 2Δ, 96 % below 10Δ; at 8: 92 / 98 %; seed 20Δ changes these by < 0.02 (fig:20-yield b).
  2Δ_Al = 0.36 meV = 87 GHz vs Ge Debye 35.7 meV (factor 99). Isotope rate at 87 GHz: Si 140 s⁻¹ (39 m), Ge 2.1e3 s⁻¹ (1.5 m);
  anharmonic decay 0.3 s (Ge) and longer (Si): ballistic → surface discrimination (Leman p.17: 13 % of the Ge signal at 3 V) and the
  physical content of ρ_g (19.5).
- Open problems: lumped film; origin of the 0.04 thick-film offset (T, α²F, recombination/reabsorption, probe); no independent transport
  check of the film cascade in the literature and no measured Y_qp at fixed phonon energy; electrode absorption share unmeasured.
