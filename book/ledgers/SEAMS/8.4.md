## The reader now knows
- Channel of an external field = {q + G}; Coulomb lines of the channel screen the DM vertex, all others belong to the material.
  Multi-species Hartree argument: the induced charge-number density acts as a field on the row n_Z(q + G); proper response of the
  channel R̃ defined by eq:8.4-proper (reduces to eq:4.4-phitot for electrons); R̃ = response with the channel's Coulomb terms deleted
  (eq:4.8-removal), so all exact properties of Chap. 4 hold for it.
- Keyresult eq:8.4-screened: R_ab = R̃_ab + Σ_GG′ R̃_{aZ_G}(ε⁻¹)_GG′ v_C(|q+G′|) R̃_{Z_G′ b}, ε_GG′ = δ − v_C R̃_ZZ, the total
  dielectric matrix of electrons and nuclei (ε₀ below the phonons, ε∞ between phonons and gap; electronic ε at clamped nuclei);
  rank-one form eq:8.4-rank1; TDDFT with f_xc inside the proper part. Computational connection → E.6 (dielectric matrix), E.4 (Born
  charges, phonons).
- Four couplings: total charge (v_C R_ZZ = ε⁻¹ − 1, S_ZZ ↔ total L exactly, moving nuclei, any ω); electron density
  (eq:8.4-electron, R̃_eZ = −R̃_ee + Σ Z_κ R̃_eκ; ε_e of A.7 exact but not measured, its inverse has the acoustic poles); nucleon number
  (screened only via R̃_NZ = Born charges at phonon energies, unscreened at electronic energies; vertex untouched, pole TO → LO);
  spin without SOC or magnetic order unscreened (SU(2) argument), with SOC/magnetic order screened (WE 8.3's S_ns ≠ 0); spin-flip pairs
  feel the direct kernel (4.9) but no exchange kernel.
- Non-density rows: j_L screened as the density (eq:8.1-transverse, eq:4.4-ward); j_T, spin current, 𝒯_e screened only through
  R̃_aZ ≠ 0 — local fields G ≠ 0 in cubic crystals along symmetry axes, L–T mixing at G = 0 otherwise; transverse-photon exchange in
  scattering ~ ω_p²/q² = 2e-5 at q₀ (Si). Rule: screen every row with R̃_aZ ≠ 0; R̃ alone or a bare 1/|ε|² is wrong except for charge.
- Keyresult eq:8.4-exact (exactness theorem): density coupling Σ c₁^{(s)} n_s, nuclei of different Z have different masses, F_N = 1:
  S_cc = λ_Z²(2q²/e²)L/(1 − e^{−βω}) at every (q, ω) iff the coupling ∝ n_Z, or nuclei clamped and c₁^{(e)} = ±λ_Z. Sufficiency
  (static difference operator); necessity from the impulse regime (separate recoil peaks, weights c_s²𝓃_s) plus the q → 0 LA phonon
  (weight ∝ q²(Σ c_s𝓃_s)², zero for n_Z at O(q²)); equal-mass groups by Cauchy–Schwarz (D.27). Sharpens Hochberg et al. 2101.08263.
  Practical content: electron-only coupling ≈ L above the phonon band to Migdal order (κ_BO powers), fails in the phonon band (S_ee
  acoustic peak of eq:8.6-fsum-e, absent in S_ZZ); pointer to 10.2's relaxed-lattice bound.
- Table 8.3 (tab:8-screening): coupling | example | screened by | exact L.
- WE 8.6 (we:8-nucleon), GaAs heavy scalar with c^p = c^n: LO vertex a_N(q) = μ_red[(A/M)_Ga e^{iq·τ_Ga} − (A/M)_As e^{iq·τ_As}];
  charge vertex Z* (defined at zero macroscopic field = proper); proper oscillator at ω_T for L and T motion; R̃_NN, R̃_NZ, ε (= eq:5.5-eps);
  screened R_NN = q²|a_N|²/(μΩ(ω² − ω_L²)), S_NN = πq²|a_N|²/(μΩω_L) δ(ω − ω_L): TO pole cancels, weight unchanged; naive 1/|ε|² gives
  zero at ω_T and ∞ at ω_L. Numbers: (A/M)_Ga 1.001072/u, (A/M)_As 1.001046/u, Δ 2.59e-5/u, a_N(q→0) = 9.4e-4; phase term dominates
  above q ≈ 0.02 eV; q_min = ω_L/v_max = 13.7 eV, |a_N| = 0.61 (650×) along [111], 4.5 at 100 eV; LA vertex 144.8 per cell, unscreened

- Keyresult Coulomb-line classes (RPA/TDH ring level, n_g = 2, one phonon in the final state): external (q + G, ω) → (ε⁻¹)_G0(q, ω);
  vertex (Q + G, ~ω_Qν) → screened DFPT g; pair (q + Q + G, ω ∓ ω_Qν) → W^scr(q + Q, ω ∓ ω_Qν); phonon line as a bridge = ionic part of
  the total ε. Proof by topology (tree of loops + one cycle; D.27); classes survive q → 0 and Q → 0. Consequences: DFPT g + external ε⁻¹

  never ζ_eff with L. Scalar (n_e, 𝒯_e) mixes via R̃_{n_eZ}, R̃_{𝒯_eZ}; ALP in B₀ as a source current along B₀; ALP spin rows do not mix
  without SOC (→ 10.7).

   lattice" is implemented as "proportional to n_Z (any lattice), or electron density at a clamped lattice", consistent with 4.4.

   8.4 writes q + Q explicitly. 13.x should pick a symbol.
6. **Spin-row statement.** "Spin-flip pairs feel the direct kernel (4.9) but not the exchange kernel" is stated in words; 4.9 does not use
