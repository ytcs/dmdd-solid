## The reader now knows
- ⟨E_st⟩(E_R, r̂_R) = Σ_b P_b E_st,b over end basins (eq:19.4-mean); only end points enter; E_d is the p_FP = ½ crossing, not a stored energy.
- Si per-defect energies (7.4): close FP 6.8–7.5 eV, separated V + I 8.1–9.7 eV, bond defect 2.6–3.0 eV; a recoil just above the lowest
  threshold that leaves a close pair stores > ½ of its energy.
- Sharp-threshold limit p_FP = Θ(E_R − E_d(r̂)); Dinmohammadi et al. use it as an ionization threshold (Eq. (8)); in a calorimeter crossing
  E_d gives a deficit, the ionization threshold is the binary/kernel threshold of 19.2.
- p_surv (as 16.6) covers t_pr → readout; in-cascade recombination is inside P_b. Computational connection: quench protocol of 19.1, E.11,
  tables E.23; Sassi et al. scans (no quench, 20 ps), minimum thresholds 18 (Si) / 13 eV (Ge) are potential properties.
- Counting equation eq:19.4-count (Kinchin–Pease rules: displaced above E_d, replacement when projectile keeps < E_d; no binding; electronic
  loss removed → argument T_d); hard spheres → eq:19.4-KP, N_FP = T_d/2E_d exactly (derived).
- Screened TF cross-section of 19.3 (x^{1/3} continuation, dσ ∝ T^{-4/3}dT near threshold): ξ_d = 0.70 (±3e-3 for E_d = 12.5–36 eV), 0.72 at
  10 E_d. NRT formula (0.8) as quoted by Nordlund et al. Eq. (1).
- Limit Check 19.4a: NRT 11–14 % above the screened count for T_d ≥ 10E_d, 20 % below hard spheres; NRT within 10 % only for T_d ≤ 8.2E_d.
- Binding and in-cascade recombination both lower the count; metals: 1/3–1/4 of NRT survive (Nordlund); Si/Ge recombine much less (Sassi).
- Defect energy loss eq:19.4-loss: ⟨E_st⟩/E_R = (ξ_d/2)(T_d/E_R)p_surv E_FP/E_d; data fix only ξ_d p_surv E_FP/E_d; Si T_d/E_R = 0.70 (10 keV),
  0.49 (100 keV) from Lindhard.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Overlap with 19.5 (parallel writing).** 19.5 derives the exact Y_ph identity and its first-order form (eq:19.5-identity,
   eq:19.5-first), the exact bound with conditions (a)–(d) (eq:19.5-bound) and the widening (eq:19.5-widen). 19.4 now uses 19.5's symbols
   and numbers (g_L 1.067, f_ion 0.346, 1.154, 0.031) and states the first-order form inline with a forward pointer, displaying only the
   bracket (eq:19.4-fDF) and the solved first-order bound (eq:19.4-bound). Conditions (a)–(d) are listed in both sections. Suggest at the
   chapter review: keep the list in 19.4 (first use) and shorten 19.5's to a pointer, or vice versa. 19.5 l.152 "Section 19.4 applies the
   bound" reads as a back-pointer from a later section, which is fine.
2. **ξ_d = 0.70 vs NRT 0.8.** The book's own screened count (Kinchin–Pease rules, TF cross-section of 19.3, no binding) gives 0.70; NRT's 0.8
   is stated by Nordlund et al. as a correction for realistic potentials. Different counting rules (no replacement, binding) give other
   factors; the text states that the factor depends on cross-section and rules. A derivation reviewer should check eq:19.4-count and the
   solver (hard-sphere check exact to 1e-4; MC check 35.13 ± 0.05 vs 35.11 at 100 E_d, 3.62 ± 0.015 vs 3.60 at 10 E_d).
3. **Numbers differing from the research return (WP5-v4 §6.3 / WP7-E-v3 §3.2).** p_surv E_FP/E_d bounds: book 0.20 (Si, 10 keV)/0.28
   (100 keV)/0.23 (Ge) vs return 0.21/0.20, because the book uses Lindhard T_d/E_R (0.70/0.49) for Si and the measured Pb yield 0.3 (T_d/E_R =
   0.7) for Ge, where the return used E_dam/E_R = 0.65/0.8 [E]. p_surv: the return's ≲ 0.47 (Si) used E_FP/E_d = 9/20; with the book's E_d =
   36 eV (average FP threshold, 7.4) and E_FP 6.8–9.7 eV the constraint is p_surv ≤ 0.73–1.04 at 10 keV and none at 100 keV. The return's Ge
   p_surv ≲ 0.66 used an unsourced 6 eV Ge pair energy; the book prints no Ge pair energy. The 0.055/0.064 bounds agree.
4. **Ge one-sided limit.** 1.645σ gives 0.0638, 2σ 0.0644; both 0.064. The return used 2σ.
5. **NOTATION**: E_oth was not introduced (aligned on 19.5's x_NR, x_ER). ξ_d is new (ξ_M, ξ_C exist) ⚑. Y_ph's first use is now 19.4
   (NOTATION says 19.5); f_DF's first use 19.4 confirmed.
6. **Lindhard g_L vs Luke g_L**: 19.4 writes g_L for the Luke gain "without argument" and names the clash in one parenthesis; the clash
   remains flagged.
7. The Ge Luke gain for surface events (±2 V on the faces; a surface event drifts through part of the potential) is not used: the Ge bound
   is quoted only at 𝔯_coll = 1 (point on Limit Check 19.4b).
8. Sassi 2022's average energy loss curves (Fig. 1) are not tabulated, so the MD stored energy against E_R cannot be overplotted; a data
   request or digitization would give 19.4a a molecular-dynamics comparison.
