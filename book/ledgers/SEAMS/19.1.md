## The reader now knows
- What the response hands over: the line ω_e = 0 of the joint response (weight 1 − P_Mig, mass M_κ + δM_κ, 12.1); Migdal events
  move the nucleus with the same lattice factor and add ω_e to the Chap. 18 cascade. Si: 1 − P_Mig differs from 1 by < 1e-3 below
  16 eV, 3 % at 500 eV (quoted from 12.1).
- Small x_R: the response's phonons are the final state; all heat; absorbed thermal phonons carry negative heat; net lattice energy ω.
- Exact kick in the Wigner picture (eq:19.1-wigner): 𝒲_+ = 𝒲_0 with p_I → p_I − q (positions unchanged, any q, any potential).
  Weyl symbol of H = Σp²/2M + E^ad is H_cl, so each phase-space point gains E_R + q·p_I/M_κ (eq:19.1-dH) = D of 11.4.
- Keyresult "Recoil hand-over": classical propagation of 𝒲_+ on E^ad reproduces eq:11.4-IA term by term (exact mean E_R and width
  σ_ω); misses c₃ (eq:D.32-c3; share ∝ x_R^{-1/2}) and quantum corrections after the kick. Harmonic Wigner function = positive
  Gaussian (zero-point at T = 0, coth widths at T > 0); anharmonic zero-point by path-integral sampling (E.17).
- Si consequences: σ_ω = (E_R⟨ω⟩)^{1/2} = 0.72 eV = 5.7 % of E_d at 12.5 eV; recoil direction scatter (E^kin/E_R)^{1/2} = 1.6° at
  12.5 eV (E^kin = 10.2 meV); threshold as a smooth step: p_FP(E_R, r̂_R) = probability that the end state lies outside the basin of
  the perfect crystal; E_d(r̂_R) = lowest crossing of ½ (eq:19.1-Ed); zero-width limit = eq:7.4-Ed; measured 12.9 ± 0.6 eV.
- Regime parameters (all derived as definitions; numbers estimates at the 0.1 criterion): cage amplitude ℓ_R = (2E_R/M⟨ω⟩²)^{1/2};
  λ_dB/ℓ_R = π⟨ω⟩/E_R exactly (mass-independent); η_R = |Φ³|ℓ_R/3Φ² (η_anh with ℓ_R), corrections ~η_R²; displacement E_R vs E_d;
  Massey ξ_M = E_g ℓ_int/v_N, nonadiabatic ~e^{−ξ_M}, ℓ_int ≈ 1 Å (bulk gap overstates adiabaticity of close collisions → 19.2).
- Four regimes (Fig. 19.1b): quantum (E_R < π⟨ω⟩/√0.1), classical in-basin (to E_d; BO-MD controlled; all heat; dynamics adds only
  the created-phonon spectrum), displacement cascade (> E_d), nonadiabatic. No wide harmonic-and-classical window in Si; the hand-over
  does not need one.
- WE 19.1 (we:19-boundary), Si: M⟨ω⟩² = 11.24 eV/Å²; classical boundary 0.41 eV (x_R 9.9); at 1 eV λ_dB 0.054 Å, ℓ_R 0.42 Å, (λ/ℓ)² 0.017;
  harmonic boundary ℓ_R 0.31 Å at 0.54 eV, η_R² = 0.18 at 1 eV; E_d 12.5 (⟨111⟩) / 36 eV (average FP); ℓ_R 1.49 Å at 12.5 eV; v_N 9.27 km/s,
  bond in 25 fs vs 67 fs shortest period; σ_ω 0.72 eV; ≈ 300 phonons; ξ_M = 15 at 20 eV (v_N 11.7 km/s), e^{−ξ} 3e-7; e^{−ξ} = 0.1 at
  0.87 keV, ξ = 1 at 4.6 keV; masses (v_max 2.650e-3): 28, 32, 154, 262 MeV, 1.3 GeV.
- MD acceptance tests (computational connection, E.11 method, E.23 specification): surface along the path (threshold to the ~0.7 eV
  step width; SW +4–6 eV along ⟨111⟩; head-on turning points 1.49 Å at 20 eV, 0.83 Å at 200 eV; splice complete above threshold
  turning points); kicked zero-point ensemble, exact classical propagation for quadratic H, zero-point leakage where anharmonic
  (tens of meV per disturbed atom); stored energy between quenched minima (Wigner start 3⟨ω⟩/4 = 31 meV/atom, 126 eV per 4096 atoms;
  potential error few tenths eV at 7 %; one shared table for 19.4 and 19.6); harmonic test: mean energy per mode |q·e_κs|²/2NM_κ,
  sums to E_R, equals the energy-weighted phonon content of 11.3's harmonic response; electronic friction below the binary threshold
  replaced by close-collision excitation events (19.2, E.23).
- Partition time t_pr = 10–100 ps (collisions ≲ 0.1 ps; cascade ≲ 1 ps; thermal activation frozen at mK; tunneling/later phonons; drift
  later, 20.1).
- Keyresult "Partition of the recoil energy" (eq:19.1-partition, eq:19.1-heat): ω = E_lat + E_st + ω_e; E_st = E^ad_min(end) −
  E^ad_min(perfect) (configurational); E_lat = vibrational energy above the perfect crystal's zero-point energy, contains the change of
  zero-point energy of the end basin (part of the heat, not sensor-absorbable, booked in 19.5); after the cascade ω = N_eh E_g + E_heat + E_st,
  E_heat = E_lat + (ω_e − N_eh E_g), reducing to eq:18.1-energy for an electronic deposit. Derived by energy conservation per trajectory.
- E_st ≠ E_d (point back to 7.4); joint law P(E_heat, E_st, N_eh | E_R, r̂_R, T) (E.23), with P_casc at near-gap seeds; correlations
  matter.
- Below E_d: E_st = 0, ω_e negligible (e^{−ξ_M} = 5e-9 at 12.5 eV), E_heat = E_lat = ω; Si: every electron-following recoil from dark
  matter below ≈ 150 MeV is pure heat; charge only from the Migdal entry and the soft tail (19.2). Lindhard = E_st ≡ 0 + continuum
  stopping (19.3). Balance law (19.5) starts from eq:19.1-partition.
- Chapter opening (ch19.tex): 200 MeV → E_R,max 21 eV in Si, 12 km/s, first neighbor within 20 fs; Lindhard built for faster ions, gapless
  electrons, no stored energy.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Title.** The spine's "The recoil hand-over" was replaced by "Nuclear recoil on the Born–Oppenheimer surface" (pattern 21: names the
   content). Revert if the spine title is preferred.
2. **E_heat in App. E.23.** E.23 calls E_heat "lattice heat". 19.1 defines E_lat (lattice share) and E_heat = ω − N_eh E_g − E_st (all
   phonon energy, lattice plus cascade), which keeps the meaning of 18.1's E_heat. E.23's joint table P(E_heat, E_st, N_eh) is
   consistent with this; only its phrase "lattice heat E_heat" (l.17) should read "heat E_heat" or "lattice share E_lat".
3. **ℓ vs ℓ_int.** E.23 writes the Massey length as bare ℓ and ξ_M; 19.1 writes ℓ_int. Align E.23 at the App. E pass (bare ℓ is the
   Lindhard logarithm in NOTATION).
4. **E_d definition.** 7.4 defines E_d deterministically (single trajectory); E.11 quotes "the lowest crossing" of p_FP. 19.1's
   eq:19.1-Ed (lowest crossing of ½ for the kicked zero-point ensemble) reconciles both and reduces to eq:7.4-Ed at zero width. NOTATION's
   p_FP row says "against P_FP of E.23", but E.23 uses p_FP: the P_FP mention can be dropped.
5. **E.11's zero-point remark.** E.11 says zero-point motion "does not matter for defect production" above 10 eV (mean thresholds). 19.1
   states that it sets the step width (σ_ω ≈ 0.7 eV, 5.7 % at 12.5 eV). Not contradictory, but E.11 could add "for the mean threshold".
6. **Regime numbers are estimates.** The boundaries (classical 0.41 eV, harmonic 0.54 eV, nonadiabatic 0.87/4.6 keV) depend on the 0.1
   criterion, the single-site cage estimate with ⟨ω⟩ and the bond-coordinate Φ³/Φ² of 5.7, and ℓ_int = 1 Å; the text says so.
   The research return quoted 0.38 Å at 1 eV with ⟨ω⟩ = 45 meV and σ_ω 0.67/0.95 eV at 10/20 eV; the book's ⟨ω⟩ = 40.9 meV gives
   0.42 Å and 0.64/0.90 eV (not printed at 10/20 eV; printed at 12.5 eV).
7. **Fig. 19.1(a) is schematic** (hand-drawn paths); its printed numbers are sourced (rec19.log 6.3 fs; tab:E-md-si 0.83 Å; 7.4
   6.8–7.5 eV).
8. **Migdal double counting** is stated once, by pointer to 12.1-counting; the yield separation stays 19.2's (12.1 commitment).
