## Open questions / for the editor
1. **m_min^ac is wrong in NOTATION and in the spine's 9.2 entry**: they give 2ω_th/(c_ν v_max). The edge is ω_th/[2c(v_max − c)]
   ≃ ω_th/(2cv_max), a factor 4 smaller (lower limit q = ω_th/c must not exceed 2m_χ(v_max − c)). Checked against 1910.10716 Eq. (36)
   (20 keV at 1 meV, c = 10⁻⁵, their v_max = 840 km/s: our form gives 17.9 keV; the NOTATION form 71 keV) and against WP8-errata §6–7
   (u₋/u₊ = m_min^ac/m_χ). 11.1 states the correct form with a one-line derivation (eq:11.1-edge). Please fix the NOTATION row and the
   9.2 brief (WE 9.2 for Si at 1 meV: 6.1 keV with c_LA,max).
2. **WE 11.2 as specified sits outside the validity of the correction.** At 3 m_min^ac the first-order factor is −0.36; the full ratio is
   0.149. The WE shows this failure and quotes 10× and 44× for contrast. The κ_η correction is within 10 % of the full rate only from
   ≈ 9 m_min^ac; target independence within 10 % only from 44 m_min^ac (not "above the edge").
3. κ_η = 3.14 is the c → 0 value (u₊ = v_max); with u₊ = v_max − c it is 3.09 at silicon's speed. NOTATION row should say so.
4. c_NR1 (17.5's symbol) is written out in 11.1 as ⟨Σ_ν(q̂·ê_ν)²/c_ν⟩ and not named. The editor may prefer moving its first use to 11.1.
5. App. F: the three box rows use the existing six-column format; the 𝔗 point is written at the end of the "chain" column and the plot
   spec at the end of the "validity" column. The spine's F table also lists LU4′ (Anderson Θ_D) and L-EW1 (Rayleigh bound) without
   boxes; no rows were added for them — please say whether non-box rows belong in F-rows.tex.
6. Limit 11.1a's "range of validity corrected": 2205.02250 applies Eq. (30) up to q = 2π/a; the PBE comparison gives 10 % only to
   0.48 q_BZ. The box uses PBE force constants (5.2), not measured dispersion; PBE c_eff 8.78 km/s vs 8.956 measured.
7. Limit 11.1c dotted curve: the second-order expansion in Δ_C converges slowly (Δ_C/(C₁₂ + C₄₄) = −0.40 for Si: 0.40 % vs 0.67 %);
   the caption says so.
8. Fig. 11.1 normalization: σ_p = 10⁻⁴⁵ cm² (light, q₀ = αm_e) and 10⁻⁴⁰ cm² (heavy); orientation-averaged; ω_th = 1 meV.
9. Diamond C_ij: McSkimin–Andreatch 1972 (Haxhijaj ref. 63, Table III "Expt." row; footnote letter-to-ref mapping a→63 assumed from
   the order of refs 63–65, not checked in the PDF). Ge as 5.3 (ref. 65). Diamond lattice constant 3.567 Å from 1901.07569 Table I.
10. Room-temperature tensors are used for c̄_LA and Θ_D; the text notes that an elastic Θ_D is a T → 0 value only if the tensor is.
11. The chapter opening says the acoustic channel reaches masses "fifty times smaller" than the electronic one (0.32 MeV vs 6.1 keV at a
    1 meV threshold).
