## The reader now knows
- Electronic energy E^el_f of a prompt final state (zero in 𝒫_ad; zero-phonon energy of the excitation otherwise, Ẽ_m for a pair);
  lattice energy E_f − E^el_f. Joint response S_ab(q;ω_l,ω_e) (eq:12.1-joint) = 8.7's resolved response sorted by the two energies;
  marginal ∫dω_e S(q;ω−ω_e,ω_e) = S(q,ω) (eq:12.1-marginal); PSD at every point where labels are good; double rate d²R/dω_l dω_e
  (eq:12.1-rate). Label validity: window O(θ_ep); Dyson convention places δ part at (0, Ẽ_m), fp/PV at ω_l = ±ω_Qν.
- Why zero-phonon (relaxed) and not vertical ω_e: relaxation energy goes to phonons (WE 12.1).
- Sectors in the plane at T = 0 (eq:12.1-sectors, underbraced): 𝒫_ad on ω_e = 0, 𝒫_0 on ω_l = 0, 𝒫_+ in the quadrant. Where each row
  sits (n_e: following/clamped/sideband; n_κ: Chap. 11 on ω_e = 0, Migdal off it; S_eκ: Born-charge on ω_e = 0, Migdal–recoil
  interference off it, |S_eκ|² ≤ S_ee S_κκ pointwise).
- Force operator in density components (eq:12.1-force); Migdal amplitude (eq:12.1-amp): nucleus keeps q − p, electrons excited by n_e(p).
  Crystal-frame Condon (lattice at q − p; KKL Eq. (3), Berghaus Eq. (3.8)) vs atom/nucleus frame (lattice at q); difference is the
  Herzberg–Teller term, relative order p/q.
- Keyresult eq:12.1-Skk (underbraces boost / vertex / electrons / lattice): S^ee_GG′(k,ω) clamped density response matrix,
  S^ad_{κ;GG′}(Q,ω) electron-following response between n_κ(Q−G), n_κ(Q−G′). Bare vertex × full response (screening inside S^ee).
- Estimate P_Mig ≈ (E_R/⟨ω_e⟩)(δM_κ/M_κ) from the drag-mass first moment (eq:8.6-fsum-k): 2×10⁻⁶ at q = 50 keV in Si.
- Fig. 12.1 (model: valence, point pseudo-ion Z = 4, diagonal G, RPA+LF KKL tables, incoherent T = 0 lattice, crystal frame): Si,
  q = 50 keV: E_R = 47.8 meV, 2W = 1.57, e^{−2W} = 0.21; P_Mig = 1.86×10⁻⁶ (21 % zero-phonon); half below 4.4 eV, 89 % below 10 eV,
  mean ω_e 5.9 eV; mean ω_l 48.4 meV = E_R + ⟨p²⟩/2M, ⟨p²⟩^{1/2} = 5.6 keV; corrections v_Np/ω_e ≈ 2×10⁻³, (ω_l/ω_e)² ≈ 10⁻⁴.
- WE 12.1 (we:12-oscillator), model of we:8-sectors: exact Migdal comb at ω_e = 1.0463 eV, ω_l = m × 65.5 meV, fractions
  0.097/0.227/0.265/0.205/0.119/0.055, mean ω_l 0.153 eV; ground-surface entry: ω_e = 1.17 eV, Poisson(1), mean 64.1 meV, total
  1.234 eV vs exact 1.199 eV; equal-frequency excited surface: Poisson((qℓ₀)² + S_HR = 3), mean 0.192 eV; Condon on computed surfaces
  P = 3.52×10⁻⁶, ZPL fraction 0.056 vs 0.097; x-dependent derivative coupling: P = 4.00×10⁻⁶, fractions 0.104/0.236/0.267/0.201/0.113/0.051,
  mean 0.148 eV. The +12 % of we:8-sectors = this Herzberg–Teller term.
- Four published forms as truncations (tab:12-truncations): Ibe (atom, full boost), KKL (diagonal, impulse, soft, 4α), Berghaus
  (diagonal, harmonic lattice at q − k − G), Liang (virtual phonon, 1/(ω_e² − ω²)). Off-diagonal G ≠ G′ = local fields (factor 1.5 in
  the electron-recoil rate at 10 MeV, 10.2) dropped by all crystal forms. Atomic formula on crystal valence = wrong electrons.
- Double counting: zeroth moment of the incoherent n_κ is 2π𝓃_κ; the ω_e = 0 line keeps 2π𝓃_κ(1 − P_Mig) (eq:12.1-zeroth) with mass
  M + δM (pointing to 8.6's first prescription); a Migdal event carries the whole recoil (48.4 vs 47.8 meV) plus ω_e. Lattice factor of a
  Migdal rate below the impulse onset = multiphonon (same 𝒦 for both pieces). Neutron-calibrated yields contain Migdal ionization.
- Gapless targets: labels via 𝒫^qp_{n,m} and bin bound; metallic L ∝ ω_e makes ∫ω_e^{-3} diverge → failure of the sudden expansion,
  full denominator (phonon propagator), ω_e ≲ ω_D belongs to the e–ph response (6.3, 6.4); S^ee intraband screening of the moving ion;
  superconductors: hierarchy reversed, pair breaking through emitted phonons.

## Open questions / for the editor
1. E.18 writes bare S(q;ω_l,ω_e); the book form is S_ab(q;ω_l,ω_e) with operator subscripts (S_κκ for the Migdal entry). Align at
   App E pass 2.
2. The lattice argument q − p (crystal-frame Condon) is the literature's choice (KKL, Berghaus); the atom gives q. The text says valence
   electrons lie between and calls the difference Herzberg–Teller, O(p/q). No calculation of the size in a crystal exists here.
3. Fig. 12.1's P_Mig = 1.86×10⁻⁶ is a labelled valence model (point pseudo-ion Z = 4); 12.3 owns the real spectra. Its electronic first
   moment (1.1×10⁻⁵ eV) is not compared with E_R δM/M because the model's δM is not the all-electron one.
4. E.18 asserts that the diagonal-G approximation errs at order one; Berghaus et al. state the off-diagonal ELF is small. 12.1 states
   only that the off-diagonal elements carry the local fields and points to E.18; adjudicate at the Ch12 review.
5. The neutron-yield rule points to 19.2 (planned "Electronic excitation by the recoil"); 19.2 must take it.
