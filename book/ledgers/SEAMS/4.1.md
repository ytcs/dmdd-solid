## The reader now knows
- Setup (eq:4.1-Hext): H_tot = H + O_bφ_ext(t), φ_ext a c-number (eV for dimensionless O_b), switched on from t = −∞, target in the
  Gibbs state. A real plane-wave potential φ_ext e^{iq·r} + c.c. on every electron = n_e(q)φ_ext + n_e(q)†φ_ext*; the dark matter between
  plane waves supplies only the n_e(q) term (Ṽ(q)n_e(q)/V, eq:A.3-vertex).
- Derivation: von Neumann equation; interaction picture w.r.t. the full interacting H (contrast with H₀ of 2.4); first Dyson term;
  trace cyclicity → general Kubo formula δ⟨A⟩(t) = −i∫_{−∞}^t dt′⟨[A(t), O_b(t′)]⟩φ_ext(t′) (eq:4.1-general); t − t′ dependence from
  2.5 stationarity (fulfils 2.5's promise).
- Momentum selection: in a translation-invariant target ⟨O_a(q)†⟩ responds to O_b(q); the h.c. term does not (needs 2q); in a crystal
  q + G components respond (local fields, → 4.4), and the h.c. term mixes only if 2q ∈ reciprocal lattice. Working definition of G with
  \cref{sec:3.1}.
- Keyresult Kubo formula (eq:4.1-kubo): δ⟨O_a(q)†⟩(t) = V∫dt′R_ab(q, t − t′)φ_ext(t′), R_ab(q, t) = −(i/V)Θ(t)⟨[O_a(q,t)†, O_b(q,0)]⟩
  — term for term eq:A.7-R. Retarded; any H, any T. 1/V → intensive; induced density ripple e^{iq·r}∫R_eeφ_ext + c.c. → R = induced
  density per unit potential energy, eV². Literature χ_AB / C^R_AB (no 1/V; A for O_a†); minus-sign coupling flips the sign; → App. B.2.
- Frequency form (eq:4.1-Romega) = eq:A.7-R; ω always ω + i0⁺, derived from switching φ_ext ∝ e^{−iωt+ηt} (same 0⁺ as 2.4).
  Reality (eq:4.1-reality): R_ab(q, t)* = R_ab(−q, t), R_ab(q, ω)* = R_ab(−q, −ω) for O(−q) = O(q)†; with inversion/TR, Re R_ee even,
  Im R_ee odd in ω.
- Computational connection (one paragraph): R_ee of silicon from Kohn–Sham states, independent-particle sum + RPA/TDDFT (→ 4.4, 4.6,
  App. E.6); electron gas closed form (→ 4.5).
- Keyresult Lehmann representation (eq:4.1-lehmann) = eq:A.7-lehmann; names "resonant term" (pole at E_f − E_i) and "antiresonant term"
  (pole at −(E_f − E_i)). PV part = off-shell, polarizability (link to 2.4); δ part = on-shell. Im R_aa = −(π/V)[𝒮_{O_a}(ω) − 𝒮_{O_a†}(−ω)]
  (eq:4.1-ImR) with 2.5's transition sums; Im R_aa < 0 for ω > 0 at T = 0; at T > 0 detailed balance → FDT left to 4.2.
- Absorbed power (eq:4.1-power): P = −(ωφ₀²/2)V Im R_bb = (π/2)ωφ₀²[𝒮(ω) − 𝒮(−ω)], checked against eq:2.1-harmonic with Ô = φ₀O_b/2;
  Im R = dissipative part; bosonic-DM absorption rate P/m_φ with φ₀² = 2ρ_χ/m_φ² (→ Chap. 8).
- Static limit: VR_bb(0) = −2Σp_i|O_fi|²/(E_f − E_i) (E_f ≠ E_i terms cancel); = 2E⁽²⁾/φ₀² at T = 0; negative (target yields); at
  T > 0 isolated vs isothermal response differ by a β-term from degenerate matrix elements (one sentence, cited to Kubo 1957).
- Analyticity (eq:4.1-Rz): R(z) analytic for Im z > 0 from causality + stability (bounded commutator); physical = boundary value from
  above; Lehmann poles on the real axis at −i0⁺; order of limits V → ∞ before η → 0⁺ turns the comb into a branch cut (same window as
  the golden rule, 2.1); continuation into the lower half plane: resonance poles, e^{−iz_p t} decays only for Im z_p < 0; damped
  oscillator poles on |ω| = ω₀, critical damping at −iω₀, overdamped on the imaginary axis; plasmon (4.5) and quasiparticles (4.7) are
  such poles. Fig. 4.1 (fig:4-poles).
- High-frequency tail (eq:4.1-tail): R = ⟨[O_a†, O_b]⟩/(Vz) + ⟨[O_a†, [H, O_b]]⟩/(Vz²) + O(z⁻³), derived; density commutes with density
  → 1/z²; coefficient = f-sum (named, → 4.3); KK named (→ 4.3, App. C.5), not derived.
- WE 4.1 (we:4-oscillator, eq:4.1-osc): O = λ(d + d†); commutator a c-number −2iλ² sin ω₀t; VR = 2λ²ω₀/((ω + i0)² − ω₀²); Lehmann with
  n_B + 1 and n_B → n_B cancels (T-independent); classical check λ² = 1/(2Mω₀), Newton with force −φ_ext; static −2λ²/ω₀ and
  E⁽²⁾ = −λ²φ₀²/ω₀; damping Γ_w (energy decay rate): VR = 2λ²ω₀/(ω² + iΓ_wω − ω₀²), peak 2λ²/Γ_w (10 λ²/ω₀ at Γ_w = 0.2ω₀), Re R
  sign change at resonance, tail 2λ²ω₀/ω².
- WE 4.2 (we:4-twolevel, eq:4.1-twolevel): VR = 2Δ|O₁₀|²tanh(βΔ/2)/((ω + i0)² − Δ²); at T = Δ/2 factor 0.762 (0.881/0.119 from 2.5),
  VR(0) = −1.523|O₁₀|²/Δ; T ≫ Δ: −|O₁₀|²/T (Curie law, spin ½ in a field); saturation vs oscillator (matrix elements grow with n);
  tail coefficient 2Δ(p₀ − p₁)|O₁₀|²; validity: oscillator linear at any strength (higher commutators vanish); two-level needs
  |O₁₀φ₀| ≪ width (weak drive of App. D.3), else Rabi oscillation.
- Chapter 4 opening: 2.00e23 cm⁻³ valence electrons (App. A.2), screening, collective oscillation near 16.6 eV (→ 4.3), golden rule puts
  everything in S, exact states unknowable → exact constraints (causality, moments, screening, dark photon in medium 1.4) and organized
  approximations; one orienting sentence (weak field → Green's functions, diagrams, excitons).

   close to it; 4.3 owns the number.
