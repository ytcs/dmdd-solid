## The reader now knows
- Kramers degeneracy at every k from inversion × time reversal; two doublets of opposite parity at ±Δ_D at a TRIM; velocity odd under
  inversion → only the interband block M(κ) = Σκ_iM_i survives (eq:7.3-block); degeneracy ⇒ MM† ∝ 1 ⇒ quaternion form ⇒ by SO(4) basis
  rotations and an SVD M = Σv_iκ_iσ_i; keyresult Dirac Hamiltonian H = Σv_iκ_iα_i + Δ_Dβ, ε± = ±(Σv_i²κ_i² + Δ_D²)^{1/2} (eq:7.3-H) =
  Geilhufe Eq. (1.1); v_D (literature v_F), Δ_D half-gap; sign of a v_i irrelevant (chirality).
- Kane two-level model of 3.3 = massive Dirac cone: Δ_D = E_g/2, v_D = |v_cX| = 4.7×10⁻³ = 1.8 v_max; edge mass Δ_D/v_D² = m_eE_g/E_P
  (eq:3.3-kane2 without the 1); GaAs 0.066 m_e, within 2 % of the measured 0.067. Narrow-gap III–V semiconductors are massive Dirac materials with v_D ≈ 1.8 v_max.
- Weyl nodes named: Δ_D = 0 splits into ±Σv_iκ_iσ_i, opposite chirality; Dirac semimetal (Na₃Bi, Cd₃As₂) vs Weyl semimetal (P or T broken);
  band inversion as sign of Δ_D; ZrTe₅ near the transition; computed gaps 24–35 meV.
- Momentum cutoff Λ_D (hard, on |κ|): where O(κ²) terms bend the bands; states outside belong to the background. g_deg = 2 per cone.
- DOS 𝒩_D(E) = g_deg E(E² − Δ_D²)^{1/2}/(2π²v_D³) (eq:7.3-dos); v_D³ → v₁v₂v₃; parabolic edge, E² far above; no Fermi surface → no TF screening.
- Thermal carriers n_c = g_deg(Δ_DT/2πv_D²)^{3/2}e^{−Δ_D/T} (eq:7.3-nc) = eq:3.3-ni with m = Δ_D/v_D², E_g = 2Δ_D; ZrTe₅-like edge mass 0.053 m_e,
  Δ_D/T = 0.60 at 300 K, 45 at 4 K, n_c ≈ 1e-5 cm⁻³ (exact integral within 4 %).
- Pair continuum ω ≥ (v_D²q² + 4Δ_D²)^{1/2} (eq:7.3-edge), by convexity at k = −q/2; gapless edge v_Dq; contrast with the gas.
- Kinematics (fig:7-dirac): v_D > v_max ⇒ no scattering at any mass (III–V narrow-gap out; absorption fine); v_D ≪ v_max ⇒ m_min = 4Δ_D/v_max²
  = 8.9 keV; ZrTe₅-like v_D = 7.6e-4 = 0.29 v_max: 10.5 keV (numerical); 9.4 keV along slowest axis, 12.7 keV along fastest;
  direction dependence → daily modulation (credit Geilhufe).
- ε_bg: remote-band (clamped) dielectric constant; ε = ε_bg − v_C R⁰_D (eq:7.3-epsbg): bare v_C on the Dirac response, ε_bg additive;
  placement matters for rates (→ 16.1).
- α̃ = α/(ε_bg v_D) (eq:7.3-alpha) as scale-free ratio of Coulomb to kinetic energy; literature folds g in (Hochberg Eq. (2.5)).
- RPA result stated (eq:7.3-eps = eq:D.22-eps, derived in D.22): interband only, overlap ∝ q² cancels Coulomb 1/q², log UV divergence
  cut by Λ_D, Lorentz invariant s = ω² − v_D²q²; = Hochberg Eq. (2.6) with Λ → e^{−5/6}Λ_D (0.43).
- ε₂ (eq:7.3-Im = eq:D.22-Im): edge square-root onset; flat g_deg α̃ ε_bg/6 deep in the continuum; Na₃Bi optics agree ~10 % (per Hochberg).
- Static ε(0,0) (eq:7.3-static = eq:D.22-static); log per pair-energy interval; doubling Λ_D changes ε/ε_bg by g α̃ ln2/3π (< 3 % ZrTe₅);
  ε_bg/Λ_D split not unique; consistent ε_bg excludes the states inside Λ_D.
- Limit box (fig:7-gapless-limit): gapless formula (Hochberg Eq. (2.7), hard cutoff); derivation by expanding the log, −5/18 integral;
  leading correction −6Δ_D²/(v_D²q² − ω²); within 0.1 (units g α̃/6π) for v_Dq > 7.5Δ_D, 3.7Δ_D with correction; ZrTe₅-like Dirac region
  ends at v_DΛ_D = 11.5Δ_D. Gapless static log growth, no screening length.
- Validity: g α̃ ln(v_DΛ_D/max(Δ_D, v_Dq)) ≪ 1; coupling weakens at small q (running); α̃ ≳ 1 open (→ 16.1).
- Anisotropy by rescaling; ε_ii(0,0) = ε_bg,ii + (g α/3π)(v_i²/v₁v₂v₃)[ln(2Λ̃/Δ_D) − 5/6] (eq:7.3-aniso), Λ̃ energy cutoff; per-axis
  coupling αv_i²/(ε_bg,ii v₁v₂v₃).
- Computational connection: v_i, Λ_D from DFT bands (E.1); Δ_D needs QP correction or measurement (E.3); ε_bg from DFPT (E.4) or RPA
  (E.6), which include the Dirac pairs at the computed gap → exclude Dirac bands or subtract eq:7.3-static; ZrTe₅ inputs → E.20.
- WE (we:7-zrte5): Geilhufe ISIF7 values v = (1.1, 0.44, 0.91)×10⁻³, Δ_D = 15.6 meV, Λ = (0.23, 0.216, 0.1) Å⁻¹, κ = (308.4, 20.75, 126.1),
  g = 2; v_iΛ_i = 0.499, 0.188, 0.180 eV; Λ̃ = 0.180 eV (Geilhufe Eq. (2.21)); isotropic: v_D = 7.6e-4 (geometric), ε_bg = 50.5 (harmonic),
  α̃ = 0.19, g α̃ = 0.38, Λ_D = 236 eV = 0.12 Å⁻¹; Hochberg: α̃ = 0.20 (their 0.80 = g α̃ with g = 4), κ̃ = 25.3; ε(0,0)/ε_bg − 1 = 0.093,
  ε(0,0) = 55.2; 0.081 at q = 2Δ_D/v_D = 41 eV, 0.053 at Λ_D/2 = 118 eV; expansion parameter 0.66; never gapless; per axis +9.8, +1.57,
  +6.7 (3.2, 7.6, 5.3 %), couplings 0.065, 0.155, 0.109; DFPT κ already contains the Dirac term (double count at the few-% level).

## Open questions / for the editor
1. **β clash.** D.22 (and so 7.3, per its symbols) writes the Dirac matrix as bare β; NOTATION reserves β for the inverse temperature (4.7,
   τ ≤ β) and E.1 already renamed β_mix to avoid bare β. C.15 writes the Dirac α matrices as `\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{\mathrm{D}}`. Suggest
   one spelling for Dirac matrices book-wide (e.g. α_i, β_D, or the C.15 form) and a sweep of D.22 and 7.3 together. 7.3 never uses β as
   inverse temperature.
2. **𝛔 as pseudospin.** NOTATION row 102 has 𝛔 = electron Pauli vector (1.4). In 7.3 σ_i act on the two states of a Kramers doublet,
   which with spin–orbit coupling are not pure spin states. Accept with a clash note, or rename (τ is taken; maybe 𝛔̃).
3. **g_deg for ZrTe₅.** Geilhufe Table 2 gives g = 2; Hochberg Table 1 gives g = 4 (their α̃ = 0.80 includes it). 7.3 uses 2 (one cone at
   Γ, one four-component spinor) and states both. 16.1 and E.20 should use the same value.
4. **Spine "Geilhufe Eq. (1.1)".** Eq. (1.1) is only the dispersion; the parameters are Tables 1 and 2 of that paper. 7.3 cites Eq. (1.1)
   for the dispersion and the tables for the numbers. Hochberg's "eq. to fix": Eq. (2.6) (gapped ε), (2.7) (gapless), (2.5) (α̃ with g).
5. **Isotropic averaging** (geometric-mean v, harmonic-mean ε_bg, Λ̃ = min v_iΛ_i) is a convention of the worked example, named as such;
   the per-axis numbers are given beside it. 16.1 may prefer the per-axis form.
6. **Keyresult duplication.** D.22 boxes eq:D.22-eps; 7.3 states it unboxed as eq:7.3-eps and boxes the Dirac Hamiltonian instead. The
   editor may move the box to the chapter.
7. **Na₃Bi optics (≈10 %)** is credited to Hochberg et al. (who cite the measurement); the primary optical paper is not in book.bib.
8. **Worked example numbering.** With 7.2 unwritten the example prints as 7.3 (spine: 7.4); it is referred to only by \cref.
10. **Dirac points off TRIMs.** The derivation is at a time-reversal-invariant momentum with parity; Na₃Bi and Cd₃As₂ have their nodes on a rotation axis, not at a TRIM. They are cited as examples of the classification only.
11. **Cutoff conventions.** Geilhufe Eq. (3.10) has the same −5/3 as eq:7.3-eps, so their Λ̃ is our hard cutoff v_DΛ_D; Hochberg's Λ in (2.6) is e^{−5/6} times ours, and their (2.7) already absorbs the factor (so the limit box cites (2.7) with no rescaling).
9. **Λ_D range in the text.** "0.1 to 0.23 Å⁻¹, or 200 to 450 eV" for ZrTe₅ (Geilhufe Table 1); Hochberg's 0.14 keV is mentioned nowhere
   in the text (only Table values in the WE comparison) — fine to add if 16.1 needs it.
