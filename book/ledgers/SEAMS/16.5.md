## Open questions / for the editor
1. **Du et al.'s (E_eff/E_0)² = 4.84.** The book states that in EMT the local-field ratio is eq:16.5-floc (close to one) and
   that the factor compensates their parameter set; after the factor their continuum carries oscillator strength 4.85 against
   3.85 for the whole donor in the silicon valleys. This is a new, checkable statement about a published normalization
   (computed in gos16_5.py). Derivation review: confirm the f-sum reference (cubic average of 1/m*) and that their data fit
   (1.8e18, 3.4e17) cannot be read as isolated-donor ionization.
2. **Line strengths are model values.** No measured Si:P oscillator strengths were fetched; the book brackets the EMT
   envelope (upper) and a Gaussian central-cell model (r_c 3.2/6.3 Å). A measured 2p0/2p± oscillator strength would fix WE 16.5.
3. **Limit Check 16.5a design.** The planned comparison was "dopant absorption" vs the fuller result; the box compares the
   published first-order form with the Clausius–Mossotti (Maxwell Garnett) medium of the same donor model against 𝓃_D. The
   line-center curves depend on an assumed width (4.2 µeV measured minimum; 0.1 meV illustrative). Accept or rule.
4. **𝓃_c ≈ 3e18** is "≳ 3e18 for uncompensated Si:P" as quoted by Du et al. (their Refs. 22–24, unread). The spine's
   𝓃_D/𝓃_c ≈ 0.3–0.6 follows. A primary citation (e.g. Rosenbaum et al., Löhneysen) could replace "quoted by".
5. **Clausius–Mossotti densities** (2.1e19 CC, 6.3e18 EMT) exceed 𝓃_c: stated as evidence that pairs/clusters drive the
   transition. The named Herzfeld (polarization-catastrophe) criterion is not cited (unread); the text derives it without the name.
6. **F row Fsize partly unsized** (impurity band). Per PARTII the row belongs in state/DEFERRED-COMPUTE.md "## Unsized leading
   corrections (App. F)"; the brief confines this writer to book/, so it is not added there (DC-51 already covers the
   calculation).
7. **ε_host = 11.4** (liquid-helium constant of 7.4) is used throughout 16.5; Du's curve in Fig. 16.5 is drawn with 11.4 (they
   take |ε(0,0)|, value unstated; with 11.7 their curve would be 0.95 of the drawn one).
8. The misprint ratios reproduce CW-era values to two digits (light mediator at 10 MeV: 1.05 here).
9. map16.py: Limit Check 16.5a is the only 16.5 box (label lim:16.5a, as planned); rerun the map at the chapter end.
