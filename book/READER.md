# Reader

**Primary persona.** A first-year PhD student entering dark-matter direct detection, from either a
particle or a condensed-matter background.
- **Assumed:** undergraduate quantum mechanics (Dirac notation, time-independent and time-dependent
  perturbation theory, harmonic oscillator, hydrogen atom, spin), classical electromagnetism, statistical
  mechanics (ensembles, Bose and Fermi distributions), special relativity, linear algebra, complex
  analysis at the level of contour integrals, Fourier analysis.
- **Not assumed:** graduate solid-state physics, many-body theory, quantum field theory, density-functional
  theory, astrophysics beyond the textbook level.

**Secondary personas, used in mock-reader review:**
- (b) a particle physicist who knows QFT but no solid-state physics;
- (c) a condensed-matter theorist who knows DFT and many-body theory but no dark-matter physics.

Persona (a) sets the pace. Personas (b) and (c) flag condescension and errors in their own field.
- **Also assumed:** a basic idea of what dark matter is and why it is searched for; the book does not re-argue the evidence at length.
