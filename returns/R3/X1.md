Conventions: D1 eq. (1) (= D2 eq. 2.1 on shell, $M_{D2}=M_{D1}/\sqrt N$). Upper sign: emission of phonon $-Q$; lower sign: absorption of $+Q$.
$\ell_Q=(2M_{\rm cell}\omega_{Q\nu})^{-1/2}$, with $M_{\rm cell}=\sum_\kappa M_\kappa$. Acoustic polarization at long wavelength: $e_{\kappa\nu}=\hat e\sqrt{M_\kappa/M_{\rm cell}}$, $\omega_{Q\nu}=c_\nu(\hat Q)|Q|$.

## 0. Results in brief
1. **Limit [D].** For fixed $(k,q)$, $c\neq v$, and $Q=\eta\hat Q$ with $\eta\to0^+$:
$$\boxed{M^{(2)}_{cv\nu\pm}\to i\ell_Q\,\rho_{cv}(k,q)\;\hat e\cdot\Big[q+\frac{D^c\hat Q\mp c_\nu P_c}{\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c_\nu}-\frac{D^v\hat Q\mp c_\nu P_v}{\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c_\nu}\Big]\;+\;O(\eta^{1/2})}\tag{X1}$$
   Here $v_c=\nabla\varepsilon_c(k+q)$ and $v_v=\nabla\varepsilon_v(k)$ are band velocities, $P_n=\langle n|p|n\rangle$ ($=m_ev_n$ for local potentials),
   and $D^n$ is the frozen-strain (lattice-frame) deformation-potential tensor of band $n$, which may depend on $\hat Q$.
   Equivalently, (X1) can be written with the lab-frame intraband slope $\Xi^n=D^n+v_n\otimes P_n$ (eq. X2).
   Both R1 forms are special cases: D1's $i\ell(\hat e\cdot q)\rho$ needs $D^c=D^v=0$ and $c_\nu\ll|\hat Q\cdot v|$; D2's needs $\Xi\equiv0$.
2. **IR [D].** Inside the PAER window ($\omega\le E_{\min}(q)-\delta_0$) the region $Q\to0$ is **kinematically empty**: $|Q|\ge Q_{\min}\simeq\delta_0/(v_{\max}+c)$.
   So the soft terms give **no infrared feature** in ε₂^{PA}. Outside the window (ZPL open) the soft factor is a Low-type bremsstrahlung factor.
   For deformation-potential coupling in 3D the soft sideband goes as $E\,dE$ at T = 0 and as $T\,dE$ at T > 0. It is integrable, the Franck–Condon exponent $2W$ is finite, and no Bloch–Nordsieck cancellation is needed.
   A BN-type cancellation (coherent-state sum rule) exists structurally. It becomes necessary only for piezoelectric coupling (log), in 2D at T > 0, or in 1D.
3. **Numerics [D].** In the window, no subtraction is needed. Use detuning-form denominators for the two resonance-prone families and drop the acoustic Γ point. The small-Q patch and subtraction formulas (§6) are needed only at the edge or outside the window.
4. **Ward identity [D].** There is an exact on-shell "gauge" identity, eq. (W). The ASR plus this identity fix every piece of the limit except $D^c$ and $D^v$, which are material constants. These generically dominate: $|D|/c_s\sim10^2$ keV ≫ q.
5. **Other branches [D].** Non-polar optical modes: finite and analytic, no 0/0. Fröhlich LO: the $1/Q$ pieces cancel (the pair is neutral), leaving a finite limit that depends on direction. Piezo-acoustic: $|M|^2\propto Q^{-3}$, a true BN log outside the window.

## 1. Hypotheses
- H1: D1 §1, i.e. harmonic phonons, linear coupling, and the unrestricted $m$ sum. The unrestricted sum is essential: all identities below use completeness.
- H2: long-wave form along each ray, $g_{nn}(k,\eta\hat Q)=i\ell_Q\,\eta\,\hat Q\cdot\Xi^n(k;\hat Q)\cdot\hat e+O(\eta^2\ell)$. This holds for deformation-potential and dynamical-quadrupole coupling, which make Ξ non-analytic in $\hat Q$. It fails for piezo and Fröhlich coupling (§8).
- H3: the ASR on g. For the acoustic branch at Q = 0, $g_{mn}(k,0)=(\varepsilon_n-\varepsilon_m)A_{mn}$ with $A=-i\ell\,\hat e\cdot p$. Hence $g_{nn}(k,0)=0$. It holds with non-local PPs that move with the ions, because $\sum_\kappa\partial_\kappa V=-i[p,V]$.
- H4: $c$ at $k+q$ and $v$ at $k$ are non-degenerate. For a degenerate multiplet (Si $\Gamma_{25'}$), see §3, last item.
- H5: the limit is taken in the function (1) at fixed $(k,q)$ with energy denominators. No energy conservation is imposed; §5 explains why.

## 2. Derivation of (X1)  [D under H1–H4]
**Interband terms** ($m\neq c$ in family A, $m\ne v$ in family B). The denominators tend to $\varepsilon_c-\varepsilon_m$ and $\varepsilon_v-\varepsilon_m$, which are O(1). The $\omega_Q$ and $O(Q)$ corrections to g and ε vanish as $Q\to0$. By H3:
$-\sum_{m\ne c}A_{cm}\rho_{mv}+\sum_{m\ne v}\rho_{cm}A_{mv}=[\rho,A]_{cv}+(A_{cc}-A_{vv})\rho_{cv}=i\ell\,\hat e\cdot(q-P_c+P_v)\rho_{cv}$.
This uses $[e^{iq\cdot r},p]=-q\,e^{iq\cdot r}$. It reproduces REF-A §4 and REF-B §4 exactly.

**Intraband terms** (both are 0/0):
$T_c=\dfrac{g_{cc}(k+q,Q)\rho_{cv}}{\varepsilon_{c,k+q+Q}-\varepsilon_{c,k+q}\pm\omega_Q}\to i\ell\frac{\hat Q\cdot\Xi^c\cdot\hat e}{\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c}\rho_{cv}$ and
$T_v=\dfrac{\rho_{cv}(k+Q,q)g_{vv}(k,Q)}{\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{v,k+Q}\mp\omega_Q}\to-i\ell\frac{\hat Q\cdot\Xi^v\cdot\hat e}{\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c}\rho_{cv}$.

**Sum (lab-frame form):**
$$M\to i\ell\rho_{cv}\Big[\hat e\cdot(q-P_c+P_v)+\frac{\hat Q\cdot\Xi^c\cdot\hat e}{\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c}-\frac{\hat Q\cdot\Xi^v\cdot\hat e}{\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c}\Big].\tag{X1'}$$
**Decomposition [D, §4]:**
$$\Xi^n=D^n+v_n\otimes P_n.\tag{X2}$$
The term $v_n\otimes P_n$ is the Galilean (rigid-frame) part of g: $(\varepsilon_{nk}-\varepsilon_{n,k+Q})A_{nn}\to i\ell(Q\cdot v_n)(\hat e\cdot P_n)$. $D^n$ is the comoving strain coupling.
Inserting (X2) and using sympy S1, $\frac{(D+vP)}{v\pm c}=P+\frac{D\mp cP}{v\pm c}$. The $P_n$ terms cancel against the interband $-P_c+P_v$, which gives (X1).

**Remarks:**
- At band extrema ($v_n=P_n=0$), (X1) reduces to $i\ell\rho_{cv}\,\hat e\cdot[q\pm(D^c-D^v)\hat Q/c_\nu]$. The relative sign between recoil and deformation is opposite for emission and absorption. Both expressions are what the coherent-state (displacement) structure requires.
- **Degenerate multiplet (H4 relaxed) [D].** Diagonalise the $O(Q)$ k·p splitting of the multiplet along $\hat Q$, giving states $m_j$ with velocities $\hat Q\cdot v_{m_j}$. Then $T_v\to-i\ell\sum_j\rho_{cm_j}\,(\hat Q\cdot\Xi^{m_jv}\cdot\hat e)/(\hat Q\cdot v_{m_j}\pm c)$, with $\Xi^{m_jv}$ the intra-multiplet slope. The same algebra applies; the result is now a matrix in the multiplet.

## 3. Check 1: 1D two-band deformation-potential model (exact)  — `soft_limit_1d.py`
**Model.** A continuum electron ($m_e=1$, $G=1$) in a monatomic lattice with an even ion form factor $W(K)$, truncated to plane waves $\{\kappa,\kappa-1\}$ at every k-point.
- This truncation keeps every identity exact: p is diagonal, $e^{iqx}$ maps PW n to PW n, and the Q = 0 displacement vertex equals $[A,H]$ element by element.
- The phonon is $u_j=\ell e^{iQX_j}$, so $\langle\kappa+Q+n'|\delta V|\kappa+n\rangle=-i\ell(Q+n'-n)W(Q+n'-n)$.
- It contains the physical rigid part and an $O(Q)$ deformation part, including the $G=0$ "volume" term $-i\ell Q\,W(0)$.

**Tests and results:**
- **A.** $\Xi^n$ is extracted from $g_{nn}(\kappa,10^{-25})$. $D^n=\langle n|\partial_\eta H|n\rangle$ is computed by Hellmann–Feynman for homogeneous strain at fixed reduced k. Result: $\max|\Xi-(D+vP)|=10^{-26}$. This confirms (X2).
- **B.** The full two-band sum (1) at $Q=\pm10^{-20}$ was compared with (X1′) for emission and absorption, both directions, and $c=0.02$ (subsonic phonon) and $c=3$. This is 16 cases at two $(k,q)$.
  - Maximum error: $1.5\times10^{-18}$.
  - The R1 forms miss by O(1): D1 by 1.3–9.3 and D2 by 0.02–8.0.
  - The result depends strongly on direction. For example, $M/\ell=-1.83i$ versus $-2.87i$ for $\hat Q=\pm1$, and $+8.3i$ where $v_v+s\sigma c\to-0.016$, close to the Cherenkov cone.

## 4. Check 2: exact Ward identity (comoving frame)  [D]
**Identity.** Take any one-body operator $s(Q)$ that carries momentum Q, and set $S=s\,(a_Q+a^\dagger_{-Q})+{\rm h.c.}$ Then $H'=e^{iS}He^{-iS}$ has coupling $g'=g+i[S,H_0+H_{ph}]$. For the on-shell amplitude (1) with an unrestricted $m$ sum,
$$M[g]=M[g']+i\langle c,k+q+Q|[s,e^{iq\cdot r}]|v,k\rangle,\qquad g'_{mn}(\kappa,Q)=g_{mn}+i\,s_{mn}\big(\varepsilon_{n\kappa}-\varepsilon_{m,\kappa+Q}\mp\omega_Q\big).\tag{W}$$
*Proof.* $\langle f|i[S,H_0]|I\rangle=iS_{fI}(E_I-E_f)$. On shell, $(E_I-E_f)/(E_i-E_I)=-1$ identically, and this holds even when both vanish (the 0/0 terms). So family A gives $-i(S\rho)_{fi}$ and family B gives $+i(\rho S)_{fi}$.
This is a form of the field-redefinition invariance of the on-shell amplitude. It needs completeness, which is the Pauli theorem of D1 §1. Test C of the script verifies (W) at **finite** Q ($Q=0.1$ and $-3/7$): residual $5\times10^{-61}$.

**Comoving choice.** Take $s=\ell\,\hat e\cdot\tfrac12\{e^{iQ\cdot r},p\}$, the local translation by the displacement field. Then:
- (i) The recoil term is $i\ell(\hat e\cdot q)\rho_{cv}(k,q+Q)$. This is the one-phonon term of $e^{iq\cdot u(r)}$, and it is regular.
- (ii) In $g'$ the rigid term $-u\cdot\nabla V$ is cancelled exactly, because $i[S,V]=+u\cdot\nabla V$. What remains is the strain coupling ($-\partial_iu_j\,p_ip_j/m_e$ plus the strain of V, which together define $D^n$) and the velocity coupling $i[S,H_{ph}]=-\tfrac12\{\dot u,p\}$.
- Hence $g'_{nn}=i\ell Q[\hat Q\cdot D^n\cdot\hat e\mp c\,\hat e\cdot P_n]+O(Q^2\ell)$, and the interband $g'_{mn}=O(Q\ell,\omega_Q\ell)\to0$.
- The interband part of $M[g']$ therefore vanishes, and the intraband part gives exactly the $D$ terms of (X1). (X1) follows independently of §2.
- Consistency of the two routes requires (X2), which test A confirms in the model.
- The 1D model contains the volume term, the form-factor-slope term and the kinetic strain, so (X2) is not built in by construction.

## 6. (b) Infrared structure of the Q integral
**6.1 Kinematics: no soft region inside the window [D].** On shell, the intraband denominators equal the zero-phonon detunings:
- family A, m = c: $\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{c,k+q}\pm\omega_Q=\omega-\Delta_{cv}(k,q)$;
- family B, m = v: $\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{v,k+Q}\mp\omega_Q=-[\Delta_{cv}(k+Q,q)-\omega]$,

with $\Delta_{cv}(k,q)=\varepsilon_{c,k+q}-\varepsilon_{vk}\ge E_{\min}(q)$. In the window both have $|\cdot|\ge\delta_0$. Their left-hand sides, however, are at most $|Q|(v_{\max}+c_{\max})$. Hence:
$$|Q|\ \ge\ Q_{\min}(q,\omega)\simeq\frac{E_{\min}(q)-\omega}{v_{\max}+c_{\max}}\quad\text{on shell, in the window}.$$
In this region $|T_c|,|T_v|\le|g_{nn}|\,|\rho_{cv}|/\delta_0=O(Q\ell)$, with no enhancement.
**Numbers [E].** For Si with $\delta_0=0.1$ eV and $v_{\max}\approx10^6$ m/s, $Q_{\min}\approx30$ eV ≈ 0.015 Å⁻¹ (about 1% of $2\pi/a$).
**Conclusion.** The soft terms produce **no IR feature** in ε₂^{PA}(q,ω) inside the window, at any T: the absorption branch obeys the same identity. D1's "integrable $Q^{-1}$" statement is true but moot there.
At $q\equiv k_c-k_v$ the threshold $E_g+\omega_{Q\to0}$ (D1 §6.4) lies where $E_{\min}(q)=E_g$, so the window is empty there.

**6.2 Window edge [E].** As $\omega\to E_{\min}(q)^-$, $Q_{\min}\to0$. The A(m = c) and B(m = v) terms become near-resonant: they are the tail of the real ZPL transition.
For a 3D minimum of $\Delta_{cv}(k,q)$ in k, $\int d^3k\,(\Delta-\omega)^{-2}$ gives ε₂^{PA} ∝ $(E_{\min}-\omega)^{-1/2}$. This is integrable in ω.
The D1 §5.4 margin $\delta_0$ (REF-A/B: with the Hadamard finite part) remains the right treatment. It is not an IR problem in Q.

**6.3 Outside the window: soft-phonon bremsstrahlung [D].** When the ZPL is open, the factorised limit (X1) is a Low-type soft theorem: $M\to(\text{soft factor})\times M^{(1)}$, with $M^{(1)}=\rho_{cv}$. The pieces are:
- the eikonal denominators $(\hat Q\cdot v_n\pm c)^{-1}$, which are the analogue of $1/(p\cdot k)$;
- the "charges" $D^n$, emitted from the outgoing electron (c) and the removed valence electron (v) with opposite signs;
- the contact term $\hat e\cdot q$, the lattice recoil $e^{iq\cdot u}$ (Lamb–Mössbauer).

Power counting (sympy S3) uses $\ell^2\propto1/Q$ and a strain coupling ∝ Q, the **Adler zero** of the translation Goldstone mode guaranteed by the ASR:
- $|M|^2(n+1)\,d^dQ\propto Q^{d-2}dQ$ at T = 0 and $Q^{d-3}dQ$ at T > 0.
- In 3D this is finite in both cases. The soft sideband of one transition is $P(E)\propto E$ (T = 0) or $\propto T$ (T > 0), for subsonic carriers.
- Its total weight, $2W_{\rm soft}=\frac{\Omega}{(2\pi)^3}\int d\Omega_Q|\hat e\cdot K_\pm|^2\,Q_p^2/(4M_{\rm cell}c)$, is dominated by the **upper** cutoff, with no IR sensitivity.
- Here $K_\pm\equiv q+\frac{D^c\hat Q\mp cP_c}{\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c}-\frac{D^v\hat Q\mp cP_v}{\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c}$.

**Supersonic carriers ($|v_n|>c$, generic away from extrema).** The soft factor has poles on the Cherenkov cone $\hat Q\cdot v_n=\mp c$, where the angular integral is non-integrable ($\int dx/x^2$). These poles are exactly where the intermediate ZPL state is on shell and emits a real phonon. This is D1 §5.2's real cascade, already contained in $\Gamma^{(1)}\times$ the branching ratio, and must not be counted again: regulate with the carrier width and keep the finite part. It is not an IR divergence.

**6.4 Franck–Condon and BN [D in the eikonal approximation: linearised dispersion, no recoil of the carriers on soft phonons, linear coupling].**
- In the comoving frame (§4) the soft field couples linearly to a classical current. This is a piecewise-constant pair "trajectory", the Bloch–Nordsieck model, so the soft phonons are emitted in a coherent state.
- Then $\sum_n|\langle n|{\rm coh}\rangle|^2=1$: ZPL weight $e^{-2W_{\rm soft}}$, one-phonon weight $2W_{\rm soft}e^{-2W_{\rm soft}}$, and so on. At O(g²) the soft sideband's zeroth moment is cancelled exactly by the soft part of the ZPL reduction. These are the Fan Z, mixing and vertex cuts of D2/REF-A; the AH tadpole is not involved.
- This is the BN structure. The inclusive rate (ZPL plus soft sideband within a resolution $\Delta\gg$ soft energies) is IR-safe by construction.
- In 3D deformation-potential coupling there is **nothing divergent to cancel**: $2W$ is finite and the cancellation is only bookkeeping. This complements REF-B 3c: the zeroth moment is conserved within the soft sector only.
- The cancellation becomes essential when the exponent in S3 is ≤ −1. That happens for piezo coupling in 3D (log at T = 0, §8), deformation coupling in 2D at T > 0 (the Mermin–Wagner divergence of the DW factor), and 1D at T = 0 (orthogonality catastrophe and power-law edge). In those cases the ZPL vanishes and only the inclusive quantity is defined.
- The $e^{-2W}$ that D1 §5.3/§6.2 call DW contains the recoil part $\langle(q\cdot u)^2\rangle$ **and** the deformation and Galilean parts of $K_\pm$, which dominate at band extrema by ~$(D/c\,q)^2\sim10^3$–$10^4$ [E: $D\sim5$ eV, $c_{LA}\approx3\times10^{-5}$, $q\sim1$–4 keV].

## 7. (c) What an implementation must do near Q = 0  [D]
**Inside the window (the production case).** No analytic subtraction is required. Three rules:
- **R1. Detuning-form denominators.** On shell, (1) may be written so that every resonance-prone denominator is a ZPL detuning:
  - A, m empty: $\omega-(\varepsilon_{m,k+q}-\varepsilon_{vk})$, i.e. D2's form;
  - B, m occupied: $(\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q})-\omega$;
  - A, m occupied and B, m empty: keep the energy form, which is bounded by $E_g\mp\omega_Q$ (REF-A §2).

  All four forms are equal on shell. With a smeared δ (width σ), the energy form of the m = c and m = v terms has **spurious soft poles** at $\hat Q\cdot v\pm c=0$, while the detuning form satisfies $|\Delta|\ge\delta_0$ everywhere. The difference is $O(\sigma/\delta_0)$ when both are regular.
- **R2.** Give the acoustic Γ point ($Q=0$, $\ell=\infty$, $g=0$) zero weight. This is exact, because the measure is zero and $Q_{\min}>0$. Take $\sigma\ll\delta_0$, so that grid points with $Q<Q_{\min}$ carry weight $\lesssim e^{-(\delta_0/\sigma)^2}$.
- **R3. Optional, cancellation-free evaluation at small Q.** Use (W) with the comoving s: $M=i\ell(\hat e\cdot q)\rho_{cv}(k,q+Q)+M[g']$.
  This needs $s_{mn}=\ell\langle m,k+Q|\hat e\cdot\frac12\{e^{iQ\cdot r},p\}|n,k\rangle$, which is Wannier-interpolable like the velocity.
  It removes the large rigid pieces that otherwise cancel between the intraband and interband terms. (W) is exact for any s, so no approximation is introduced.

**At the edge shell or outside the window (C3, C8, Ge checks, ZPL-region work).** For $|Q|<Q_p$, replace the grid sum by the analytic eikonal patch (T = 0, emission, per acoustic branch). Write $x_+=\hat Q\cdot v_c+c$ and $E=\omega-\Delta_{cv}(k,q)$. Then
$$\Delta S^{PA}_{\rm patch}=\frac{4\pi}{\Omega}\,\Omega^2\!\int\!\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\sum_{cv}|\rho_{cv}(k,q)|^2\!\int\!\frac{d\Omega_{\hat Q}}{(2\pi)^3}\,\frac{|\hat e\cdot K_+|^2\,E}{2M_{\rm cell}\,c\,x_+^2}\,\Theta\!\Big(0<\frac{E}{x_+}<Q_p\Big),$$
- This uses $\int Q^2dQ\,\ell_Q^2\,\delta(E-Qx)=E/(2M_{\rm cell}c\,x^2)$.
- At T > 0, multiply by $n+1\approx T/(cQ)$ and add the absorption term with $x_-$ and $K_-$.
- Treat cone poles ($x\to0$) with a width and keep the finite part (§6.3).
- Pair the patch with the ZPL factor $(1-2W_{\rm soft})$ when quoting inclusive quantities.
- Choose $Q_p\ll$ the scale where (X1) fails, i.e. $Q_p\ll\min(\delta_{\rm interband}/v,\,1/a)$. Check stability as $Q_p$ is halved.

## 8. (d) Does the rigid-translation constraint fix the combination?  [D]
**What the ASR fixes:** (i) the interband amplitudes at Q = 0, $g_{mn}=(\varepsilon_n-\varepsilon_m)A_{mn}$, and $g_{nn}(k,0)=0$. This gives the term $i\ell\hat e\cdot(q-\Delta P)\rho$.

**What the Ward identity (W) adds** (comoving translation): (ii) the Galilean part $v_n\otimes P_n$ of the $O(Q)$ intraband slope, and (iii) the velocity term $\mp c\,\hat e\cdot P_n$. Neither has a free parameter.

**Combination fixed by symmetry.** The rigid part is "pure gauge" under (W). The physical content of the soft limit is therefore $\{\hat e\cdot q;\ D^c,D^v\}$:
$$K_\pm-q=\frac{D^c\hat Q\mp cP_c}{\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c}-\frac{D^v\hat Q\mp cP_v}{\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c}.$$

**What symmetry does not fix: $D^c$ and $D^v$.** These are material constants from frozen strain. Rotational invariance makes the analytic part symmetric (standard elasticity; not re-derived here) [C as used].
- Special case: the ASR **alone** returns D1's result only if $D^c=D^v=0$ and $c\ll|v|$. It returns $i\ell\hat e\cdot q\,\rho$ at band extrema only if $D^c=D^v$.
- This answers REF-A's remark that "only the sum is meaningful": the gauge-invariant split is (X1)/(X2).

## 9. (e) Optical branches, Fröhlich LO and (for completeness) piezo-acoustic
**Non-polar optical [D].** $g_{nn}(k,0)=\ell_0d^n$ is finite (the optical deformation potential), and the intraband denominators tend to $\pm\omega_0$, so there is no 0/0:
- $M\to\pm\ell_0(d^c-d^v)\rho_{cv}/\omega_0+\sum_{\rm interband}$, analytic in Q. There is no ASR constraint and no IR structure.
- The absorption-side intraband poles occur at finite $|Q|=\omega_0/|\hat Q\cdot v|$. They are a real cascade, excluded in the window by §6.1.

**Polar LO, Fröhlich [D].** The long-range part is $g_{mn}=\frac{iC_F(\hat Q)}{|Q|}\rho_{mn}(k,Q)$, with $C_F\propto\frac{4\pi e^2}{\Omega}\frac{\hat Q\cdot Z^*\cdot e}{\hat Q\cdot\varepsilon_\infty\cdot\hat Q}\ell$ (DFPT-screened).
- Leading order: with equal intraband denominators, $(1/a)[\rho(Q),\rho(q)]=0$. **The $1/Q$ cancels**, because the created e–h pair is neutral.
- Remainder (sympy S2), with $a=\pm\omega_{LO}$ and covariant $\mathcal D=\nabla_k-i\mathcal A_{cc}(k+q)+i\mathcal A_{vv}(k)$:
$$M\to-\frac{iC_F}{a}\Big[\hat Q\cdot\mathcal D\rho_{cv}+\rho_{cv}\frac{\hat Q\cdot(v_c-v_v)}{a}\Big]+iC_F\Big[\sum_{m\ne c}\frac{i\hat Q\cdot\mathcal A_{cm}\rho_{mv}}{\varepsilon_c-\varepsilon_m+a}+\sum_{m\ne v}\frac{\rho_{cm}\,i\hat Q\cdot\mathcal A_{mv}}{\varepsilon_v-\varepsilon_m-a}\Big]+(\text{short-range, finite}).$$
  The first bracket is the pair dipole plus Doppler; the second is the interband polarization.
- The limit is finite and depends on direction, so there is **no IR feature** (∫d³Q of a constant).
- For $|Q|\gtrsim\omega_{LO}/|v|$ the electron and hole radiate separately, giving $|M|^2\propto Q^{-2}$, which is still integrable in 3D.
- The gap $\omega_{LO}$ plays the role of a photon mass: the BN machinery is not needed.

**Piezo-acoustic (GaAs; not Si, which is centrosymmetric) [D power counting, C relevance].** $g_{nn}\approx i\ell\,C_{pz}(\hat Q)$ has no Adler zero.
- The soft factor is $C_{pz}[(\hat Q\cdot v_c\pm c)^{-1}-(\hat Q\cdot v_v\pm c)^{-1}]/Q$. It is the exact QED-bremsstrahlung structure, from the sudden change $v_v\to v_c$ of the current.
- $|M|^2(n+1)d^3Q\propto dQ/Q$ at T = 0 (log) and $dQ/Q^2$ at T > 0. This is a **genuine BN situation**, but only where the ZPL is open: the ZPL line shape near E_dir in C8 has a power-law edge, not a δ, and only inclusive quantities are defined.
