## 0. Conventions fixed here (proposed NOTATION.md updates)
- N0 (sign of q). The DM goes $p\to p'=p-q$. Its matrix element is
  $\langle p'|V(r_\chi-r_e)|p\rangle=V^{-1}\tilde V(q)\,e^{+iq\cdot r_e}$, with $\tilde V(q)=\int d^3x\,e^{iq\cdot x}V(x)$.
  The crystal operator is therefore $\hat\rho_q=\sum_e e^{+iq\cdot r_e}$.
- N1 (**defect in NOTATION**). With $\psi_{nk}=e^{ik\cdot r}u_{nk}/\sqrt N$ normalised over $V$, the matrix element
  $\langle\psi_{m,k+q}|e^{iq\cdot r}|\psi_{nk}\rangle=\int_\Omega u^*_{m,[k+q]}e^{iG\cdot r}u_{nk}$ (with $k+q=[k+q]+G$) is already $O(1)$.
  The factor "$\cdot N$" in NOTATION is wrong. Define
  $\rho_{mn}(k,q)\equiv\langle\psi_{m,k+q}|e^{iq\cdot r}|\psi_{nk}\rangle$ (no $N$). Then $\rho_{mn}(k,0)=\delta_{mn}$.
- N2. $H_{ep}=N^{-1/2}\sum_{kQmn\nu}g_{mn\nu}(k,Q)\,c^\dagger_{m,k+Q}c_{nk}\,(a_{Q\nu}+a^\dagger_{-Q\nu})$, with $g$ exactly as in NOTATION (EPW, screened).
  This follows from $u_{\kappa p}=\sum_{Q\nu}(2NM_\kappa\omega_{Q\nu})^{-1/2}e_{\kappa\nu}(Q)e^{iQ\cdot R_p}(a_{Q\nu}+a^\dagger_{-Q\nu})$ **[D]**.
- N3 (final state). The electron goes from $(v,k)$ to $(c,k')$ with $k'=k+q+Q$ (folded, Umklapp kept in $\rho$).
  Either a phonon $-Q$ is emitted (upper sign, factor $\sqrt{n_{Q\nu}+1}$) or a phonon $+Q$ is absorbed (lower sign, factor $\sqrt{n_{Q\nu}}$).
  Both cases use the same $g(\cdot,Q)$. Energy conservation reads $\omega=\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_{Q\nu}$.
- N4. $S(q,\omega)\equiv\frac{2\pi}{V}\sum_f|\langle f|\hat\rho_q|0\rangle|^2\delta(\omega-E_f+E_0)$ (thermal average over the initial phonon state at $T>0$).
  Then $\Gamma(v)=\int\!\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}|\tilde V(q)|^2S(q,\omega_q)$, with $\omega_q=q\cdot v-q^2/2m_\chi$.
- N5. $|\tilde V(q)|^2=\pi\bar\sigma_e|F_{DM}(q)|^2/\mu_{\chi e}^2$. This is the Born cross-section $\sigma=\mu^2|\tilde V|^2/\pi$ at $q=\alpha m_e$.
- N6. $\varepsilon=1-\frac{e^2}{q^2}\chi^0$, where $\chi^0$ is the proper density response per unit volume. Hence
  $\varepsilon_2=\frac{e^2}{2q^2}S^0$, where $S^0$ is the unscreened (proper) structure factor.

## 1. Amplitude (T1)
**Hamiltonian [D].** Take $H=H_e(r;R)+H_{ion}+H_\chi+H_{int}$. Expand $H_e$ to linear order in the ionic
displacements about the static lattice, and quantise the harmonic phonons. Write the electrons in the
Kohn–Sham quasiparticle basis, so that $H_0=\sum\varepsilon_{nk}c^\dagger c+\sum\omega_{Q\nu}(a^\dagger a+\tfrac12)+p^2/2m_\chi$.
Hypotheses: Born–Oppenheimer, harmonic approximation, independent KS quasiparticles (KS-RPA level; excitons
are neglected, §5.5), a spin-independent DM coupling, no spin–orbit coupling.
The perturbation is $H'=H_{ep}+V^{-1}\tilde V(q)\hat\rho_q$, with
$\hat\rho_q=\sum_{kmn}\rho_{mn}(k,q)c^\dagger_{m,k+q}c_{nk}$.

**Initial and final states.** The initial state is $|i\rangle=|p\rangle|\Phi_0\rangle|\{n\}\rangle$, where $\Phi_0$ is the filled-valence Slater determinant.
The final state is $|f\rangle=|p'\rangle\,c^\dagger_{ck'}c_{vk}|\Phi_0\rangle\,|\{n\}\pm1_{\mp Q\nu}\rangle$.
The second-order T-matrix is $T_{fi}=\sum_I\langle f|H'|I\rangle\langle I|H'|i\rangle/(E_i-E_I+i0)$.
Each vertex moves one fermion. So the only connected routes to a single electron–hole pair plus one phonon are the following four [D].

| path | 1st vertex | 2nd vertex | allowed $m$ | $E_i-E_I$ | sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | DM: $(v,k)\to(m,k+q)$ | ph: $(m,k+q)\to(c,k')$ | empty | $\omega-\varepsilon_{m,k+q}+\varepsilon_{vk}$ | $+$ |
| B | ph: $(v,k)\to(m,k+Q)$ | DM: $(m,k+Q)\to(c,k')$ | empty | $\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}\mp\omega_{Q\nu}$ | $+$ |
| C | DM: $(m,k+Q)\to(c,k')$ | ph: hole $(m,k+Q)\to(v,k)$ | occupied | $\omega-\varepsilon_{ck'}+\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}$ | $-$ |
| D | ph: $(m,k+q)\to(c,k')$ | DM: hole $(m,k+q)\to(v,k)$ | occupied | $\varepsilon_{m,k+q}-\varepsilon_{ck'}\mp\omega_{Q\nu}$ | $-$ |

**Fermionic sign of C and D [D].** Compare with A:
$c^\dagger_m c_v c^\dagger_c c_m|\Phi_0\rangle=-c^\dagger_c c_v|\Phi_0\rangle$ when $m$ is occupied, against
$c^\dagger_c c_m c^\dagger_m c_v|\Phi_0\rangle=+c^\dagger_c c_v|\Phi_0\rangle$ when $m$ is empty.

**Pauli blocking.** In paths A and B the intermediate band $m$ is restricted to empty bands. This is
Pauli blocking. The blocked part ($m$ occupied) reappears as the hole paths D and C, with the opposite sign
and a different denominator.

**Pauli/exchange theorem [D, on shell].** Insert $\omega=\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_{Q\nu}$:
- D equals the A-form expression with $m$ occupied;
- C equals the B-form expression with $m$ occupied.

(Checked symbolically: S1a, S1b.) The many-body amplitude is therefore exactly the one-electron amplitude
with an **unrestricted** sum over $m$:
$$
M^{(2)}_{cv\nu,\pm}(k,q,Q)=\sum_{m}^{\rm all}\Big[\frac{g_{cm\nu}(k+q,Q)\,\rho_{mv}(k,q)}{\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{m,k+q}\pm\omega_{Q\nu}+i0}
+\frac{\rho_{cm}(k+Q,q)\,g_{mv\nu}(k,Q)}{\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}\mp\omega_{Q\nu}+i0}\Big],\qquad k'=k+q+Q. \tag{1}
$$
The full transition matrix element is
$T_{fi}=\frac{\tilde V(q)}{V}\frac{1}{\sqrt N}\sqrt{n_{Q\nu}+\tfrac12\pm\tfrac12}\;M^{(2)}_\pm$ (using $n_{-Q}=n_Q$).

**Consequences of the theorem.**
- The Pauli terms cancel against exchange, and no net Pauli term survives. This matches the forecast:
  the first family has denominators of order $\varepsilon_c-\varepsilon_m$, the second of order $\varepsilon_v-\varepsilon_m$, each shifted by $\pm\omega_Q$.
- Restricting $m$ to empty bands in (1) (the one-electron caricature with naive blocking) is **wrong**.
  It breaks the completeness relation that §6 needs for C1 and C7.
- The equality holds only on shell. Off-shell quantities, such as $\varepsilon_1^{PA}$, need the four-path form or Kubo.

**Disconnected and diagonal terms [D].** Path A with intermediate $I=\Phi_0$ carries
$\langle\Phi_0|\hat\rho_q|\Phi_0\rangle=\sum_{occ}\rho_{nn}(k,q)$. This is nonzero only for $q\in$ reciprocal lattice.
It cancels against the matching piece of path B with $I=f$ (linked-cluster cancellation), and leaves the
diagonal one-electron terms $m=v$ in A and $m=c$ in B that (1) already contains. Phonon-diagonal terms
($Q=0$, $m=n$) form a set of measure zero in the $Q$ sum.

**Umklapp [D].** $k+q$, $k+Q$ and $k'$ are folded to the 1BZ, and the reciprocal vectors are kept inside $\rho$ (N1).
$g$ uses the periodic gauge $\psi_{k+G}=\psi_k$. Momentum is conserved modulo $G$ automatically.

## 2. Rate (T1)
**Golden rule [D].** Use $\Gamma=2\pi\sum_f|T_{fi}|^2\delta(E_f-E_i)$, $\sum_{p'}=V\!\int d^3q/(2\pi)^3$, a spin factor 2
(spin-independent vertices, no SOC; with SOC drop the 2 and sum over spinor bands), electrons at $T=0$
($T\ll E_g$), and thermal phonons. This gives $\Gamma=\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}|\tilde V|^2S^{PA}(q,\omega_q)$ (N4) with
$$
S^{PA}(q,\omega)=\frac{4\pi}{\Omega}\Big\langle\!\Big\langle\sum_{cv\nu,\pm}\big(n_{Q\nu}+\tfrac12\pm\tfrac12\big)\,|M^{(2)}_{\pm}|^2\,
\delta(\varepsilon_{c,k+q+Q}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_{Q\nu}-\omega)\Big\rangle\!\Big\rangle_{k,Q},\tag{2}
$$
where $\langle\langle\cdot\rangle\rangle=N^{-2}\sum_{kQ}=\Omega^2\!\int_{BZ}\!\frac{d^3k\,d^3Q}{(2\pi)^6}$. Bookkeeping:
$V\cdot N^2/(V^2N)=1/\Omega$.

**Rate per unit target mass [D].** Multiply by the DM number density and divide by $\rho_TV$:
$$
\frac{dR}{d\omega}=\frac{\rho_\chi}{m_\chi\rho_T}\frac{\pi\bar\sigma_e}{\mu_{\chi e}^2}\int\!\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}|F_{DM}(q)|^2\,
\mathcal G(q,\omega)\,S(q,\omega),\qquad \mathcal G\equiv\!\int\! d^3v\,f_\chi(v)\,\delta\!\big(\omega-q\cdot v+\tfrac{q^2}{2m_\chi}\big).\tag{3}
$$
$S$ here is the screened total (§3). For an isotropic angular average (standard approximation),
$\mathcal G=\eta(v_{min})/q$ with $v_{min}=\omega/q+q/2m_\chi$, and
$\frac{dR}{d\omega}=\frac{\rho_\chi\bar\sigma_e}{2\pi m_\chi\rho_T\mu^2}\int q\,dq\,F^2\eta\,\bar S(q,\omega)$.
Dimensions check (S4: $[dR/d\omega]=E^{-1}$). The $v_{esc}$ and $v_E$ dependence enters only through $f_\chi$.

**Zero-phonon limit [D].** Replace $M^{(2)}\sqrt{n+\dots}/\sqrt N$ by $\rho_{cv}$ and drop $Q$. Then
$S^{(1)}=4\pi\!\int_{BZ}\!\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\sum_{cv}|\rho_{cv}(k,q)|^2\delta(\omega-\varepsilon_{c,k+q}+\varepsilon_{vk})$.
Rewrite the angular average of $q$ as $\int d^3q'\delta(|q'|-q)/q^2$ and use $\int d^3q'=\sum_{G'}\int_{BZ}d^3k'$.
Eq. (3) then becomes exactly the Bloch crystal form-factor rate:
$$\frac{dR^{(1)}}{d\omega}=\frac{\rho_\chi\bar\sigma_e\alpha m_e^2}{m_\chi\rho_T\Omega\mu^2}\int\frac{dq}{q^2}\eta F^2|f_{\rm cr}|^2,\quad
|f_{\rm cr}(q,\omega)|^2=\frac{4\pi^2}{\alpha m_e^2\Omega}\sum_{cv}\!\int\!\!\frac{\Omega d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{\Omega d^3k'}{(2\pi)^3}\sum_{G'}q\,\delta(q-|k'-k+G'|)|\rho_{cv}|^2\delta(\omega-\Delta\varepsilon),$$
equivalently $|f_{\rm cr}|^2=\Omega q^3\bar S^{(1)}/(2\pi\alpha m_e^2)$. This reproduces the Essig et al. structure (same $\eta/q$, $F^2$, $G'$
sum and $q\,\delta(q-|k'-k+G'|)$). **Open item [E]:** from memory I cannot fix whether that paper's printed
numerical prefactor ($2\pi^3$?) is defined with or without the spin factor and the same $\bar\sigma_e$. The literature
agent should compare it with $4\pi^2$ above. Eq. (3) itself is fixed independently by the FDT anchor in §3:
$S=\frac{2q^2}{e^2}L$, i.e. $\Gamma=\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}|\tilde V|^2\frac{q^2}{2\pi\alpha}{\rm Im}\frac{-1}{\varepsilon}$, the dielectric DM-rate form.

## 3. Response function (T2a, T2b-screening)
**FDT [D].** Write $\varepsilon_e^{-1}=1+\frac{e^2}{q^2}\chi_{ee}$, where $\chi_{ee}$ is the exact retarded electron-density response
of the coupled electron–ion system to a potential acting on electrons only. At $T=0$ and $\omega>0$:
$S=-2{\rm Im}\chi_{ee}=\frac{2q^2}{e^2}{\rm Im}[-\varepsilon_e^{-1}]=\frac{2q^2}{e^2}L$. This holds with or without phonons.

**PA dielectric function [D, KS-RPA].** With $\chi_{ee}=\chi^0/(1-v\chi^0)$, the proper $\chi^0$ at $O(g^2)$ has an absorptive part given by (2) with unscreened DM vertices. So
$$
\varepsilon_2^{PA}(q,\omega)=\frac{e^2}{2q^2}S^{PA}=\frac{8\pi^2\alpha}{\Omega q^2}\Big\langle\!\Big\langle\sum_{cv\nu\pm}\big(n_{Q\nu}+\tfrac12\pm\tfrac12\big)|M^{(2)}_\pm|^2\delta(\varepsilon_{c,k+q+Q}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_{Q\nu}-\omega)\Big\rangle\!\Big\rangle.\tag{4}
$$
- The zero-phonon analogue of (4) is $\frac{8\pi^2\alpha}{\Omega q^2}\frac1N\sum_k\sum_{cv}|\rho_{cv}|^2\delta$, the Adler–Wiser form ($e^2_{\rm Gauss}=\alpha$). This is a consistency check.
- At $T>0$ the absorptive $\varepsilon_2$ also carries $(1-e^{-\omega/T})$ and $f_v(1-f_c)$. Both equal 1 to within $e^{-E_g/T}$.
- The total is $L=\varepsilon_2/(\varepsilon_1^2+\varepsilon_2^2)$ with $\varepsilon_2=\varepsilon_2^{(1)}+\varepsilon_2^{PA}$, inserted in (3).

**Using a screened $g$ inside $\chi^0$ [D].** The DFPT bubble chains that dress $g$ carry momentum $Q$ and frequency $\approx\omega_Q$.
Cutting one of their Coulomb lines does not split the PA diagram, because the phonon line still connects
the two halves. They are therefore proper with respect to the external $(q,\omega)$ line. So screening is
applied once to $g$ (static, in channel $Q$) and once to the DM vertex (via $|\varepsilon(q,\omega)|^{-2}$).
It is not applied twice. Static screening of $g$ is valid because $\omega_Q\ll$ the electronic gap and plasma frequency.

**When static-lattice $\varepsilon_1$ is the correct screening [D under H1–H3].**
- H1: $\omega\gg\omega_{LO}$. The phonon-reducible piece $\chi^0gDg\chi^0$ of $\chi_{ee}$ contributes $\sim(\Omega_{p,ion}/\omega)^2$ to $\varepsilon_1$.
  This vanishes at $q\to0$ in non-polar Si. Its absorptive part sits at $\omega\simeq\omega_Q$, a different final state (single phonon) that is not PAER.
- H2: $\varepsilon_2\ll|\varepsilon_1|$ in the window.
- H3: work to $O(g^2)$.

Then
$L^{PA}=\varepsilon_2^{PA}/[\varepsilon_1^{(0)}(q,\omega)]^2\,[1+O(\varepsilon_2^2/\varepsilon_1^2)+O(g^2)]$.
The $O(g^2)$ corrections to $\varepsilon_1$ (ZPR, PA real part) multiply $\varepsilon_2^{PA}$, giving $O(g^4)$, or multiply $\varepsilon_2^{(1)}$, which is zero in the window.
- $\varepsilon_1^{(0)}$ is the static-lattice (better: ZPR-renormalised) RPA value, from Kramers–Kronig of $\varepsilon_2^{(1)}$ with local fields.
- At $|q|\gg2\pi/a$ the scalar $\varepsilon\to1$, and the matrix $\varepsilon_{GG'}$ matters only for $|q|\lesssim$ a few $2\pi/a$.
- **[C]** Near $E_g$, excitonic (electron–hole ladder) effects enhance $\varepsilon_2^{PA}$ by a Sommerfeld-like factor (Elliott). KS-RPA misses this.

T2b sum rules (C4) are not in my brief beyond §5.3.

## 4. Adiabatic (Herzberg–Teller / DFPT) form (T3b)
**First-order wavefunctions.** Displace the ions along the pattern of mode $(Q\nu)$ with zero-point amplitude, so that $\partial V\to$ the operator whose matrix elements are $g$.
First-order perturbation theory gives:
- $|\partial\psi_{vk}\rangle=\sum_{m}|\psi_{m,k+Q}\rangle\frac{g_{mv}(k,Q)}{\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}}$;
- $|\partial\psi_{ck'}\rangle$ gets components on $(m,k+q)$ with $\frac{g^*_{cm}(k+q,Q)}{\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{m,k+q}}$, from the $-Q$ half of the real pattern.

The derivative of the frozen-phonon transition density $D(\lambda)=\langle\psi_{ck'}(\lambda)|e^{iq\cdot r}|\psi_{vk}(\lambda)\rangle$ is then [D]:
$$
\partial_\lambda D=\sum_m\Big[\frac{g_{cm}\rho_{mv}}{\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{m,k+q}}+\frac{\rho_{cm}g_{mv}}{\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}}\Big]
=M^{(2)}\big|_{\omega_Q\to0}.\tag{5}
$$
**Error of the adiabatic form [D].** $M^{(2)}_\pm-\partial_\lambda D=\mp\omega_Q\sum_m[g\rho/\Delta_{A,m}^2-\rho g/\Delta_{B,m}^2]+O(\omega_Q^2/\Delta^3)$.
The relative error is $\sim\omega_Q/\delta(q,\omega)$. Here $\delta$ is the smallest $|\Delta|$, which (§5.1) equals the distance of
$\omega$ to the nearest open first-order threshold at the same $q$. Emission and absorption are identical in
the adiabatic form, and their difference is first order in $\omega_Q/\delta$. **[E]** For Si, $\omega_Q\le64$ meV.
Deep in the window, where $\delta\gtrsim0.3$ eV, the error is $\lesssim20\%$ in the amplitude. The adiabatic form fails near the window's upper edge.

**What DFPT's projection misses [D].** DFPT (Sternheimer) returns only $P_c|\partial\psi_v\rangle$, the empty-manifold part.
That suffices for the density response, but (5) needs three more pieces:
- (i) $P_v|\partial\psi_v\rangle$, the occupied $m\neq v$ components. These are the **hole path C**. They are physical here, because $c$ lies outside the occupied manifold.
- (ii) all of $|\partial\psi_c\rangle$ for the empty final state. These are **paths A and D** (both $m$ empty and $m$ occupied).
  DFPT never computes them. They require a non-self-consistent Sternheimer solve $(H_0-\varepsilon_{ck'})|\partial\psi_c\rangle=-(1-|\psi_c\rangle\langle\psi_c|)\partial V_{SCF}^{\dagger}|\psi_c\rangle$ with the DFPT $\partial V_{SCF}$.
- (iii) degenerate valence subspaces at $k$ (Si $\Gamma_{25'}$) need degenerate perturbation theory, i.e. a gauge-covariant $D$.

Only path B with empty $m$ comes for free from standard DFPT. Using it alone violates completeness, and so violates C1 (the $q=0$ cancellation) and C7.

## 5. Well-definedness (T1)
**5.1 Resonances [D].**
- The A-denominator vanishes iff $\omega=\varepsilon_{m,k+q}-\varepsilon_{vk}$. This is a first-order DM transition $v\to m$ at the same $(q,\omega)$, followed by a real phonon process.
- The B-denominator vanishes iff $\omega=\varepsilon_{ck'}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}$. This is a first-order transition $m\to c$, followed by real hole–phonon scattering.
- Define $E_{\min}(q)=\min_{k,\,n\,occ,\,m\,emp}(\varepsilon_{m,k+q}-\varepsilon_{nk})\ge E_g$.
- **Theorem:** for $\omega<E_{\min}(q)$ every denominator in (1) satisfies $|\Delta|\ge E_{\min}(q)-\omega$. No $i0$ is needed.
- Note: for DM, $q$ is not small. The zero-phonon channel is closed only where $q$ does not fold near $k_c-k_v$.
  The window is a region of the $(q,\omega)$ plane, not just $\omega<E_{dir}$.

**5.2 Double counting with first order [D].**
- Zero-phonon final states have the initial phonon occupation, so they are orthogonal to the PA final states.
  The cross term $M^{(1)*}M^{(2)}$ is therefore zero at $O(g)$.
- Where first order is open, keeping a linewidth $\gamma$ splits the resonant part into two pieces:
  $|\Delta+i\gamma/2|^{-2}\to\frac{2\pi}{\gamma}\delta(\Delta)+{\rm P}$.
  The $\delta$ part, summed over phonons, equals $\Gamma^{(1)}\times$(phonon branching ratio of the hot carrier). That is already inside $\Gamma^{(1)}$.
  Counting it again is double counting. Only the principal-value remainder is a sideband, and it is $O(g^2/\delta^2)$ relative to $\Gamma^{(1)}$ (C3).

**5.3 $O(g^2)$ zero-phonon renormalisation [D for structure, E for size].** At $O(g^2)$ and $O(\bar\sigma_e)$ the phonon-number-conserving channel receives:
- the Fan–Migdal and Debye–Waller shifts of $\varepsilon_{nk}$ (ZPR; Si gap $\approx-0.06$ eV [E]);
- the $e^{-W}$ dressing of the vertex. This includes the acoustic-$Q\to0$ piece found in §6.2, which is the one-phonon term of $e^{iq\cdot u}$.

These terms interfere with $M^{(1)}$, not with $M^{(2)}$. In the window $M^{(1)}=0$, so they only move the edge
$E_{\min}(q)\to E_{\min}^{ren}(q,T)$. The sideband weight is compensated by the reduction of the zero-phonon
weight (C4). The formula (4) alone does not satisfy the f-sum rule; sideband plus renormalisation does.

**5.4 Prescription for a separately computable sideband [D under §1 hypotheses].** The one-phonon sideband
at $(q,\omega)$ is well defined and computable from (1)–(4), with no $i0$, no subtraction and no renormalisation counterterm, iff
$$\omega\le E^{ren}_{\min}(q,T)-\delta_0,\qquad \delta_0\gg\max(\omega_{Q\nu},\ \gamma_{qp},\ k_BT).$$
- Use renormalised energies consistently in the $\delta$ functions and in the denominators. The difference is $O(g^4)$, except at the edge.
- In $dR/d\omega$, apply the condition pointwise in $q$. The $q$-shell within $\delta_0$ of the edge needs the resonant (linewidth) treatment, and must be reported as a separate systematic.
- Outside the window, only "renormalised first order + principal-value sideband" is meaningful.

**5.5 [C]** Excitonic and local-field corrections to the DM vertex are not included (KS-RPA level).

## 6. Controls
**6.1 C1, optical limit [D].** Expand $\rho_{mn}(k,q)=\delta_{mn}+i\,q\cdot r_{mn}+O(q^2)$.
- *Step 1 ($q=0$ cancellation, S2).* The $\delta_{mn}$ part of (1) gives $g_{cv}/\omega-g_{cv}/\omega=0$ on shell. Hence $M^{(2)}(q=0)=0$ exactly, because $\hat\rho_0=N_e$ is conserved. This needs the $m=v$ term in A and the $m=c$ term in B, i.e. the unrestricted sum.
- *Step 2.* Let $X=iq\cdot r$ and $V=q\cdot v$, with $iR_{ab}(E_a-E_b)=V_{ab}$. Apply the partial fractions S3a and S3b:
$$M^{(2)}=\frac1\omega\Big[\underbrace{\sum_m\Big(\frac{g_{cm}(k,Q)\,q\!\cdot\! v_{mv}(k)}{\varepsilon_{vk}+\omega-\varepsilon_{mk}}+\frac{q\!\cdot\! v_{cm}(k+Q)\,g_{mv}(k,Q)}{\varepsilon_{vk}-\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}\mp\omega_Q}\Big)}_{-q\,\hat q\cdot(S_1+S_2)}+i[g,q\cdot r]_{cv}\Big]+O(q^2).$$
  The commutator vanishes for local $\partial V$. With non-local pseudopotentials it is the same correction as $v=p/m_e+i[V_{NL},r]$, and must be kept for consistency.
- *Step 3.* Insert into (4). The $q^2$ cancels:
$$\varepsilon_2^{PA}(\omega)=\frac{8\pi^2\alpha}{\Omega\omega^2}\Big\langle\!\Big\langle\sum_{cv\nu\pm}(n+\tfrac12\pm\tfrac12)|\hat q\cdot(S_1+S_2)|^2\delta(\varepsilon_{c,k+Q}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_Q-\omega)\Big\rangle\!\Big\rangle,$$
  with $S_1=\sum_m\frac{g_{cm}v_{mv}}{\varepsilon_{mk}-\varepsilon_{vk}-\omega}$ and $S_2=\sum_m\frac{v_{cm}g_{mv}}{\varepsilon_{m,k+Q}-\varepsilon_{vk}\pm\omega_Q}$.

Factor-by-factor comparison with my best statement of Noffsinger et al. 2012 (Hall–Bardeen–Blatt):
| factor | ours | theirs |
|---|---|---|
| prefactor | $8\pi^2\alpha/(\Omega\omega^2)$ | $8\pi^2e^2/(\Omega\omega^2)$ |
| spin | included in the 8 | included in the 8 |
| BZ sums | $1/(N_kN_Q)$ | $1/(N_kN_Q)$ |
| phonon factor | $n+\frac12\pm\frac12$ | same |
| paths | $S_1$ (photon then phonon), $S_2$ (phonon then photon) | same, with all $m$ |
| polarisation | $\hat q\cdot$ | $\hat e\cdot$ |

In a cubic crystal, after summing over $k$, $Q$ and bands, $\langle|\hat q\cdot S|^2\rangle=\langle|\hat e\cdot S|^2\rangle$ ($\varepsilon^L=\varepsilon^T$ at $q\to0$).
The overall sign of $M$ is irrelevant. **[E]** One residual difference: their velocity matrix elements usually omit $[V_{NL},r]$ and $[g,r]$.
**Result:** $q\cdot r$ replaces $e\cdot r$, as forecast. The identity holds only on shell and only with the complete $m$ sum.

**6.2 C7, acoustic $Q\to0$ [D] — surprise, the limit is not zero at finite q.**
*Acoustic sum rule on g.* A rigid translation of all ions, with electrons following, gives $\sum_\kappa\partial_{\kappa}V_{KS}=-\nabla V_{KS}$.
Including non-local parts this is $-i[p,H_0]$. For the acoustic mode, $e_\kappa/\sqrt{M_\kappa}=\hat u/\sqrt{M_{cell}}$, so
$$g^{ac}_{mn}(k,Q\to0)=\ell_Q\langle m|-\hat u\cdot\nabla V|n\rangle=-i\ell_Q(\varepsilon_{nk}-\varepsilon_{mk})\,\hat u\cdot p_{mn},\qquad \ell_Q=(2M_{cell}\omega_Q)^{-1/2}.$$
The EPW/DFPT $g$ must be corrected to satisfy this ASR (the analogue of the dynamical-matrix ASR), because xc-grid errors violate it.

*Commutator form.* Write $G=[A,H_0]$ with $A=-i\ell_Q\hat u\cdot p$, so $G_{ab}=(E_b-E_a)A_{ab}$. By S3c and S3d,
$$M^{(2)}_{ac}(Q\to0)=[e^{iq\cdot r},A]_{cv}+\omega_Q(\ldots)= i\,\ell_Q(\hat u\cdot q)\,\rho_{cv}(k,q)+O(\omega_Q/\delta).$$
So M is the first-order amplitude times the one-phonon term of $e^{iq\cdot u}$. The rigid shift moves the
electron density that the DM sees. It vanishes identically only for $q=0$ (so the CONTROLS statement holds

## 7. Computable recipe (T3a, and the DFPT variant of T3b)
**Inputs.**
- DFT bands; Wannier functions for $N_W$ bands (Si: $sp^3$ valence plus disentangled conduction, $N_W\simeq8$–16).
- DFPT on a coarse $Q$ grid, giving EPW $g^W(R_e,R_p)$, $\omega_{Q\nu}$ and $e_{\kappa\nu}$.
- Enforce the ASR on $g$ (§6.2). Add the Fröhlich long-range part for polar targets (not Si).

**Density vertex at large q, with Umklapp [D].** Define $F_{ij}(R,q)=\langle w_{iR}|e^{iq\cdot r}|w_{j0}\rangle$, which is smooth in continuous $q$ and decays in $R$.
Then
$$\rho_{mn}(k,q)=\big[U^\dagger(k+q)\,\tilde F(k+q,q)\,U(k)\big]_{mn},\qquad \tilde F(\kappa,q)=\sum_Re^{-i\kappa\cdot R}F(R,q),$$
with $k+q$ unfolded and $U$ in the periodic gauge. Umklapp is automatic, because $q$ is never reduced.
- Tabulate $F$ on a $q$ mesh up to $q_{\max}\sim$ a few keV ($\simeq1$–2 a.u.; 1 a.u. $=3.73$ keV). Use FFTs of $w_i^*(r-R)w_j(r)$.
- For $q\gtrsim3$–4 keV, add the PAW or all-electron core reconstruction (pseudo-wavefunctions underestimate high-$q$ form factors).
- Control: $\rho$ from Wannier against direct plane-wave $\rho$ on coarse $(k,q)$.

**Intermediate sum.**
- Within the Wannier manifold, use exact denominators from (1).
- For bands above it, use a Sternheimer remainder with static denominators (error $\omega_Q/\Delta_m$, small for high $m$).
- This needs $|\partial\psi_c\rangle$ and $P_v|\partial\psi_v\rangle$ as in §4, or equivalently a converged band count $N_m$.

**Sampling.** For fixed $q$, sum over $(k,k')$ with $Q=k'-k-q$ folded. Near the edge the $\delta$ in (4) restricts $k$ to valence pockets (Si: $\Gamma$) and $k'$ to conduction pockets (Si: 6 $\Delta$ valleys).
Use adaptive or tetrahedron integration, or Gaussian smearing $\sigma\ll\omega_Q$ with extrapolation $\sigma\to0$.

**Convergence parameters.**
- coarse $k$ (DFT) $8^3$–$12^3$; coarse $Q$ (DFPT) $4^3$–$8^3$;
- fine pocket grids, density equivalent to $60^3$–$120^3$;
- $N_W$, $N_m$ or Sternheimer tolerance;
- $q$ mesh: $|q|$ step and number of angles (or the full 3D $q$ for anisotropy and daily modulation);
- smearing $\sigma$; $\varepsilon_1^{(0)}$ including local fields; window margin $\delta_0$.

**Cost [E].** Per $(q,\omega)$:
$$O\big(N_{v}^{pk}N_{c}^{pk}\,[N_W^3+N_mN_\nu N_cN_v]\big),$$
plus a one-off $O(N_RN_W^2N_q^{tab}N_{FFT}\log N_{FFT})$ for $F$ and the EPW interpolation $O(N_{R}N_W^2N_\nu)$ per $(k,Q)$.
The total scales as $N_q\times N^{pk}_vN^{pk}_c$, roughly $10^3\times10^4\times10^3$ to $10^{10}$–$10^{11}$ flops: remote CPU, not GPU-bound.

**DFPT/adiabatic variant.**
- For each needed $(Q\nu)$, run DFPT for $\partial V_{SCF}$, then non-SCF Sternheimer for $\partial\psi_v$ (full, both projectors) and $\partial\psi_c$.
- Evaluate (5) and attach the $\omega_Q/\delta$ error bar.
