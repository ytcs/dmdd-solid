## 1. The O(g²) diagrams for Π(q,iΩ)

**Building blocks [D].**
- G_n(k,iν) = 1/(iν − ε_nk).
- D_ν(Q,iω_l) = −⟨T A A⟩ = 2ω_Qν / ((iω_l)² − ω²_Qν).
- Density vertex ρ_mn(k,q). Phonon vertex N^{-1/2} g_mnν(k,Q).
- Spin is diagonal at every vertex, so every closed loop carries a factor 2.

Zeroth order:
Π⁰(q,iΩ) = (2/V) Σ_{k,mn} |ρ_mn(k,q)|² (f_nk − f_{m,k+q}) / (iΩ + ε_nk − ε_{m,k+q}).

The terms of order g² in the proper Π are listed in the table. Overall sign: each fermion loop gives (−1), and that sign is
absorbed into the sign convention for Π⁰. Every term below is therefore written relative to Π⁰, with the same
prefactor 2/V.

| label | topology | content |
|---|---|---|
| (SE-p), (SE-h) | Fan–Migdal self-energy Σ_{mm′} on the upper (particle, k+q) or lower (hole, k) line of the bubble. **Band-off-diagonal Σ must be kept** (m ≠ m′). | (2/V) Σ ρ_{mn} G_m Σ^{FM}_{mm′} G_{m′} ρ*_{m′n} G_n |
| (V) | vertex correction: a phonon line joins the particle and hole lines, so it crosses the bubble | (2/V)(1/N) Σ_{Q,ν} ρ_{mn}(k,q) G_m(k+q) g_{m′m}(k+q,Q) G_{m′}(k+q+Q) ρ*_{m′n′}(k+Q,q) G_{n′}(k+Q) g*_{n′n}(k,Q) G_n(k) D_ν(Q) |
| (DW-p), (DW-h) | Debye–Waller tadpole: the A² vertex of H_DW on one line, with a closed phonon loop | static, frequency-independent Σ^{DW}_{mm′} inserted like SE |
| (T) | Hartree tadpole: one phonon at Q = 0 attached to a closed electron loop | ∝ the force at the reference geometry; **= 0 at equilibrium** [D] |
| (X) | phonon exchange: bubble(ρ, g) – D_ν(q mod G) – bubble(g*, ρ*) | Coulomb-proper but phonon-reducible; describes lattice (ionic) polarization |

In compact form:

Σ^{FM}_{mm′}(k,z) = (1/N) Σ_{Q,ν,n} g*_{nmν}(k,Q) g_{nm′ν}(k,Q) [ (n_Qν + f_{n,k+Q}) / (z − ε_{n,k+Q} + ω_Qν) + (n_Qν + 1 − f_{n,k+Q}) / (z − ε_{n,k+Q} − ω_Qν) ]   [D, standard Matsubara sum]

Σ^{DW}_{mm′}(k) = (1/N) Σ_{Q,ν} g^{DW}_{mm′ν}(k,Q,−Q) (2n_Qν + 1)

**Hypotheses.** Harmonic phonons. Linear plus quadratic coupling. Coulomb interaction enters only through screened g (§4) and
through the external 1/ε. Phonon anharmonicity and phonon self-energy are left out: they are O(g²) corrections to D, which
enters at O(g²) already, so they are O(g⁴) in Π.

## 2. Cuts: sideband versus zero-phonon line

**Cutting theorem used [D].** At O(g²), Im Π^R equals the Lehmann sum over exact eigenstates, expanded with Rayleigh–
Schrödinger perturbation theory to O(g²):
- final states |f⟩ whose unperturbed part contains one phonon give the **sideband**;
- final states with no phonon give the **renormalised zero-phonon line (ZPL)**.

H_ep is odd in A and n_q is even. Hence ⟨0-phonon f|n_q|i⟩ is even in g and ⟨1-phonon f|n_q|i⟩ is odd in g.
At O(g²) in |·|² there is **no cross term between the sideband and the ZPL** [D]. Hypothesis: the final states are
non-degenerate between the two sectors. This fails exactly at resonance, §2b.

In diagram terms, the cut through the phonon line and two electron lines gives:
- from (SE-p): |A₁|²;
- from (SE-h): |A₂|²;
- from (V): 2Re A₁A₂*.

The final electron is the *inner* line of Σ, and the outer lines are the intermediate band m. This is why Σ_{mm′} has to be
off-diagonal. The total is |A₁+A₂|², and the forecast holds.
The cuts of (SE), (V) and (DW) that do not cross the phonon line, together with all of (T), make up the ZPL renormalisation.
Diagram (X) has one-phonon cuts with no electron–hole pair (ω ≤ ω_max, §5) and ZPL-type cuts with a virtual phonon.

### 2a. The one-phonon amplitude (with Pauli blocking handled) [D]

Final state: electron (c, k+q+Q), hole (v, k), and either phonon −Q emitted or phonon +Q absorbed. Momentum is conserved
mod G through the Umklapp G contained in ρ and g.

  M^{(2),±}_{cvν}(k,q,Q) = N^{-1/2} Σ_m [ g_{cmν}(k+q,Q) ρ_{mv}(k,q) / (ω − ε_{m,k+q} + ε_{vk})
                                         + ρ_{cm}(k+Q,q) g_{mvν}(k,Q) / (ε_{vk} − ε_{m,k+Q} ∓ ω_Qν) ]      (2.1)

- Upper sign (−ω_Qν in the second denominator): emission, with ω = ε_{c,k+q+Q} − ε_vk + ω_Qν.
- Lower sign (+ω_Qν): absorption, with ω = ε_{c,k+q+Q} − ε_vk − ω_Qν.
- A₁ (the first term): the density acts first and the electron then scatters.
- A₂ (the second term): the hole scatters. Equivalently, the phonon vertex acts first.

**Pauli blocking.** The sum over m in (2.1) is **unrestricted (all bands)**.
- Proof for A₁ with m = v′ occupied. The direct ordering H_ep n_q|0⟩ is blocked. The surviving ordering n_q H_ep|0⟩ (a
  ground-state Z-graph) has denominator −(ε_c + ω_Q − ε_{v′}) and fermion sign −1, because
  [c†_{v′}c_v, c†_c c_{v′}] = −c†_c c_v. Using the on-shell condition, its product equals the "naive" A₁ term with m = v′.
- The same argument covers A₂ with m empty.

So Pauli-blocked intermediates do not have to be removed by hand: their time-reversed partners restore them exactly. The
same algebra makes the filled-band result equal the single-electron formula with complete band sums. The only
assumptions are T ≪ E_g and a non-interacting reference (H_KS).

Checks:
- **q = 0.** Since n_0 = N̂ is conserved, M must vanish. It does: the m = v term of A₁ gives g/ω, the m = c term of A₂
  gives −g/ω, and they cancel (sympy check (1)). So M = O(q) in the optical limit.

### 2b. Sideband formula, and when it is well defined [D]

  ε₂^{PA}(q,ω) = (2π e²/(q² V)) Σ_{k,Q} Σ_{c,v,ν} [ (n_Qν+1) |M^{(2),+}|² δ(ω − ε_{c,k+q+Q} + ε_vk − ω_Qν)
                                                   + n_Qν |M^{(2),−}|² δ(ω − ε_{c,k+q+Q} + ε_vk + ω_Qν) ]        (2.2)

The factor 2 is the spin. S^{PA}_proper = (2q²/e²) ε₂^{PA}. The intensive limit is (1/V) Σ_{k,Q} → (N/Ω)(1/N_k N_Q) Σ.
Dimension check: sympy check (3).

**Resonances and double counting.** A₁ diverges where ε_{m,k+q} − ε_vk = ω, which is where the first-order transition to
(m, k+q) is on shell. There, |A₁|² ~ |ρ|²|g|²/(ω−Δ)² is not integrable. In the full theory this is resummed by Im Σ_m into
|ρ|² (Γ_m/2π) / ((ω−Δ)² + Γ²/4) → |ρ|² δ(ω−Δ). This is a *real* first-order transition followed by phonon emission, and
its weight already belongs to the ZPL. Hence:

**The sideband (2.2) is a separately computable quantity if and only if every first-order transition reachable by the
intermediate states is off shell at that (q,ω).** Concretely:
- A₁ needs ω < min_{m,k} [ε_{m,k+q} − ε_vk] over the k in the support;
- A₂ needs ε_vk − ε_{m,k+Q} ∓ ω_Q ≠ 0, which holds automatically for m conduction. For m valence it is a hole-relaxation
  resonance, which matters only for an intraband hole with real phonon emission, and only near band crossings.

Where the ZPL is open, the sideband with the resonant pieces subtracted is an O(g²) correction to it (C3, §6).

### 2c. Pieces that do not take the |amplitude|² form: ZPL renormalisation [D]

The non-phonon cuts give

  ε₂^{ZPL}(q,ω) = (2π e²/(q²V)) Σ_{k,c,v} |ρ̃_cv(k,q)|² δ(ω − ε̃_{c,k+q} + ε̃_vk)

expanded to O(g²). The pieces are:

1. **Energy shift (Fan + DW).** ε̃_n = ε_n + Re Σ^{FM}_nn(ε_n) + Σ^{DW}_nn.
   Expanding the δ gives a δ′ term, −(δε_c − δε_v)|ρ|²δ′(ω−Δ). It moves weight without changing the zeroth moment.
2. **Fan Z-factors.** |ρ|² → (1 + ∂_z ReΣ_cc + ∂_z ReΣ_vv)|ρ|², with ∂_z ReΣ < 0. This is the weight loss.
3. **Band mixing.** δρ^{mix}_cv = Σ_{m≠c} [Σ_cm/(ε_c − ε_m)] ρ_mv + Σ_{m≠v} ρ_cm [Σ_mv/(ε_v − ε_m)], with static plus on-shell
   Fan and DW. It enters as 2Re(ρ* δρ^{mix}).
4. **Virtual-phonon vertex.** δρ^V = Σ_{I,J} ⟨f|H_ep|I⟩⟨I|n_q|J⟩⟨J|H_ep|0⟩ / [(E_f − E_I)(E_0 − E_J)], with one-phonon
   intermediates I and J and unrestricted band sums (same Pauli argument as §2a). It enters as 2Re(ρ* δρ^V).
5. **Second-order state corrections.** The norm parts of |0^{(2)}⟩ and |f^{(2)}⟩ are the Z-factors in item 2.

**None of these has the form Σ|A|²δ. They are interference terms between O(1) and O(g²) amplitudes inside the same
zero-phonon sector.** This is not the "surprise" the brief describes, because it is not sideband–ZPL interference.

## 3. f-sum rule (C4)

**Exact statement [D].** Hypothesis: every electron–ion term is a multiplicative (local) function of r_e. This holds for the
all-electron Hamiltonian at every order of its Taylor expansion in u, and it fails for non-local pseudopotentials. Then
[H_ep, n_q] = [H_DW, n_q] = 0, and

  ∫₀^∞ ω S(q,ω) dω/2π = (1/2V) ⟨[[H,n_q],n_q†]⟩ = n_e q²/2m_e   ⇒   ∫₀^∞ ω ε₂ dω = (π/2) ω_p²,  ω_p² = e² n_e/m_e

at every order in g and for any phonon state. The same holds for the proper Π and for L (Coulomb is local). So when all
diagrams of §1 are kept, the O(g²) change of the first moment is exactly zero:

  M₁^{SB} + ΔM₁^{ZPL} = 0,   with M₁^{SB} ≡ ∫ω ε₂^{PA} dω = (2πe²/q²V) Σ_{k,Q,c,v,ν,±} ω_f (n_Qν+½±½) |M^{(2),±}|² > 0                                           (3.1)

**Who compensates (this refines the forecast) [D].**
- The DW tadpole is a static local one-body potential, ½⟨uu⟩:∂²V. A static local potential leaves the first moment
  unchanged on its own: it only reshuffles weight inside the ZPL (items 1 and 3).
- The Fan tadpole (T) vanishes.
- **The compensating negative weight therefore comes from the Fan–Migdal Z-factors, the dynamic band mixing and the
  virtual vertex term (items 2–4).** These are the non-phonon cuts of the same three diagrams (SE-p, SE-h, V) whose phonon
  cut is the sideband. This makes a "cutting pair": each diagram's phonon cut and non-phonon cut add to zero first
  moment.

The forecast "compensated by the Debye–Waller reduction" is correct **only if "DW" means the Huang–Rhys / Franck–Condon
ZPL factor e^{−S}**. That factor comes from Fan-type linear-coupling diagrams, mainly from the g_cc − g_vv difference
in Z_c Z_v and (V). It is not correct if "DW" means the Allen–Heine A² tadpole. Two different objects share the name
"DW", and the paper must fix one meaning.

**Sideband only.** The first moment exceeds (π/2)ω_p² by M₁^{SB}, which is O(⟨|g|²⟩/ΔE²) relative to the local ZPL
weight [E]. So the formula alone does not satisfy C4. It fails at O(g²), and the failure has a definite positive sign.

**ELF bounds [C/E].** The bounds cited in C4 are derived from exact sum rules and positivity/causality. They therefore hold
for the exact L at every order. The truncated "static-lattice ZPL + sideband" L overshoots the first moment by M₁^{SB}.
- If a bound uses only the first moment, the truncated L can violate it by at most M₁^{SB}.
- In the window where the ZPL is closed, the sideband is the whole L, so a bound of the form "partial integral ≤ total"
  holds strictly.

## 4. Screening (T2b)

**4a. External vertex [D].** Π^{PA} is Coulomb-proper. In RPA/TDDFT with local fields,
χ = χ_KS + χ_KS K χ, with K = v + f_xc. To first order in δχ_KS = χ_KS^{PA},

  χ^{PA} = (1 − χ_KS K)^{-1} χ_KS^{PA} (1 − K χ_KS)^{-1}

The 00 element gives the screened response.

Without local fields: ε = 1 − v_q(Π⁰ + Π^{PA}), so L = ε₂/|ε|² exactly. Expanding to O(g²):
δL = δε₂/|ε|² − 2ε₂(ε₁δε₁ + ε₂δε₂)/|ε|⁴.

**In the window where ε₂⁰(q,ω) = 0** the last term is O(g⁴). This gives **L^{PA} = ε₂^{PA}/(ε₁⁰)² + O(g⁴), with ε₁⁰ the
static-lattice electronic ε₁(q,ω)**. The O(g²) Fan/ZPR shift of ε₁ enters only at the next order.

Outside the window, use the full expansion, including δε₁ from the renormalised ZPL (Kramers–Kronig of §2c).

In a polar crystal, ε₁ must also include the ionic term from (X). At ω ≫ ω_LO this term is ≈ −(ε₀ − ε_∞)ω²_TO/ω².

**4b. Phonon vertex: use the screened DFPT g, and screen once [D].**
- Every Coulomb line in a diagram either carries the external (q,ω) or carries the phonon momentum.
  - Chains carrying (q,ω) are Coulomb-reducible and resum into 1/ε(q,ω) (§4a).
  - Bubble chains between a bare ion vertex and the electron line carry (Q, ω_Q). They resum into
    g^{bare} ε^{-1}(Q, ω_Q) ≈ g^{bare} ε^{-1}(Q, 0), which is the DFPT ΔV_SCF (Hartree + adiabatic xc).
- No Coulomb line can belong to both classes. So using DFPT g inside Π^{PA}, plus one external 1/ε, does not double count.
- Using the bare g would omit the Q-channel screening. That overestimates |g| by ~ε_∞ in the long-range part.
- Applying 1/ε(q,ω) to g would screen at the wrong momentum.
- Error of the static screening of g: O(ω_Q/E_g), which is the adiabatic approximation. In polar crystals the Fröhlich part
  of DFPT g is already screened by ε_∞ (correct). Phonon frequencies from DFPT are consistently the screened ones.

## 5. Dark photon: coupling to total charge (T2c) [D]

The coupling is ρ^{ch}_q = −e n_q + e Σ_{pκ} Z_κ e^{iq·(R_p + τ_κ + u_pκ)}. Expand the ion term in u:
1. **O(u⁰).** Σ_p e^{iq·R_p} = N δ_{q,G}: coherent Bragg scattering at ω = 0. It does not contribute at ω ≥ E_g.
2. **O(u¹).** i Z_κ q·u_pκ creates one phonon with Q ≡ q mod G.
   - If the phonon is the final state, the result is DM–phonon scattering. It adds coherently to the electronic
     density → (virtual e-h) → phonon amplitude from (X). Together they build the effective-charge vertex
     Σ_κ q·Z*_κ(q)·e_κν/√(2NM_κω).
   - If the phonon is virtual and converted by g into an e-h pair, the final state has no phonon, so it is a ZPL correction.
     This is the ionic screening of the ZPL, of relative size ~ω²_p,ion/ω² ~ (m_e/M) ω_p²/ω² ≪ 1 at ω ~ eV [E].
3. **O(u²).** The ionic Debye–Waller factor multiplies item 1. It is irrelevant.

**There is no new amplitude to an (e-h + one phonon) final state at O(e·g).** An ion vertex with one u must create the
phonon. A single g vertex then either annihilates it, giving a 0-phonon final state, or adds a second phonon, giving a
2-phonon final state.

**Result.** The dark-photon PAER rate is the electron-only result (2.1)–(2.2) with L built from the *total* ε = ε^{el} + ε^{ion}.
- The one-phonon, no-e-h channel lives at ω ≤ ω_max ≈ 60–70 meV. It is kinematically disjoint from PAER (ω ≥ E_g − ω_max),
  so it does not interfere.
- **Si (non-polar):** Z* = 0, and the q → 0 one-phonon dark-photon coupling is O(q²). ε^{ion}(ω) = 0, so dark-photon PAER =
  electron-only PAER screened by ε^{el}.
- **Polar (e.g. GaAs):** the only change in the PAER window is ε₁^{ion} ≈ −(ε₀−ε_∞)ω²_TO/ω² in |ε|². This is at the
  10⁻³–10⁻² level at ω ≈ 1.5 eV [E]. The large polar effect, DM–LO-phonon scattering, sits below 0.1 eV.

## 6. Controls

**C1, optical limit [D].** The identity is exact for local potentials. Since [V, n_q] = [H_ep, n_q] = 0 and
[p²/2m, e^{iq·r}] = q·j_q, with j_q = (p e^{iq·r} + e^{iq·r} p)/2m:

  ⟨f|n_q|i⟩ = ⟨f|[H,n_q]|i⟩/(E_f − E_i) = (q/ω)·⟨f|j_q|i⟩   ⇒   q → 0: M^{(2)} → (q/ω) q̂·M_v

M_v is (2.1) with ρ_mn → v_mn = ⟨m|p|n⟩/m_e, including the intraband v_nn, with the *same* denominators.
Inserting this into (2.2):

  ε₂^{PA}(ω) = (2π e²/ω² V) Σ_{k,Q,c,v,ν} |ê·M_v^{±}|² (n_Qν + ½ ± ½) δ(ω − ε_{c,k+Q} + ε_vk ∓ ω_Qν)

The structure matches Hall–Bardeen–Blatt / Noffsinger et al.:
- two orderings ("photon first" with denominator ε_m − ε_v − ω, "phonon first" with ε_m − ε_v ± ω_Q);
- unrestricted m;
- Bose factors n+1 and n.
Our denominators equal theirs up to an overall sign, which drops out of |·|².

  - At fixed q the Γ-valley ZPL opens only at E_dir + q²/2(m*_c + m*_h) ≳ E_dir + 0.5 eV (parabolic m*_c = 0.067,
    m*_h = 0.5). Non-parabolicity and the L/X valleys (≈ 1.7–1.9 eV) cap this.
  - PAER returns the electron to the Γ valley with Q ≈ −q and opens at E_dir + ω_LO.
- So **a window E_dir + ω_ph ≲ ω ≲ min(E_L, ZPL threshold at q_min) may exist where PAER leads even in GaAs**.
- Either C8 must be restated as "above the *finite-q* zero-phonon threshold", or this window is a real effect. It needs a
  band-structure check (§9). Treat it as a flag, not a result.

**C5 [D].** From the δ's in (2.2): at T = 0, the emission term only, with threshold E_g + min ω_Qν. At T > 0 the
absorption branch starts at E_g − max ω_Qν, weighted by n_Qν. The threshold is set over Q's that connect the band edges.

## 7. Temperature [D]

- Bose factors (n+1) and n come from the phonon spectral function of D_ν. They multiply |M^{±}|², and the amplitudes are
  T-independent (harmonic). In Π the factors enter via Σ^{FM} (n_Q + f) and (n_Q + 1 − f).
- Electron factors: f_vk(1 − f_{c,k+q+Q}), and in the A₁/A₂ intermediates the Pauli-continued sums stay unrestricted.
  Thermal corrections are O(e^{−E_g/T}).
- Kubo detailed balance: S = −2Im χ/(1 − e^{−βω}), which → −2Im χ for ω ≫ T. The reverse (de-excitation) terms are
  ∝ e^{−E_g/T}, which is negligible.
- The ZPL renormalisation (§2c) is T-dependent through (2n+1) in Σ^{DW} and through Σ^{FM}. This is the usual gap ZPR(T),
  which shifts E_dir(T) and E_g(T) and hence the edges of the PAER window.

## 8. Summary of claims for the term-by-term comparison with D1 (golden rule)

1. **Amplitude.** (2.1): two orderings, unrestricted m, denominators ω − Δ_{m,k+q;v,k} and ε_vk − ε_{m,k+Q} ∓ ω_Q, and
   an explicit 1/√N.
2. **Rate.** (2.2): spin factor 2, Bose factors (n+1) and n, ε₂ ↔ S conversion factor e²/2q².
3. **Interference.** No sideband–ZPL interference at O(g²) (phonon-parity argument). The O(g²) ZPL corrections are
   interference terms within the zero-phonon sector.
4. **Sum rule.** f-sum exact; the compensation comes from the Fan Z, mixing and vertex non-phonon cuts. The A² DW tadpole
   adds zero net first moment.
5. **Validity window.** The sideband is well defined only where all intermediate first-order transitions are off shell.
6. **Screening.** DFPT g, plus one external 1/ε(q,ω). L^{PA} = ε₂^{PA}/(ε₁⁰)² in the window.
7. **Dark photon.** No new one-phonon e-h amplitude; only ε^{ion} in |ε|.
8. **Controls.** C7 amended. C8 possibly fails for DM kinematics.
