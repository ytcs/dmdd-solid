## 1. Set-up: Feshbach reduction on the Theorem D sectors

**(O4.1) [D] Hamiltonian and sectors.**
- H = H₀ + V: H₀ = clamped, ZPR-free e–h Hamiltonian plus harmonic phonons; V = H_ep, odd in phonon operators (D2 §2).
- X0, X+ = eigenspaces of the **reference** net-phonon number (0, ≥ 1); projectors P₀ + P₊ = 1 on the excited block.
- At O(g²) only one-phonon states of X+ enter. Without (H5) the PST unitary U_n does not exist (no gap; SOTA coordinator
  note), so the sectors are defined on H₀ and dressing is handled perturbatively.
- Source |s⟩ = O_b|i⟩ = |s₀⟩ + |s₊⟩: |s₀⟩ = ρ (zero-phonon vertex, O(1)); |s₊⟩ = O(g), holding the hole-path A₂ and the
  ground-state Z-graphs, off shell by ≥ E_g − ω_Q (REF-B).

**(O4.2) [D] Feshbach map.**
- G₀₀(z) = [z − H₀₀ − Σ(z)]⁻¹ with Σ(z) = P₀VP₊(z − H₊₊)⁻¹P₊VP₀.
- At O(g²), H₊₊ → H₀ on X+. Σ is then the Fan–Migdal self-energy of the e–h pair (electron plus hole lines). Adding the Allen–Heine
  tadpole (real; not "Debye–Waller", which here means only the FC e^{−2W} factor) gives the full O(g²) ZPR.
- Ward identity / optical theorem: −2 Im Σ(ω+i0) = 2π P₀VP₊ δ(ω − H₀) P₊VP₀ ≡ Γ̂(ω). This is exact for the map and is
  the unitarity of the restricted dynamics.

**(O4.3) [D] Exact X+ final amplitude.** Let f = (c, k_e; v, k_h; Qν), with energy E_f.

  A_f(ω) = ⟨f|s₊⟩ + Σ_{m,m′} V_{fm} [G₀₀(ω)]_{mm′} ( s₀ + P₀V(ω − H₀)⁻¹ s₊ )_{m′},
  S^{exc}(ω) = Σ_f 2π δ(ω − E_f) |A_f|²  +  (asymptotic X0 weight).

- The asymptotic X0 weight is non-zero only where Γ̂_m(ω) = 0 (X0 pairs within ω_Q,min of the X0 bottom at T = 0; below
  the window edge). Elsewhere, every zero-phonon pair decays, and the exact (t → ∞) X0 weight is zero. This is the
  condensed-matter form of "the inclusive soft emission cannot be separated".
- Quasiparticle basis m of H₀₀ + Re Σ: Ẽ_m = E_m + Re Σ_mm(Ẽ_m), Z_m = [1 − ∂_ω Re Σ_mm]⁻¹; Σ taken diagonal in m
  (off-diagonal Γ̂_{mm′} matters only for pairs degenerate within Γ, §8).
- Write A_f = g_{fm}[λ_f + G_m(ρ_m + Υ_m)], with G_m = (ω − E_m − Σ_mm)⁻¹; λ_f = off-shell paths (A₂, non-resonant m′)
  normalised by g_{fm}, O(1/δ); Υ_m = Σ_f g_{fm}* λ_f g_{fm}/(ω − E_f + i0) = O(g²).

## 2. (iv′) Definition: the Dyson resonant-part assignment (convention C1)

**(O4.4) Lemma [D] (sympy S1, S2).** For Γ > 0 and φ ∈ C² on |Δ| < δ:

  Γ/(Δ² + Γ²/4) = 2π δ(Δ) + Γ fp(1/Δ²) + R,   |⟨R, φ⟩| ≤ (π/4) Γ² ‖φ″‖ + O(Γ²/δ²)‖φ‖.

- Normalised Lorentzian: L_Γ = δ + (Γ/2π) fp(1/Δ²) + O(Γ²). 1/Δ² has no PV; for Gaussian φ, ⟨Γ fp, φ⟩ = −2√πΓ < 0 (S1c).

**(O4.5) Definition (C1).** Expand |A_f|² with (O4.4), using Δ = ω − Ẽ_m and keeping O(g²).

  S^{X0}_{C1}(ω)    = Σ_m 2π Z_m |ρ̃_m|² δ(ω − Ẽ_m),    |ρ̃_m|² = |ρ_m|² + 2 Σ_f Re(ρ_m* λ_f) Re Σ^{(f)}_mm,
  S^{X+}_{C1,f}(ω) = 2π δ(ω − E_f) |g_{fm}|² [ |ρ_m|² fp 1/(ω−E_m)² + 2 Re(λ_f* ρ_m) P 1/(ω−E_m) + |λ_f|² ].

- The δ part goes to X0 and is handed to the WP5 cascade; the fp and P parts are the genuine sideband. Σ^{(f)} is the part
  of Σ with intermediate f; |ρ̃|² is the on-shell virtual-phonon vertex (D2 §2c item 4).
- Inside the window every term is regular and C1 = D1 eq. (4) = D2 eq. (2.2), with no δ part.

## 3. (iv″a) The total is assignment-independent to O(g²)

**Theorem (a) [D].**
- *Hypotheses:* (A1) O(g²), T ≪ E_g; (A2) the test function φ (resolution, q/k integration, DM energy weight) is C² on a
  scale δ_m around each Ẽ_m; (A3) Γ_m ≪ δ_m; (A4) X0 and X+ codes use the same Σ (same g, ω_Q, energies, T; §6).
- *Claim:*

  ⟨S^{X0}_{C1} + Σ_f S^{X+}_{C1,f}, φ⟩ = ⟨S^{exc}_{exact}, φ⟩ + O(g⁴, Γ²/δ²).                                         (O4.6)

*Proof.*
- (i) Expand |A_f|² = |g|²[|G_m|²|ρ+Υ|² + 2Re(λ* G_m(ρ+Υ)) + |λ|²].
- (ii) Rewrite |G_m|² = Z_m²/[(ω−Ẽ_m)² + Z_m²Γ_m²/4] + O(g⁴) near the pole. Multiply by Γ_m^{(f)} = 2π Σ_{f∈ν} |g_{fm}|²δ(ω−E_f) and
  apply (O4.4). This gives 2πZ_m (Γ^{(f)}/Γ_m) δ + |ρ|²Γ^{(f)} fp. Summed over f, the branching fractions add to 1 by the
  Ward identity (O4.2).
- (iii) G_m → P(1/Δ) − iπδ(Δ). The cross term then gives 2Re(λ*ρ)P(1/Δ) + 2π Im(λ*ρ) δ(Δ).
- (iv) In |ρ+Υ|², Im Υ_m = −Σ_f λ_f Γ^{(f)}/2. Summed over f, its δ-coefficient cancels the Im(λ*ρ) δ-terms of (iii), so the X0
  total is 2πZ|ρ̃|² with no Fano term (O4.5). The cancellation is per branch only after the sum; see (b).
- (v) The fp term centred at E_m differs from the one centred at Ẽ_m by Re Σ · 2fp(1/Δ³)·Γ = O(g⁴).
- (vi) Zeroth moment: Z_m + (1/2π) fp∫Γ_m(ω′)/(ω′−Ẽ_m)² dω′ = 1 + O(g⁴), because ∂_ω Re Σ = −(1/2π) fp∫Γ/(ω′−ω)² (KK) (sympy S4;
  toy T2, residual 3·10⁻¹³). So each m keeps its weight. Combined with D2 §3, this localises the SOTA #3 compensation: the
  Z-loss of X0 *is* the off-shell fp weight of X+.
- **Toy T1:** ⟨S_{C1} − S_{exact}, φ⟩ ∝ g^{3.97} (6.7·10⁻², …, 1.8·10⁻⁵ for g = 0.16 … 0.02). ∎

*"Independent of the assignment."* A different convention c moves a distribution D_c = S^{X0}_c − S^{X0}_{C1} out of X0. The theorem
holds for c iff the X+ code subtracts exactly D_c. That is condition (d1). The checks show that the most natural mixed
pairs violate it at O(g²) or O(1):
- **T3a [D]:** a Lorentzian ZPL of width Γ plus the fp sideband double counts (Γ/2π)|ρ|² fp(1/Δ²), which is the full
  off-shell sideband. The error ∝ g^{1.99}.
- **T3b [D]:** a Lorentzian ZPL plus the Dyson-resummed sideband |Δ+iΓ/2|⁻² double counts the whole δ, a relative error of
  −1.00. This is D1 §5.2 rule (b), now quantified.

## 4. (iv″b) Cascade-convolved quanta distribution

Let P(N_eh | q, ω, T) = N/S with N = Σ_Λ ⟨S_Λ P_casc(N_eh|Λ), φ⟩ (WP0 7.2).

**Theorem (b) [D].**
- *Hypotheses:* (A1–A4), plus (B1) the cascade is Markov in the prompt label Λ (first-step analysis):
  P_casc(N|m at Ẽ) = Σ_f K_{m→f} P_casc(N|f) + Σ_x K_{m→x} P_casc(N|x), x = non-phonon channels (impact ionisation,
  radiative); (B2) K_{m→f} = [2π|g_{fm}|²δ(Ẽ_m − E_f) + 𝔉_{mf}]/Γ_m^{tot}, Γ^{tot} = Γ^{ph} + Γ^x, 𝔉 below.
- *Claim:* N and S, hence P, are the same at O(g²) under C1 and under the exact (asymptotic) assignment. They are also the same
  under any convention c that satisfies (d1).

*Proof.*
- Insert (B1) into N under C1. The X0 term becomes Σ_f [2πZ|ρ̃|² K_{m→f}] P(N|f).
- Under C1 the response contains **no Γ^{tot}**: δ + fp needs no width. So the branching Γ^{ph}/Γ^{tot} applied by the cascade
  is exactly the δ-coefficient of the exact |A_f|² in a theory that includes Γ^x in Σ: the Lorentzian resummed with Γ^{tot}
  gives (2π/Γ^{tot})Γ^{(f)}δ.
- The X+ term carries fp and P parts that are seeded at f directly. By (O4.6) per final state f, N_{C1} = N_{exact} + O(g⁴).
  By (a), S is invariant. ∎
- *Corollary:* the cascade may contain channels x that the response omits (exact under C1; an advantage of (iv′) over
  "keep the Lorentzian" conventions, which need Γ^{tot} in the response).

**(O4.7) Amendment, the Fano term [D].** Step (iii) of §3 leaves a per-branch δ-coefficient

  𝔉_{m,ν} = 2π Γ_m^{(ν)} [ c̄_m − c_ν ],   c_ν = Im(ρ_m* λ_ν),   c̄_m = Σ_ν Γ^{(ν)}c_ν/Γ_m,   Σ_ν 𝔉_{m,ν} = 0.

- The term redistributes the X0 events among first-emission branches without changing their number. Relative to the
  incoherent branching it is O(Γ|λ|/|ρ|) = O(Γ/δ), which is O(g²). So it is the same order as (b) itself.
- It is a coherence between the off-shell production path (A₂) and the real decay of the zero-phonon pair. An incoherent
  Markov cascade cannot generate it. It must therefore be fed into (B2) from the response code, which has λ.
- **Toy T4:** complex λ, incoherent kernel: |ΔP| ∝ g^{2.18}; with 𝔉: g^{4.18}; real λ, matched cascade: g^{3.93}
  (max |ΔP| = 1.1·10⁻⁵ at g = 0.02).
- **When 𝔉 = 0 [D, sketch].** With PT symmetry (inversion × time reversal; spinless, or Kramers-summed with SOC), the Bloch
  gauge can be chosen so that every u_{nk} is PT-real. The operators e^{iq·r} and ∂_{κQ}V are PT-even, so ρ, g and the
  off-shell denominators are real, so c_ν = 0: 𝔉 = 0 for Si, Ge, diamond. For GaAs and other non-centrosymmetric
  targets 𝔉 ≠ 0 generically [C]; its size is an open numeric item.
- **Mismatch is O(1) [D, toy T4].** ω_TO off by 10%: |ΔP| = 2.7·10⁻²; g₂ off by 20%: 3.4·10⁻²; both g-independent. The
  resonant X0 weight is O(1), so the cascade's first step carries the leading-order final-state distribution outside the
  window: the (d) conditions are leading order, not a refinement.

## 5. (iv″c) The split is convention-dependent at O(Γ/δ): explicit bound

**Admissible class 𝒜(ℓ) [definition].** A convention c is admissible if it differs from C1 only in one or more of the following:
- (α) the residue power: X0 weight Z^p|ρ̃|² with p ∈ [1, 2]. Here p = 2 is the time-domain survival-pole convention
  |⟨m|e^{−iHτ}|s⟩|² extrapolated over 1/δ ≪ τ ≪ 1/Γ;
- (β) the kernel: δ is replaced by any normalised kernel of width ≤ Γ_m inside |Δ| < ℓ;
- (γ) the window: all spectral weight within |Δ| < ℓ is assigned to X0, with Γ ≪ ℓ ≤ δ.

All reproduce C1 as Γ/δ → 0 with coarse-graining time τ ∈ [1/δ, 1/Γ], the plateau that gives the split meaning.

**(O4.8) Lemma [D].** Hypotheses: Γ_m(ω′) ≥ 0, Γ_m ∈ C² on [Ẽ−δ, Ẽ+δ] with |Γ″| ≤ Γ_max/δ², and Γ_m ≤ Γ_max everywhere. Then

  |1 − Z_m⁻¹| = |∂_ω Re Σ_mm| ≤ (1/2π)[ 2Γ(Ẽ)/δ + 2Γ_max/δ + δ‖Γ″‖ ] ≤ (5/2π) Γ_max/δ.

*Proof.* Split the KK fp integral at |ω′−Ẽ| = δ. Inner part: Taylor remainder ≤ δ‖Γ″‖, and the constant term contributes
fp∫_{−δ}^{δ} dx/x² = −2/δ. Outer part: ≤ 2Γ_max/δ. ∎

The sign of 1 − Z is not fixed: inside a continuum Re Σ can rise with ω, so Z > 1 (toy: 1.0014–1.09); Z is a resonance
residue, not a probability, and the fp weight (1 − Z) is then negative.

**(O4.9) Theorem (c) [D].** For c, c′ ∈ 𝒜(ℓ) and any bin B ⊃ [Ẽ−ℓ, Ẽ+ℓ]:

  |w₀^{c} − w₀^{c′}| / w₀ ≤ (5/2π) Γ_max/δ + Γ_max/(πℓ) + b_λ Γ_max ℓ/δ² + O(Γ²/δ²),   b_λ ≤ 1 + 2|λ|δ/|ρ| + |λ|²δ²/|ρ|²,

where w₀ = ∫_B S^{X0}.
- Terms: (α) via (O4.8); (β, γ) via the Lorentzian tail Γ/πℓ; fp/P/|λ|² weight inside the window. Optimising at ℓ ≈ δ:
  **|Δw₀|/w₀ ≲ (1.1 + b_λ) Γ/δ**, the precise form of the SOTA statement.

**Toy T5.** Values of Δw₀/(w₀Γ/δ); the bound for each column is given at the end.

| g | Γ/δ | window ℓ = δ | window ℓ = δ/4 | Z²-pole (time domain) | asymptotic t → ∞ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.16 | 0.16 | −0.58 | −1.31 | +0.57 | −6.3 |
| 0.02 | 0.0025 | −0.63 | −1.33 | +0.57 | −400 (O(1) shift) |

Bounds (first two terms): 1.11 (ℓ = δ), 2.07 (ℓ = δ/4). All admissible conventions lie inside; ratios are g-independent.

**(c) fails outside 𝒜 [D].** The asymptotic convention sets X0 = 0 wherever Γ > 0, an O(1) change; it remains valid for
(a) and (b) (the cascade then receives only X+). The totals and P(Q) are physical; the split is bookkeeping whose ambiguity is
O(Γ/δ) only once the coarse-graining scale is fixed in [1/δ, 1/Γ].

**Si sizes [E].**
- Hot-carrier e–ph widths 0.1–1 eV above the CBM: Γ ≈ 10–60 meV (ab-initio rates 10¹³–10¹⁴ s⁻¹, from memory, unverified).
  δ = min(distance to E_min(q), L/X valley thresholds, JDOS-feature scale) ≈ 0.1–0.5 eV. So Γ/δ ≈ 0.02–0.6. The split is sharp deep outside the window and meaningless in a shell |ω − E_min| ≲ Γ (D1 §5.4 edge
  shell). There only the total and P(Q) may be quoted, computed with the unexpanded Dyson form.

## 6. (iv″d) Consistency conditions for a code pair (response R-code + cascade C-code)

The conditions are necessary for (O4.6) and for Theorem (b). "Same" means identical inputs, not merely converged to the
same tolerance. Each is tagged [D] when the toy or the proof shows that violating it is an O(g²) or O(1) error.
- **d1 One resonant kernel [D].** The X0 kernel (δ, Lorentzian, window) and the X+ subtraction must be the same
  distribution; recommended C1 (δ in X0, fp/P in X+). Forbidden: Lorentzian ZPL + fp sideband (T3a, O(g²)); Lorentzian ZPL
  + |Δ+iΓ/2|⁻² sideband (T3b, O(1)). Implementable fp: Γ(ω)·Re[(Δ + iϵ_r)⁻²], ϵ_r → 0, error O(ϵ_r/δ) (S3), ϵ_r independent of Γ.
- **d2 Same g [D].** Same screening level (incl. X2 pair channel if used), same gauge U(k) for g and ρ, same ω_{Qν} and
  eigenvectors. The cascade's Γ_m^{ph}(Ẽ) must equal −2 Im Σ_mm of the R-code branch by branch (20% off: O(1) in P, T4).
- **d3 Same energies [D].** The X0 seed starts at the R-code's Ẽ_m (with ZPR); E_f is identical in the X+ δ and in the
  cascade's first-emission δ (ω_TO off by 10%: O(1), T4). A bare-band C-code must shift the seed by the same Re Σ.
- **d4 Energy-dependent width [D].** The X+ fp/P terms use Γ^{(f)}(ω) at the actual final energy, i.e. Σ_f|A₁|² over real
  final states, not Γ(Ẽ). The difference Γ′·P(1/Δ) is O(g²).
- **d5 Z and vertex in X0 [D].** X0 carries Z_m|ρ̃_m|², with the Fan Z-factor and the on-shell virtual-phonon vertex Re Υ. Dropping
  Z while keeping the fp sideband violates the zeroth moment by (1 − Z) = O(Γ/δ).
- **d6 First emission counted once [D].** X0 seeds at (m, Ẽ_m, N_ph = 0) and the cascade's first step emits; X+ seeds at
  (f, N_ph = 1, E_ph = ω_Q) with **no** re-applied first emission, E_ph to the heat channel once. No resonant X+ is seeded.
- **d7 Fano term [D].** For non-PT-symmetric targets the R-code exports 𝔉_{m,ν} (O4.7) for the cascade's first-step branching.
  For PT-symmetric targets it is zero; check this numerically once.
- **d8 Same smearing [D/E].** The broadening σ of δ(ω − E_f) in Γ̂ (C-code) and in S^{X+} (R-code) is equal, level
  spacing ≪ σ ≪ δ; limits N → ∞ then σ → 0 (van Hove). σ ≳ Γ smears the X0 δ into the fp region: harmless only if the
  same σ is applied to both sectors **after** summation.
- **d9 Same T [D].** n_Q for absorption and n_Q + 1 for emission, identical in Σ, in the sideband and in the cascade (detailed
  balance). At T = 0, X0 pairs within ω_Q,min of the X0 bottom are stable and are genuine asymptotic X0 (no cascade first step).
- **d10 Non-phonon channels [D].** Under C1 the R-code needs Γ^x (impact ionisation, Auger, radiative) **nowhere**; the
  C-code supplies it. Under any convention that keeps Lorentzians, Γ^{tot} must be the same in both codes.
- **d11 Edge shell [D].** Within |ω − E_min(q)| ≲ few·Γ, and within Γ of a JDOS singularity, (A2)/(A3) fail. Report only the total and P(Q),
  from the unexpanded Dyson form, plus a systematic (D1 §5.4).

## 8. Where a mathematically rigorous version fails, and what it would need

Everything above is physics rigour: formal O(g²) expansion, distributional limits, and Markov cascade. A theorem fails at four
places.
1. **Continuum–continuum coupling.**
   - Rigorous resonance theory treats an *isolated* eigenvalue of H₀ embedded in the continuum of a field. Examples are
     complex dilation / spectral deformation (Jakšić–Pillet), the Bach–Fröhlich–Sigal renormalisation group, and the
     van Hove–Davies weak-coupling limit, which gives the Fermi golden rule as λ²t-scaling.
   - Here X0 is itself absolutely continuous (the k-sum), so there is no eigenvalue to deform, and Ẽ_m, Γ_m are labels of a
     *fibre* decomposition, not spectral data of H.
   - Needed: a fibred (direct-integral over total crystal momentum) limiting-absorption principle with a Mourre estimate for
     H₀ + gV, uniform in g, on the window complement. That gives Hölder-continuous boundary values of the resolvent in
     weighted spaces, and hence (O4.6) as a statement about φ ∈ C^{1,α}.
   - Degenerate X0 pairs, where Γ̂ is not diagonal in m, need a matrix version (Fano–Mies).
2. **Thermodynamic limit.**
   - The filled band plus phonons is an infinite quasi-free system (KMS at T > 0); the H₀ sector projectors are not affiliated
     with the interacting dynamics. Needed: a C*-dynamical (Araki–Woods) formulation, N → ∞ before σ → 0 (d8), and "net phonon
     number" as an out-state observable, i.e. asymptotic completeness for the e–ph field (open even for the polaron at
     positive density; return-to-equilibrium results cover only finite systems coupled to free reservoirs).
3. **Coulomb interactions.**
   - g, ε and the phonons are derived from H_C (WP0 §1). A proof needs Lieb–Lebowitz-type stability and screening in the
     thermodynamic limit with a dynamical lattice; control of excitonic X0 (Sommerfeld/Fano structure in ρ̃ on E_b ≈ 15 meV
     in Si, which can be < δ and violates (A2) near the edge [E]); and control of the X2 pair channel inside Σ.
   - The PST construction behind Theorem D (i) also assumes the Coulomb singularities are harmless, which is [C] (WP0).
4. **Infrared phonons.**
   - "≥ 1 phonon" is not IR-safe: Γ contains soft emission (X1). Finite in 3D for ASR-suppressed acoustic coupling; log-divergent
     for piezo (GaAs) where the ZPL is open, so X0, X+ separately are ill defined there.
   - Needed: a Kinoshita–Lee–Nauenberg / Bloch–Nordsieck definition, X+ := at least one phonon with ω_Q > ε_res. Then (c)
     acquires an extra ln(δ/ε_res) dependence of the split [C], while (a) and (b) are unaffected.
