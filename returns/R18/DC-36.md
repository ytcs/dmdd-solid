# R18 / DC-36: reflection/refraction force on the MICROSCOPE test masses above σ_crit; the DWBA term

Spec: returns/R16/CW2.md §22 (LE-69; CW2-errata: STANDS, only C15 rebuilt) and returns/R12/WP7-B2-v2.md §3 read through CORRECTIONS §19.8. Sources read in full text from the arXiv TeX: 2512.16451 (draft.tex), 1909.10598 (test-mass table), 2012.11232 (instrument), 2209.15487. Code: code/DC-36/. Status: CLAIMED.

**Verdict.** Force delivered for a stated parametric model, Born acceptance PASS [CALC]. DWBA: **exact gap**, as allowed (no ⟨δnδn⟩ data). Any bound from this channel stays [C]: the sign, size and ceiling depend on the housing and on the surface state, and the MICROSCOPE number is a benchmark at the wrong frequency.

## 1. Quantity
Δa(σ_χN, m_χ) = a₁^{outer} − a₁^{inner}, where a₁ is the first harmonic of the axial acceleration as the DM wind rotates in a plane through the cylinder axis (the most favourable orientation; the true wind has a component off that plane). Spin frequency f_s.

Universal scaling [D]. With μ_χN ≈ m_χ everything depends on one velocity, u² ≡ v_c,Ti² = 2V_opt/m_χ = 4π𝔟n_Ti/m_χ², so

  Δa(σ, m) = G(u),  σ = m⁴u⁴/(4πn_Ti²) = σ_crit(u)  (WP7-B2 §3 with v → u).

One curve G covers all masses, as long as λ_dB ≪ body size, and ρ_χ enters linearly.

## 2. Model and method
Optical potential, 𝔟 common to all nucleons, n = ρ/m_u. Sharp faces, no absorption. Per face a 1D step along the local normal: k′² = k_n² − 2m_χ(V₂ − V₁), R = |(k − k′)/(k + k′)|². Faces of a wall are combined incoherently, which equals the coherent thickness average 2R/(1+R). Monte Carlo ray tracing through both coaxial hollow cylinders with reflection, refraction and all multiple bounces: 5D per orientation (v, impact point), 4 Gauss nodes in the wind angle, 1.5×10⁶ rays per point. Momentum to a body is summed per ray, so a parallel-faced wall gives exactly zero for transmitted rays. Script: micro.py, run_force.py.

Inputs. Geometry [FT 1909.10598 metrology table]: IS1-SUEP PtRh 30.801/39.390 mm, L 43.330 mm, 0.401706 kg, 19.972 g/cm³; IS2-SUEP TA6V 60.802/69.401 mm, L 79.831 mm, 0.300939 kg, 4.420 g/cm³. SUREF: PtRh inner 43.331 mm, 0.401533 kg, outer 79.821 mm, 1.359813 kg. Halo: CONVENTIONS §8 (v₀ = 238, v_esc = 544, wind 250.5 km/s), ρ_χ = 0.3 GeV/cm³ (2512.16451 uses 0.4: scale by 4/3). n_Pt/n_Ti = 4.52, so v_c,Pt = 2.13u.

Not modelled in the baseline: electrode cylinders, housing, satellite, satellite orbital velocity. Variations in §5.

Findings:
1. **Saturation.** With V > 0 and u > v_max = 795 km/s all DM reflects. a saturates at 1.04×10⁻¹² (TA6V) and 4.2×10⁻¹³ (PtRh), in line with CW2's ≲ 10⁻¹²R_slab scale.
2. **Sign change.** Δa(SUEP) is negative (PtRh wins, higher v_c) at low u and positive (TA6V wins, smaller column ρL) at high u, with a zero at u = 306 km/s (V > 0), near 268 km/s (V < 0). So Δa is not monotonic in σ.
3. **Reflection and attraction are comparable.** V < 0 gives the same order and sign pattern up to u ≈ 200, smaller above. Refraction force at curved faces does not change the axial result qualitatively, and specular side faces give no axial force.
4. **A composition difference is not required.** SUREF (identical PtRh) has Δa = −1.9×10⁻¹³ at saturation, purely from the different lengths L, no zero crossing, and it closes the SUEP zero at 306 km/s. WP7-B2 §3's "scales with the composition difference of 𝔟̄n₀" holds for neither pair above σ_crit.
5. **Regime map.** |Δa| = 4.8×10⁻¹⁴ at u = 98 km/s (SUEP, V > 0), 119 km/s (SUREF), 131 km/s (SUEP, V < 0). Low-u slope: a ∝ u^{3.4–3.5}, not the u⁴ of Born: rays with v_n < u reflect fully even when σ ≪ σ_crit(v̄), because the Born integrand ∝ v_n²R ∝ v_n⁻² is cut off at v_n ~ u. So the surface term is non-Born at every σ.

- **Multiple reflections between nested cylinders:** ≤ 2 % at the points away from the zero. Not important.
- **Roughness:** decisive. Diffuse faces make the long side walls act axially, raise a_TA6V ×2.7, and flip the sign at u = 100–200. Specular needs 2k_nσ_rough ≪ 1. At m = 1 keV, v_n ≈ u = 100 km/s, λ_n ≈ 3.7 μm [E], so nm–100 nm finish is needed; the test-mass finish was not found in the sources read. For 20 eV (λ_n ≈ 186 μm) specular is safe.
- **Housing:** parametric closed can, ρ = 8.0 g/cm³ (Invar is named in 2012.11232; R = H = 90 mm and the density are **my assumptions**). It stops all DM once v_c,can = 1.35u exceeds v_max: the force is exactly zero for u ≥ 590 km/s, and |Δa| < 4.8×10⁻¹⁴ already at u ≈ 365 km/s. A closed shell therefore gives any bound in this channel a **ceiling** in σ. At 20 eV that is 2.6×10⁻³⁸ cm², below the published 4.6×10⁻³⁸ (u = 423 km/s), and the shielding acts on the fluctuation force equally. So under this parametric can the published 20 eV point sits above the ceiling. 2512.16451 has no ceiling. Silica electrodes and the satellite were not modelled.

## 6. DWBA term: exact gap
⟨δnδn⟩ SAXS/USAXS data for the flight TA6V are unavailable; 2512.16451 itself says the flight microstructure is not public and uses a Debye form from other samples. The exact missing input is S_δn(q) on q = 10⁻²–1 eV. Given it, the remaining step is the fluctuation term of (B2.3.2) with distorted waves: k → k′ = (k² − 2m_χV_opt)^{1/2} inside the body, each ray weighted by its transmission 1 − R at entry and exit, on the same ray tracer. Not evaluated. The parametric alternative (Debye, ξ = 2 μm [FT 2512.16451; the underlying Mahdavi measurement not read]) would only reproduce an assumption, so I leave it.

Size of what is missing. With 2512.16451's own form factors, a_homo/a_fluc = 84 (20 eV), 6.5 (50 eV), 1.8 (100 eV), 0.55 (300 eV), 0.27 (1 keV), for the sphere (data/born_check.csv). The homogeneous term the paper drops is larger than the term it keeps below ≈ 170 eV.

## 8. Errors found in the spec and the sources
1. **WP7-B2 §3 / CW2 §22 "scales with the composition difference of 𝔟̄n₀".** Not above σ_crit: geometry alone gives a force difference (finding 4).
2. **"Born regime must reproduce (B2.3.2)".** True at fixed v_n. The f(v)-integrated surface force is never Born (finding 5), so σ ≪ σ_crit(v̄) does not imply Born for this term; the criterion of WP7-B2 §3 applies to bulk propagation only.
3. **CW2 §22 "4D (v, face)".** The force needs 5D per wind angle once nesting and refraction are kept. Cosmetic.
4. **2512.16451, "|F_fluc|² ≫ |F_homo|²".** Its argument assumes δn² ≃ n₀²; for Ti-6Al-4V δn²/n₀² = 7.3×10⁻⁴ (its own Eq.), which reverses the inequality at low mass.
5. **2512.16451, benchmark.** It takes the EP bound at f_EP = 3.11 mHz for a signal it places at f_s = 2.94 mHz. MICROSCOPE's bound is not a measurement at f_s. A real bound needs the spectrum at f_s and the SUREF data.
