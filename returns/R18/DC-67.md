## 1. Quantity
P_casc(N | x_e, x_h; θ), N = number of e-h pairs including the primary, x_e, x_h carrier kinetic energies in units of E_g. Stored per carrier: P_t(n | x; θ) = [zⁿ]𝔊_t(x; z), t = e, h; the seed law is the composition (CW7-153b), P(N | x_e, x_h) = Σ_{n₁+n₂=N−1} P_e(n₁|x_e) P_h(n₂|x_h), P(0|Λ) = 0.
**Coverage.** Si, the 27 GAP-EF prediction rows, 3C- and 6H-SiC. Of the 42-pool, 28 are covered (InSb and KTaO₃ are extra). Not covered, 14: Al, Nb (metals, no cascade of this type); Bi₂Se₃, Cr₂O₃, Li₂MoO₄, LiAlO₂, MnO, NiO, NiPS₃, PE, YIG, ZrTe₅, c-BN, h-BN (table v4 holds no measured E_g and ω₀ pair: DFT proxies or GAP rows; several have E_g^{DFT} = 0).
**θ set (my choice; no grid is specified anywhere).** One M-type factor on 𝒜_ph (CW7 §4(d)): m = 1/1.2, 1/1.1, 1, 1/0.9, 1/0.8, spanning θ_GW ±20% and θ_Z 0.8–1 taken **singly** (WP5-v4 §8); the joint corner 1/(0.8·0.8) is not tabulated. Structural: V1 (θ_ii = 3/2), V3 (a_DOS = 1), and Wv (W_v = E_g) on narrow-band rows. Each uses its GAP-EF-v3 𝒜_ph. Inputs are the copied PRED/VAL tables; Si: E_g 1.164, ω₀ 0.063, W_v 12 eV.
**Λ-sectors.** In the energy-only level the sector label enters only through (x_e, x_h): X0: x_e + x_h = (ω − E_g)/E_g; X+: (ω − ω_Q − E_g)/E_g. The reader takes (x_e, x_h) as given. The band-resolved partition needs `S_Lambda` of the WP6 library, which does not exist yet, so "every Λ-sector" is delivered as the table, not as a material-by-material map.

## 2. Method
**Solver (`casc2.py`).** (CW7-153a) in the two-table form specified in CW7-errata §1.4, never coded before: required because Si's and the narrow-band rows' hole cutoff makes ρ_e ≠ ρ_h. Same discrete arithmetic as cascade.py. Roots z_k = e^{2πik/N_z}, only k ≤ N_z/2 solved (𝔊(z̄) = 𝔊̄(z)); N_z = 32 or 64 by the rule N_z ≥ N̄ + 8σ_N (max N̄ + 8σ_N = 27.6, verified per row). N from GAP-EF-v3 model.grid (60–608), X by the predict.py rule (25; 60 for λ_ph > 5; Si 40). Table rows at grid points every 0.05 E_g to x = 5, every 0.25 E_g above.
**True ω₀.** First attempt: the GAP-EF-v3 recipe, solves at commensurate nodes k/N and Lagrange interpolation of P to the true w. It failed: F off by up to 2.65×10⁻³ against v3 and P as low as −3.6×10⁻³ (data/rejected_nodeP/). Reason: interpolation of a distribution over nodes with different means is not moment preserving (it adds a mixture variance) and negative Lagrange weights give P < 0. GAP-EF-v3 interpolates ε, F, where this does not arise, so **v3 stands**; the recipe just does not transfer to P.
Replacement: the phonon branch reads 𝔊(x − w) as the convex mixture (1 − a)𝔊_{n−n_f} + a𝔊_{n−n_f−1}, n_f = ⌊wN⌋, a = wN − n_f, and p_t uses ρ_t at the exact x − w. This is a two-step phonon with mean exactly ω₀ (extra step variance a(1 − a)h²), a p.g.f. mixture, so ΣP = 1 and P ≥ 0 hold by construction. a = 0 reproduces the commensurate recursion to 4×10⁻¹⁶. It is not a rounding of ω₀, and h still must divide E_g and θ_iiE_g (guard kept). One solve instead of three.

- **D3.** CW7 §4 "C6 shows the O(h) error", "Richardson with h/2": with phonons the order is 3/2 (confirmed: GAP-EF-v3-C2 for moments, here for pointwise P). Linear Richardson does not apply. Spec h = E_g/50 replaced by v3's N ≥ 60.
- **D4.** CW7-errata §1.4 "X doubling until the matched-Gaussian tail changes P by < 10⁻⁶" conflicts with CW7 §4 property (c), corrections O(x^{−1/2}) in the skewness. Measured 10⁻² at x = 40 falling roughly as x^{−1/2}, which would reach 10⁻⁶ only at x ~ 10⁹–10¹⁰. Even with the exact mean and variance the error is 7.9×10⁻³ at x = 40. Recommended text: direct tables to the N needed (WP6 N ≤ 20), Gaussian only with its stated 1–2% pointwise error, or an Edgeworth term (not done).
- **D5.** X = 20 does not reach N = 20 of WP6 (7.2): table reach is N̄ ≈ 14 at X = 25 in wide gaps. Direct extension is cheap (cost ∝ X²).
- **D6.** "5 θ values" is undefined in every source read; §1 states the choice.
- **D7.** The DC-67 row and the brief cite WP8-errata "§14 / IF-EW-1": IF-EW-1 is in §5 (and rows l.454, l.518); §14 is unrelated.
