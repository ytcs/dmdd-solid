## 2. Method
**Direct path, as specified (`bubbles2d.py`).** (1.1) from the spinors |+⟩ = (u^B, v^B), |−⟩ = (−v^B, u^B), M^N_i = ⟨+,ξ′|τ^N_i|−,ξ⟩ formed numerically. Gauss–Legendre in μ with panels at μ* = √(y² − 4)/Q and y/Q. ξ = η − s/2, s = Qμ, Gauss panels graded at the root η₀ and at η = s/2. Im from δ(ω − E − E′) at ±η₀. Re with both poles subtracted, 2rη₀/(η₀² − η²), analytic PV. Cutoff Λ = 10³max(1, |s|, y). Π^N₂₂(0,0) enters pointwise at the same cutoff, so the log cancels in the integrand.
**Closed-form path [D] (`bcs1d.py`).** The specified quadrature reduces to one dimension. Write W = E + E′, u² = ω² − s², κ = u²/4Δ²_BCS. Three identities (C1, C1b):
- EE′ + ξξ′ + Δ² = (W² − s²)/2 and EE′ − ξξ′ − Δ² = 2Δ²s²/(W² − s²);
- with ξ = Δ sinh a, ξ′ = Δ sinh b, A = (a + b)/2: W² − s² = 4Δ²cosh²A and dξ W/(EE′) = 2dA;
- hence ∫dξ W/[EE′(W² − s²)] = 1/Δ² for every s.

All bubbles at fixed s then follow from the Tsuneto function f(ω,s) ≡ ∫dξ W/[EE′(ω² − W²)]:
  f = −arcsinh√|κ| / (Δ²√(|κ|(1 + |κ|))) (κ < 0); f = −arcsin√κ / (Δ²√(κ(1 − κ))) (0 < κ < 1); f = [arccosh√κ − iπ/2] / (Δ²√(κ(κ − 1))) (κ > 1, ω + i0),
and with ⟨X⟩ = ∫₀¹dμ X, M₃*M₂ = iΔW/(2EE′), Π^N₂₃ = −Π^N₃₂:

  P₃₃ = ⟨(s² + Δ²ω²f)/u²⟩,  Π^N₃₂Π^N₂₃/(2N(0))² = (Δω/2)²⟨f⟩²,  [Π^N₂₂(0,0) − Π^N₂₂]/2N(0) = −¼⟨u²f⟩,
  P̃^{GRPA} = P₃₃ − Δ²ω²⟨f⟩²/⟨u²f⟩.                                                                                                     (DC41-1)

Consequences [D]: (a) Π^N₂₂'s log cancels analytically. (b) The pole of P₃₃ at u = 0 is removable. (c) Δ → 0 gives Lindhard. (d) Im P₃₃ = −2πΔ²ω²⟨θ(u² − 4Δ²)/(u³√(u² − 4Δ²))⟩. For y² − 4 ≤ Q² the substitution s → u gives (π/2)(y/Q) times 𝔤_I(y) = 4y∫₂^y du/[u²√(u² − 4)√(y² − u²)], the same 𝔤_I as WP4 [4.2] with no Q dependence (item 2).
Both channels with odd parity under η → −η vanish (M₃*M₁ ∝ ξ + ξ′, M₂*M₁ ∝ ξ/E + ξ′/E′), so the amplitude channel drops out at this level.
**AB pole.** ⟨u²f⟩ > 0 at ω → 0 and → −∞ (log) at ω → 2Δ⁻, so it has a zero ω_AB(Q) < 2Δ_BCS for **every** Q: P̃ ≈ Z/(ω − ω_AB) − Z/(ω + ω_AB). ω_AB → Q/√3 at small Q; 2 − ω_AB = 0.55, 0.071, 4.8×10⁻⁶ at Q = 3, 5.64, 12.8. This is a pole of R̃, so ε → ∞ there and L has a zero, not a peak (Coulomb removes the mode, as it should).
**KK (`kk.py`).** Log-spaced Gauss panels in ω′ from 2Δ to 10³max(Q, 2), graded at 2Δ and the pair edge √(Q² + 4); subtraction of I(y); AB pole added explicitly for GRPA. L in the tables is formed from Im and the KK Re. 𝔰_eff ≡ Q[Re(−P) − Re 𝔣_Lin] uses the direct Re, because Q multiplies the 10⁻⁶ KK offset.

## 5. Discrepancies found in the source spec
- **D1. "O((Δ_BCS/v_Fq)²) ≤ 0.04" (WP4 §4.2 item 3, K16; CW4 §1; LE-97).** As a size of 𝔳_GRPA it fails: 𝔳 reaches 0.46 at the y = 2.2 edge and 1.02 at Q = 9.7. Two reasons. The coefficient of 1/Q² in |ΔP/P| is 10–30, not O(1), from log² in ⟨f⟩ and a small ⟨u²f⟩ near the AB zero. And the Im fraction scales as 1/(Q ln Q), since Im P₃₃ = O(y/Q). 2609.06423's statement (relative suppression of Π_θ) survives parametrically in magnitude at large Q only. I have not re-read that paper; this rests on WP4's quotation.
- **D2. WP4 §4.2 "suppresses that weight by (Qv_F/ω)²".** Bare tail ∝ ω⁻², GRPA tail ∝ ω⁻⁶: the suppression is **(qv_F/ω)⁴** (measured exponents in C5f). Expanding (DC41-1) at ω ≫ Q: the Δ²ln ω/ω² term of P₃₃ cancels against the vertex through first order in s²/ω², leaving a remainder ∝ Δ²Q⁴/ω⁶.
- **D3. Sign of the bubbles.** (1.1) has Π^N₂₂(0,0) = −N(0)∫dξ/E = **−2/g_pair**; WP4 [4.1] and K2 use the opposite overall sign (Π^N₃₃ = +4Δ²/…, Π^N₂₂(0,0) = +2/g_pair), which with ε = ε_bg − v_CR̃ would give antiscreening. [4.1] is covariant under a global flip so R̃ only changes sign; (1.1) is the sign consistent with ε = ε_bg − v_CR̃ and is used here. Wording fix.
- **D4. CW4 §8 "missing step … O(Δ²_BCS/q²v_F²) corrections to Im ε^{BCS}".** Zero for the bare bubble while y² − 4 ≤ Q² (item 2). All corrections to Im are the vertex.
- **D5. CW4 §1, §8 specs omit the AB pole.** A KK of the continuum alone fails: at (y, Q) = (2.2, 5.64) the pole term is +1.10 in Re P̃, larger than Re P̃ itself. The f-sum also fails without it (11.6% at this Q).
- **D6. CW4 §8 "KK ∞ and cut bracket the unknown Im ε^{nor} above qv_F".** The clean Lindhard has no weight above qv_F(1 + q/2k_F), so the normal state is not the uncertainty. The real uncertainty was the vertex, and the spread does not bracket the GRPA answer.
- **D7. CW4 §8 "L^{BCS} → 0 as 1/ln²|y − 2| at threshold".** True for the bare bubble only once ln(1/(y − 2)) ≳ Q: at y − 2 = 10⁻¹⁰ the bare L/L^{nor} is 0.026 at Q = 5.2 but 1.46 at Q = 10³ (data/threshold.csv). GRPA also goes to zero, through Im P̃ → 0, not through Re.
- **D8.** Numerics of WP4 "§4.3 Al: Δ_BCS = 0.17 meV" vs CONVENTIONS row 33 (0.18 meV [E]): no effect on the tables, axis label only.
