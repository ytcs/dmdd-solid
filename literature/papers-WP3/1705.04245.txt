                                                                        The effects of the electron-phonon interaction on optical excitations can be understood in terms of
                                                                     exciton-phonon coupling, and require a careful treatment in low-dimensional materials with strongly
                                                                     bound excitons or strong electron-hole interaction in general. Through phonon absorption and emis-
                                                                     sion processes, the optically accessible excitons are scattered into otherwise optically dark finite-
                                                                     momentum exciton states. We derive a practical expression for the phonon-induced term of the
                                                                     exciton self-energy (denoted as the exciton-phonon self-energy) that gives the temperature depen-
                                                                     dence of the optical transition energies and their lifetime broadening resulting from the exciton’s
                                                                     interaction with the phonons. We illustrate this theory on a two-dimensional model, and show that
                                                                     our expression for the exciton-phonon self-energy differs qualitatively from previous expressions
                                                                     found in the literature that neglect the exciton binding or electron-hole correlations.

other excitations in a system), which yields the energy renor-
