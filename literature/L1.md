## 2. Lemma map: extracted formulas

### (a) First-principles phonon-assisted optical absorption

**Noffsinger, Kioupakis, Van de Walle, Louie, Cohen, PRL 108, 167402 (2012), arXiv 1202.3454** (Gaussian units)
- Eq. (1): α(ω) = 2 · (4π²e²)/(ω c n_r(ω)) · (1/V_cell) · (1/(N_k N_q)) Σ_{νijkq} |λ·(S₁+S₂)|² · P · δ(ε_{j,k+q} − ε_{ik} − ħω ± ħω_{νq}).
- Eq. (2): S₁(k,q) = Σ_m v_{im}(k) g_{mj,ν}(k,q) / (ε_{mk} − ε_{ik} − ħω + iΓ_{m,k}).
- Eq. (3): S₂(k,q) = Σ_m g_{im,ν}(k,q) v_{mj}(k+q) / (ε_{m,k+q} − ε_{ik} ± ħω_{νq} + iΓ_{m,k+q}).
- P = (n_{νq} + 1/2 ± 1/2)(f_{ik} − f_{j,k+q}). Upper sign is emission, lower sign is absorption. The leading 2 is spin.
- Numerics: GW on 6³, Wannier with 26 WFs, EPW. Fine grids are 40³ k and 40³ q for the edge (14 meV resolution) and 24³ for 1.1–3.4 eV. Intermediate Γ = 100 meV (insensitive). Theory is rigidly shifted by 0.15–0.23 eV to match the onset. Experiment: Macfarlane+ PR 111, 1245 (1958) near the edge; Braunstein+ PR 109, 695 (1958) in the visible.

**Lee et al. (EPW v6), npj Comput. Mater. 9, 156 (2023), arXiv 2302.08085, Sec. III.5** (SI units)
- Eq. (60): W_{mnν}(k,q;ω) = (2π/ħ) e² A₀² Σ_{β=±1} |e·[S_{1,mnν} + S_{2,mnνβ}]|² δ(ε_{m,k+q} − ε_{nk} − ħω + βħω_{qν}). Here β = +1 is emission and β = −1 is absorption.
- Eq. (61): S_{1,mnν} = Σ_j g_{mjν}(k,q) v_{jn}(k) / (ε_{jk} − ε_{nk} − ħω + iη) (photon first, then phonon).
- Eq. (62): S_{2,mnνβ} = Σ_j v_{mj}(k+q) g_{jnν}(k,q) / (ε_{j,k+q} − ε_{nk} + βħω_{qν} + iη) (phonon first).
- Eq. (63): Im ε(ω) = 2 (π e²)/(ε₀ Ω) (1/ω²) Σ_{mnν,β} ∫dk/Ω_BZ ∫dq/Ω_BZ |e·[S₁+S₂]|² P_{mnνβ} δ(…).
- Eq. (64): P_{mnνβ} = (n_{qν} + (1+β)/2) f_{nk}(1−f_{m,k+q}) − (n_{qν} + (1−β)/2)(1−f_{nk}) f_{m,k+q}.
- Eq. (65): α = ω Im ε / (c n(ω)). n is taken from experiment (Schinke+ AIP Adv. 5, 067168 (2015)) or obtained by Kramers–Kronig.
- Eq. (66) applies GW velocity renormalisation by a finite-difference ratio.
- Si example: GW gap 1.31 eV, shifted by −0.19 eV to 1.12 eV (RT); 32³ k and q grids; 50 meV Gaussians; 300 K. Theory underestimates experiment (Aspnes–Studna; Tiedje+ 1984) by at most 50%.
- The authors note that resonant singularities at the direct-gap onset are an "intrinsic limitation of second-order perturbation theory" (Tiwari & Giustino, in prep.).
- **Relevance:** replacing e·v_{jn} by the density vertex ⟨j,k+…|e^{iq·r}|n,k⟩ in Eqs. (61)–(62) gives the finite-q generalisation. No paper does this.

**Zacharias, Patrick, Giustino, PRL 115, 177401 (2015), arXiv 1510.06352** (Williams–Lax)
- Eq. (1): ε₂(ω;T) = Z⁻¹ Σ_n e^{−E_n/k_BT} ⟨ε₂(ω;x)⟩_n.
- Eq. (2): ε₂(ω;T) = ∫Π_ν dx_ν G[x_ν; ⟨x_ν²⟩_T] ε₂(ω;x), with ⟨x_ν²⟩_T = (2n_ν+1) l_ν².
- Eq. (3): ε₂(ω;x) = (2π/(mN)) (ω_p²/ω²) Σ_{cv} |p^x_{cv}|² δ(ε^x_c − ε^x_v − ħω).
- Eq. (4): p^x_{cv} = p_{cv} + Σ′_{nν} [p_{cn} g_{nvν}/(ε_v − ε_n) + g_{cnν} p_{nv}/(ε_c − ε_n)] x_ν/l_ν, with g_{mnν} = ⟨m|∂V/∂x_ν|n⟩ l_ν.
- Eq. (5): adiabatic HBB, ε₂ = (2π/(mN))(ω_p²/ω²) Σ_{cvν} |Σ′_n p_{cn}g_{nvν}/(ε_n−ε_v) + g_{cnν}p_{nv}/(ε_n−ε_v−ħω)|² δ(ε_c−ε_v−ħω)(2n_ν+1).
  - The paper notes this differs from HBB only by dropping ±ħω_ν in the denominators and the δ.
  - In the adiabatic form the T→0 weight is (2n+1) → 1, not the emission/absorption split (n+1, n).
- Near the edge HBB gives α ∝ ω⁻¹(ħω − E_g ± ħω_ν)². Hence [ω κ]^{1/2} is linear in ω, which is the standard Tauc extraction.
- E_g(T) single-oscillator fit: zero-point renormalisation a_B = 60–72 meV and Θ = 368–494 K. Experiment: 62 meV and 395 K.

**Zacharias & Giustino, PRB 94, 075125 (2016), arXiv 1604.02394** (special displacements)
- Eq. (3): ε₂(ω;T) = Π_ν ∫dx_ν e^{−x_ν²/2σ²_{ν,T}}/(√(2π)σ_{ν,T}) ε₂(ω;x). Eq. (4): σ²_{ν,T} = (2n_{ν,T}+1) l_ν², with l_ν = (ħ/2M_pΩ_ν)^{1/2}.
- Eq. (5) (ZG displacement): Δτ_{κα} = (M_p/M_κ)^{1/2} Σ_ν (−1)^{ν−1} e_{κα,ν} σ_{ν,T}. Eq. (10): ε₂^{1C} → ε₂(ω;T) as N → ∞.
- Eqs. (15)–(17) give p^x and ε^x expansions plus the Debye–Waller h. Eq. (20) is Allen–Heine; Eq. (21) is the width Γ²_{cv} = Σ_ν|g_{ccν}−g_{vvν}|²(2n+1).
- Eq. (23): indirect gaps with AH-renormalised Gaussian lineshape.
- Eq. (24): ε₂^{HBB} = (2π/(m_eN_e))(ω_p²/ω²) Σ_{cvν} |Σ′_n [p_{cn}g_{nvν}/(ε_v−ε_n) + g_{cnν}p_{nv}/(ε_c−ε_n)]|² δ(ε_c−ε_v−ħω)(2n_{ν,T}+1).
- Eq. (25): ε₂^{HBB} = Σ_ν ∂²ε₂(ω;x)/∂x_ν² σ²_{ν,T}, evaluated by finite differences (2N frozen-phonon runs).
- Note: "to capture the absorption onset, we need a supercell which can accommodate phonons connecting the band extrema."
- **Relevance:** Eq. (3) with ε₂(ω;x) replaced by the clamped-nuclei Im ε(q,ω;x) or the ELF(q,ω;x) of a ZG supercell is the cheapest route to a finite-q phonon-assisted ELF. EPW Eq. (71) is the supercell ε₂.

### (b) DM–electron rate in the dielectric formalism

**Knapen, Kozaczuk, Lin, PRD 104, 015031 (2021), arXiv 2101.08275** (natural, Gaussian α_em)
- Eq. (4): S(ω,k) ≡ (2π/V) Σ_{i,f} (e^{−βE_i}/Z) |⟨f|n_{−k}|i⟩|² δ(ω + E_i − E_f).
- Eq. (6): S(ω,k) = (k²/(2πα_em)) · 1/(1−e^{−βω}) · Im[−1/ε_L(ω,k)].
- Eq. (10): R = (1/ρ_T)(ρ_χ/m_χ)(πσ̄_e/μ²_{χe}) ∫d³v f_χ(v) ∫d³k/(2π)³ ∫dω δ(ω + k²/2m_χ − k·v) |F_DM(k)|² S(ω,k).
- Eq. (11): σ̄_e = μ²_{χe} g_e² g_χ² / [π((αm_e)² + m_V²)²]. Eq. (12): F_DM(k) = (m_V² + α²m_e²)/(m_V² + k²).
- Eq. (13), master formula: R = (1/ρ_T)(ρ_χ/m_χ)(σ̄_e/μ²_{χe})(π/α_em) ∫d³v f_χ ∫d³k/(2π)³ k²|F_DM|² ∫dω/(2π) [1/(1−e^{−βω})] Im[−1/ε_L(ω,k)] δ(ω + k²/2m_χ − k·v).
- Eq. (16): |f_crystal(k,ω)|² = k⁵V_cell/(8π²α_em²m_e²) · Im ε^{RPA}(ω,k) (link to QEdark).
- ELF: GPAW RPA with local-field effects, TB09 functional, scissor to the measured T = 0 gap, 70 bands, 8³ k grid (Si), cutoff |k| ≲ 22 keV.
- Ionisation model: Q = 1 + ⌊(ω − E_g)/ε⌋ with Si E_g = 1.11 eV and ε = 3.6 eV. 2e⁻ ≈ 4.7 eV.
- Halo: v_esc = 500, v₀ = 220, v_e = 240 km/s. Reach is 95% CL, kg·yr, zero background, threshold "set by the electron band gap" (1e⁻).
- Fig. 3 x-axis spans 10⁻¹ to 10³ MeV for Si/Ge; the exact curve endpoint is plot-only.

**Hochberg, Kahn, Kurinsky, Lehmann, Yu, Berggren, PRL 127, 151802 (2021), arXiv 2101.08263** (Heaviside–Lorentz e)
- Eq. (1): Γ(v_χ) = ∫d³q/(2π)³ |V(q)|² [2 (q²/e²) Im(−1/ε(q,ω_q))], with ω_q = q·v_χ − q²/(2m_χ).
- Eq. (2): W ≡ Im(−1/ε) = Im ε/|ε|². V(q) = g_χg_e/(q² + m²_{φ,V}). σ̄_e = (μ²_{eχ}/π)|V(q₀)|² with q₀ = αm_e.
- Eq. (S.7): Im(−1/ε(q,ω)) = (πe²/q²) Σ_f |⟨f|ρ̂(q)|0⟩|² δ(ω_f − ω). The paper notes "Eq. (9) of [54] is missing a factor of π".
- Eq. (S.9): dR/dω = ρ_χ/(2π²e²ρ_Tm_χ) ∫dq q³|V(q)|² W(q,ω) η(v_min), with v_min = ω/q + q/2m_χ (ρ_T is mass density). Γ is therefore per unit target volume.
- Halo: v₀ = 220, v_esc = 550, v_E = 232 km/s. Reach is drawn at 3 events/kg/yr.
- The paper also states: "Of course, the electrons will also couple to ions … we will assume that these contributions are negligible". The ion term is exactly where the phonon-assisted weight would enter.
- **Normalisation cross-check:** KKL Eq. (13) and Hochberg Eq. (1) agree once e²_HL = 4πα. Using |V|² = πσ̄_e|F_DM|²/μ², the 2q²/e² factor becomes q²/(2πα) at T = 0, which is KKL's S/ELF factor.

### (c) Classic indirect-absorption formula and Si data

**Rajkanan–Singh–Shewchun form** (Solid-State Electron. 22, 793 (1979)), as used and refit in **Stanford+ 2010.15844, Eq. (4)–(7)**:
- α(T) = Σ_{i,j=1,2} C_iA_j [ (E_γ − E_{gj}(T) + E_{pi})²/(e^{E_{pi}/kT} − 1) + (E_γ − E_{gj}(T) − E_{pi})²/(1 − e^{−E_{pi}/kT}) ] + A_d(E_γ − E_{gd}(T))^{1/2}.
  - The first term is phonon absorption (Bose n) and the second is emission (n+1). At T→0 only emission survives: Σ C_iA_j(E−E_gj−E_pi)².
- E_{gj}(T) = E_{gj}(0) − βT²/(T+γ), with β = 7.021×10⁻⁴ eV/K and γ = 1108 K.
- E_p(TA) = 18.27 meV and E_p(TO) = 57.73 meV. These equal Macfarlane+ θ = 212 K and 670 K (×k_B, my arithmetic).
- C_TA = 5.5 and C_TO = 4.0. E_{g2}(0) = 2.5 eV is fixed.
- Stanford fit (0.5, 5, 77, 295 K data):
  - E_{g1}(0) = 1.134(4) eV. This is an effective fit parameter, not the true gap.
  - A₂ = 6(3)×10³ eV⁻² cm⁻¹; A₁(T) = c₀e^{−c₁T} with c₀ = 325(6) eV⁻² cm⁻¹ and c₁ = 1.7(1)×10⁻³ K⁻¹.
  - "No statistically significant difference in σ_p.e. for temperatures up to 77 K."
- The direct term A_d, E_gd is not refit by Stanford. My recollection of Rajkanan is A_d = 1.052×10⁶ cm⁻¹ eV^{−1/2} and E_gd(0) = 3.2 eV **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Measured photon energies (Stanford Table 1): 1.21, 1.28, 1.31, 1.58, 1.88, 1.94, 2.34, 2.77 eV. Points are at 0.5 K; values are figure-only.
- Gap: E_g(0) = 1.170 eV (Bludau, Onton, Heinke, JAP 45, 1846 (1974)) and 1.169 eV (Alex+ JAP 79, 6943 (1996)). Both are quoted in Ramanathan–Kurinsky 2004.10709, Table 1 (secondary).
- Excitonic offset: the optical edge sits ≈ 15 meV below E_g(0). The MQ "excitonic gap" is ≈ 1.155 eV **[UNVERIFIED, from memory: E_x ≈ 14.7 meV]**. This explains why fitted E_g1(0) values (1.134, 1.1557) are below 1.17.
- LA and LO assisting-phonon energies were not found in an open full text **[UNVERIFIED: commonly LA ≈ 46 meV, LO ≈ 56 meV]**. R–K Table 1 quotes DFT optical phonons TO 59 meV and LO 62 meV (zone-centre-ish, from their Ref. 17).

### (d) Data-driven ELF coverage of E_g < ω < E_dir at small q
See verdict rows 2d and 2d′. In addition:
- **KKL / DarkELF GPAW** is a T = 0 static lattice. In such an ELF, the only weight below E_dir ≈ 3.3–3.4 eV is from transitions whose crystal-momentum mismatch is supplied by q itself (q ≈ |k_c − k_v| mod G). There is no phonon term.
- **DarkELF Sec. V** (sub-gap phonon ELF): Si data are optical IR at 6 K (their Ref. 88), plus a DFT multiphonon calculation at 6 K (Ref. 90).
- **Hochberg 1608.01994** used Si 77 K σ₁ (Holland+ IEEE TED 50 (2003)) for absorption only.

### (e) Si sub-MeV reach as computed (lowest m_χ shown, thresholds)
- **EXCEED-DM 2105.05253:**
  - Eq. (33): m_χ^min = 2E_g/v_max² = 0.25 MeV (E_g/eV)(840 km/s / v_max)². This gives 0.28 MeV for Si (E_g = 1.11 eV) and 0.17 MeV for Ge.
  - Reach (Fig. 14) is 3 events, kg·yr, Q ≥ 1 (also Q ≥ 10 in Fig. 15). The x-axis starts at 10⁻¹ MeV; the curve endpoint is plot-only.
  - Ionisation model: Q = 1 + ⌊(ω−E_g)/ε⌋ with ε = 3.6 eV (Si) and 2.9 eV (Ge), Eq. (47).
  - Screening uses an analytic ε(q,ω) (Eq. 46) with ε₀ = 11.3, α = 1.563, ω_p = 16.6 eV, q_TF = 4.13 keV (Si).
  - The 2210.14917 code paper uses the same Q formula with E_g = 1.11 eV and ε = 3.6 eV.
- **QCDark 2306.14944:**
  - Si uses the Ramanathan–Kurinsky ionisation model at 100 K (2004.10709). The alternative step model has E_gap = 1.1 eV and E_pp = 3.8 eV, and the 1e⁻ and 2e⁻ bins differ significantly between the two.
  - The reach plot x-axis spans 10⁻¹ to 10³ MeV (Si, Ge).
- **QCDark2 2603.12326:** halo reach plots start at 1 MeV (axis 10⁰–10³). Sub-MeV Si appears only for solar-reflected DM (50 keV and 0.5 MeV examples). R–K at 100 K; 90% CL.
- **DarkELF 2104.12786:** Si uses a 2e⁻ threshold (Mermin) in Fig. 4. For 1e⁻ "the GPAW method is recommended", but the ELF grids are "fairly noisy for ω ≲ 2E_gap … O(1) theoretical uncertainties".
- **Ramanathan–Kurinsky 2004.10709:**
  - ε_eh(E_r) = E_r for E_g ≤ E_r < 2E_g (exactly 1 pair), and F = 0 below 2E_g.
  - E_g(T) = E_g(0) − aT²/(T+b), with E_g(0) = 1.1692 eV, a = 4.9×10⁻⁴ eV and b = 655 K (Alex+ 1996).
  - Every model above treats any ω ≥ E_g as ≥ 1 e⁻. The open question is whether S(q,ω) is non-zero there at small q, which it is not in a static lattice.

**Context computation (mine, not literature).**
- Inputs: Si, a = 5.431 Å, 2π/a = 2.28 keV; CBM valleys at 0.85 X, so |Δk|_min ≈ 1.94 keV; v_max ≈ 794 km/s.
- Static-lattice VBM→CBM needs ω ≤ q v_max − q²/2m_χ at q ≈ 1.94 keV, which with E_g = 1.17 eV requires m_χ ≳ 0.47 MeV.
- Phonon-assisted transitions need only ω ≥ E_g + ω_ph at any q, which reaches m_χ ≳ 2(E_g + ω_TA)/v_max² ≈ 0.34 MeV.
- The window m_χ ≈ 0.34–0.47 MeV is therefore phonon-assisted only.
- This should be checked by the derivation agent with the exact halo and band edges.
